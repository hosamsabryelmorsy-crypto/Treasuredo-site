About Treasuredo

Treasuredo is a vintage shop based in Giza, Egypt. We find, check and photograph vintage watches, sunglasses and small collectibles, one piece at a time.

Where we sell

Every piece on this site is sold through our eBay store, so your purchase is covered by eBay buyer protection. We have been selling on eBay since 2022, with more than 1,000 feedback ratings and 99% positive feedback.

Shipping

Free DHL Express shipping worldwide with tracking. Watches ship within 4 business days and other items within 2 business days. Import duties, where they apply, are paid by the buyer.

Returns

Returns are accepted within 30 days. The buyer pays the return shipping.

Condition

These are vintage pieces and show their age. Each listing has many real photos and an honest condition note. Watches are tested and working at the time of listing.
