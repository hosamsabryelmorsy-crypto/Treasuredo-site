How to Find a Watch From Your Birth Year: Dating Vintage Watches

To find a watch from your birth year, look for brands that are easy to date from serial numbers or codes, such as Seiko, Bulova, Omega and Longines, and confirm the year with the seller using the case back or movement numbers. A "birth year watch" is best when the date can be proven, not just estimated from the style.

Brands that are easiest to date

  • Seiko: the case back serial number usually starts with the last digit of the year and then the month. Combined with the model's production years, this gives the exact year and month.
  • Bulova: from 1950, a code on the case back or movement: L for the 1950s, M for the 1960s and N for the 1970s, plus a digit for the year (M2 = 1962).
  • Omega and Longines: the movement serial number can be compared with published serial charts to give an approximate year.
  • Citizen and Orient: case back numbers often help, but always cross-check with the model's production years.

Steps to confirm the year

  1. Ask the seller for clear photos of the case back (outside and inside) and the movement.
  2. Read the serial number or date code.
  3. Check it against the brand's dating method.
  4. Make sure the model was made in that period; a serial alone can be ambiguous by a decade on some brands.
  5. Check for a dated original warranty card if available.

When the year cannot be proven

Some brands do not have accessible serial records. In that case, the style, movement and case details can narrow it to a range of a few years. Be honest in the gift note: "around 1972" is better than an unproven exact year.

Tips for searching

  • Search by year plus brand, for example "1975 Seiko" or "1968 Omega".
  • Ask sellers if they can date a specific watch.
  • Choose a watch in good working order; the date is meaningful only if the watch can be worn.

Frequently asked questions

How can I tell what year my watch was made?

Use the serial number or date code on the case back or movement and check it against the brand's dating method.

Which watches are easiest to date?

Seiko and Bulova are among the easiest, with codes that show the year directly.

How do I date a Seiko?

The first digit of the case back serial usually gives the last digit of the year and the next digit the month.

How do Bulova date codes work?

From 1950, L means the 1950s, M the 1960s and N the 1970s, followed by a digit for the year.
