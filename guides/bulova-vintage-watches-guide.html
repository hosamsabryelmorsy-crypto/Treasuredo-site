Vintage Bulova Watches and the Accutron: How to Date and Buy One

Vintage Bulova watches are easy to date: from 1950 the case back or movement usually carries a code where L means the 1950s, M the 1960s and N the 1970s, followed by a digit for the year (M2 = 1962). The most famous vintage Bulova is the Accutron, launched in 1960, which keeps time with a tuning fork and gives off a gentle hum instead of a tick.

Bulova in brief

  • Bulova was founded in 1875 in New York by Joseph Bulova.
  • The company had a factory in Biel, Switzerland, so many vintage Bulovas have Swiss-made movements.
  • Bulova is remembered for early broadcast advertising, including one of the first TV commercials in 1941.
  • Caravelle was Bulova's lower-priced sub-brand.

How to read Bulova date codes

  1. Look on the case back (outside or inside) or on the movement for a letter and number.
  2. L = 1950s, M = 1960s, N = 1970s. The digit gives the year in that decade: N4 = 1974.
  3. Before 1950, Bulova used symbol codes; these are harder to read and best checked against a reference chart.
  4. If case and movement codes are far apart, parts may have been swapped.

The Accutron

  • The Accutron uses a tuning fork that vibrates at 360 times per second, driven by a battery.
  • The seconds hand sweeps smoothly and the watch hums faintly; hold it to your ear to check.
  • Spaceview models have no dial, so you can see the tuning fork and coils.
  • Common calibres are the 214 (crown at the back on many models) and the 218 (crown at the side, often with date).
  • Accutron movements were used in instruments for the US space programme.

What to check on an Accutron

  1. It must hum and sweep: a silent Accutron may need only a battery, or may have a broken coil, which is costly.
  2. Battery: the original mercury cells are no longer sold; a watchmaker fits a modern equivalent with the correct voltage.
  3. Index wheel: this tiny wheel wears; erratic timekeeping often points to it.
  4. Spaceview originality: some Spaceviews were converted from dial models; check the case back and markings.

What to check on mechanical Bulovas

  • Dial printing should be sharp and centred; repaints are common on gold-filled dress watches.
  • Gold-filled cases (marked on the back) wear better than plating but can show base metal at edges.
  • Ask for a movement photo; it should be Bulova signed and match the dial.

Questions to ask the seller

  • What date code is on the case back and movement?
  • For an Accutron: does it hum, and does the seconds hand sweep smoothly?
  • What battery is fitted, and at what voltage?
  • Is the case gold-filled, plated or solid gold?

Clear answers with photos usually mean an honest watch; vague answers are a reason to look elsewhere.

Frequently asked questions

How do I date a vintage Bulova?

From 1950, find the letter and digit code: L = 1950s, M = 1960s, N = 1970s, with the digit giving the year, so M2 = 1962.

Why does my Bulova Accutron hum?

It keeps time with a tuning fork vibrating 360 times per second, which makes a faint hum.

What is a Bulova Accutron Spaceview?

An Accutron with no dial, showing the tuning fork movement.

Are vintage Bulova watches Swiss or American?

Bulova is an American company, but many vintage movements were made in its Swiss factory in Biel.
