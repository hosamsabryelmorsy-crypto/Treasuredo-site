Vintage Carrera and Porsche Design by Carrera Sunglasses: A Buying Guide

Carrera is an Austrian eyewear brand founded in 1956 by Wilhelm Anger, known for sport sunglasses, ski goggles and frames made from Optyl, the material Anger developed. Its most collected vintage pieces are the Porsche Design by Carrera models of the late 1970s and 1980s, including the 5621 with interchangeable lenses. Check the model number, the hinges and the lenses before buying.

Carrera in brief

  • Founded in Austria in 1956 by Wilhelm Anger.
  • Named after the Carrera Panamericana road race.
  • Known for sport and aviator sunglasses and ski goggles.
  • Made Porsche Design eyewear under licence from the late 1970s.
  • Later became part of the Safilo group.

Porsche Design by Carrera

  • 5621: an aviator with interchangeable lenses, swapped by sliding them out of the frame.
  • 5652, 5657 and similar: metal frames, often aviator or round shapes.
  • Many come with original cases and cards, which add value.

How to read the markings

  • Model number (four digits) and colour code inside the temple, for example "5652 40".
  • Size: lens width and bridge in millimetres.
  • "Made in Austria" or "Germany" on many vintage pieces.
  • "Optyl" on plastic frames.

What to check before you buy

  1. Interchangeable lenses (5621): check the lens release works smoothly and both lens sets are included if advertised.
  2. Metal frames: look for plating wear at the bridge and temples.
  3. Nose pads: original pads yellow with age; replacements are easy.
  4. Optyl frames: check for cracks near hinges.
  5. Box and card: original packaging adds value.

General checks for vintage frames

  1. Hinges: should open and close smoothly with no wobble; loose hinges can often be tightened, cracked hinge areas cannot.
  2. Frame material: look for cracks, white haze or shrinkage, especially near hinges and the bridge.
  3. Lenses: check for scratches, coating damage or delamination; replacement lenses can be fitted but originals add value.
  4. Size: compare lens width, bridge and temple length with glasses you already own.

Frequently asked questions

Who founded Carrera eyewear?

Wilhelm Anger, in Austria in 1956.

What is Porsche Design by Carrera?

Porsche Design eyewear made under licence by Carrera from the late 1970s.

What is special about the Porsche Design 5621?

It has interchangeable lenses that slide out of the frame.

Where were vintage Carrera sunglasses made?

Many are marked Made in Austria or Germany.
