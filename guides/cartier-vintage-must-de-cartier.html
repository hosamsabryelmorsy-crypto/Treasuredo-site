Must de Cartier Tank: What Vermeil Means and How to Check a Vintage One

The Must de Cartier Tank, introduced in 1977, is a Cartier Tank with a case of vermeil: sterling silver (925) plated with gold. That is why it costs much less than a solid gold Tank while keeping the same classic shape. Most are quartz, the case back is marked "Argent 925" or similar, and the main thing to check is how much of the gold plating has worn away.

What "vermeil" means

  • Vermeil is sterling silver with a layer of gold on top.
  • The case back typically shows "Must de Cartier", "Argent 925" (or "925"), a reference number and a serial number.
  • Because the base is silver, worn areas show a white-silver colour, not brass.

Typical features

  • Movement: mostly quartz; some early pieces are manual-wind.
  • Crown: beaded crown with a blue cabochon stone.
  • Dials: plain colour dials (black, burgundy, navy, ivory) or Roman numeral dials with the "secret signature" sometimes hidden in a numeral.
  • Strap: leather with a Cartier buckle or deployant clasp.

What to check before you buy

  1. Plating wear: look at the corners, the brancards (the vertical sides) and the case back edges. Silver showing through is normal with use, but large patches reduce value. Re-plating is possible.
  2. Dial: colour dials should be even with no fading or spotting. Lacquered dials crack with age.
  3. Crown cabochon: should be present, undamaged and properly seated.
  4. Movement: ask for a photo. It should look clean with no battery corrosion. Quartz pieces should run on a new battery.
  5. Case back markings: should be crisp and consistent: brand, 925, reference and serial number.
  6. Buckle: original Cartier buckles add value.

How to spot a fake Must Tank

  • Fakes often have the wrong proportions: too thick, or brancards that are too wide.
  • Printing on the dial should be sharp and fine; thick or blurred text is a warning.
  • The crown cabochon on fakes is often glass or poorly cut.
  • Genuine Must Tanks are silver underneath; a fake in plated brass shows yellow or brown metal at wear points.

Sizing

Must Tanks come in small (ladies') and larger sizes. Measure case width and length without the crown and check which size you are buying.

Questions to ask the seller

  • Is the case back marked 925?
  • Does it run on a new battery?
  • How much plating wear is there at corners and brancards?
  • Is the buckle original Cartier?

Frequently asked questions

What does vermeil mean on a Cartier watch?

Vermeil is sterling silver (925) plated with gold.

When was the Must de Cartier Tank introduced?

In 1977.

Is the Must de Cartier Tank solid gold?

No. It is gold-plated sterling silver, which is why it costs less than a solid gold Tank.

Can a Must Tank be re-plated?

Yes. A jeweller or watchmaker can re-plate worn vermeil, though original condition is usually preferred by collectors.
