Vintage Christian Dior Sunglasses: Optyl Frames of the 1970s-80s and How to Date Them

Vintage Christian Dior sunglasses from the 1970s and 1980s were mostly made under licence in Austria and Germany, many in Optyl, a lightweight material that holds its shape. They are recognised by four-digit model numbers followed by a colour code, such as "2187 30", printed inside the temple with "Optyl" and the country of manufacture. Oversized butterfly, aviator and Monsieur men's frames are the most sought after.

How to identify a vintage Dior frame

  • Model number: usually four digits, for example 2056, 2187 or 2306.
  • Colour code: a two-digit number after the model, such as 30 or 80.
  • Material: "Optyl" printed on many frames; others are metal or mixed metal and Optyl.
  • Origin: "Made in Austria" or "Germany" on many 1970s-80s frames.
  • Size: lens width and bridge, for example 57-15.
  • Logo: "Christian Dior" or "CD" on the temples, sometimes with decorative metal details.

Popular styles

  • Oversized and butterfly: large women's frames typical of the 1970s.
  • Aviators: metal and Optyl aviators from the late 1970s-80s.
  • Monsieur: the men's line, often with heavier frames.
  • Rimless: lens-only designs with metal temples.

Why Optyl matters

Optyl is lighter than acetate and keeps its shape. It is a sign of quality production in the period. Optyl frames need warming before adjustment; forcing them cold can crack them.

General checks for vintage frames

  1. Hinges: should open and close smoothly with no wobble; loose hinges can often be tightened, cracked hinge areas cannot.
  2. Frame material: look for cracks, white haze or shrinkage, especially near hinges and the bridge.
  3. Lenses: check for scratches, coating damage or delamination; replacement lenses can be fitted but originals add value.
  4. Size: compare lens width, bridge and temple length with glasses you already own.

NOS frames

Unworn new-old-stock Dior frames still turn up with original stickers and cases. Check for original lens stickers and unworn hinges and temple tips.

Frequently asked questions

Where were vintage Dior sunglasses made?

Many 1970s-80s Dior frames were made under licence in Austria and Germany.

What is Optyl on Dior sunglasses?

A lightweight material that holds its shape, used for many vintage Dior frames.

How do I read a Dior sunglasses model number?

Usually a four-digit model number followed by a two-digit colour code, printed inside the temple.

Can Optyl frames be adjusted?

Yes, but only after warming; forcing them cold can crack the frame.
