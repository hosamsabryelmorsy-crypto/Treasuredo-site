How Do You Set the Day and Date on a Vintage Watch? Day-Date Explained

On most vintage day-date watches, the day and date change automatically around midnight, and you set them either with a quickset crown position or by turning the hands through 24 hours. The key rule for many mechanical watches is to avoid setting the date when the time shows between about 9 pm and 3 am, because the date mechanism is already engaged and forcing it can damage it.

How the change works

  • Instant change: the date jumps at midnight in a moment.
  • Semi-instant or slow change: on many vintage movements, the date and day roll over gradually between about 11 pm and 1 am. This is normal.
  • Month ends: after a 30-day month or February, you must advance the date by hand. Only perpetual calendars handle this automatically.

Setting methods

  1. Quickset: pull the crown to the first position and turn one way for the date and the other way for the day. Many Japanese automatics work this way.
  2. Push-crown day set: on some older watches you press the crown in from the normal position to advance the day.
  3. No quickset: older day-date watches without quickset are set by turning the hands round and round; each full 24 hours advances the day and date by one.

The "danger zone"

On many mechanical day-date watches, the change mechanism engages a couple of hours before midnight. Using quickset during that window can bend or break parts. Safe method: set the hands to around 6 o'clock, set the day and date to yesterday, then advance the hands forward through midnight to today's date, and finally set the correct time.

Bilingual day wheels

Many Japanese and some Swiss watches have day wheels in two languages, for example English and Spanish, or English and Japanese. Quickset scrolls through both; just stop on the language you want.

What to check before buying

  1. Day and date should both change around midnight and sit centred in their windows.
  2. Quickset should advance one step per click without slipping.
  3. The day and date discs should be clean, with clear printing.
  4. Ask for a short video of the date changing, or confirmation from the seller.
  5. A date that changes at midday means the time is set 12 hours off; that is not a fault.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my watch date change at noon?

The time is set 12 hours off. Advance the hands by 12 hours.

When should I not set the date on my watch?

Avoid using quickset when the time is between about 9 pm and 3 am on many mechanical watches.

Why does my day-date change slowly?

Many vintage movements roll the date over gradually between about 11 pm and 1 am; this is normal.

What is a bilingual day wheel?

A day disc printed in two languages, such as English and Spanish.
