Vintage Ebel Sport Classic: The Wave Bracelet, Five-Screw Bezel and What to Check

The vintage Ebel watch most people search for is the Sport Classic, launched in 1977, recognised by its wave-shaped bracelet links and the five screws on the bezel. Most are quartz, some are automatic, and they come in steel, steel and gold, or solid gold. A good one has a tight bracelet, a clean dial and a case and bezel that have not been over-polished.

Ebel in brief

  • Ebel was founded in 1911 in La Chaux-de-Fonds by Eugène Blum and Alice Lévy; the name comes from their initials: Eugène Blum Et Lévy.
  • The brand used the slogan "The Architects of Time".
  • Ebel became part of Movado Group in 2004.

The Sport Classic

  • Wave bracelet: curved links that interlock, designed to sit smoothly on the wrist.
  • Five-screw bezel: five screws on the bezel, a key design feature.
  • Sizes: men's, mid-size and ladies' versions. Check the case width before buying, as the smaller sizes are very common.
  • Movements: mostly quartz; automatic versions exist and are worth more.

What to check before you buy

  1. Bracelet stretch: hold the watch by the case with the bracelet hanging down. A tight bracelet stays almost straight; a worn one sags in a curve and the links show gaps.
  2. Bracelet length: missing links are hard to find. Ask for the wrist size it fits.
  3. Bezel screws: all five should be present, matching and not chewed.
  4. Gold parts: on two-tone models, check the gold for wear through to steel.
  5. Quartz models: look for battery corrosion in the movement photo, and ask whether it runs on a new battery.
  6. Dial: should be clean with crisp printing; some have date windows which must be centred.
  7. Case back: look for Ebel signing and reference numbers.

Other vintage Ebel lines

  • Beluga: elegant ladies' watches, often with rounded cases.
  • 1911: a sportier line named after the founding year.
  • Chronographs: automatic Ebel chronographs from the 1980s-90s are well regarded by collectors.

Sizing

Men's Sport Classics are moderate in size and slim; mid-size and ladies' models are much smaller. Always check the case width without crown in the listing.

Questions to ask the seller

  • Is the movement quartz or automatic?
  • What wrist size does the bracelet fit, and are there spare links?
  • Does it run on a new battery (quartz)?
  • Are all five bezel screws original?

Frequently asked questions

What does Ebel stand for?

Eugène Blum Et Lévy, the founders who started the company in 1911.

When was the Ebel Sport Classic introduced?

In 1977, with its wave-link bracelet and five-screw bezel.

Are Ebel Sport Classics quartz or automatic?

Most are quartz; automatic versions exist and are worth more.

How do I check an Ebel bracelet for stretch?

Hold the watch by the case: a tight bracelet stays nearly straight, a stretched one sags with visible gaps between links.
