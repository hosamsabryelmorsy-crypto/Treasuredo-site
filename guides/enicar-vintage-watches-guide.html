Vintage Enicar Watches: Sherpa, Star Jewels and How to Buy One

Vintage Enicar watches are Swiss watches best known for the Sherpa sports line of the late 1950s-60s, with dress lines such as Star Jewels and Ultrasonic. The name is the founder's surname, Racine, spelled backwards. Sherpa divers and chronographs are the most collected; dress models are an affordable way into a respected Swiss brand.

Enicar in brief

  • Enicar was founded in 1913 by Ariste Racine, whose surname reversed gives the brand name.
  • The logo is a ringed planet, often seen on dials, crowns and case backs.
  • Enicar made its own movements, so most vintage pieces have Enicar-signed calibres.
  • The Sherpa line is associated with Swiss Himalayan expeditions of the 1950s, which gave it its name.

Models collectors look for

  • Sherpa divers: many use the "Super Compressor" case, where water pressure presses the case back tighter, and an internal rotating bezel turned by a second crown.
  • Sherpa Graph and other chronographs: the most valuable Enicars.
  • Star Jewels and Ultrasonic: everyday and dress automatics. "Ultrasonic" referred to the ultrasonic cleaning of parts during assembly.

What to check

  1. Super Compressor case back: genuine cases are marked as such; two crowns on a diver usually means one controls the inner bezel.
  2. Inner bezel: should turn smoothly with its crown. A stuck bezel needs a watchmaker.
  3. Dial: look for crisp printing and an evenly printed planet logo. Repaints are common on popular Sherpa dials.
  4. Movement: should be Enicar-signed and match the dial (automatic, date, chronograph).
  5. Crowns: signed crowns with the planet logo are a plus; replacements are common.
  6. Lume: aged lume should match between hands and dial; mismatched colours suggest replaced parts.

Water resistance

A Super Compressor case was designed to seal better as depth increases, but after 50-60 years the gaskets are worn out. Treat any vintage Enicar as not water-resistant until it has new gaskets and a pressure test.

Is a vintage Enicar a good buy?

Yes. Enicar's own movements are well made, the designs are distinctive, and dress models cost much less than better-known brands. Pay a premium only for Sherpa models with original dials, bezels and crowns.

Questions to ask before you buy

  • When was it last serviced, and was the movement fully overhauled?
  • Can you show the movement and the inside of the case back?
  • Are the crown, hands and crystal original or replaced?
  • Does it keep time and, on date models, change the date around midnight?

Sizing and wear

Sherpa divers with Super Compressor cases are among the larger watches of their era and wear well on modern wrists. Star Jewels and Ultrasonic dress models are smaller and slimmer, so check case width without crown and lug width before choosing a strap. Integrated or unusual bracelets on some 1970s models are hard to replace if missing.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the name Enicar come from?

It is the founder Ariste Racine's surname spelled backwards.

What is an Enicar Super Compressor?

A Sherpa case design where water pressure presses the case back tighter, usually with an internal bezel turned by a second crown.

What does Ultrasonic mean on an Enicar dial?

It referred to the ultrasonic cleaning of parts during assembly.

Which Enicar models are most collectible?

Sherpa divers and chronographs, especially with original dials and bezels.
