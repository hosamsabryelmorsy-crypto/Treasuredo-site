Vintage Fendi Watches: Designer Quartz from Italy and How to Check One

Vintage Fendi watches are designer quartz watches, usually Swiss made, built around Fendi's fashion style and the "FF" logo. They are bought for their design rather than their movements, so the main checks are a running quartz movement, an undamaged dial and crystal, original bracelet or strap, and plating without wear-through.

What to know about the brand

  • Fendi is an Italian fashion house founded in Rome in 1925.
  • The double-F logo was designed by Karl Lagerfeld in the 1960s and appears on many Fendi watches, dials and clasps.
  • Fendi watches are typically Swiss-made quartz.

How to identify a vintage Fendi watch

  • Dial: Fendi name or FF logo, often with "Swiss Made".
  • Case back: Fendi name, model number and materials.
  • Bracelet or clasp: often signed with the Fendi name or FF logo.
  • Design: square and rectangular cases, interchangeable straps or bezels on some models, and leather straps in Fendi patterns.

Checking a vintage designer quartz watch

  1. Running: ask whether it runs on a fresh battery. A quartz watch that will not start may need only cleaning, but a failed circuit can mean a replacement movement.
  2. Battery corrosion: a dead battery left inside for years can leak. Ask for a photo inside the case back.
  3. Plating: most vintage fashion watches are gold plated. Check lug tips, crown, clasp and bracelet edges for base metal showing through.
  4. Crystal: mineral crystals scratch less than acrylic but chip at the edges.

What affects value

  • Distinctive designs with the FF motif are more sought after than plain models.
  • Original bracelets and signed clasps add value.
  • Box, papers and spare straps or bezels raise the price.

Fakes

Check that the FF logo is crisp and evenly spaced, the case back is properly engraved and the movement is a clean Swiss quartz. Cheap, light cases and blurred logos are warning signs.

Sizing

Many vintage Fendi watches are ladies' sizes. Check case width and bracelet length before buying.

Questions to ask the seller

  • Does it run on a new battery?
  • What model number is on the case back?
  • Is the bracelet or strap original?
  • Are spare straps, bezels, box or papers included?

Frequently asked questions

Are vintage Fendi watches Swiss made?

Most are Swiss-made quartz; check the dial and case back.

Who designed the Fendi FF logo?

Karl Lagerfeld, in the 1960s.

When was Fendi founded?

In 1925 in Rome.

What should I check on a vintage Fendi watch?

That it runs on a new battery, the plating has no wear-through, and the bracelet and clasp are original.
