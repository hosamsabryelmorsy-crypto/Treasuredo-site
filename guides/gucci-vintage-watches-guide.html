Vintage Gucci Watches: The 11/12 Change-Bezel, Bangles and How to Check Them

Vintage Gucci watches are Swiss-made quartz fashion watches, best known for the 11/12 "change bezel" model with snap-on coloured bezels and for slim bangle watches. Gucci watches were made from 1972 by Severin Montres, which Gucci later bought in 1997. To check one, read the model number on the case back, make sure the movement runs, and count the bezels or bangle parts before you buy.

Gucci watch history in brief

  • Gucci watches began in 1972 under a licence with Severin Montres.
  • Gucci acquired Severin Montres in 1997.
  • Most vintage Gucci watches are Swiss-made quartz.

The 11/12 interchangeable bezel

  • The 11/12 came with a set of coloured bezels that snap on and off, so the wearer could match outfits.
  • A complete set in the original case is worth much more than the watch with one bezel.
  • Check each bezel clicks firmly into place and is not cracked at the clips.

Bangle models

  • Several vintage Gucci watches are bangles: a rigid bracelet that slips over the hand or opens on a hinge.
  • Bangles come in sizes. Check the inner measurements, because a bangle cannot be adjusted like a link bracelet.
  • Look for dents and bends; a bent bangle is hard to restore without marks.

Reading the case back

Gucci case backs usually show the Gucci name, a model number (such as 11/12) and "Swiss Made". The model number is the easiest way to identify the watch and compare it with others.

Checking a vintage designer quartz watch

  1. Running: ask whether it runs on a fresh battery. A quartz watch that will not start may need only cleaning, but a failed circuit can mean a replacement movement.
  2. Battery corrosion: a dead battery left inside for years can leak. Ask for a photo inside the case back.
  3. Plating: most vintage fashion watches are gold plated. Check lug tips, crown, clasp and bracelet edges for base metal showing through.
  4. Crystal: mineral crystals scratch less than acrylic but chip at the edges.

Fakes

Gucci is widely copied. Genuine pieces have crisp engraving on the case back, sharp dial printing and a Swiss quartz movement. Light weight, blurred logos and generic movements are warning signs.

Questions to ask the seller

  • What model number is on the case back?
  • How many bezels come with it (11/12)?
  • Does it run on a new battery?
  • What wrist size does the bangle or bracelet fit?

Frequently asked questions

Who made vintage Gucci watches?

Severin Montres, under licence from 1972; Gucci bought the company in 1997.

What is the Gucci 11/12 watch?

A quartz model with interchangeable snap-on coloured bezels.

Are vintage Gucci watches Swiss made?

Yes, most are Swiss-made quartz watches.

Can a Gucci bangle watch be resized?

Not easily; rigid bangles come in fixed sizes, so check measurements before buying.
