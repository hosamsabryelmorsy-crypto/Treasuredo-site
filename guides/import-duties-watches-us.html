Buying a Watch From Abroad: US Import Duties Explained

When you buy a watch or sunglasses from outside the US, US Customs may charge import duty and fees when the parcel arrives, and the buyer usually pays them to the carrier before delivery. With DHL Express, the courier clears the parcel and sends you a bill or asks for payment online. The amount depends on what the item is, what it is made of and its value, and is usually a fraction of the price, not the full price.

How it works with DHL Express

  1. The seller declares the item and its value on the shipping documents.
  2. DHL clears the parcel through US Customs on your behalf.
  3. If duty is due, DHL contacts you by email or text with the amount and a payment link, or collects it on delivery.
  4. Once paid, the parcel is delivered.

What affects the amount

  • Item type: watches are classified by movement, case and strap; sunglasses by frame and lens.
  • Materials: for watches, the case and bracelet material (steel, gold, plated) can change the rate.
  • Value: the declared value of the item.
  • Country of origin: where the item was made, not where it ships from, may affect the rate.
  • Carrier fees: couriers may add a small processing or advancement fee.

Recent changes

In 2025 the US ended duty-free entry for most low-value imports, so most parcels from abroad are now assessed for duty regardless of value. Check the current rules before buying if you are unsure.

Tips for buyers

  • Read the listing's customs or shipping section; many sellers explain who pays.
  • Keep your phone and email available so DHL can reach you quickly; delays can mean storage fees.
  • Refusing to pay duties usually means the parcel is returned or abandoned, and the seller may not refund shipping costs.
  • Ask the seller about the item's materials if you want to estimate duty.

Is it worth buying from abroad?

Often yes. Vintage watches and sunglasses from overseas sellers can be cheaper or rarer than local ones, even after duty. Factor the duty into your budget and choose sellers who ship by fast tracked couriers.

Frequently asked questions

Who pays import duty on a watch bought from abroad?

Usually the buyer, paid to the courier before delivery.

How does DHL collect import duty?

DHL clears the parcel and contacts you with the amount and a payment link, or collects on delivery.

Is there still duty-free entry for small parcels to the US?

In 2025 the US ended duty-free entry for most low-value imports, so most parcels are now assessed.

What happens if I refuse to pay customs?

The parcel is usually returned or abandoned, and shipping costs may not be refunded.
