How to Buy a Vintage Ladies Watch: Sizes, Gold Marks and What to Check

To buy a vintage ladies watch, check four things: the size suits the wrist, the case material matches its markings (plated, gold filled or solid gold), the bracelet is intact and the right length, and the small movement runs well. Vintage ladies watches are often 15-25 mm wide, much smaller than modern watches, and many are elegant cocktail or bracelet watches.

Typical styles

  • Cocktail watches: small dials, often with decorative lugs or stones, from the 1940s-60s.
  • Bracelet watches: integrated bracelets in mesh, rope or link designs.
  • Bangle watches: rigid bangles with a small dial; check the inner size.
  • Hidden or covered dials: a lid that opens to show the dial.
  • Dress watches: small versions of men's models from brands such as Omega, Longines, Tissot and Rado.

Reading gold markings

  • Solid gold: 375 (9k), 585 (14k) or 750 (18k) stamped on the case back or inside.
  • Gold filled / rolled gold: marks like "10K GF" or "Rolled Gold".
  • Gold plated: marks like "Gold Plated", "Plaqué" or a micron rating such as "20 microns".
  • Bracelets may have their own marks; check both case and bracelet.

What to check before you buy

  1. Size: measure case width without crown and bracelet length. Small bracelets often cannot be extended.
  2. Movement: small movements are delicate; ask for a photo and confirm it runs and keeps time.
  3. Crystal: small shaped crystals can be hard to replace; check for cracks.
  4. Bracelet and clasp: check for stretch, broken links and a secure clasp.
  5. Dial: look for clean printing; small dials show damage clearly in close-up photos.
  6. Service cost: servicing a small movement costs about as much as a larger one; factor it in.

Quartz vs mechanical

Many vintage ladies watches from the 1970s onward are quartz, which needs only a battery. Older pieces are usually manual-wind and need winding daily.

Frequently asked questions

What size is a vintage ladies watch?

Typically 15-25 mm wide, much smaller than modern watches.

What does 585 or 750 mean on a watch?

585 is 14k gold and 750 is 18k gold.

Is a vintage ladies watch expensive to service?

Servicing usually costs about the same as a men's watch, even though the movement is smaller.

Can a vintage ladies bracelet be extended?

Often not; many integrated bracelets cannot be lengthened, so check the length before buying.
