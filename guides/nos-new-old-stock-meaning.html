What Does NOS (New Old Stock) Mean for Vintage Watches and Sunglasses?

NOS means "new old stock": an item made years or decades ago that was never sold or used, usually kept in a shop or warehouse. A NOS vintage watch or pair of sunglasses should show no wear, often still has its original stickers, tags or protective film, and may come with its box. NOS does not mean the item is ready to use without checks, especially for mechanical watches.

NOS vs "like new" vs "unworn"

  • NOS: never sold to a customer, from old dealer or factory stock.
  • Unworn: may have been sold but never worn.
  • Like new / mint: used, but very lightly, with no visible wear.

Signs of genuine NOS

  1. Original stickers on the case back, clasp or lenses.
  2. Protective plastic film on the crystal or case.
  3. Original price tags, hang tags or shop stickers.
  4. No scratches on buckles, clasps or the inside of straps.
  5. Original box, papers or packaging of the right era.

NOS watches: what to watch for

  • Oils in a mechanical movement dry out over decades even if the watch was never worn. Plan a service before daily use.
  • Gaskets age, so a NOS watch is not water-resistant until tested.
  • Quartz NOS watches may have an old battery that leaked; check inside.
  • Leather straps may have dried out even if unused.

NOS sunglasses: what to watch for

  • Acetate frames can shrink or become brittle with age; check the hinges and temples carefully.
  • Lens coatings can age even in storage.
  • Original lens stickers and tags are good signs of genuine NOS.

NOS parts

"NOS dial", "NOS crystal" or "NOS bracelet" means an original spare part never fitted. These are valuable for restoration, because they are original rather than reproductions.

Frequently asked questions

What does NOS mean on eBay?

New old stock: an item made years ago that was never sold or used.

Does a NOS watch need servicing?

Usually yes; the oils in mechanical movements dry out over time even without use.

How can I tell if a watch is really NOS?

Look for original stickers, protective film, tags, no wear on clasps, and period packaging.

Is NOS better than unworn?

Both describe unused items; NOS means it was never sold to a customer.
