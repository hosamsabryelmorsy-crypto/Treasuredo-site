Oman Khanjar Dial Watches: The Sultan Qaboos Emblem and How to Collect Them

An Oman khanjar dial watch carries the national emblem of Oman: a khanjar, the traditional curved dagger, over two crossed swords. These watches were made as presentation and commemorative pieces, many linked to Sultan Qaboos bin Said, who ruled Oman from 1970 to 2020. Brands from luxury makers to everyday Swiss names produced them, including models from West End Watch Co. and other Swiss brands.

What the emblem means

  • The khanjar is a symbol of Omani identity, worn traditionally at the waist.
  • The national emblem shows the khanjar and its belt over two crossed swords.
  • On watches it usually appears in red, gold or black on the dial, sometimes with Arabic text.

Why these watches exist

  • Presentation watches were given as official gifts to staff, military personnel and guests.
  • Commemorative watches marked national days and anniversaries.
  • Some brands produced khanjar dials for the retail market in Oman and the Gulf.

Which brands made them

The most famous are luxury pieces, which are highly valuable and widely copied. Many more were produced by mid-range Swiss and other brands, with quartz or mechanical movements, which are more affordable to collect.

What to check before you buy

  1. Emblem printing: should be crisp, correctly coloured and centred. Blurred or off-centre emblems suggest a later dial.
  2. Brand logo: should match the brand's style for the era.
  3. Movement: must match the brand on the dial.
  4. Case back: may carry the emblem or an inscription; check it is properly engraved.
  5. Box and papers: presentation boxes, often with the emblem, add value.
  6. Condition: plating, crystal and dial condition affect value as with any watch.

Beware of fakes on luxury models

Because luxury khanjar watches are very valuable, fake dials are common on them. For high-value pieces, expert authentication is essential. Mid-range khanjar watches are less often faked but still deserve a close look at the dial.

Frequently asked questions

What is a khanjar on a watch dial?

The national emblem of Oman: a curved dagger over two crossed swords.

Who was Sultan Qaboos?

The ruler of Oman from 1970 to 2020, linked to many presentation watches.

Are Oman khanjar watches valuable?

Luxury pieces can be very valuable; mid-range versions are more affordable collectibles.

How do I check an Oman khanjar dial?

Look for crisp, centred emblem printing and a movement that matches the brand.
