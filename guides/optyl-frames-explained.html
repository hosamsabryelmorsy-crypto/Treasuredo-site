What Is Optyl? The Material Behind Many 1970s-80s Sunglasses

Optyl is a lightweight, heat-cured epoxy resin developed in Austria by Wilhelm Anger in the 1960s for eyewear frames. It is lighter than acetate, hypoallergenic and has a shape memory: when warmed, a bent frame returns to its original shape. It was used for many 1970s and 1980s frames from Carrera, Christian Dior, Viennaline, Playboy and others.

Optyl vs acetate

  • Weight: Optyl is lighter than acetate of the same size.
  • Shape memory: Optyl returns to its moulded shape when heated; acetate stays in the new shape.
  • Adjustment: Optyl needs higher heat to adjust and can crack if bent cold; acetate is easier to adjust.
  • Colour: Optyl frames are dyed after moulding, giving clear, glossy colours, often in amber, honey and tortoise tones.
  • Skin: Optyl is hypoallergenic.

How to identify Optyl

  1. Look for "Optyl" printed on the temple, common on 1970s-80s frames.
  2. Optyl frames feel noticeably light for their size.
  3. Many are marked "Made in Austria" or "Germany".

Brands that used Optyl

  • Carrera: founded by Wilhelm Anger, the inventor of Optyl.
  • Christian Dior: many licensed frames of the 1970s-80s.
  • Viennaline and Playboy: oversized 1970s frames.
  • Persol and Dunhill: some vintage models.

Caring for Optyl frames

  • Do not bend them cold; ask an optician to warm them first.
  • Keep them out of very hot cars; extreme heat can change the shape.
  • Clean with mild soap and water; avoid solvents.
  • Store in a case to protect from pressure.

General checks for vintage frames

  1. Hinges: should open and close smoothly with no wobble; loose hinges can often be tightened, cracked hinge areas cannot.
  2. Frame material: look for cracks, white haze or shrinkage, especially near hinges and the bridge.
  3. Lenses: check for scratches, coating damage or delamination; replacement lenses can be fitted but originals add value.
  4. Size: compare lens width, bridge and temple length with glasses you already own.

Frequently asked questions

What is Optyl?

A lightweight heat-cured epoxy resin developed in Austria by Wilhelm Anger for eyewear frames.

Is Optyl better than acetate?

It is lighter and hypoallergenic, with shape memory, but harder to adjust.

Which brands used Optyl?

Carrera, Christian Dior, Viennaline, Playboy and some Persol and Dunhill frames.

Can Optyl frames be repaired?

Bent frames can often be reshaped with heat; cracks are hard to repair.
