Persol Meflecto: How the Flexible Temple Works and How to Check It

Meflecto is Persol's flexible temple system: a series of small segments inside the temple arm lets it bend gently to the shape of the head, so the sunglasses grip without pressure. You can recognise it by the segmented core visible through the acetate and the "Meflecto" marking. Many vintage Persol Ratti sunglasses use it, and it is a key feature buyers look for.

How it works

  • Inside the temple, a flexible core made of linked segments replaces a single rigid wire.
  • The temple can flex sideways and curve to the head.
  • Pressure is spread along the temple instead of concentrated behind the ears.

How to recognise it

  1. Hold the temple up to the light: you can often see a segmented core inside the acetate.
  2. Look for "Meflecto" printed on the temple.
  3. Gently flex the temple; it bends more easily than a standard temple.

Other Persol signatures

  • Supreme Arrow: the silver arrow-shaped metal detail on the hinges and temples.
  • Persol Ratti: marking on older pairs from before Luxottica acquired Persol in 1995.
  • Model and size numbers printed inside the temple.

What to check before you buy

  1. Flex section: look for cracks in the acetate around the flexible core; this is the weak point on old pairs.
  2. Core: the segments should not poke through the acetate.
  3. Hinges: should open and close smoothly with no wobble.
  4. Acetate: check for shrinkage, white haze or brittleness on older frames.
  5. Lenses: scratches and coating damage affect use and value.
  6. Fit: compare lens width, bridge and temple length with glasses you own.

Care tips

Do not force the temples open wider than they want to go, keep them out of hot cars (acetate softens with heat), and store them in a case.

Frequently asked questions

What is Persol Meflecto?

A flexible temple system with a segmented core that bends to the head for a comfortable fit.

How do I know if my Persol has Meflecto?

Look for the segmented core through the acetate and the "Meflecto" marking on the temple.

What is the Supreme Arrow on Persol sunglasses?

The arrow-shaped metal detail on the hinges and temples, a Persol signature.

What breaks on old Meflecto temples?

The acetate around the flexible section can crack with age.
