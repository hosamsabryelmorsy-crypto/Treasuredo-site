How to Read Persol Ratti Model Numbers and Sizes on Vintage Sunglasses

On vintage Persol Ratti sunglasses, the inside of the temple usually shows the model number, the size (lens width and bridge in millimetres) and markings such as "Persol Ratti", "Meflecto" or "Made in Italy". Reading these tells you which model you have, whether it fits, and roughly how old it is.

Where to find the markings

  • Inside the temples: model number, size and often a colour code.
  • Front or temple outside: "Persol" or "Persol Ratti" logo, and the Supreme Arrow detail.
  • Lenses: sometimes a small Persol mark etched in the lens.

Reading the numbers

  • Model number: identifies the design, for example 649 or 714. Some vintage models use longer numbers or letter-number codes.
  • Size: usually shown as two numbers such as 52□20: 52 mm lens width and 20 mm bridge. A third number may give temple length in millimetres.
  • Colour code: a short number or code for the frame colour.

Clues to age

  1. "Persol Ratti": marks older pairs, before Luxottica acquired Persol in 1995.
  2. "Meflecto": the flexible temple system on many vintage pairs.
  3. Font and logo style: older logos and fonts differ from modern ones.
  4. Material: some vintage Persols use Optyl or other materials rather than acetate.

What if the numbers are worn off?

Printed numbers can fade with use. Measure the lens width across the widest part of one lens, the bridge between the lenses, and the temple from hinge to tip. Compare the shape with known models.

General checks for vintage frames

  1. Hinges: should open and close smoothly with no wobble; loose hinges can often be tightened, cracked hinge areas cannot.
  2. Frame material: look for cracks, white haze or shrinkage, especially near hinges and the bridge.
  3. Lenses: check for scratches, coating damage or delamination; replacement lenses can be fitted but originals add value.
  4. Size: compare lens width, bridge and temple length with glasses you already own.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the model number on Persol sunglasses?

Usually printed inside one of the temples.

What does 52□20 mean on sunglasses?

52 mm lens width and 20 mm bridge width.

How do I know if my Persol is vintage?

"Persol Ratti" markings indicate pairs from before Luxottica acquired Persol in 1995.

What if the size is worn off?

Measure lens width, bridge and temple length with a ruler.
