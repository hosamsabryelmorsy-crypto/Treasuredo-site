Pierre Cardin Watches of the 1970s: Space Age Design and How to Buy One

1970s Pierre Cardin watches are collected for their bold space-age designs: unusual case shapes, bright colours and futuristic dials. The best-known early pieces were made with the French watch company Jaz and use mechanical movements. Later Pierre Cardin watches, mostly quartz and produced under various licences, are cheaper and less sought after.

Why the 1970s pieces matter

  • Pierre Cardin was a leading designer of space-age fashion in the 1960s and 1970s, and his watches followed the same style.
  • Early 1970s pieces were made with Jaz, a French watch and clock maker.
  • Designs include asymmetric cases, cut-out shapes, coloured dials and unusual lug designs.

How to tell early from later pieces

  • Movement: early pieces are typically mechanical (manual or automatic); later ones are mostly quartz.
  • Dial and case back markings: early pieces may show Jaz or French markings; later ones show other makers or "Japan" or "Swiss" movements.
  • Design: the most distinctive, sculptural cases are mainly from the 1970s.

What to check before you buy

  1. Movement: ask for a photo and confirm it runs. Mechanical pieces should wind and keep time; quartz pieces should run on a fresh battery.
  2. Case: many cases are chrome or gold plated; check for pitting and wear through on edges.
  3. Crystal: unusual case shapes often use shaped crystals that are hard to replace. Check for cracks and deep scratches.
  4. Dial: coloured and printed dials fade; even fading is acceptable, but stains and flaking lower value.
  5. Strap or bracelet: some designs use integrated straps or special fittings; missing originals are hard to replace.

Is a 1970s Pierre Cardin worth buying?

Yes, if you like design history. The best early pieces are art objects as much as watches. Pay more for distinctive cases, original crystals and running movements, and treat later plain quartz models as everyday fashion watches.

Questions to ask the seller

  • Is the movement mechanical or quartz?
  • Does it run and keep time?
  • Is the crystal original and free of cracks?
  • Is the strap or bracelet original?

Frequently asked questions

Who made 1970s Pierre Cardin watches?

Many early pieces were made with the French watch company Jaz.

Are vintage Pierre Cardin watches mechanical?

Early 1970s pieces are typically mechanical; later ones are mostly quartz.

Why are 1970s Pierre Cardin watches collectible?

For their bold space-age designs with unusual case shapes and colours.

What is hard to fix on a Pierre Cardin watch?

Shaped crystals and integrated straps, which are difficult to replace.
