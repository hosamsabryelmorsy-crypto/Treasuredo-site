Vintage Raymond Weil Watches: Are They Good? Othello, Amadeus and What to Check

Vintage Raymond Weil watches are good-quality Swiss watches, mostly quartz from the 1980s and 1990s, known for elegant dress designs and ultra-thin cases such as the Othello. They are affordable and wearable, but they are not investment pieces: buy for condition, the plating and a working movement.

Raymond Weil in brief

  • The company was founded in Geneva in 1976 by Raymond Weil, during the quartz crisis when many Swiss brands were struggling.
  • Collections are named after music and the arts: Amadeus, Othello, Tango, Parsifal, Maestro, Toccata and others.
  • It remained a family-run independent company for decades.

Vintage collections you will see

  • Othello: 1980s ultra-thin quartz dress watches, often gold plated with geometric details.
  • Amadeus: a classic dress line from the late 1980s.
  • Parsifal: a more luxurious line with steel and gold.
  • Tango: sport watches, usually quartz, with a rotating bezel.

What to check before you buy

  1. Gold plating: many vintage models are marked "18K gold electroplated". Check lug tips, crown and edges for steel or brass showing through. Small wear is normal; large patches reduce value.
  2. Battery corrosion: quartz watches left with dead batteries can leak. Ask for a movement photo or confirmation it runs on a new battery.
  3. Thin cases: ultra-thin models bend more easily; check the case and crystal sit flush.
  4. Dial: look for clean, crisp printing; damage on thin dials cannot be repaired cheaply.
  5. Bracelet: integrated bracelets on Othello and Parsifal are model-specific; check for stretch and missing links.
  6. Case back: should show Raymond Weil signing, Geneva and a reference number.

Quartz vs mechanical

Most vintage Raymond Weils are quartz. Mechanical models exist, usually using Swiss supplier movements, and are worth a little more. A quartz model needs only a battery and occasional service, which makes it a practical everyday dress watch.

Sizing

Many 1980s-90s models are small by today's standards, and ladies' versions are very common. Check case width without crown in the listing.

Questions to ask the seller

  • Does it run on a new battery?
  • Is the case gold plated, and is there any wear through?
  • What wrist size does the bracelet fit?
  • Is the movement quartz or mechanical?

Frequently asked questions

When was Raymond Weil founded?

In 1976 in Geneva, Switzerland, by Raymond Weil.

Are vintage Raymond Weil watches quartz?

Most are quartz; mechanical models exist and are worth a little more.

What is a Raymond Weil Othello?

A 1980s ultra-thin quartz dress watch line, often gold plated.

Are Raymond Weil watches good quality?

Yes. They are Swiss made with good finishing, though they are not investment pieces.
