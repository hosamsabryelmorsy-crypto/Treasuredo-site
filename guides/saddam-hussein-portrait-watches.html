Saddam Hussein Portrait Watches: History, Makers and How to Tell Originals

Saddam Hussein portrait watches are watches with his portrait printed on the dial, made mainly in the 1980s and 1990s as presentation gifts and commemorative pieces in Iraq. Many were produced by established Swiss and Japanese brands, which is why you see names such as Doxa, Edox, Oris, Raymond Weil, Camy, Candino and Orient on them. Today they are collected as historical objects, and the key question is whether the dial is an original factory presentation dial or a later custom print.

Why they exist

  • Portrait watches were given to officials, military personnel and supporters, and sold to the public.
  • Producing presentation watches for governments in the Middle East was a common business for Swiss watchmakers in the 20th century.
  • Many are quartz from the 1980s, often gold plated; some are mechanical.

Original vs custom dials

  • Original presentation dials: printed at the factory with sharp detail, correct brand logo and "Swiss" or "Swiss Made" marking where expected.
  • Custom or aftermarket dials: portraits added later to ordinary watches. Printing is often less sharp, the logo may be missing or misplaced, and the dial may not match the watch's era.
  • Honest sellers describe custom dials as such; they are worth less than originals.

What to check before you buy

  1. Portrait print quality: look for fine detail and clean edges.
  2. Brand logo and text: should be crisp, centred and appropriate for the brand.
  3. Movement: should match the brand; a Swiss brand dial with a generic movement is a warning.
  4. Case back: sometimes carries Arabic inscriptions or emblems; check they are engraved, not stickers.
  5. Box: original presentation boxes add value.
  6. Condition: gold plating wear and dial damage reduce value, as with any vintage watch.

Collecting today

These watches are part of the wider category of Middle Eastern presentation watches, alongside Omani and Gulf commemorative pieces. Buyers collect them for their historical interest. Original factory dials from known brands are the most sought after.

Frequently asked questions

Who made Saddam Hussein portrait watches?

Many were made by Swiss and Japanese brands such as Doxa, Edox, Oris, Raymond Weil, Camy, Candino and Orient.

When were Saddam Hussein watches made?

Mainly in the 1980s and 1990s.

How can I tell an original Saddam Hussein dial?

Look for sharp factory printing, a correct brand logo and a movement matching the brand.

Are custom portrait dials worth less?

Yes, later custom dials are worth less than original factory presentation dials.
