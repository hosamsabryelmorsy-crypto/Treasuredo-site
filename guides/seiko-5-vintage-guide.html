The Vintage Seiko 5: What the Five Stands For

Seiko introduced the Sportsmatic 5 in 1963. The idea was simple: an automatic watch tough and affordable enough to wear every day. It became one of the best-selling mechanical watch families ever made.

The five features

  1. Automatic winding.
  2. Day and date shown together in one window.
  3. Water resistance.
  4. A recessed crown at the 4 o'clock position.
  5. A durable case and bracelet.

Why collectors like them

Vintage Seiko 5 models come in a huge range of dial colours and case shapes, the movements are robust, and parts are easy to find. Prices are still friendly, which makes them a good first vintage watch.

Buying tips

Many old Seiko 5 watches have been fitted with new aftermarket dials. Compare the dial code printed at the bottom edge of the dial with the code on the case back, and be careful with dials that look brand new on a worn case. Check that the day wheel shows the languages you expect, as many were sold with English and Arabic or English and Spanish days.
