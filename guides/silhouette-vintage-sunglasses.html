Vintage Silhouette Sunglasses From Austria: Models, Markings and What to Check

Silhouette is an Austrian eyewear brand founded in 1964 in Linz by Arnold and Anneliese Schmied, known for lightweight frames. Vintage Silhouette sunglasses from the 1970s and 1980s include bold oversized frames and metal navigator and aviator styles. Their model numbers, often prefixed with "M" or "Mod", are printed inside the temple and help identify the frame.

Silhouette in brief

  • Founded in 1964 in Linz, Austria.
  • Known for lightweight design, later famous for rimless Titan Minimal Art frames.
  • Vintage pieces are typically marked "Made in Austria".

Vintage styles

  • Oversized 1970s frames: large plastic frames in mottled browns, ambers and pastels.
  • Navigator and aviator: 1980s metal frames, often with gradient lenses.
  • Titanium frames: lightweight metal frames from later decades.

Reading the markings

  • Model: often "M" or "Mod" plus a number, for example "M 7064" or "Mod 48".
  • Colour code: a number such as "Col 141".
  • Size: lens width and bridge in millimetres.
  • Origin: "Made in Austria".

General checks for vintage frames

  1. Hinges: should open and close smoothly with no wobble; loose hinges can often be tightened, cracked hinge areas cannot.
  2. Frame material: look for cracks, white haze or shrinkage, especially near hinges and the bridge.
  3. Lenses: check for scratches, coating damage or delamination; replacement lenses can be fitted but originals add value.
  4. Size: compare lens width, bridge and temple length with glasses you already own.

Extra checks for Silhouette

  • Thin metal temples can bend; check they sit straight.
  • Gradient lenses should be even with no peeling.
  • Original cases with the Silhouette logo add value.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Silhouette eyewear from?

Linz, Austria, where it was founded in 1964.

How do I read a Silhouette model number?

Look for "M" or "Mod" plus a number and a colour code inside the temple.

Are vintage Silhouette sunglasses good quality?

Yes, they are known for lightweight, well-made frames.

What styles did Silhouette make in the 1970s-80s?

Oversized plastic frames and metal navigator and aviator styles.
