Heuer vs TAG Heuer: What Changed in 1985 and How to Buy a Vintage One

Watches made before 1985 are signed "Heuer"; after the TAG group bought the company in 1985 they are signed "TAG Heuer". Collectors value pre-1985 Heuer chronographs such as the Carrera, Autavia and Monaco highly. The 1980s-90s TAG Heuer sports watches, like the Formula 1 and the 2000 series, are affordable, tough quartz and automatic watches.

Heuer history in brief

  • Edouard Heuer founded the company in 1860 in Saint-Imier, Switzerland.
  • Heuer specialised in stopwatches, dashboard timers and chronographs, with strong links to motor racing.
  • In 1985 the TAG group (Techniques d'Avant Garde) bought Heuer, creating TAG Heuer.
  • LVMH has owned TAG Heuer since 1999.

Vintage Heuer models collectors want

  • Carrera (1963): a clean, readable racing chronograph.
  • Autavia (1962): chronograph with a rotating bezel, named after the dashboard timer.
  • Monaco (1969): a square, water-resistant chronograph made famous by Steve McQueen in the 1971 film Le Mans.
  • Calibre 11 (1969): one of the first automatic chronograph movements, developed with partners; Heuer models using it have the crown on the left.
  • Camaro and Silverstone: 1960s-70s chronographs with distinctive cases.

Popular early TAG Heuer models

  • Formula 1 (1986): colourful, inexpensive sports watches with plastic or steel cases.
  • 1000, 2000, 3000 series: diver-style watches with rotating bezels and "claws" around the bezel on some models.
  • S/el (1987): known for its S-shaped bracelet links.

What to check before you buy

  1. Signature: "Heuer" alone means pre-1985; "TAG Heuer" means 1985 or later. A Heuer-only dial on a later model reference is a warning sign.
  2. Movement: ask for a photo; on chronographs, start, stop and reset should work cleanly.
  3. Dial and hands: vintage Heuer dials are frequently restored or replaced; compare with known references.
  4. Bezel: on divers, check the insert and the bezel's click; replacement inserts are common.
  5. Case back: look for the reference and serial numbers.
  6. Quartz models: check for battery corrosion.

Fakes

Vintage Heuer chronographs are expensive and widely faked. Look for consistent fonts, correct sub-dial layout and a matching movement. For an early TAG Heuer, the risk is lower, but check the bezel and bracelet are original.

Questions to ask the seller

  • Is it signed Heuer or TAG Heuer?
  • Can you show the movement and case back with reference number?
  • For chronographs: does it start, stop and reset correctly?
  • Are the bezel insert and bracelet original?

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Heuer and TAG Heuer?

Heuer is the name before 1985; TAG Heuer is the name after the TAG group bought the company in 1985.

When was the Heuer Monaco released?

In 1969. It became famous after Steve McQueen wore it in the 1971 film Le Mans.

Why is the crown on the left on some Heuers?

Models with the Calibre 11 automatic chronograph movement of 1969 have the crown on the left.

Are 1980s TAG Heuer watches collectible?

Models like the Formula 1 and 2000 series are popular and affordable, though less valuable than vintage Heuer chronographs.
