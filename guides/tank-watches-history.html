The Cartier Tank: History, Main Models and How to Tell Them Apart

The Tank is the rectangular watch Cartier designed in 1917, inspired by the shape of a tank seen from above, with the vertical sides (brancards) acting as the lugs. It went on sale around 1919 and became one of the most copied watch shapes in history. The main versions are the Tank Louis Cartier, Tank Cintrée, Tank Américaine, Tank Française and the vermeil Must Tank.

How to tell the main Tanks apart

  • Tank Louis Cartier (1922): softer rounded brancards, classic proportions, usually in gold.
  • Tank Cintrée (1921): long, narrow and curved to follow the wrist.
  • Tank Américaine (1989): curved case with wider, rounded brancards.
  • Tank Française (1996): integrated bracelet with chain-like links.
  • Must de Cartier Tank (1977): vermeil (gold-plated silver) case, mostly quartz, colour dials.

Classic Tank design features

  • Roman numerals with a railway minute track.
  • Blued steel sword or Breguet-style hands.
  • A beaded crown with a cabochon (often blue).
  • On many models, a "secret signature" with Cartier's name hidden in a numeral, often the VII or X.

What to check before you buy

  1. Proportions: genuine Tanks have slim, balanced brancards. Thick or wide cases often point to fakes or "tank-style" watches from other brands.
  2. Case material: read the case back: gold, steel or 925 vermeil. Price depends heavily on this.
  3. Dial: printing should be fine and sharp; check for the secret signature where expected.
  4. Movement: quartz or mechanical depending on model; ask for a photo and service history.
  5. Crown cabochon: should be present and undamaged.
  6. Buckle or bracelet: original Cartier buckles and bracelets add value.

Tank-style watches from other brands

Many brands made rectangular "tank" watches. These can be good, affordable dress watches, but they are not Cartier Tanks and should be priced accordingly.

Questions to ask the seller

  • Which Tank model is it, and what is the reference?
  • What is the case material according to the case back?
  • Is the movement quartz or mechanical, and when was it serviced?
  • Is the buckle or bracelet original?

Frequently asked questions

When was the Cartier Tank designed?

In 1917, inspired by the shape of a tank seen from above.

What is the secret signature on a Cartier Tank?

A tiny "Cartier" hidden in one of the Roman numerals on many dials.

What is the difference between Tank Louis and Tank Française?

The Tank Louis has a classic strap case from 1922; the Tank Française (1996) has an integrated chain-link bracelet.

Is a Must de Cartier Tank a real Cartier?

Yes. It is a genuine Cartier, made in vermeil (gold-plated silver), not solid gold.
