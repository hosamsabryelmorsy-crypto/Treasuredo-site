How Accurate Should a Vintage Watch Be? Normal Seconds-Per-Day Explained

A serviced vintage mechanical watch in good condition typically keeps within about 15-30 seconds a day, and many do better. Certified chronometers aim for -4 to +6 seconds a day under modern standards. A vintage quartz watch should keep within about 15-30 seconds a month. If a mechanical watch gains or loses minutes a day, it usually needs service or regulation.

Normal accuracy ranges

  • Good vintage mechanical: within about ±15 seconds a day after service.
  • Acceptable vintage mechanical: within about ±30 seconds a day.
  • Pin-lever watches: often less accurate, with wider daily variation.
  • Chronometers: built and tested for -4/+6 seconds a day.
  • Quartz: about ±15-30 seconds a month for typical vintage models; higher-grade quartz does better.

What affects accuracy

  1. Position: watches run differently face up, face down and crown up. Overnight resting position changes the rate.
  2. Power reserve: many watches run less accurately when nearly unwound. Keep them wound.
  3. Magnetism: phones, speakers and bag magnets can magnetise a watch, causing it to run fast. A watchmaker can demagnetise it in seconds.
  4. Temperature: extreme cold or heat changes the rate.
  5. Service condition: dry oils and worn parts make timekeeping erratic.

How to test your watch

  1. Set it exactly to an accurate time source.
  2. Wear it normally for 24 hours.
  3. Compare with the time source and note the difference.
  4. Repeat for a few days to get an average.

When to see a watchmaker

  • Gaining or losing more than about a minute a day.
  • Sudden change in accuracy.
  • Running very fast after being near a magnet.
  • Stopping or erratic timekeeping.

A watchmaker can measure the watch on a timing machine and adjust it.

Frequently asked questions

How many seconds a day is normal for a vintage watch?

About 15-30 seconds a day is normal for a serviced vintage mechanical watch.

Why does my watch run fast?

It may be magnetised, need regulation, or need a service.

How accurate is a chronometer?

Chronometers are certified to -4 to +6 seconds a day.

How accurate is a vintage quartz watch?

Typically within about 15-30 seconds a month.
