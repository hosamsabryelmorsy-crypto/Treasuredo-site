Giving a Vintage Watch as a Gift: How to Choose the Right One

A vintage watch makes a meaningful gift when it connects to the person: a watch from their birth year or an important anniversary, a brand they love, or a style that suits how they dress. Choose a watch that runs well and has been serviced, check the size against their wrist, and present it with a box and a short note about its history.

Ideas that make it personal

  • Birth year watch: a watch made in the year the person was born.
  • Anniversary year: a watch from the year of a wedding or another milestone.
  • Brand connection: the brand a parent or grandparent wore.
  • Style match: a dress watch for someone formal, a diver for someone active.

Practical checks

  1. Size: borrow a watch they wear or estimate wrist size. Men's vintage watches of 34-38 mm suit most wrists; ladies' watches are often 20-28 mm.
  2. Working condition: ask about recent service. A gift should run well from day one.
  3. Strap: a fresh leather strap makes an old watch look ready to wear.
  4. Quartz or mechanical: quartz needs no winding; mechanical has more charm but needs care.
  5. Battery: fit a fresh battery in quartz watches before giving.

Engraving

Engraving the case back makes the gift personal, but it can lower resale value. If the watch is collectible, consider engraving the box or a card instead.

Presentation

  • Use a quality watch box, even if not original.
  • Add a short note with the watch's year, brand history and movement type.
  • Include simple care instructions: how to wind, set the date safely and keep it dry.

Timing your gift

Vintage watches ship from different countries and may need time for delivery. Order early for birthdays and holidays.

Frequently asked questions

Is a vintage watch a good gift?

Yes, especially from a meaningful year or brand, and when it has been serviced.

How do I know what size watch to buy as a gift?

Borrow a watch they wear or estimate their wrist; 34-38 mm suits most men's wrists.

Should I engrave a vintage watch?

Engraving makes it personal but can lower resale value; consider engraving the box instead.

What is a birth year watch?

A watch made in the year the recipient was born.
