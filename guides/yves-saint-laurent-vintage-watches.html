Vintage Yves Saint Laurent (YSL) Watches: Who Made Them and What to Check

Vintage Yves Saint Laurent (YSL) watches from the 1980s-90s were made under licence, many of them in Japan with Japanese quartz movements, and they are known for elegant, minimal designs. To judge one, read the movement calibre on the case back, check it runs, and look closely at the plating and the bracelet.

How to identify a vintage YSL watch

  • Dial: "Yves Saint Laurent" or the YSL monogram on the dial.
  • Case back: usually shows the YSL name and a calibre and case reference. On Japanese-made pieces, the reference often looks like a four-digit calibre followed by a case number.
  • Movement: most are quartz. Japanese movements are reliable and parts are usually available.
  • Design: slim cases, small dials, integrated bracelets and the YSL monogram on bracelets or clasps.

What affects value

  • Distinctive designs, such as unusual case shapes or monogram bracelets, are worth more than plain models.
  • Complete original bracelets matter; they are rarely available as spares.
  • Box and papers add value for fashion watches.

Checking a vintage designer quartz watch

  1. Running: ask whether it runs on a fresh battery. A quartz watch that will not start may need only cleaning, but a failed circuit can mean a replacement movement.
  2. Battery corrosion: a dead battery left inside for years can leak. Ask for a photo inside the case back.
  3. Plating: most vintage fashion watches are gold plated. Check lug tips, crown, clasp and bracelet edges for base metal showing through.
  4. Crystal: mineral crystals scratch less than acrylic but chip at the edges.

Sizing

Many vintage YSL watches are ladies' or small unisex sizes, around the 20-30 mm range. Check the case width without crown and bracelet length, as integrated bracelets cannot always be extended.

Fakes

Look for crisp monogram printing and engraving, a solid case back and a clean quartz movement. Blurred logos or very light cases are warning signs.

Questions to ask the seller

  • Does it run on a new battery?
  • What calibre and case number are on the back?
  • Is the bracelet original and complete?
  • Is there wear through the plating?
