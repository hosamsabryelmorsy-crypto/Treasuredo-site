About this watch

A bold 1970s Olma Sea-Cup with a warm brown gradient dial, applied two-tone block markers with luminous inserts, baton hands and a day-date window at 3 o'clock (day shown in French, e.g. LUN). The dial is signed Olma Automatic, Sea-Cup, 25 Jewels, Swiss Made.

The cushion-shaped TV case is gold plated with a brushed finish and a stainless-steel back marked Stainless Steel Back, Super Waterproof, Automatic, Swiss. Fitted with a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Olma was a Swiss brand of the 20th century known for practical, reliable Swiss watches at accessible prices, exported widely across Europe and beyond. Its best-known lines include the Caravelle and the Sea-Cup, and its 1970s automatics with bold cases and colourful dials are popular with collectors today.
