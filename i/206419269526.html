About this watch

A striking square Japanese automatic from the 1970s. The black dial has a gold-tone inner frame, applied gold markers, gold hands and a date window at 3 o'clock, with the Ricoh logo below 12.

The 32mm square gold-plated case has decorative screw details at the corners and a steel back. Inside is the Ricoh calibre 280 automatic movement with 22 jewels, signed "Ricoh Automatic Japan". It is fitted with a black textured leather strap.

Brand heritage

Ricoh's watchmaking goes back to Takano, a Japanese clock maker with roots in 1899. Takano was taken over by Ricoh in 1962, became Ricoh Watch Co. in 1963, and its watches were sold under the Ricoh name from then on. In the 1960s and 1970s Ricoh made wristwatches for both the Japanese and international markets; the watch division was renamed Ricoh Elemex in 1986.

Vintage Ricoh automatics are far less common than Seiko or Citizen, which makes them an interesting find for collectors of Japanese watches.
