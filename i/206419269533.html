About this watch

A true Art Deco rectangular "tank" watch. The patinated dial has stepped geometric framing, blued Arabic numerals, blued hands and a small seconds subdial at 6 o'clock.

The 22 x 36mm curved silver-tone case has a knurled crown and a snap-on back. Inside is a German manual-wind movement signed with the FB mark of Bernhard Förster and numbered 2075. It is fitted with a brown lizard-pattern leather strap.

Brand heritage

Bernhard Förster was a movement maker based in Pforzheim, the historic centre of the German watch and jewellery industry. Its movements, stamped with the FB mark, were supplied to many German watch brands.

Rectangular tank watches like this were the height of fashion from the 1920s through the 1940s, and this example captures the geometric style of the period.
