About this watch

A bold example of the space-age designs of the 1970s. Instead of hands, this Tenor-Dorly shows the time digitally through windows in the dial: a jumping hour digit in a small window and rotating minutes visible through an arrow-shaped aperture, with a separate date window at four o'clock. The deep burgundy-red dial is signed TD Tenor-Dorly and marked "21 Jewels, Automatic, Incabloc, Swiss".

The large cushion TV-shaped gold-plated case measures approximately 42mm and has a high domed crystal and a stainless-steel caseback marked Swiss Made. Inside is a TD-signed 21-jewel Swiss automatic movement. It is fitted with a burgundy leather strap.

Brand heritage

Tenor-Dorly (TD) was a Swiss watch and movement maker best known in the 1960s and 1970s for its jump-hour, direct-read movements, which powered many of the futuristic digital-display mechanical watches of the period and were also supplied to other brands. Today these watches are collected as icons of 1970s Swiss design.
