About this watch

A mechanical alarm wristwatch, one of the most charming vintage complications. The silver dial is signed Jovial with the bee emblem, "Alarm" and "17 Jewels", with applied gold-tone baton markers, Arabic numerals at 3, 6 and 9, and a red inner alarm track with a red-tipped alarm pointer.

The 38mm cushion-shaped gold-plated case has two crowns: one to wind and set the time, the other to wind and set the alarm. Inside is a Swiss manual-wind mechanical alarm movement. It is fitted on a tan leather strap and comes with a Jovial "Since 1929" wooden box.

Brand heritage

Jovial was founded in 1929 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, and later moved its headquarters to Biel/Bienne. From its earliest years the brand concentrated on the Middle Eastern market, where its dependable Swiss mechanical watches became widely known, a focus it has kept to this day.
