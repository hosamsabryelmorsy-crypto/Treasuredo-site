About this watch

A classic 1960s Roamer Anfibio with a silver-white textured dial, gold-tone applied baton markers and Arabic numerals at 12, 3, 6 and 9, gold dauphine-style hands and the Roamer logo. The dial is signed "Anfibio" and "Incabloc", the shock-protection system fitted to the movement.

The 35mm gold-plated case has a stainless steel screw-down back marked "Roamer", "Mod. 414-1332.003", "Swiss Made", "Breveté" with patent numbers and "Waterproof". In Roamer's reference system the first digits give the calibre: this is the in-house MST 414, a 17-jewel manual-wind movement. It is fitted on a taupe leather strap.

Brand heritage

Roamer was founded in 1888 in Solothurn, Switzerland, by Fritz Meyer, and grew into one of the few Swiss houses making its own movements, cases and dials; its movements are signed MST (Meyer & Stüdeli). In 1955 Roamer introduced the Anfibio line, built around its patented water-resistant case with a screw-in caseback, and in the 1960s and 1970s its watches were sold in very large numbers around the world, including the Middle East.
