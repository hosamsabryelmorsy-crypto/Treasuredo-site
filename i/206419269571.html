About this watch

A neat 1960s Swiss gents' watch. The gold dial has a fine textured pattern, applied gold-tone block markers, gold hands, a red seconds hand and a date window at 3 o'clock, signed "Chivas 17 Jewels Incabloc".

The 33mm stainless-steel case has sharp tapered lugs and a steel back marked "Stainless Steel Back, 100% Swiss Made, Antimagnetic, Incabloc". Inside is a Swiss 17-jewel manual-wind movement marked "Seventeen 17 Jewels Swiss". It is fitted to a black leather two-piece strap.

Brand heritage

Chivas is one of the many smaller Swiss watch labels of the 1960s whose company history is not well documented. Brands like this used proven Swiss jewelled-lever movements with Incabloc shock protection, giving buyers genuine Swiss quality at a modest price.
