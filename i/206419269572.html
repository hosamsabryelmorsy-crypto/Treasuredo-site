About this watch

The black dial has a gold-tone chapter ring with a fine minute track, the West End Watch Co. and "Sowar" signatures, and a day window in Arabic with an Arabic-numeral date window below. A red-tipped seconds hand adds a touch of colour.

The 33mm case has a screw-down back stamped with the West End Watch Co. "Everbright" seal, Swiss Made, and the numbers D 9587 / 9899. Inside is a 17-jewel Swiss manual-wind movement. It is worn on a steel expanding bracelet and comes with a West End Watch Co. box.

Brand heritage

The West End Watch Company was established to export Swiss watches to India and took its name in 1886, when Arnold Amstutz and Constant Droz bought the business. It had offices in Switzerland, Bombay and Calcutta. For India and the East the company used the name Sowar, a Persian word for "one who rides" that was given to cavalry troopers in India. West End watches were famous for their toughness in hot, humid climates.
