About this watch

A clean, confident 1970s design: the silver sunburst dial is signed "Santima Executive" with a crown logo and "21 Jewels Automatic Incabloc", with black-filled baton markers, matching hands and a day-date window at 3 o'clock with a red date. The dial is marked "T Swiss Made T".

The 37mm cushion-shaped case has polished surfaces and houses a Swiss Ronda-Matic automatic movement with 21 jewels and Incabloc shock protection. It is fitted on a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Santima is one of the many smaller Swiss brands of the 1970s that built watches around proven Swiss movements. This one uses a Ronda-Matic calibre from Ronda AG, the movement maker founded in 1946 by William Mosset in Lausen, Switzerland, and still family owned today.
