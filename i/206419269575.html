About this watch

A rare piece of Kuwaiti football history: a commemorative watch issued for the retirement of Hamoud Fleteikh Al-Shammari, one of the legendary players of Kazma Sporting Club and the Kuwait national team. The dial carries the Arabic dedication "on the occasion of the retirement of Hamoud Fleteikh Al-Shammari" around the blue Kazma club shield with its red torch.

The watch has a round natural-wood case and a light wooden dial with black numerals at 12, 3, 6 and 9, black dot markers and slim gold-tone hands, set off by a thin gold-tone bezel ring and gold-tone lugs. It has a quartz movement and is fitted on a tan-brown leather strap.

Brand heritage

Hamoud Fleteikh (Fleitah) Al-Shemmari, born in 1960, was a defender for Kazma Sporting Club from 1977 to 1989 and earned 68 caps for Kuwait between 1979 and 1989. He was part of the Kuwait squad at the 1982 FIFA World Cup in Spain, the country's only World Cup appearance, during the golden era of Kuwaiti football.
