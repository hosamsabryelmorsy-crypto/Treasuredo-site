About this watch

A classic 1960s Swiss gents' dress watch. The silver sunburst dial is signed "Novelti" and "17 Rubis Incabloc", with Art-Deco-style Arabic 12 and 6, slim baton markers and silver-tone hands.

The 32mm stainless steel case has a screw-down back marked Waterproof and Swiss Made. Inside is a Swiss 17-jewel manual-wind movement (signed "Seventeen 17 Jewels Swiss") with Incabloc shock protection, shown in the photos. It is fitted to a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Novelti is one of the many smaller Swiss brand names of the post-war decades, when independent assemblers built watches around Swiss-made movements for export markets around the world. Little is documented about the company itself, but watches like this one show the honest quality of everyday Swiss watchmaking of the 1960s, with jewelled movements and Incabloc shock protection.
