About this watch

A bold 1990s Seiko in a sculpted tonneau-shaped steel case. The silver dial has three silver sub-dials (chronograph minutes, small seconds and a dedicated alarm sub-dial at 6 o'clock), slim applied baton markers, a date window at 3 and a tachymeter scale around the flange.

The 39mm case sits on an integrated stainless steel bracelet with a Seiko-signed fold-over clasp. The case back is marked 7T32-5A50, Stainless Steel, Water Resistant 10 Bar, Made in Japan, with serial number 880599.

Brand heritage

Seiko traces its roots to 1881, when Kintaro Hattori opened a watch and clock shop in Tokyo; the company's Seikosha factory followed in 1892. Seiko changed watchmaking history in 1969 with the Astron, the world's first quartz wristwatch, and its quartz chronographs of the 1980s and 1990s, such as the 7T32 calibre, are now popular with collectors of that era.
