About this watch

A commemorative watch for King Saud University in Riyadh. The silver dial carries the KSU emblem with the university's name in Arabic and English, crystal-set hour markers and gold-tone hands, signed Quartz.

The 33mm gold-tone case has a silver-tone twisted-cable bezel with ball accents and a snap caseback, on a brown crocodile-pattern leather strap.

Brand heritage

King Saud University was established in 1957 by King Saud bin Abdulaziz and is the first university in Saudi Arabia. Known as Riyadh University from 1964, it returned to its original name in 1982. Watches bearing the university emblem were produced for the institution and are collected today as Saudi memorabilia.
