About this watch

A classic Mickey Mouse watch by Bradley from the 1970s. The cream dial shows Mickey in his red shorts and yellow shoes, with his gloved hands pointing out the hours and minutes, bold black Arabic numerals and a printed minute track. The dial is signed Bradley, Swiss Made and © Walt Disney Productions.

The 26mm silver-tone case has a brushed bezel, flared lugs and a brushed case back stamped Base Metal, Swiss. It runs on a Swiss mechanical hand-wound movement and is fitted on a tan leather strap.

Brand heritage

The first Mickey Mouse watch was introduced by Ingersoll in 1933, during the Great Depression, and Mickey's moving arms telling the time quickly became one of the most loved designs in American popular culture.

In the early 1970s the Disney character watch licence passed to Bradley Time, a division of Elgin National Industries. Bradley produced a wide range of Mickey Mouse watches through the 1970s, many with Swiss-made mechanical movements, and they are today collected by Disney and vintage watch enthusiasts alike.
