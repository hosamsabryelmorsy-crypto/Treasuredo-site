About this watch

A vintage Delma Nostalgia automatic from the 1980s with a striking shimmer-brown sunburst dial that shifts from chocolate to bronze in the light. Applied gold-tone baton markers and matching hands frame a day-date window at 3 o'clock with an Arabic/English day disc, and the dial is signed "Delma of Switzerland", "Nostalgia" in script and "Automatic".

The 43mm jumbo stainless-steel case flows into an integrated stainless-steel bracelet with folding clasp, giving the watch a bold, sculptural 1980s presence. It comes in its Delma presentation box.

Brand heritage

Delma was founded in 1924 in Lengnau, near Biel/Bienne, Switzerland, and is one of the few Swiss watch brands that has remained independent and family-owned throughout its history. The brand is best known for robust, good-value Swiss mechanical watches, including its dive watches.
