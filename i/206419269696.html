About this watch

A collectible special-edition Guess Steel. The white dial carries a full-colour emblem with the Kuwaiti flag colours at the centre, applied baton markers, the Guess logo, "Steel" signature and a date window at 4 o'clock.

The tonneau-shaped stainless-steel case (approx. 31 x 40 mm) is fitted to a matching integrated steel bracelet. The caseback is marked Guess Steel, Stainless Steel, Japan Movement and water resistant to 100 feet. It comes with a Guess box and booklet.

Brand heritage

Guess was founded in Los Angeles in 1981 by the Marciano brothers and quickly became one of America's best-known fashion brands. In 1984 Guess introduced its watch lines, including "Guess Steel", the name signed on this dial.
