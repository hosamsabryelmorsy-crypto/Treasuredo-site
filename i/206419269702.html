About this watch

A colourful 1970s Swiss watch with real presence. The red sunburst dial has two-tone applied baton markers with green and gold inlays, gold-tone hands, a red seconds hand and a date window at 3 o'clock, signed "Rayolux Super" and "17 Jewels Shockprotected".

The 36mm stainless-steel case has broad faceted flanks typical of the decade. Inside is a Swiss 17-jewel manual-wind movement. It is fitted to a brown leather two-piece strap.

Brand heritage

Rayolux is one of the many smaller Swiss watch labels of the 1970s whose company history is not well documented. Watches like this used Swiss 17-jewel shock-protected movements and bold, colourful dials, typical of the era's export models.
