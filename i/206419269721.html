About this watch

A slim, elegant Ted Lapidus dress watch with a black dial, the TL monogram and Ted Lapidus Paris signature, four stone-set markers at 12, 3, 6 and 9 and gold-tone hands. The dial is marked Quartz and France.

The rectangular gold-plated case has a black frame and continues into a striking black and gold multi-row bracelet. The caseback is signed with the Ted Lapidus signature, Paris, Swiss Quartz, Showerproof, 3.387.

Brand heritage

Edmond "Ted" Lapidus (1929-2008) was a Paris-born couturier who trained at Dior and launched his own label in 1951. He became famous in the 1960s, dressing stars such as Brigitte Bardot and Alain Delon, and is credited with pioneering unisex fashion and bringing military and safari styles into haute couture. His house later became well known for accessories, including watches and fragrances.
