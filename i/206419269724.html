Brand heritage

Watchmaking in Ruhla, Thuringia, began with the brothers Christian and Georg Thiel in the second half of the 19th century. Gebrüder Thiel became one of the first German companies to mass-produce affordable pocket watches. After the Second World War the works became state-owned in East Germany, trading as UMF Ruhla, and in 1967 Ruhla, Glashütte and Weimar were merged into VEB Uhrenkombinat Ruhla. Ruhla made millions of robust, affordable mechanical watches that were exported around the world.
