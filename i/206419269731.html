About this watch

A bold piece of 1970s design. The warm yellow-champagne dial is signed "JUPITER" with its star logo, "Crystal", "De Luxe", "17 Jewels Super Shock" and "T Swiss Made T". An octagonal printed frame with green dot minute track surrounds the centre, with faceted applied markers at 12, 3 and 6, a red-tipped seconds hand and a date window at 3 o'clock.

The 38mm stainless-steel cushion case has wide, sunburst-finished flanks around a faceted crystal that gives the watch its striking look. It runs on a 17-jewel Swiss hand-wound movement and is fitted with a brown leather strap.

Brand heritage

Jupiter was one of the many small independent Swiss watch brands of the post-war decades. Labels like this did not make their own movements; they bought proven calibres from the specialist Swiss movement makers, cased them under their own name and sold them mainly through export distributors. Few company records survive, and many such brands disappeared during the quartz crisis of the 1970s, which is why signed examples in working order are an interesting find for collectors today.
