About this watch

A cheerful vintage Swiss dress watch: the silver sunburst dial has applied gold Arabic numerals, luminous dots and hands, and a red date at 3 o'clock, signed "Helsa 21 Jewels", "Antimagnetic", "Unbreakable Mainspring" and "T Swiss Made T".

The 34mm gold-plated case has a textured, engine-turned style bezel and a screw-down back. Inside is a 21-jewel Swiss manual-wind movement. It is fitted on a brown leather strap.

Brand heritage

Helsa is one of the many small Swiss brands of the mid-20th century that offered well-made mechanical watches built on reliable Swiss movements. Dials of the period proudly listed features such as an antimagnetic movement and an "unbreakable mainspring", the alloy mainsprings that made watches of this era more dependable.
