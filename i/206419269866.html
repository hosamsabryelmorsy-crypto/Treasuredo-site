About this watch

A classic mid-century Nivada with warm rose-gold tones. The silver dial has applied gold-tone Arabic numerals, dauphine-style hands, the Nivada Compensamatic signature and a "17" jewel mark, with Swiss Made at 6.

The 33mm rose gold-plated case has a stainless-steel screw-down back marked Waterproof, Shockprotected, Compensamatic. Inside is a Nivada-signed Swiss manual-wind movement with seventeen jewels. It is fitted with a dark brown lizard-pattern leather strap.

Brand heritage

Nivada Grenchen was founded in Grenchen, Switzerland, in 1879 by Jacob Schneider and grew into a respected maker of reliable, legible watches after the Second World War, until the quartz crisis.

The Compensamatic line is associated with Nivada's emphasis on accuracy, using a temperature-compensating balance to reduce timing deviations from heat and cold.
