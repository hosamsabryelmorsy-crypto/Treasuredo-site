About this watch

A charming mid-century Titoni with real character. The grey-silver dial, signed "Titoni Airmaster" with the brand's flower emblem, has developed an even speckled patina and carries gold dagger markers, gold Arabic numerals at 3, 6 and 9, a crosshair and a dotted minute track, with gold dauphine hands.

The 36mm gold-plated case has sculpted lugs and a screw-down back. Inside is a signed Titoni Rotomatic automatic movement, Swiss made with 21 jewels. It is fitted on a tan leather strap.

Brand heritage

Titoni was founded in 1919 by Fritz Schluep in Grenchen, Switzerland, originally under the name Felca. The family company looked beyond Switzerland from the very beginning, with early sales in Germany, America and Japan, and became especially well known in Asia. After Fritz Schluep's death in 1959 his son Bruno took over, and the company is still owned and run by the founding family today. The Airmaster name remains one of Titoni's signature lines.
