About this watch

An elegant Art Deco tank watch signed Zedon Genève. The aged cream dial has gold-tone Arabic numerals, a sub-seconds register at 6 o'clock and blued hands, framed by a curved rectangular case with stepped, flared sides typical of the period.

Inside is a Swiss manual-wind movement signed ZEDON GENEVE, 17 Jewels, Swiss Made. The watch is fitted with a black fabric strap.

Brand heritage

Zedon was a Swiss brand whose name is an anagram of Donzé. The business traces back to Louis Donzé-Bilat in Saignelégier in the Swiss Jura in 1928, and the Zedon trademark was registered to Donzé Frères & Cie in 1943. The brand was later re-registered in Geneva and was aimed mainly at the French market.
