About this watch

A piece of 1970s mechanical futurism. Instead of hands, the Aristo shows the time through rotating white discs: the hour in one window and the minutes on a second disc beside it, framed by a dark band bearing the Aristo signature. The angular, faceted gold-plated case has a brushed front with a long recessed display window, a shape enthusiasts often call a "coffin" case.

The snap-on stainless-steel caseback is marked Waterresistant, Stainless Steel Back and "5 7". Inside is a mechanical manual-wind movement. It comes on a new dark brown leather strap with white contrast stitching.

Brand heritage

Aristo was founded in Pforzheim, Germany's watch and jewellery capital, in 1907 by watchmaker Julius Epple. The company began with jewellery components, moved into watch cases and then complete watches, and in 1934 even produced its own hand-wound calibre. Several million Aristo watches were made between 1907 and 1992, and the name lives on today as Aristo Vollmer.
