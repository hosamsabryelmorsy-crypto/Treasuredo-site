About this watch

An elegant ladies' Raymond Weil from the sport-chic Tango collection. The white dial has black Roman numerals, slim steel hands, a date window at 3 o'clock and the Raymond Weil Geneve and Tango signatures.

The round stainless-steel case (approx. 30 mm with crown) has a polished bezel and a screw-down caseback marked Swiss Made, stainless steel, W.R. 50m / 165 ft, sapphire crystal and reference 5389. It is worn on a stainless-steel link bracelet with folding clasp and comes with a Raymond Weil box.

Brand heritage

Raymond Weil was founded in Geneva in 1976 by watchmaker Raymond Weil and has remained an independent, family-run Swiss company. The brand is known for naming its collections after music and the arts, such as Tango, Parsifal and Toccata.
