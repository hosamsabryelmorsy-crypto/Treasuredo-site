About this watch

A complete 1998 Delma dress watch set. The 35mm gold-plated case has fluted, stepped lugs and a slim profile, and frames a crisp white dial with black Roman numerals, slim gold-tone leaf hands and a date window at 3 o'clock, signed DELMA and Swiss Made.

It is new old stock: the original protective film is still on the case back. The watch is fitted on a black lizard-grain genuine leather strap with its original gold-tone Delma-signed pin buckle, and comes with the Delma presentation box, booklets and the stamped international guarantee card (Ref. 667200, dated 31-12-98).

Brand heritage

Delma was founded in 1924 in Lengnau, near Biel/Bienne, in the heart of Swiss watchmaking. In 1966 Ulrich Wüthrich took over the company and renamed it Delma, after one of its earlier brands, and it has remained an independent, family-owned Swiss watchmaker ever since, still based in Lengnau.
