Brand heritage

Saint Honoré was founded in 1885 by watchmaker Victorin Frésard in the Doubs region of eastern France, close to the Swiss border, and has remained in the hands of the Frésard family ever since. In 1984 Thierry Frésard, the founder's great-grandson, became president, and in 1985 the company adopted the name Saint Honoré Paris, after the famous Parisian street of luxury. The brand built a strong international following, particularly in the Middle East and Asia, with collections such as Orsay, Opéra and Lutécia.
