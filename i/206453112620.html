About this watch

A slim, elegant S.T. Dupont dress watch whose centre dial carries the house's signature "Clous de Paris" (hobnail) pattern in champagne gold, the same micro-pyramid motif made famous on its luxury lighters. The steel outer ring is marked with Roman numerals at XII, III, VI and IX and applied dot markers, with slim dark hands.

The 32.5mm stainless-steel case is just 5mm thick, with gold-tone accents on the crown. The snap-on caseback is engraved S.T. Dupont Paris, Swiss Made, Stainless Steel Case, Water Resistant and Ref. 199.11.1. It is fitted on a tan brown leather strap with contrast stitching and a gold-tone pin buckle, and comes in its black lacquer S.T. Dupont presentation box.

Brand heritage

S.T. Dupont was founded in Paris in 1872 by Simon Tissot-Dupont, initially as a maker of fine leather goods and travel cases for an elite clientele. The house later became world-famous for its precious-metal and lacquer lighters, introduced in the 1940s, and its fine writing instruments. Its signature "Clous de Paris" guilloché pattern became one of the defining motifs of French luxury, and its watches carry the same design language.
