About this watch

A genuine Soviet-era Vostok Amphibia, the legendary dive watch of the USSR. The two-tone dial pairs a white centre with a black outer ring and large luminous Arabic numerals and batons. It is signed "Восток" (Vostok), "Антимагнитные" (antimagnetic), "17 камней" (17 jewels) and "Сделано в СССР" (Made in USSR), with an orange seconds hand and an arrow-tipped hour hand.

The 40mm steel case has a rotating dive bezel and the Amphibia's famous screw-down caseback, stamped "Амфибия", "Водонепроницаемые 200м" (water resistant 200 m) and "Противоударные" (shock-resistant). It is fitted on a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Vostok's story begins at the First Moscow Watch Factory, which was evacuated in 1941 to Chistopol in Tatarstan. The Chistopol factory became an official supplier to the Soviet Ministry of Defence in 1965, launched the Komandirskie the same year and introduced the Amphibia in 1967, a 200 m dive watch engineered with its own clever case design rather than licensed Western solutions. From 1969 the factory's watches were sold under the Vostok name.
