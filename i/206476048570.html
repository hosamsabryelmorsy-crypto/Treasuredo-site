About this watch

A Bentley men's dress watch, reference BL1615, in two-tone gold-tone and stainless steel. The 43mm case frames a white guilloche-patterned dial with large black Roman numerals, a printed outer minute track, gold-tone leaf hands and a small seconds sub-dial at 6 o'clock, signed Bentley with the B emblem and Made in Germany.

The stainless steel case back is secured by four screws and engraved Bentley, BL1615, 5 ATM, Stainless Steel, Made in Germany, around a honeycomb-patterned B emblem. The watch is worn on a two-tone stainless steel link bracelet with folding clasp and comes with its Bentley presentation box.

Brand heritage

This watch is signed Bentley and, as the dial and case back state, made in Germany. It is a separate line from the Breitling for Bentley collection, and is offered here as the Bentley-branded German watch it is.
