Brand heritage

Buler was founded in 1945 in Lengnau, Switzerland, by Charles and Albert Bühler as a maker of affordable Swiss watches. In the 1960s Montres Buler SA was a member of the Roskopf association, focused on inexpensive pin-lever watches, and in the 1970s the brand became known for adventurous designs, including early LCD watches and a 1976 "Stratos by Bertone" model. In 1991 Buler was bought by Renley Watch Manufacturing and moved to La Chaux-de-Fonds.
