About this watch

A superb example of 1970s Swiss space-age design. The sunburst dial graduates from warm champagne gold to deep chocolate brown, with applied hour indices, the Tressa lightning-bolt branding and a vertical day-date display.

The 39mm brushed stainless steel tonneau case flows into its original integrated H-link steel bracelet with a Tressa-signed fold-over clasp. Factory protective film is still present on the case back and clasp, and the watch comes with its red, gold-trimmed Tressa presentation box.

Brand heritage

Tressa was a Swiss watch brand popular in the 1960s and 1970s, known for bold, modern case shapes, colourful dials and reliable Swiss automatic movements, including an automatic chronograph in 1972. Like many independent Swiss names it disappeared during the quartz crisis, and its period pieces are now collected for their adventurous design.
