About this watch

A clean and classic piece of 1970s Swiss watchmaking: a vintage Waltham Selfwinding automatic, reference B-411 / 908. The sunburst champagne-silver dial has applied 3D block indices with black inlays, lumed baton hands and a framed day-date window at 3 o'clock, and is signed Waltham Selfwinding, Incabloc 17 Jewels.

The 34mm stainless-steel case has prominent angular, faceted lugs and a screw-down steel caseback. Inside, the 17-jewel Swiss automatic movement with Incabloc shock protection carries a rotor signed Waltham Watch Co., Seventeen 17 Jewels, Unadjusted, Swiss. It is mounted on a vintage geometric stainless-steel expandable bracelet.

Brand heritage

The Waltham Watch Company was founded in 1850 and settled in Waltham, Massachusetts, in 1854, where it pioneered the mass production of watches with interchangeable machine-made parts. Waltham became one of America's great watchmakers, famous for railroad-grade pocket watches, and produced more than 30 million watches before its American factory closed in 1957. The Waltham name continued on Swiss-made watches from the 1950s onwards, combining the American heritage with Swiss movements.
