About this watch

A 1950s Emko with an Art Deco-style "tuxedo" sector dial: the centre is divided into alternating light and darker silver quadrants, framed by a dark outer track with applied gold-tone wedge markers. The dial is signed Emko 17 Rubis, Shock-Resist and Water-Protected, with gold-tone dauphine hands and centre seconds.

The 34mm round case is gold plated, with sharp angular lugs and a textured bezel. The steel caseback is marked 20 Microns, Antimagnetic, Antichoc, Waterprotected and D.B.G.M. (Deutsches Bundes-Gebrauchsmuster, the German registered-design mark). Inside is a 17-jewel manual-wind mechanical movement with shock protection, shown in the photos. It comes on a new black croc-grain leather strap.

Brand heritage

Emko is a little-documented mid-century watch name, found on mechanical watches of the 1940s to 1960s. The D.B.G.M. mark on this caseback shows the case was registered for the German market, where many such watches were sold in the post-war years.
