About this watch

A fine mid-century Swiss dress watch. The silver sunburst dial carries faceted applied gold-tone markers, gold hands, the Sandoz shield emblem and script signature, a date window at 3 o'clock and "17 Jewels Incabloc".

The 36mm gold-plated case has angled lugs and a stainless-steel back engraved with the Sandoz emblem and "Tropicalized", "Super Waterproof", "Antimagnetic" and "Incabloc" markings. Inside is a 17-jewel Swiss manual-wind movement signed Henri Sandoz & Fils. It is fitted to a dark brown lizard-pattern leather strap.

Brand heritage

The Sandoz name goes back to Henri Frédéric Sandoz of Le Locle, who founded Henri Sandoz & Fils around 1870 and in the 1890s helped establish the Tavannes Watch Co., which grew into a model factory with a thousand workers. The movement in this watch still carries the historic Henri Sandoz & Fils signature.
