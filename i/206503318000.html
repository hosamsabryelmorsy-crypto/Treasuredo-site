About this watch

A refined two-tone Delma dress watch. The silver dial has a waffle (tapisserie) textured centre, a gold Roman XII, slim gold baton markers, gold hands and a date window between 4 and 5 o'clock.

The 35mm stainless-steel case has a gold-plated bezel and a sapphire crystal, and continues into an integrated two-tone multi-link bracelet. The caseback reads Delma Swiss, Sapphire Crystal, Waterresistant 3 ATM, E.O.L., All Stainless Steel, 467.115Y.

Brand heritage

Delma was founded in 1924 in Lengnau, near Biel, Switzerland, by Adolf and Albert Gilomen. The company first made watches and movements for other brands, and the Delma name was registered in 1946. Delma became known for robust dive watches from the 1960s onward and remains an independent, family-owned Swiss watchmaker.
