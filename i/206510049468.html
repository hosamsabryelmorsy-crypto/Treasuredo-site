About this watch

A handsome post-war Swiss dress watch with real period charm. The cream dial, signed LIGA and ANTIMAGNETIC, carries gold-tone Arabic numerals at the even hours, arrowhead markers in between, an outer minute track numbered in fives and slim blued-steel style hands, with central seconds. The dial has an even warm vintage patina.

The 34mm gold-tone case has elegant sweeping teardrop lugs and a stainless steel back stamped STAINLESS STEEL BACK. Inside is a Swiss 15-jewel hand-wound movement, signed 15 JEWELS and SWISS MADE. It is fitted with a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Liga was a Swiss watchmaker based in Solothurn (Soleure), founded shortly before 1900. It began with Roskopf watches of better-than-usual quality, and by the 1940s it made pocket and wristwatches of all kinds, including sports timers, waterproof watches and alarm clocks. Its mid-century wristwatches, like this "Antimagnetic" model, are honest, well-made Swiss pieces that are rarely seen today.
