About this watch

A sleek, minimalist dress watch with retro-futuristic flair. The octagonal case frames a matte black dial with polished silver hands, signed "GrandJoy" at 12 and "Romanson" at 6, and "Swiss Quartz".

The 28mm octagonal stainless-steel case has ribbed hooded lugs and a crown set with a black cabochon. The screw-secured caseback is marked "GrandJoy, Sapphire Glass, St. Steel Back, Water Resist, Swiss Quartz, FZ6302M". It is fitted to a black leather strap and comes with its Romanson box.

Brand heritage

Romanson was founded in South Korea in 1988 and became one of the country's best-known watchmakers, combining Korean design with Swiss quartz and mechanical movements. The company became known for its slim dress watches and cut-crystal designs, and GrandJoy was one of its upscale lines.
