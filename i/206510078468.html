About this watch

An all-original 1970s Fortis Sublime HiFi-Matic Swiss automatic, with the iconic TV-style cushion case and a rare Arabic day-date calendar. The rich dark-blue brushed dial is accented by polished steel indices and clean hands, with a day-date window at three showing the weekday in Arabic.

Powered by a Swiss 25-jewel automatic movement, the 31mm stainless-steel case retains crisp geometry. The watch is fitted with its original 15mm integrated steel link bracelet and its original signed Fortis crown.

Brand heritage

Fortis was founded in 1912 by Walter Vogt in Grenchen, Switzerland. In 1926 it partnered with British watchmaker John Harwood to produce the first serially manufactured automatic wristwatch, the Harwood Automatic, securing Fortis a permanent place in watchmaking history.

The brand is known for robust, precise automatic watches, and in the 1970s produced bold, design-led models such as this TV-dial HiFi-Matic.
