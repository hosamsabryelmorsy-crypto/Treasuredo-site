About this watch

A clean, design-led 1990s watch from the Benetton and Bulova partnership. The off-white dial carries the boxed "United Colors of Benetton" logo, stylised gold-outlined numerals at 12, 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 with slim baton markers between, black hands and a date window at 3 o'clock.

The 35mm round case has a matte black finish and a sculpted bezel. The stainless-steel caseback is stamped "United Colors of Benetton by Bulova, Mod. Dep." and still carries its red-line factory protective sticker. It runs on a quartz movement and comes on its Benetton-signed black leather strap with black buckle, in its original Benetton presentation box.

Brand heritage

Bulova was founded in New York in 1875 by Joseph Bulova and became one of America's best-known watch companies. In the early 1990s it partnered with the Italian fashion house United Colors of Benetton on a line of designer watches, including the Ecology collection, combining Bulova's watchmaking with Benetton's bold Italian style.
