About this watch

A mid-century German-market dress watch with a two-tone sector dial: a champagne centre with small-seconds register above 6, and a silvered outer ring with gold Arabic numerals and dagger markers. The dial is signed ANKER, "15 Steine" (15 jewels) and "Bruchsicher" (break-proof).

The 33mm gold-plated case has a stainless-steel snap back and sits on a dark navy Hirsch leather strap.

Brand heritage

Anker is a German watch name; the dial wording is German, with "15 Steine" for 15 jewels and "Bruchsicher" advertising a shock-protected, break-resistant balance. Watches like this were everyday dress pieces of post-war Germany and are now an accessible way into mid-century German-market mechanical watches.
