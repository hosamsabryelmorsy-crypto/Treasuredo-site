About this watch

A classic rectangular tank watch from Jovial. The clean white dial carries the Jovial logo, crystal-set hour markers, gilt Roman numerals at III and IX, patterned gold squares at 12 and 6, gold sword hands and a framed date window above 6 o'clock. The dial is signed Quartz and Swiss Made.

The two-tone case measures approximately 26 x 32mm and combines polished stainless steel with 22K electro gold-plated accents; the back is marked "Jovial No. 148422, 22K Electro Gold Plated, Water Resistant, Swiss Made". It sits on a matching two-tone integrated bracelet with a Jovial-signed clasp and comes with its long Jovial box and outer sleeve.

Brand heritage

Jovial was founded in 1929 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, and later moved its headquarters to Biel/Bienne. From its early days the brand specialised in the Middle Eastern market, where its elegant Swiss dress watches became widely known.
