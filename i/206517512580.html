About this watch

A handsome mid-century Roamer dress watch. The champagne sunburst dial has an applied Roamer logo block below 12, faceted silver-tone baton markers, dauphine hands and the "Incabloc 17" designation above 6, with Swiss Made at the base.

The approx. 34.5mm case features Roamer's patented Breveté construction, with the stainless-steel back deeply engraved Roamer, Waterproof, Stainless Steel Back and Swiss patents 305467, 305776, 307382 and 308491. It comes with a white Roamer of Switzerland presentation box.

Brand heritage

Roamer was founded in 1888 in Solothurn, Switzerland, by Fritz Meyer; the firm later became Meyer & Stüdeli (MST) and built an international reputation for its own Swiss movements and innovative, patented case designs aimed at dust and moisture protection.
