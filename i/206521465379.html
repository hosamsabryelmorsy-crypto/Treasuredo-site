About this watch

A standout from Swatch's Irony Big Classic range. The grey sunburst dial is lit up by bright orange hands and a sweeping semicircular date display that shows the date numbers around a sector on the dial.

The stainless-steel case has a coin-edge textured bezel ring and a caseback stamped "Swatch, Swiss Made, Stainless Steel, Water-Resistant, Patented" with battery hatch (377 / SR626SW). It is fitted to the original multi-link stainless-steel bracelet with folding clasp and comes in its Swatch plastic case with paper inserts.

Brand heritage

Swatch was launched in Switzerland in 1983 as an affordable, colourful Swiss quartz watch and helped revive the Swiss watch industry after the quartz crisis. Its Irony line moved the famous designs into metal cases, usually stainless steel, while keeping the brand's playful approach to colour and dial design.
