About this watch

A bold piece of 1970s design. The liquid-looking green and blue wave dial shifts colour as it catches the light, with gold-tone applied markers and hands, the Cannon signature, "21 Jewels Antimagnetic", "Swiss Movt" and a red and black day-date window at 3 o'clock.

The oversized 42mm black cushion case is framed by a polished steel inner bezel, with a steel screw-down back engraved "Cannon Automatic Shockproof". It comes on an integrated black strap with a steel folding clasp stamped CANNON.

Brand heritage

Cannon was one of the many export brands of the 1970s that housed Swiss mechanical movements in fashionable, often daring cases. Such watches were sold widely around the world in the years before quartz took over, and today their colourful dials and bold shapes are collected as pure 1970s style.
