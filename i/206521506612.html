About this watch

A bold piece of 1970s Swiss design: the warm champagne dial has a fine vertical pinstripe texture, applied gold-tone baton markers, gold hands and a framed day-date window at 3 o'clock. Below 12 sits Rado's signature red-backed anchor emblem.

The 37mm gold-plated cushion case has a stainless-steel screw-down caseback stamped with the Rado seahorse emblem, three stars, "Water Sealed" and the case reference 603.3184.2. It is fitted to a gold-tone multi-link bracelet with a folding clasp signed Rado, and runs on a Swiss automatic movement.

Brand heritage

Rado was founded in 1917 in Lengnau, Switzerland, as Schlup & Co. by the brothers Fritz, Ernst and Werner Schlup, who began as movement makers. The company launched watches under the Rado name in the 1950s and adopted Rado as its company name in 1957. In 1962 it introduced the scratch-resistant DiaStar, which set the course for the brand's material innovation. Rado joined ASUAG in 1968 and is today part of the Swatch Group.
