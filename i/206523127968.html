About this watch

Swiss-made Dalil (دليل, Arabic for "guide") watches are distinctive 1970s pieces designed for travellers in the Islamic world. The centrepiece of this watch is a central compass mounted above the hands, used to find the direction of the Qibla. Around the black dial, with its green and gold Islamic artwork and prominent Arabic Dalil logo, runs an inner rotating ring inscribed with world cities in Arabic, operated by the secondary crown, for working out time-zone differences.

The approximately 41mm black-coated cushion case with two crowns and the ribbed integrated bracelet embody classic 1970s design. The dial is signed Automatic and Swiss Made.

Brand heritage

Dalil watches were produced in Switzerland in the 1970s specifically for Muslim customers, combining a Swiss automatic movement with practical features for daily prayer such as the Qibla compass and Arabic world-time and prayer-time scales. Little documentation survives about the company behind them, and they are now an obscure and collectible chapter of Swiss watchmaking aimed at the Middle East market.
