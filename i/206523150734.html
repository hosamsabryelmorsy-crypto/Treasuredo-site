About this watch

A Swiss-made Felca day-date automatic with a dial printed with the colour portrait of former Libyan leader Muammar Gaddafi in a white headdress. Styled after classic "President" type watches, it combines a full day window at twelve, a date at three under a cyclops magnifier, a ring of small clear rhinestones as the hour track, gold baton markers and the Felca signature with its red emblem.

The approximately 39mm gold-plated case (including crown guards) has a faceted, multi-angled bezel and a stainless-steel screw back, and is fitted with a matching gold-tone three-row "President-style" bracelet. The dial is marked 25 JEWELS and SWISS MADE.

Brand heritage

Felca was founded in 1919 by Fritz Schluep in Grenchen, Switzerland, originally under the name Felco, and grew into one of the larger Swiss watch factories of the mid-20th century, selling its watches all over the world. The same family later developed the Titoni brand for Asian markets. Custom portrait dials like this one were commissioned in limited numbers for diplomatic and state presentation in the Middle East and North Africa.
