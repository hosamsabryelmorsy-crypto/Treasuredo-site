About this watch

A playful jewellery watch from Cerruti 1881. The polished steel flower-shaped cover carries a crystal-set C emblem and swings open on a hinge to reveal a black dial signed Cerruti 1881, Swiss Made, with silver hands, framed by a crystal-paved bezel.

The stainless-steel case has an engraved Cerruti 1881 monogram caseback and sits on a deep brown textured leather strap with buckle. Comes with its long Cerruti 1881 presentation box.

Brand heritage

The Cerruti story began in 1881, when the Cerruti brothers opened a wool mill in Biella, Italy. In 1967 Nino Cerruti founded the fashion house Cerruti 1881 in Paris, opening his first boutique on Place de la Madeleine. Famous for elegant tailoring, the house later extended into fragrances and Swiss-made watches.
