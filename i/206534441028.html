About this watch

A collectible club watch: the textured white-silver dial with bold black Roman numerals carries a blue "Al Nasr SC" emblem at the centre, the crest of the Dubai sports club, together with the Continental logo and "Swiss Made". The curved tonneau tank case measures 27 x 33 mm and gives the watch an elegant Art Deco profile.

The steel back is stamped with the Continental wreath emblem, "Steel Back", "Water Resistant" and reference 5049. It is fitted on a dark blue leather strap with the Continental crest on the keeper and comes with its white Continental box.

Brand heritage

The Continental name was registered in 1924 by Schild & Cie, makers of the Hebdomas watch. From 1953 it was run by Continental Uhren AG of Sissach, which registered the compass-arrow logo still seen on this dial, and in 1967 the brand passed to Economic Swiss Time, becoming its principal brand in 1977. Continental enjoyed a revival in the late 1980s and 1990s, when watches like this were sold widely in the Gulf.
