An original vintage Piaget watch presentation box with period Piaget hang tag, dating approximately to the 1970s.


This listing is for the presentation box and tag only — no watch is included.


The box is finished in black with decorative gold-tone edging and opens to a black satin and velvet-lined interior. The inside lid carries the classic period Piaget inscription:


PIAGET

Luxe et Précision

1874


This style of presentation box is associated with Piaget watches from the 1970s and is an attractive period accessory for completing or displaying a vintage Piaget timepiece.


The underside retains the partially preserved “BOX MADE IN SWITZERLAND” marking, consistent with Swiss-made Piaget presentation boxes of this period.


Also included is the small period Piaget hang tag shown in the photographs, with Piaget branding and reference-information field. Original loose accessories such as these are frequently missing from vintage watch sets, making the combination particularly useful to a collector assembling a period-correct Piaget presentation set.


Condition:

Authentic vintage pre-owned condition with visible age and storage wear. The exterior shows scratches, rubbing, creasing and areas where the surface finish has worn away. The gold decorative edging remains visible but shows age-related wear. The interior satin and velvet have dust, lint, marks and general aging. The underside branding is partially worn but still identifiable. The hang tag also shows age-related wear.
