A highly distinctive vintage Jaeger-LeCoultre Memovox Travel Alarm Clock, dating to approximately the 1970s, featuring a square blue lapis-style case, gilt Roman-numeral bezel, mechanical alarm complication, date display and the celebrated manually wound Jaeger-LeCoultre Calibre 911.


This compact travel/desk clock measures approximately 45mm x 45mm and was designed to function both as a portable timepiece and as a small desk or bedside clock. A hinged semicircular stand on the reverse allows it to be displayed upright.


The champagne/gold-tone dial features the classic Memovox layout with central hours, minutes and sweep seconds, a date aperture at 3 o’clock, and a triangular alarm indicator operated through the movable alarm-setting system.


The dial is signed:


JAEGER-LECOULTRE

SWISS MADE


Surrounding the dial is a broad gilt bezel decorated with black Roman numerals, set within a striking square blue stone-effect / lapis-style outer body. Similar period Jaeger-LeCoultre Memovox travel clocks were produced in several decorative finishes, including blue lapis-lazuli configurations.


Two separate crowns are fitted to operate the clock and its alarm mechanism: one for the timekeeping movement and the other for winding and setting the mechanical alarm.


Inside is the period Jaeger-LeCoultre Calibre 911, a manually wound mechanical movement developed specifically for these Memovox travel clocks, providing time, central seconds, date and mechanical alarm functions.
