The stainless-steel caseback is marked No. 3161, Stainless Steel Case, Water Resistant, Swiss. It is worn on a black leather strap with gold-tone buckle and comes in its matching Jovial presentation box.

Brand heritage

Jovial was founded in 1929 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, and later moved to Bienne (Biel), where it is still based. From the start the brand specialised in the Middle Eastern market, where it became one of the best-known names for Swiss-made dress watches. Crystal-set bezels and two-tone cases have long been part of its design language.
