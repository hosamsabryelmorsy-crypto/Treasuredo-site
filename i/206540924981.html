About this watch

A striking black-and-gold dress watch. The glossy black dial carries slim gold-tone baton markers and hands, the Jules Jurgensen crown emblem with "Since 1740", "Quartz" and a framed day-date window at 3 o'clock.

The 34mm gold-tone case has a faceted 12-sided bezel that gives it more character than a plain round dress watch. The stainless-steel caseback is stamped "Base Metal Bezel, Stainless Steel Back, 3 ATM Water Resistant", battery 364 and reference 4537. It is fitted to a gold-tone expansion bracelet.

Brand heritage

The Jurgensen name traces back to 1740 and the Danish watchmaker Jürgen Jürgensen. His grandson Jules Frédérik Jürgensen set up the family's Swiss house in Le Locle in 1836, and Jules Jürgensen watches were prized for their quality in the 19th and 20th centuries. From 1974 the brand was owned by the American distributor Mort Clayman, and watches of this period were sold mainly in the United States, often with quartz movements.
