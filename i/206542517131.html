About this watch

This vintage Jovial has a dial built in two layers. A pale silver-champagne centre carries the Jovial bee emblem and signature, "17 Jewels" and Swiss Made, with small red and green dot accents on the minute track. Around it, a transparent outer chapter holds large three-dimensional gold-tone Arabic numerals and arrow markers that seem to float above the dial, giving the watch real depth and an Art Deco feel.

The 35mm gold-plated case has long tapered lugs and a broad bezel, with a stainless-steel screw-down caseback marked Antimagnetic, Waterproof, Shockprotected and Swiss Made. Inside is a Swiss 17-jewel manual-wind mechanical movement. It is fitted on a brown leather strap.

Brand heritage

Jovial was founded in 1929 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, and later moved its headquarters to Biel/Bienne. From its earliest years the brand concentrated on the Middle Eastern market, where its dependable Swiss mechanical watches became widely known, a focus it has kept to this day.
