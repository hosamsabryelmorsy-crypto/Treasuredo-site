About this watch

Some vintage dials merely grow old; this one has acquired a signature of its own. The warm cream dial carries a beautiful, naturally developed speckled patina, framed by faceted gold-tone arrowhead markers, a dotted minute track and dauphine hands. The dial is signed "CYMA Watersport" and "Cymaflex", the name of CYMA's own shock-protection system.

The 30mm stainless-steel case (reference 2-5679-6) has classic post-war proportions with tapered lugs and a screw-down caseback. Inside is the signed CYMA calibre R.459, a 17-jewel Swiss manual-wind movement with centre seconds.

Brand heritage

CYMA was founded in 1862 in Le Locle, Switzerland, by the brothers Joseph and Théodore Schwob, and in the early 20th century worked closely with Tavannes, one of the largest Swiss movement makers. CYMA supplied watches to the British Army during the Second World War and became known for robust, in-house movements. Its patented Cymaflex shock absorber, introduced in the 1950s, protected the balance staff and was a selling point of the brand's sporty "Watersport" line, CYMA's water-resistant everyday watches of the period.
