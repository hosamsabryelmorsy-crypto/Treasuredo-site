Brand heritage

Helvetia was the house brand of the General Watch Co. of Biel/Bienne, a company set up in the 1890s by the Brandt family, the owners of Omega, before becoming independent in the early 20th century. Helvetia built a reputation for tough, practical watches and was an early adopter of shock protection and waterproof cases, which is why its 1930s and 1940s watches are so often found in military-style form. Helvetia also supplied watches to the British Army in the Second World War.
