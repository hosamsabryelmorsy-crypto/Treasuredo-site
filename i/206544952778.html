About this watch

An unusual vintage Citizen whose deep blue dial has aged into a varied turquoise-blue patina that looks different from every angle. Stylised Arabic numerals, slim baton hands and a centre seconds hand keep the layout clean and legible.

The faceted 33mm stainless steel case has a screw-down back marked All Stainless Steel and Water Resistant. Inside is the signed Citizen calibre 8270A, a 17-jewel Japanese hand-wound movement, shown in the photographs.

Brand heritage

Citizen began in Tokyo in 1918 as the Shokosha Watch Research Institute. Its first pocket watch, named "Citizen", appeared in 1924, and the Citizen Watch Company was formed in 1930. The firm pioneered shock-resistant and water-resistant watches in Japan in the 1950s and grew into one of the largest watchmakers in the world.
