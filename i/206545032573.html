About this watch

A charming and seldom-seen Waltham WB 825 quartz with a nostalgic celestial dial. The warm champagne dial has bold stylised Arabic numerals, a blue moon-phase display decorated with stars, a 29½-day moon scale and a framed date window at 6 o'clock, and is signed "Waltham Quartz, Japan Movt".

The 27mm gold-tone case has sweeping decorative lugs that give it a more elaborate presence than an ordinary ladies' dress watch. The stainless steel back is marked Base Metal Bezel, Stainless Steel Back, Waltham WB 825, 012, 2005. It is worn on a brown leather strap.

Brand heritage

Waltham was founded in Massachusetts in 1850 and became the first company to mass-produce watches with interchangeable parts, growing into one of the great names of American watchmaking and supplying railroad and military timepieces for a century. The American factory closed in 1957, and the Waltham name continued on watches made in Switzerland and later elsewhere.
