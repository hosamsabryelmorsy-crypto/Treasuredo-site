About this watch

The whole watch is designed as a stylised golden football. Raised, sculpted polygonal panels form the case and bezel, and the same geometry continues across the smoky charcoal dial, which surrounds a sunburst gold pentagon at the centre. The dial is signed F.M. DuRoy and Swiss Made, with slim hands, centre seconds and an angled date window between 4 and 5 o'clock.

The 34mm gold-plated case has a stainless-steel snap caseback signed F.M. DuRoy and marked 3 ATM water resistant. It runs on a Swiss quartz movement and is fitted on a white genuine-leather strap marked "Echt Leder".

Brand heritage

F.M. DuRoy is a little-documented Swiss-made watch name of the late 20th century; the F.M. DuRoy trademark was registered for watches in the 1980s and 1990s. Its pieces were aimed at playful, design-led wristwear rather than classic dress watches, and this football design is one of its most distinctive.
