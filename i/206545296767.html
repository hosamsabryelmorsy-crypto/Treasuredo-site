About this watch

Sculpted teardrop lugs curve away from the rounded gold-tone case like small gilded leaves, framing an almost instrument-like dial. The ivory-cream dial has large green Arabic numerals, a fine black minute track, dark-edged hands with pale green inserts and a red centre seconds hand, with "15 Jewels Waterproof", "Antimagnetic" and "Made in France" printed on the dial.

The 34mm case has a stainless-steel back and a large ridged crown. Inside is a hand-wound mechanical movement marked 15 Jewels. The watch is unsigned by brand and fitted with a tan crocodile-pattern leather strap.

Brand heritage

France had a strong watchmaking tradition centred on Besançon and the Franche-Comté region near the Swiss border, which supplied many affordable mechanical watches in the post-war decades. Many were sold unbranded or under shop names, with "Made in France" on the dial.

Its dating to circa 1950s is a stylistic attribution rather than a documented production year.
