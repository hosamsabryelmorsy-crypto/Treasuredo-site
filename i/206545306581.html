About this watch

The yellow-and-black crest of Kuwait's Qadsia Sporting Club stands out on the ivory-silver dial of this Jean Jacques Vivier Paris watch, its colour echoed by the gold-tone bezel. Small gold-tone dot markers alternate with four stone accents around a fine chapter ring, with slender gold-tone hands and the Paris signature below centre.

The 33mm case and bracelet have sculpted, puzzle-like links with polished gold-tone centres against satin silver-grey steel, and four small bow-shaped ornaments decorate the bezel. The caseback is signed Jean Jacques Vivier Paris, All Stainless Steel, Water Resistant.

Brand heritage

Jean Jacques Vivier is a Paris-signed fashion watch brand. Watches carrying the crests of Gulf football clubs were popular with supporters, and emblem dials of this kind are now collected in their own right. Qadsia, founded in 1953 and known as the Yellow Castle, is one of Kuwait's most successful football clubs.
