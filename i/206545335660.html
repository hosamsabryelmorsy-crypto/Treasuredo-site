About this watch

A 1970s mechanical "digital" watch: instead of hands, the time is read from rotating discs, with the hour in a square window and the minutes on a curved disc beside it, plus a date window below. The dark grey dial is signed Kramer and "17 Jewels Incabloc", with fine horizontal lines that give it an instrument-like look under a high-domed crystal.

The 39mm gold-tone case has a broad cushion profile with a deeply recessed display window, a true Space Age shape. The stainless-steel snap caseback is marked Waterproof, Swiss Made and Mod. Depose. It is fitted on a brown leather strap.

Brand heritage

Kramer is a little-documented Swiss watch name of the 1970s. Like many smaller Swiss houses of that decade, it answered the fashion for early electronic digital watches with mechanical jump-hour and disc-display models, which are now collected for their bold Space Age design.
