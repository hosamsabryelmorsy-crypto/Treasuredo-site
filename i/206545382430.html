The watch comes with the vintage West End Watch Co. presentation box shown.

Brand heritage

The West End Watch Co. was founded in 1886 in Bombay (now Mumbai) by the Swiss firm Droz & Amstutz of Saint-Imier, to make Swiss watches for the Indian market; the name was inspired by London's fashionable West End. Its robust Sowar and Sowar Prima watches became legends in India and the Middle East, and in 1934 the Sowar Prima was one of the early adopters of the Incabloc shock-protection system.

In 1973 the company was taken over by its main supplier, Aubry Frères of Le Noirmont in the Swiss Jura. West End watches were widely sold across the Gulf, where special-dial versions such as this one were made for regional customers.
