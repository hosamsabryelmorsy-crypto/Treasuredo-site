About this watch

A wonderfully flamboyant 1970s Swiss Omax. The rich gold sunburst dial catches the light dramatically, surrounded by a pale outer chapter ring with bold applied markers. It is signed OMAX AUTOMATIC, Crystal, 25 JEWELS and SWISS MADE, and at 3 o'clock the calendar shows the day in Arabic beside the date.

The large 39mm case has a broad polished steel body with a flowing 1970s profile and a gold-tone bezel. The Omax-signed stainless steel screw back is marked Automatic, Shockproof, Waterproof, All Stainless Steel and 3559K (historic markings only). Inside is a Swiss 25-jewel automatic movement.

Brand heritage

Omax was founded in 1946 in Geneva, Switzerland, and grew into one of the largest exporters of Swiss watches, becoming a leading Swiss brand in the Indian subcontinent in the 1960s and one of Switzerland's most exported names in the 1970s. In the 1980s the company moved its base to Dubai and turned to affordable quartz watches.
