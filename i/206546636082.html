About this watch

A clean, architectural dress watch: the black dial is framed by a fine silver border, with elongated baton markers and dramatically stylised Arabic numerals at 12 and 6 that give it a distinctive Art Deco character. Two slim hands keep the layout simple and symmetrical. The dial is signed Kolber Genève and Swiss Made.

The gently curved tonneau case measures approx. 28 x 38mm in polished stainless steel and flows into a matching articulated steel bracelet with fold-over clasp. The caseback is signed Kolber Genève and marked K5400, All Stainless Steel, Sapphire Crystal, Water Resistant and Swiss Made.

Brand heritage

Kolber Genève was created in Geneva in 1983, part of the wave of new Swiss brands that followed the quartz crisis. The brand is known for clean lines, balanced proportions and understated elegance, using dependable Swiss quartz movements.
