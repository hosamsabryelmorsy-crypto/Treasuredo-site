About this watch

A scarce piece of advertising memorabilia: a Swiss mechanical Cronel with the Marlboro name printed in red on an aged deep-blue dial, with applied hour markers, an Arabic 12, baton hands and a date window at 6 o'clock.

The polished cushion case measures approx. 38mm, with more wrist presence than most promotional watches of its time. The stainless steel snap back carries period wording for Stainless Steel Back, Shock-Resistant, Antimagnetic and Waterproof. It is fitted with a purple crocodile-pattern leather strap.

Brand heritage

Cronel was a small Swiss watch name of the 1960s and 1970s whose watches were sold mainly in export markets. Like many independent labels of the period it used bought-in Swiss movements, and little company documentation survives. Logo-dial watches like this one were a popular form of corporate promotion in the 1970s.
