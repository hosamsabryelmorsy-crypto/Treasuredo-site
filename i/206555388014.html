About this watch

A clean, classic Citizen dress watch with a commemorative dial. The white dial carries polished baton markers, slender hands and a framed date window at 3 o'clock, with the Bushu Gas emblem at 12, the company name and a discreet 75th Anniversary inscription.

The 35mm stainless-steel case has a broad polished bezel and angular lugs. The caseback, marked E110-345665, identifies Citizen's Eco-Drive light-powered quartz movement. Fitted with a black crocodile-pattern strap and supplied with a Citizen Eco-Drive box.

Brand heritage

Citizen traces its origins to the Shokosha Watch Research Institute, founded in Tokyo in 1918, and adopted the Citizen name for its first pocket watch in 1924. The company pioneered light-powered analogue watches and launched its Eco-Drive technology in the 1990s, which charges from any light source. Bushu Gas, the utility company named on the dial, was founded in 1926 in Kawagoe, Saitama, so its 75th anniversary fell in 2001.
