About this watch

A quiet, well-proportioned mid-century dress watch. The silvered sunburst dial is signed "NOVELTI" with "17 Rubis Incabloc" and "Swiss Made", and carries applied baton markers with applied Arabic numerals at 12 and 6, slim dauphine-style hands and a centre seconds hand.

The 32mm round case has a stainless-steel back engraved "Waterproof, Antimagnetic, Incabloc, Unbreakable Mainspring, Swiss Made". Inside is a 17-jewel Swiss hand-wound movement with Incabloc shock protection. It is fitted with a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Novelti was one of the many small independent Swiss watch brands of the post-war decades. Labels like this did not make their own movements; they bought proven calibres from the specialist Swiss movement makers, cased them under their own name and sold them mainly through export distributors. Few company records survive, and many such brands disappeared during the quartz crisis of the 1970s, which is why signed examples in working order are an interesting find for collectors today.
