Vintage Telion Swiss Manual Wind, 17 Jewels, 30mm Gold-Tone Case with Fancy Lugs, Patina Dial, Red Seconds, 1950s

A compact mid-century Swiss watch with plenty of character: a cream dial with heavy mottled patina, bold Arabic numerals, a fine outer minute track with dot-and-triangle markers, and a vivid red centre seconds hand. The 30mm gold-tone case has sharply sculpted, angular lugs.

Brand heritage

Telion is one of the many small Swiss dial names of the 1940s and 1950s that were sold through jewellers and export agents rather than under a famous manufacture. These watches typically used dependable jewelled lever movements from the large Swiss movement makers, and surviving company records are scarce.

Model history

Small, legible, military-influenced watches like this were the standard man’s wristwatch of the post-war years: 30–33mm cases, luminous-style numerals, centre seconds and a screw back marked waterproof. The decorative faceted lugs lift this example above the ordinary and point to 1950s production.
