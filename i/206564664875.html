Vintage Candino Elite Automatic, Chocolate Brown Dial, Date, 32 x 38mm Gold-Plated Case and Bracelet, 1970s, with Box

A sculptural 1970s dress watch in chocolate brown and warm gold. The dial has a geometric gold minute track, paired dot markers, three stone-set accents at 12, 6 and 9 o’clock, a framed date window at 3 and the “Elite” signature. The shaped case flows into a finely ribbed gold-tone bracelet.

Brand heritage

Candino was founded in 1947 by Adolf Flury-Hug in Herbetswil, in the Swiss canton of Solothurn, and built its reputation on dependable Swiss-made watches at accessible prices. The brand later became part of the Festina group and is still made in Switzerland today.

Model history

Elite was one of Candino’s dressier lines in the 1970s, a period when the brand produced shaped, integrated-bracelet automatics in the fashionable brown and gold colourways of the decade. This example pairs a Swiss automatic movement with a rectangular gold-plated case of about 32 x 38mm.
