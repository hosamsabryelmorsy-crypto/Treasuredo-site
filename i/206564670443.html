Swiss watchmaking with a distinctive Emirati identity. This vintage JOVIAL features an original UAE Armed Forces emblem on its white dial, giving a restrained everyday watch a compelling focal point for collectors of military-emblem and Gulf-related timepieces.


The coloured emblem sits above 6 o’clock, balanced by the JOVIAL name and winged logo beneath 12. Applied silver-tone baton markers, slender hands and a finely printed outer minute track complete the uncluttered dial.


Founded in 1929 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, JOVIAL relocated to Bienne in 1946. The brand subsequently developed a longstanding presence in the Gulf region. This emblem-dial example combines that Swiss heritage with a distinctly regional collecting interest.


Measuring approximately 34mm excluding the crown, the stainless-steel case has a slim profile, sculpted bezel and distinctive shaped strap attachments. A tan leather strap with contrasting dark stitching brings warmth and texture to the silver-and-white composition.
