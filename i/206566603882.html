A champagne-gold dial in a broad brushed TV-shaped steel case gives this Seiko its seventies character. The dial carries the applied Seiko signature, the Seiko 5 shield and DX lettering, with applied baton markers, a contrasting outer minute track and a framed day-date window at 3 o’clock.

Brand heritage

Seiko began in 1881 when Kintaro Hattori opened a watch shop in Tokyo. The company made Japan’s first wristwatch in 1913 and the world’s first quartz wristwatch in 1969, and its affordable automatics of the 1960s and 1970s were sold all over the world.

Model history

The Seiko 5 line began in 1963 and stood for five features: automatic winding, day and date in one window, water resistance, a recessed crown and a durable case. “DX” marked the better-finished versions. The case back of this watch carries reference 6319-5020, which belongs to Seiko’s 21-jewel 6319 movement family of the later 1970s.
