Brand heritage

Seiko traces its origins to 1881, when Kintaro Hattori opened a watch and clock shop in Tokyo; the company made Japan's first wristwatch in 1913 and launched the world's first quartz wristwatch, the Astron, in 1969. In 1988 Seiko introduced Kinetic technology, which converts the wearer's movement into electricity to power a quartz movement, combining automatic-style winding with quartz accuracy.
