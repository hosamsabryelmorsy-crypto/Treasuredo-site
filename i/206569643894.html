About this watch

A refined late-1960s ladies' Seiko with a restrained silver-champagne sunburst dial, applied baton hour markers, slender hands and a framed date window at 3 o'clock. The dial is signed SEIKO and "Water Proof 17 Jewels", and a finely patterned inner surround adds subtle texture.

The 27mm stainless-steel case carries reference 2107-0110 and serial 941074 on the back, consistent with April 1969 production. Inside is Seiko's hand-wound 17-jewel calibre 2107 with date, from the manually wound Chorus Calendar family. It is paired with a Seiko-signed multi-link steel bracelet that gives the small case a jewellery-like look.

Brand heritage

Seiko traces its origins to 1881, when Kintaro Hattori opened a watch and jewellery shop in Tokyo; his Seikosha factory followed in 1892, and in 1913 it produced Japan's first wristwatch, the Laurel. By the 1960s Seiko had become one of the world's major watchmakers, and the Chorus line was its range of elegant, hand-wound ladies' watches, many of them with a calendar like this one.
