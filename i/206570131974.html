About this watch

A vintage Rado Silver Horse automatic with applied silver-tone hour markers, the red-backed Rado anchor emblem, twin seahorses above six o'clock and a framed date window at three. The black dial has aged dramatically: across the upper-right half the black finish has worn away to reveal the silver base beneath, giving a striking two-tone look.

The approximately 38mm stainless-steel case has a signed crown and a screw-down Silver Horse caseback decorated with Rado's twin-seahorse motif. It is paired with a textured metal bracelet with a Rado-signed folding clasp, and a brown Rado presentation box accompanies the watch.

Brand heritage

Rado traces its roots to the Schlup & Co. movement workshop founded in Lengnau, Switzerland, in 1917, and began selling watches under the Rado name in the 1950s. Its first collections of the late 1950s and 1960s, the Green Horse, Golden Horse and Silver Horse automatics with the seahorse emblem on the caseback, established the brand before the arrival of the scratch-resistant DiaStar in 1962. These early mechanical Rados are now collected as the foundation of the brand's history.
