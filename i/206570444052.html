About this watch

A green marble-effect mystery dial gives this vintage Zodiac Astrographic SST its distinctive character. Soft turquoise-green tones and darker mottling contrast with the warm gold-plated case, while the hour and minute indicators appear to float above the dial.

Transparent rotating discs carry the time indicators, with a red dot marking the seconds. The gold Zodiac crosshair emblem remains fixed at the centre, complemented by a black outer minute track, gold-tone markers and a circular date window near 12 o'clock. The approximately 36mm cushion-shaped case has a stainless-steel screw-down back marked Automatic, Shock-Resistant and Antimagnetic, and the matching gold-plated bracelet has a Zodiac-signed folding clasp.

Brand heritage

Zodiac was founded in 1882 by Ariste Calame in Le Locle, Switzerland. The brand is best known for its sports and tool watches, above all the Sea Wolf of 1953, one of the first purpose-built dive watches sold to the public.

The Astrographic was introduced in 1969, the year of the first Moon landing. Its "mystery" display, in which the hands are printed on transparent discs so they seem to float, captured the space-age spirit of the period and remains one of Zodiac's most celebrated designs.
