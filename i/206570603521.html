About this watch

A striking sapphire front and an automatic mechanical movement give this vintage Rado Coupole an appealing combination of sculptural design and traditional watchmaking. A broad, glossy black surround frames a smaller silver-tone dial, creating a floating-dial effect, with reflections shifting across the crystal as the wrist moves.

Gold-tone hands and delicate gold-tone dot markers bring warmth to the dial, while larger dark markers define the quarter hours and a discreet date aperture sits at six. The compact 31mm case, reference 129.3577.4N on the caseback, connects to a black leather strap through unusual polished central attachments, finished with a Rado-signed gold-tone buckle.

Brand heritage

Rado grew out of Schlup & Co., a movement workshop founded by the Schlup brothers in Lengnau, Switzerland, in 1917; watches were first sold under the Rado name in the 1950s. In 1962 the brand launched the DiaStar, the first scratch-resistant watch, earning Rado its reputation as the "Master of Materials" and a pioneer of hardmetal, sapphire crystal and high-tech ceramic.

The Coupole collection reflects Rado's longstanding interest in unconventional forms and the expressive use of sapphire crystal.
