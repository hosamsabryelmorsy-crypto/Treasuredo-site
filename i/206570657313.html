An elegant vintage Maurice Lacroix Les Classiques ladies’ wristwatch, reference 5212, dating approximately to the 1990s and accompanied by its branded presentation box.


Swiss-made and measuring approximately 26mm across the case, excluding the crown, this watch combines a polished stainless steel case with a stepped gold-plated bezel. Its petite proportions and coordinating two-tone bracelet give it a refined, jewellery-like presence on the wrist.


The white dial features Roman numerals at 12, 3 and 9, slender baton hour markers, an inner railway minute track and gold-tone hands. A small circular date display at 6 o’clock adds a distinctive finishing touch.


Powered by a Swiss quartz movement, the watch is fitted with a matching steel bracelet featuring narrow gold-tone accents and a Maurice Lacroix-signed clasp. The caseback carries the brand emblem and reference 5212.


Maurice Lacroix introduced its first watch in 1975. This example reflects the restrained dress-watch styling associated with its vintage pieces: compact dimensions, traditional dial details and warm two-tone finishing.


The watch shows visible signs of previous use, including surface scratches to the case, bracelet and clasp, together with wear to the gold-tone bezel. The accompanying navy Maurice Lacroix box also shows scuffing and age-related wear.
