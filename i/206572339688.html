Deep blue and polished gold give this vintage Rado Florence a distinctive jewellery-like presence. Reference 322.3762.2 combines a subtly striped blue dial with a sculptural gold-plated bracelet, accompanied by a Rado presentation box.


Swiss-made and dating approximately to the 1990s–2000s, this petite ladies’ watch measures approximately 24mm across the case, excluding the crown. Its dark blue outer surround frames a smaller central dial, creating an elegant sense of depth beneath the sapphire crystal.


The minimalist dial carries gold-tone RADO and FLORENCE signatures and two slender gold-tone hands. Its uncluttered, marker-free layout allows the colour and fine vertical striping to take centre stage.


The gold-plated bracelet is a particularly attractive feature, pairing broad, smoothly rounded links with narrower ribbed central connectors. A Rado-signed clasp completes the design. The watch is powered by a Swiss quartz movement, and the caseback is marked with reference 322.3762.2 and “SAPPHIRE CRYSTAL.”


Named after the Italian city, the Florence collection expresses Rado’s interpretation of classical elegance through rounded forms and clean proportions. This example brings that character together with a striking blue-and-gold colour combination.


The watch shows visible signs of previous wear, including surface marks to the front, bracelet, clasp and caseback. The accompanying brown Rado presentation box also shows age-related wear. Please examine the original photographs closely for the condition and included accessories.


An appealing choice for someone who appreciates petite Swiss watches with the presence of a decorative bracelet.
