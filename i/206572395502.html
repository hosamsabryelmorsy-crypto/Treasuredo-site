A distinctive early Traser S3000, combining Swiss quartz timekeeping with the bold, functional styling associated with the brand’s tritium-equipped watches.


The black dial features clear Arabic numerals, contrasting white hands with tritium tube inserts, a detailed outer minute track and a date display at 3 o’clock. An oversized elapsed-time bezel and angular case guards give the watch its recognisable instrument-like appearance.


Estimated to date from the 1990s–early 2000s, this example represents an earlier chapter of Traser’s history. The brand emerged in 1989 from Swiss illumination specialist mb-microtec, whose miniature tritium light sources became central to its watchmaking identity. The S3000 was also documented in period diving-equipment catalogues.


Its appeal lies in that combination of specialist Swiss heritage, practical design and unmistakable vintage sports-watch character.
