About this watch

Bold proportions meet expressive typography in this Jahan Genève wristwatch. The large black dial is filled with oversized, flowing white Arabic numerals of varying sizes, crowded together towards the top of the dial, with luminous filling that glows in the dark. Two silver-tone baton hands with luminous inserts, a date window above six o'clock and the JAHAN GENÈVE signature complete a very distinctive design.

The slim, polished round case measures approximately 44.5mm and has a thin bezel and slender lugs. The screwed caseback is engraved "Excellence de Jahan", "Fabriqué à Genève, Suisse", "Étanche à 30M" and the individual number "N°12". It is fitted with a black crocodile-pattern leather strap and a Jahan-signed steel pin buckle.

Brand heritage

Jahan is a family-owned high-jewellery house based in Geneva, Switzerland, whose history in fine jewellery spans more than a century and several generations of the family. The house is known for one-of-a-kind jewels and for a long relationship with royal and private clients in the Middle East and beyond. Its watches carry the house's jewellery-design sensibility into Swiss-made timepieces.
