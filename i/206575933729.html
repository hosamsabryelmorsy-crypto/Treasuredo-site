About this watch

A distinctive Swiss-made Rado Elegance distinguished by its beautifully aged tropical dial, sculptural stainless-steel case and integrated bracelet. Over time, the once-dark dial has developed into a rich burnt chocolate-brown tone with naturally irregular golden flecking and warm metallic highlights, and its appearance changes under different lighting.

The minimalist dial has four applied square markers at 12, 3, 6 and 9 o'clock, two slim silver-tone hands, the vintage Rado anchor emblem and signature, and the cursive "Elegance" inscription. The broad cushion case measures approximately 35mm with a domed crystal and snap back, and houses a Rado-signed Swiss manual-wind movement with 17 jewels. It comes on the integrated stainless-steel Rado bracelet with signed clasp.

Brand heritage

Rado traces its history to the Schlup & Co. watchmaking factory established in Lengnau, Switzerland, in 1917, and began selling watches under the Rado name in the 1950s. The brand became known as the "Master of Materials" for pioneering hardmetal, sapphire crystal and, later, high-tech ceramic. The Elegance was among the brand's more experimental designs of the 1970s, combining a broad case, circular dial opening and integrated bracelet.
