Brand heritage

Rado grew out of Schlup & Co., the movement maker founded in 1917 in Lengnau, Switzerland, by the brothers Fritz, Ernst and Werner Schlup. Watches were sold under the Rado name from the 1950s, and the 1962 DiaStar, marketed as the world's first scratchproof watch, made Rado the "master of materials". The Coupole collection, named after the domed form of its case and crystal, was introduced in 1987 and is recognised by its slim, distinctive lugs flowing into the bracelet.
