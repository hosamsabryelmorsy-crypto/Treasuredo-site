About this watch

A distinctive Swiss automatic that pairs a curved rectangular stainless-steel case with Raymond Weil's signature overlapping "figure-eight" dial. The textured black dial shows two times at once: an upper 24-hour second time zone with AM/PM indications, and a lower main dial with a date window at 3 o'clock.

Two signed crowns on the right flank set the two displays. The case carries a curved sapphire crystal, and the signed Raymond Weil calibre RW2200 automatic movement can be seen through the round exhibition window on the caseback, stamped with reference 4888.

Brand heritage

Raymond Weil was founded in Geneva in 1976 by Raymond Weil and has remained an independent, family-owned Swiss watchmaker, today led by the founder's grandson Elie Bernheim. The brand is known for its strong links to the arts, naming its collections after musical works and styles, such as Don Giovanni, Tango, Parsifal and Maestro. The Don Giovanni line, named after Mozart's opera, is the brand's home for rectangular, Art Deco-inspired dress watches, and the Cosi Grande models are its larger, bolder expression.
