About this watch

A striking red-and-white carbon-fibre case and Eastern Arabic numerals give this Overdrive D2 Special Edition its distinctive sporting character. The curved tonneau case features contrasting layered striations, elongated openings and a prominent red crown.

The cream-white, basket-weave-textured dial combines burgundy numerals with dark outlined hands, a red seconds hand and a black minute track, signed "Special Edition" and "Swiss Movement". The signed solid caseback is secured by four screws, and the integrated white rubber strap with moulded Overdrive branding closes with a signed black buckle with a contrasting red tang.

Brand heritage

Overdrive is a contemporary watch brand that brings together German design, Swiss movements and Arabic-inspired detailing. Its story draws on the Damascus sword: the case shapes echo the curve of the Arabic blade, and its dials carry Arabic numerals and calligraphy. Overdrive makes extensive use of carbon fibre, which keeps its watches remarkably light. The D2 belongs to the brand's early collection family and is documented in its 2019 catalogue.
