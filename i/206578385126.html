An elongated white case and rich brown dial give this vintage Sutton a striking architectural presence. Measuring 26 mm wide by 52 mm long, its slender rectangular silhouette offers a distinctive alternative to the conventional round wristwatch.


The dial pairs a vertically brushed brown finish with dark edges, minimalist silver-tone markers and two small gold-tone hands positioned below the midpoint. A raised rectangular crystal and rounded white case sides add depth to the design, while the brown stitched leather strap complements its warm palette.


Its sculptural styling suggests a circa-1970s date, an estimated attribution rather than a documented production year. The overall look captures the experimental, Space Age character sought by collectors of unconventional vintage watches.
