A rich blue dial gives this compact Seiko 5 its distinctive character. Shifting between deep navy and brighter cobalt with the light, its circular two-zone design adds depth beneath raised silver-tone markers, slender 12 and 6 numerals, and a finely detailed minute track. The framed black day-date display provides a striking contrast, with the weekday shown in Arabic.


The rounded stainless steel case is complemented by a bracelet combining broad polished links with narrow, finely ribbed detailing. A discreet crown near four o’clock and an exhibition caseback complete the design, offering a view of the automatic movement.


The Seiko 5 family traces its origins to the Sportsmatic 5 introduced in 1963. This example carries that familiar combination of mechanical watchmaking, practical day-date functionality and understated everyday style.
