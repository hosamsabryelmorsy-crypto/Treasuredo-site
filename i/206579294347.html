About this watch

A vintage Candino hand-wound watch with a printed portrait of Saddam Hussein on an aged ivory-to-champagne dial. The dial has raised gold-tone markers, Arabic numerals at 12 and 6, matching baton hands and central seconds, and is signed Candino, 17 Jewels, Incabloc.

The compact 32mm gold-plated cushion case has softly squared corners, a rounded crystal and a screw-down stainless steel back marked Swiss Made, Waterproof. It comes on a brown leather strap with an embossed leaf pattern, 18mm wide, with gold-tone tang buckle, and with the Candino presentation box shown.

Brand heritage

Candino traces its origins to 1947, when Adolf Flury-Hug established his watch workshop in Herbetswil, in the Swiss canton of Solothurn. The Candino name was adopted as the firm grew into an exporter of reliable, well-made Swiss watches, and the brand continues today as part of the Festina Group. Portrait and emblem dials were produced on Swiss watches in large numbers during the 1970s and 1980s for the Middle East, typically as presentation pieces, and Iraqi portrait dials from this period are now collected as historical and political memorabilia.
