About this watch

A vintage Rado DiaStar 90 quartz, reference 729.1707.3, from the 1980s. The broad, faceted, polished gold-tone tungsten-carbide hardmetal case front frames a circular, finely textured champagne dial with applied baton markers, slender gold-tone hands, the Rado anchor logo, the DiaStar Quartz signature and a date window at 3 o'clock.

The case measures approximately 36 x 38mm and continues into an integrated two-tone bracelet in stainless steel and gold plate with Rado-signed clasp. The caseback is marked DiaStar 90, 729.1707.3 and Rado Quartz Water-Sealed. It comes with the brown Rado presentation box shown.

Brand heritage

Rado grew out of Schlup & Co., a movement workshop founded in Lengnau, Switzerland, in 1917, and began selling watches under the Rado name in the 1950s. Its trademark is the small anchor on the dial, which on automatic models is mounted so that it can swing freely.

Rado became known as the "Master of Materials" after launching the DiaStar in 1962, promoted as the world's first scratch-resistant watch thanks to its hardmetal (tungsten carbide) case and sapphire crystal. Through the 1960s and 1970s its bold automatic watches were especially popular across the Middle East and Asia.
