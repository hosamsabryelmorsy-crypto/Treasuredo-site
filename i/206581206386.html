Slim applied baton markers, gold-tone dauphine-style hands, a framed date window at 2 o'clock and the script "Quartz" signature complete a clean, classic layout; "Swiss Made" is printed at the base of the dial.

The round gold-plated case measures 32mm with a finely reeded bezel and a steel caseback stamped "Stainless Steel Back". It is fitted with a brown leather strap, 19mm wide.

Brand heritage

Doxa was founded in 1889 in Le Locle, Switzerland, by Georges Ducommun. The firm won early recognition for its 8-day movements, which were also widely used in dashboard clocks for cars and aircraft. Doxa is best known today for the SUB 300 of 1967, its orange-dialled professional dive watch, which was distributed in the United States through U.S. Divers, the company associated with Jacques-Yves Cousteau.

Portrait-dial watches were produced under a number of Swiss brand names during the 1970s and 1980s for Iraq, typically as commemorative or presentation pieces. They are now collected as historical and political memorabilia as well as by vintage watch enthusiasts. No documentation of the presentation history of this particular watch is included.
