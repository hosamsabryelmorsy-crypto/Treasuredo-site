About this watch

A vintage Zédon Genève "Calendo" triple calendar. The day and month, in French, are shown through twin apertures beneath 12 o'clock, and the date is read from a numbered outer track by a central pointer hand with a red arrow tip. The dial has aged to a deep, smoky patina and carries gold Arabic numerals and rose-gold toned dauphine hands; it is signed "Zedon Genève – Calendo – 17 Rubis – Shock-Resist – Antimagnetic".

The round gold-plated case measures 33mm across and 10mm thick, with distinctive faceted teardrop lugs and a screw caseback. Inside is a Swiss manual-wind movement with 17 jewels, signed "Zedon-Geneve". It is fitted with a later tan leather strap with contrast stitching, 17mm wide.

Brand heritage

Zédon was one of the smaller Geneva watch brands of the mid twentieth century, selling Swiss-made mechanical watches under its own name. Like most independent houses of the period it built its watches around quality movements supplied by specialist Swiss makers, signed for the brand. The triple calendar, showing day, date and month, was among the most popular complications of the 1940s and 1950s; the classic layout seen here, with twin windows under 12 and a pointer date around the edge of the dial, was offered by many Swiss houses, and "Calendo" was the name Zédon gave to its version.
