About this watch

An RCNY Rico's "Premier" dress watch with the emblem of the UAE Armed Forces printed in full colour at the centre of a crisp white dial: a falcon bearing the UAE flag on its chest within a green laurel wreath with stars. The dial has black Roman numerals at III and IX, slim hands with circular counterweights, a day window beneath the RCNY signature and a date window at 6 o'clock.

The softly curved cushion case measures 34mm across and 6mm thick, with a four-screw back engraved "RICO'S – Premier Collection – Full Brass Case – 3ATM Water Resistant – RS-1101G – Swiss Quartz". It comes on a brown crocodile-embossed leather strap, 20mm wide.

Brand heritage

Rico's, signed RCNY on the dial, is a little-documented watch label whose Premier Collection offered classically styled dress watches with Swiss quartz movements. Emblem dials such as this are typically associated with presentation or commemorative gifts, and watches bearing the crests of Gulf armed forces and government bodies are collected today as memorabilia of the region. The identification of the emblem is based on the dial print; no issuing papers or certificate are included.
