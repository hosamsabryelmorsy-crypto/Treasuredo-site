Vintage 1970s Omax "Digital" Jump Hour – Mechanical Disc Display – Manual Wind 17 Jewels – Gold Plated Space Age Case

A vintage Omax "Digital" from the 1970s. Instead of hands, the time is shown by rotating discs in the jump-hour style, with hours and minutes in bright blue numerals visible through a sculpted window at the lower edge of the dial. The brushed gold-tone dial is framed by bold black lines and signed "Omax – Digital – 17 Jewels". The wide, flying-saucer style gold-plated case measures 31mm across and 8mm thick, with a polished outer rim around a brushed centre. Inside is a hand-wound mechanical movement with 17 jewels. It is fitted with a later brown suede-style leather strap.

Brand heritage

Omax is a Swiss watch brand established in 1946. It built its business on affordable, dependable mechanical watches for export, and became especially well known across the Middle East, Africa and Asia, where it remains a familiar name.

Model history

Mechanical "digital" watches with jump hour and disc displays were a hallmark of the early 1970s, when watchmakers answered the arrival of LED and LCD watches with space-age designs of their own. They show the time in windows using printed discs driven by a conventional mechanical movement. The fashion lasted only a few years, which makes surviving examples appealing to collectors of 1970s design.
