About this watch

A 1970s Camy Genève automatic with an unusual calendar dial. Alongside the date window at 3 o'clock, the lower half of the silver-white dial carries a printed month grid numbered 1 to 31, with a window beside it showing a column of weekday abbreviations, so the day of the week can be read against the date. Bold black baton markers and a red-tipped seconds hand sit beneath a faceted crystal.

The angular stainless steel tonneau case measures 37mm across and 12mm thick and flows into an integrated steel bracelet, 23mm wide. The screw-down caseback is engraved Antimagnetic, Shockprotected, Automatic, All Stainless Steel, Swiss Made, Waterresistant, with the Camy logo and the number 7909. The rotor is signed Camy, Seventeen, 17 Jewels, Swiss.

Brand heritage

Camy Watch Co. SA was founded in Geneva in 1917 and became known for reliable, well-priced Swiss mechanical watches sold widely across Europe, Asia and the Middle East; by the 1970s it also ran the Cronel, Enzo and Mirexal brands. Camy also holds a notable place in Swiss watch history: Raymond Weil joined the company in 1949 and rose to become its director, before founding his own brand in Geneva in 1976.
