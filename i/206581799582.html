About this watch

A neat example of 1970s Swiss dress-watch styling. The champagne dial has a fine linen-brushed texture, slim black baton markers, black hands, a date window at 3 o'clock and the fleur-de-lis emblem above the Pronto signature. The "T Swiss Made T" marking indicates tritium luminous material, as used at the time.

The 33mm gold-plated cushion case has a stainless steel screw-down back engraved "Incabloc, Automatic, Stainless Steel Back, Swiss Made, Water Resistant, 5 ATM" with case number. Inside is a 25-jewel Swiss automatic movement. It is fitted to a black leather strap stamped "Gucci, Made in Switzerland" on the reverse (not original to the watch).

Brand heritage

Pronto was a Swiss watch name of the mid-20th century, known for Swiss-made mechanical watches signed with its own name and fleur-de-lis emblem. Like many smaller Swiss brands of the period, it offered reliable jewelled movements in fashionable cases at accessible prices, before the quartz era of the late 1970s and 1980s changed the industry.
