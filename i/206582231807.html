Brand heritage

The Yves Saint Laurent couture house was founded in Paris in 1961 by designer Yves Saint Laurent and his partner Pierre Bergé. Saint Laurent revolutionised fashion with icons such as Le Smoking tuxedo for women (1966) and was the first couturier to launch a luxury ready-to-wear line, Rive Gauche, in 1966.

From the 1970s to the 1990s the house licensed its name to accessories, including Swiss-made watches sold under the Yves Saint Laurent Collection label, which combined couture styling with Swiss movements.
