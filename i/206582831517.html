About this watch

A bold multifunction chronograph from the Adventure line of Jahan Genève. The black dial is the centrepiece: four silver sub-registers with a concentric-ringed finish stand out sharply against the deep black background, led by a moon-and-stars phase display with surrounding date scale at 12 o'clock. Large cream Arabic numerals, luminous hands and dense outer scales give it a true instrument-watch character.

The 40mm stainless steel case is equally distinctive, with oversized sculpted stirrup-style lugs, a bezel engraved with the ADVENTURE signature and a screw-down caseback marked All Stainless Steel, Swiss Made, Water Resistant 100m, Shockproof and Registered Model. It is fitted on a brown croc-embossed leather strap with a steel buckle.

Brand heritage

Jahan is a Geneva-based jewellery and watch house whose Swiss-made pieces combine jewellery-house styling with Swiss movements. The Adventure line, from around the turn of the millennium, is its sporty collection, recognisable by the large stirrup lugs and engraved bezel.
