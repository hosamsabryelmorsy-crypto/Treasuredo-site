About this watch

A true icon of space-age design: the Spaceman, created by Swiss designer André Le Marquand, here in its signature oval form and signed Omax on the dial.

The black dial sits behind a trapezoid window framed by a wide, sweeping chrome oval bezel, a shape that instantly recalls an astronaut's helmet visor. Bright orange baton hands and indices stand out against the black, with a red date window at the upper right and a pale arc along the lower edge of the dial. The dial is signed SPACEMAN Automatic, OMAX and SWISS MADE T<25. The caseback carries the engraved Spaceman astronaut motif, marked Swiss Made and Incabloc. The watch is fitted on a black cut-out leather strap that frames the case, with steel buckle.

Brand heritage

André Le Marquand (born 1930), an Anglo-Swiss architect and designer from Neuchâtel, created the Spaceman at the end of the 1960s as a tribute to the conquest of space, at the request of Claude Lebet of the Swiss brand Catena. Launched in the early 1970s, it became one of the defining fashion watches of its era, with around 150,000 sold within a decade, and was later produced for distributors in many countries; this example is signed Omax.
