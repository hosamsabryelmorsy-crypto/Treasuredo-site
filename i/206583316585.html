This vintage Seiko Sports 150 ref. H801-6000 is a classic of Seiko's ana-digi era, combining analog hands with a digital LCD display on one dial. The rotating outer bezel carries a pilot's slide rule with tachymeter scale, framing a black dial with applied baton markers, luminous hands, a red-tipped seconds hand and the LCD window at the lower right. The gold-tone case with crown and two pushers gives it a bold, instrument-like presence on the wrist.


Case & Movement:

- Case: Gold-tone plated base metal case with stainless steel caseback, 40mm

- Movement: Seiko quartz ana-digi module H801

- Bezel: Rotating slide rule bezel with tachymeter scale

- Caseback: Engraved "Seiko – Water Resistant 15 Bar – Base Metal St. Steel Back – H801-6000 – Japan," with serial number

- Dial: Black, signed "Seiko Quartz – Sports 150," with LCD digital display

- Strap: Black leather strap (not original)

- Included: Seiko Quartz presentation box (not confirmed original to this watch)


Condition:

Genuine vintage piece in pre-owned condition consistent with its age. The slide rule bezel shows significant wear, with the black printing chipped and flaking in several areas, most noticeably on the left side and around 7 to 8 o'clock. The gold-tone plating shows wear on the case edges. The analog hands and LCD display are shown working in the photos; the individual digital functions have not been fully tested. No service history is available, so the watch is sold as-is; current timekeeping accuracy and water resistance are not guaranteed.
