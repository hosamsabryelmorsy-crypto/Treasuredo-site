Tissot T-Wave Ref. T112.210.36.111.00 – Ladies Swiss Quartz – Rose Gold PVD – Mother-of-Pearl Dial with 22 Diamonds – 30mm

A Tissot T-Wave, reference T112.210.36.111.00, designed around the idea of a flowing ribbon. The white mother-of-pearl dial is crossed by fine rose-gold waves, two of them set with a line of diamonds (22 diamonds, approx. 0.06 ct in total according to Tissot's specifications), with a teardrop index at 12 o'clock and openwork leaf-shaped hands. The 30mm stainless steel case has a rose gold PVD coating, sweeping ribbon-like lugs and a sapphire crystal. The caseback is engraved with Tissot 1853 branding, "Stainless Steel", "Water Resistant 3 bar (30m/100ft)", reference T112210 B and serial number, and the factory protective film and sticker are still in place. It comes on a black leather strap with rose gold PVD Tissot buckle and with a Tissot presentation box.

Brand heritage

Tissot was founded in 1853 in Le Locle, in the Swiss Jura, by Charles-Félicien Tissot and his son Charles-Émile, and is still based there today. The house introduced the first mass-produced pocket watch with two time zones in 1853 and the first anti-magnetic wristwatch in 1930, and has long been an official timekeeper for major international sports.

Model history

The T-Wave collection was introduced in 2016 as part of the T-Lady range. Its design takes the form of a ribbon in motion, with the lugs flowing out of the case in a continuous curve and the wave motif repeated across the dial. This reference is the rose gold PVD version with mother-of-pearl dial and diamond-set waves.
