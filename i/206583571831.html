Vintage Sandoz Diver-Style Automatic – 25 Jewels – Orange Gradient Dial – Red and Black "Coke" Bezel – 34mm – 1970s

A 1970s Sandoz diver-style automatic in a compact cushion-shaped stainless steel case. The dial fades from a bright orange centre to deep red at the edge and is set with framed rectangular lumed markers, bold lumed hands and a date window at 3 o'clock; it is signed "Sandoz – Automatic – 25 Jewels". Around it sits a red and black "Coke" style timing bezel with a knurled edge. The case measures 34mm, with a signed crown and a caseback engraved "Water Resistant – All Stainless Steel – Incabloc – Swiss – 10 ATM – 1230 Z-25-2" with the maker's logo. It comes on a stainless steel link bracelet with Sandoz-signed fold-over clasp.

Brand heritage

Sandoz is a Swiss watch name with its roots in the Sandoz watchmaking family of the Jura in the nineteenth century. In the 1960s and 1970s the brand became widely known for colourful automatic sports and dive-style watches with Swiss 25-jewel movements, which were exported in large numbers to Asia and the Middle East.

Model history

Compact dive-style automatics such as this one were a staple of the Sandoz range in the 1970s. They combined a water-resistant steel case and timing bezel with strongly coloured dials, and the two-tone red and black bezel, nicknamed "Coke" by collectors, was one of the most popular combinations of the decade. The smaller 34mm case makes this version suitable for slimmer wrists.
