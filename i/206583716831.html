It is fitted with a replacement black croc-embossed leather strap with gold-tone buckle.

Brand heritage

Camy was a Swiss watch house founded by the Stroun brothers and based in Geneva from 1943. It became known for reliable, well-priced mechanical watches sold widely across Europe, Asia and the Middle East. Raymond Weil joined the company in 1949 and later became its director, before establishing his own brand in 1976.

Model history

Alongside its sports and automatic models, Camy produced slim hand-wound dress watches through the 1960s and 1970s. Dials without hour markers, relying on a few lines or a plain surface, were a fashionable modernist style of the period, and the fluted bezel gives this example the texture of a more formal dress watch.
