Vintage Kelek Dress Watch – Swiss Manual Wind 17 Jewels – Silvered Sunburst Dial – Gold Plated – 34mm – 1960s

A vintage Kelek dress watch from the 1960s. The bright silvered sunburst dial carries applied gold-tone hour markers, doubled at 2, 4, 8 and 10, gold-tone leaf hands and a slim centre seconds hand, with "Swiss Made" at the base. The round gold-plated case measures approximately 34mm, and the caseback is marked "Kelek", "17 Jewels", "Waterproof", "Antimagnetic", "Incabloc" and "Unbreakable Mainspring". Inside is a Swiss manual-wind movement with 17 jewels and Incabloc shock protection. It is fitted with a later black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Kelek was founded in 1896 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland. The firm made reliable everyday watches through the mid twentieth century and later became known for complicated automatic chronographs and modular complications, some of which it supplied to other houses. In 1997 the company was taken over by Breitling and became Breitling Chronométrie.

Model history

Before its chronograph era, Kelek produced classic three-hand dress watches such as this one for the export market. The 34mm case, sunburst dial with applied markers and hand-wound 17-jewel movement with Incabloc represent the standard Swiss gentleman's watch of the 1960s.
