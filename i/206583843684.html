Brand heritage

Seiko traces its origins to 1881, when Kintaro Hattori opened his watch and clock shop in Tokyo. The company produced Japan's first wristwatch in 1913 and its first automatic in 1956, and by the 1960s was challenging the Swiss industry in both precision and volume.

Model history

The Sportsmatic 5 was launched in 1963 and laid the foundation for the Seiko 5 family, the five standing for automatic winding, day-date in a single window, water resistance, a recessed crown at 4 and a durable case. The calibre 6619A, introduced in 1964, was the first Seiko calibre with a quick-set date operated by pressing in the crown.
