About this watch

A charming Disney character watch by Bradley. The white dial has bold black Arabic numerals around a sky-blue centre disc with Snow White and her two bluebirds, and her arms serve as the hands. The dial is signed "Bradley", "Swiss Made" and "© Walt Disney Productions".

The case measures approximately 26mm, with a silver-tone base-metal bezel and a back marked "Stainless Steel Back, Swiss Made, Base Metal Bezel, 023 S". The movement is Swiss, mechanical and hand-wound. It is fitted with a later brown leather strap with contrast stitching.

Brand heritage

Bradley Time was a division of Elgin National Industries, which acquired the Disney character watch licence on 1 January 1972. Before that, Ingersoll / U.S. Time / Timex had made Disney watches from 1933 to 1968. Bradley produced most Disney character watches from 1972 until 1985, many of them with Swiss mechanical movements, which dates this watch to that period.
