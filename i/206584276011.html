Vintage Breil Okay – Swiss Automatic 21 Jewels – White Roman Dial with Date – Steel – 33mm – 1970s

A vintage Swiss-made automatic from the Italian brand Breil, from its "Okay" line. The white dial has bold black Roman numerals, an inner railway-style minute track with luminous dots, luminous hands and a framed date window at 3 o'clock, and is signed "OK Breil", "Okay", "21 Rubis", "Automatic", "Incabloc" and "Swiss Made". The steel-tone case measures approximately 33mm, with a generously sized crown and a screw-down back marked "10 ATM" and "637". Inside is a Swiss automatic movement with 21 jewels and Incabloc, with a gilt rotor signed "21 Jewels Swiss Made" and the movement marked "2783". It is fitted with a later black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Breil was created in 1939 in Milan by the Binda family, whose watch business dates back to 1906. The brand became one of the best-known names in Italian watchmaking, pairing Italian design with Swiss movements, and remains part of the Binda Group today.

Model history

The Okay was one of Breil's main lines of the 1970s, a family of robust Swiss-made automatics for everyday wear. The movement marked 2783 is the ETA 2783, a dependable self-winding calibre with date from the early 1970s that was used by many Swiss and Italian brands of the period.
