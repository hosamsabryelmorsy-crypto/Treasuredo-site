Brand heritage

Rado grew out of the Schlup & Co. movement workshop founded in Lengnau, Switzerland, in 1917, and began selling watches under the Rado name in the 1950s. The brand became known as the "Master of Materials" after launching the scratch-resistant DiaStar in 1962, and it has long been one of the most popular Swiss brands in Asia and the Middle East.

Model history

Shangri-La was one of the many evocatively named lines in Rado's catalogue of the 1960s and 1970s, alongside Golden Horse, Green Horse and Purple Gazelle. The ladies versions were small, jewellery-like watches with decorative dials and stone-set markers. The moving anchor emblem on the dial was Rado's mark for its automatic models.
