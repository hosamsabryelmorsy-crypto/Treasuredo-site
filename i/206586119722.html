About this watch

A bold 1970s Omax automatic. The silver dial is signed "Omax", "Automatic" and "17 Jewels", and is set with sparkling faceted crystal hour markers, which give these models their "Crystal" nickname. A day-date window sits at 6 o'clock, framed by a black inner bezel ring.

The 37mm all-stainless-steel cushion case has a screw-down caseback marked "Omax Automatic Shockproof Waterproof All Stainless Steel" with reference 3559 K, and is paired with a steel bracelet.

Brand heritage

Omax was founded in Geneva, Switzerland, in 1946 and in its early decades specialised in Swiss automatic and mechanical watches for the Middle East and the Indian subcontinent; by the 1960s it was one of the biggest Swiss watch exporters to India. From the 1980s the brand moved its focus to quartz watches and later relocated its headquarters to Dubai.
