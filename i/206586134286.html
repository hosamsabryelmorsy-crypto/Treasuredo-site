About this watch

A 1970s Rado Companion with a rich brown sunburst dial that shifts in tone with the light. It has applied steel baton markers, the red Rado anchor emblem, an orange centre seconds hand and a framed day-date window at 3 o'clock.

The 36mm stainless-steel cushion case has a screw-down caseback with the Rado seahorse "Water Sealed" medallion and reference 603.3202.4. Inside is a Rado-signed 17-jewel Swiss automatic movement. It is worn on a stainless-steel bracelet.

Brand heritage

Rado traces its origins to the Schlup & Co. movement workshop founded in Lengnau, Switzerland, in 1917, and began selling watches under the Rado name in the 1950s. The brand became known for bold design and innovative materials, culminating in the scratch-resistant DiaStar of 1962. The red anchor on the dial is traditionally used on Rado automatic models.

The Companion was one of Rado's popular automatic day-date models of the 1970s.
