About this watch

A striking and seldom-seen Rado. The textured silver dial carries the red Rado anchor, applied baton markers, a day-date window at 3 o'clock and the GOLDEN CITY signature, all framed by a dramatic black octagonal surround with four exposed corner screws. The look is bold, architectural and unmistakably 1970s.

The 37mm stainless steel case has a screw-down caseback with the gold-tone Rado seahorse medallion and Water Sealed inscription, and is engraved with the reference 603.3190.2. It sits on a steel bracelet with a RADO-signed clasp marked Steel Inox and Swiss Made.

Brand heritage

Rado grew out of Schlup & Co., a movement workshop founded in 1917 in Lengnau, Switzerland, by the brothers Fritz, Ernst and Werner Schlup. Watches under the Rado name appeared in the 1950s, and in 1962 the brand launched the DiaStar, the first scratch-resistant watch, made from hardmetal. Rado has since become known as the "Master of Materials". In the 1970s its bold, sculptural automatic models with Rado-signed movements and the swimming seahorse caseback medallion sold in large numbers, especially in the Middle East and Asia.
