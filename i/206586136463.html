About this watch

A classic 1970s Rado with a clean, confident look. The silver dial carries the red Rado anchor, the VOYAGER TWO signature, applied baton markers with a grey chapter ring and a day-date window at 6 o'clock with a Spanish-language day wheel.

The 36mm stainless steel tonneau case has a polished finish and a screw-down caseback with the Rado seahorse medallion, engraved with the reference 625.3124.4. It sits on an integrated steel bracelet with a RADO-signed clasp. Inside is the Rado-signed Swiss automatic movement, marked RADO 17 JEWELS SWISS on the rotor.

Brand heritage

Rado grew out of Schlup & Co., a movement workshop founded in 1917 in Lengnau, Switzerland, by the brothers Fritz, Ernst and Werner Schlup. Watches under the Rado name appeared in the 1950s, and in 1962 the brand launched the DiaStar, the first scratch-resistant watch, made from hardmetal. Rado has since become known as the "Master of Materials". In the 1970s its bold, sculptural automatic models with Rado-signed movements and the swimming seahorse caseback medallion sold in large numbers, especially in the Middle East and Asia.
