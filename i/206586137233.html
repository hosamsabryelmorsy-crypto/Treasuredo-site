About this watch

Swiss minimalism with a maritime twist. This Movado Museum has the iconic single gold dot at 12 o'clock on a champagne-grey dial, customised with the colourful ship's-wheel and dhow emblem of the Dubai International Marine Club (DIMC) above the "Swiss Movado Quartz" signature. Watches like this were commissioned as gifts for club members and events.

The slim 34mm case pairs a gold-tone bezel with a steel back, on a two-tone multi-link bracelet with a hidden clasp.

Brand heritage

Movado was founded in 1881 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, by Achille Ditesheim; the name means "always in motion" in Esperanto. The Museum dial was designed in 1947 by the American industrial designer Nathan George Horwitt, with a single dot evoking the sun at noon. In 1960 it became the first watch dial selected for the permanent design collection of the Museum of Modern Art in New York.

The Dubai International Marine Club was officially established in 1988 and organises offshore powerboat racing, sailing and traditional dhow regattas.
