Its understated proportions and warm monochromatic design capture the quiet sophistication of the classic De Ville. It is fitted on a black Omega-signed croc-embossed leather strap with an Omega-signed gold-tone buckle.

Brand heritage

Omega was founded in 1848 by Louis Brandt in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, and took its name from its celebrated Omega calibre of 1894. Official timekeeper of the Olympic Games since 1932 and maker of the Speedmaster worn on the Moon in 1969, it is one of the best-known names in Swiss watchmaking.

The De Ville line began in the 1960s as an elegant offshoot of the Seamaster and became an independent collection in 1967. Through the 1970s and 1980s it was Omega's home for slim, refined dress watches.
