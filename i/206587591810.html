About this watch

A finely fluted bezel frames a two-tone dial: a silver sunburst base crossed by a striped grey-blue band, with applied baton markers and a date window at 3 o'clock, signed Rigi, 17 Rubis, Incabloc and Swiss Made.

The 36mm stainless-steel case has an acrylic crystal and screw-down back, fitted with a later black leather strap, 19mm.

Brand heritage

Rigi was one of the smaller Swiss watch brands of the mid-20th century. Like many small independent labels of the post-war decades, it built its watches around movements supplied by specialist Swiss makers and sold them mainly through export distributors. Little documented history of the brand survives today, and most such names disappeared during the quartz crisis of the 1970s and 1980s, which makes surviving examples an honest, affordable corner of vintage collecting. The Incabloc shock-protection system signed on the dial was developed in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, and introduced in 1934; it protects the delicate balance pivots if the watch is knocked or dropped.
