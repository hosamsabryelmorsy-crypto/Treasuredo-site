About this watch

A slim Swiss hand-wound watch with a brushed stainless-steel octagonal case, a gold-tone bezel and crown, and a grey dial with applied gold-tone batons, matching hands and a framed date window at 3 o'clock, signed Felca and 17 Jewels.

The case measures about 35mm and just 7mm thick, with an acrylic crystal and screw-down back, on a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Felca was founded in 1919 in Grenchen, Switzerland, by watchmaker Fritz Schluep. In the 1950s the company began selling watches in Asia under the name Titoni, which later became the name of the whole company. It is still owned and run by the Schluep family today.
