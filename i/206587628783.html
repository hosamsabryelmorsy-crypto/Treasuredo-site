About this watch

A slim, classic dress watch. The white dial has black Roman numerals, slender black hands and the Bezzi Selection and Swiss Made signatures, with a blue cabochon crown.

The 32mm gold-plated case is about 8mm thin with a snap caseback, on a black leather strap. Inside is a Swiss 17-jewel manual-wind movement; its size suits both men and women.

Brand heritage

Bezzi was one of the many small Swiss watch labels of the mid-20th century. Like many small independent labels of the post-war decades, it built its watches around movements supplied by specialist Swiss makers and sold them mainly through export distributors. Little documented history of the brand survives today, and most such names disappeared during the quartz crisis of the 1970s and 1980s, which makes surviving examples an honest, affordable corner of vintage collecting.
