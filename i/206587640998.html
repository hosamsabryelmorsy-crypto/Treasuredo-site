About this watch

A 1970s Dalil Monte Carlo Swiss automatic made for the Middle Eastern market. The champagne sunburst dial is signed "Dalil" in Arabic, Automatic and Swiss Made, with gold-tone baton markers and a date window at 3 o'clock. Rotating inner rings, set by the side crowns, show world cities and time zones in Arabic, and a green ring carries the Shahada.

The gold-plated case measures approximately 41mm excluding the crowns and 15mm thick. The screw-down caseback is engraved with the complete Ayat al-Kursi and marked Monte-Carlo, Waterproof, Antichoc and World Pat. Pending. It comes on a tan leather strap, 21mm wide.

Brand heritage

Dalil, Arabic for "guide", was a Swiss-made watch brand of the 1970s created for Muslim customers. Its Monte Carlo models combined Swiss automatic movements with rotating rings and Arabic inscriptions to serve as practical companions for the faithful. Little is documented about the company itself, and its watches are now sought after by collectors of Islamic and Middle Eastern market pieces.
