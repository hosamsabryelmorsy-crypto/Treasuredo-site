About this watch

A slim, minimalist dress watch with the national emblem of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (palm tree over crossed swords) in gold on a deep black dial, signed Almas in gold script and with slim gold-tone hands. The ultra-thin gold-plated case is framed by distinctive ribbed gold-tone end pieces that lead into a white leather strap.

The caseback is marked "18K Electroplated", "Water Resistant" and 1001 S.

Brand heritage

Almas (Arabic for "diamond") was a watch name sold in the Gulf region in the 1980s and 1990s. Watches bearing the Saudi national emblem were popular as gifts and keepsakes in the Kingdom, and today they are collected by enthusiasts of Gulf and emblem-dial watches.
