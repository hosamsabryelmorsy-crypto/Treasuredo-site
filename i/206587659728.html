About this watch

The white dial is signed "Tramway Watch", "17 Jewels" and "Nivaflex", with "Shock Absorber, Waterproof, Antimagnetic" below the centre, copper-tone Arabic numerals at 12, 3, 6 and 9, dart indices and matching hands. It has an honest, speckled patina from its long history.

The stainless-steel case measures approximately 34mm excluding crown and 11mm thick, with a screw-down caseback signed Tramway Watch. Inside is a Swiss 17-jewel hand-wound movement, also signed Tramway Watch. It is fitted with a tan leather strap with cream stitching.

Brand heritage

Tramway Watch is a little-documented Swiss label. Like many small brands of the period it fitted proven Swiss movements, in this case with a Nivaflex mainspring, and signed the dial, case and movement with its own name. Its watches are found today mainly in the Middle East, where they were sold new.
