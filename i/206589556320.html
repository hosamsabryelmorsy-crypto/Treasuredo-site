About this watch

A true Space Age conversation piece. Instead of hands, rotating discs show the hour and the minutes through an arrow-shaped window, set in a dark blue-grey dial with white racing stripes, signed "Orion" and "Digital Swiss".

The big 42mm TV-shaped case has a gold-tone front and a steel snap back, giving bold retro wrist presence. It runs on a Swiss manual-wind mechanical movement with 17 jewels and is fitted on a navy blue leather strap.

Brand heritage

In the late 1960s and 1970s many Swiss makers experimented with mechanical "digital" watches, using rotating hour and minute discs instead of hands to give a futuristic look before electronic displays took over. These jump-hour or direct-read watches, produced under smaller Swiss names such as this Orion, are now collected as icons of 1970s design.
