About this watch

A mid-century dress watch with a silver-gold textured sunburst dial, applied gold numerals at 12, 3, 6 and 9, baton markers and dauphine-style hands, signed Hislon, 17 Rubis, Waterproof and Antimagnetic.

The slim 33mm gold-plated case has a steel screw-down caseback and acrylic crystal. It is fitted with a replacement brown leather strap, 18mm.

Brand heritage

Hislon is a little-documented mid-century watch label whose watches are found today mainly in Turkey and the Middle East. Like many such brands it used Swiss mechanical movements, and its textured-dial dress watches are typical of the period's style.
