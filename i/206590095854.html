About this watch

A 1970s Sandoz Swiss automatic in a stainless-steel "TV screen" case. The brown sunburst dial fades towards the edge and has faceted applied markers with red and white accents, a framed day-date window with an Arabic and English day wheel, and is signed "Sandoz Automatic 25 Jewels".

The case measures approx. 38mm, with a brushed top, polished bevels and an acrylic crystal. The caseback is stamped "Waterresistant, Incabloc, Automatic, All Stainless Steel, Swiss Made" with reference 1839Z-84-8. Inside is a Swiss 25-jewel automatic movement with quick-set date. It comes on a tan ostrich-pattern leather strap, 19mm wide.

Brand heritage

The Sandoz name goes back to Henri Frédéric Sandoz of Le Locle, who founded Henri Sandoz & Fils around 1870 and in the 1890s helped establish the Tavannes Watch Co., which grew into a model factory with a thousand workers. In the 1960s and 1970s Sandoz automatics with Swiss jewelled movements were exported in large numbers, especially to the Middle East and Asia, as the Arabic day wheel on this example shows.
