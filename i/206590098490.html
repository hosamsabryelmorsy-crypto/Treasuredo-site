About this watch

A vintage West End Watch Co. Sowar Prima automatic. The black dial carries luminous Arabic numerals, a railway minute track, the "West End Watch Co Sowar" script, the Prima cross logo and "Automatic", with a date window between 6 and 7 o'clock.

The 35mm stainless-steel case (excluding crown) has a decagonal screw-down caseback. Inside is a Swiss 17-jewel automatic with signed rotor and quick-set date. It comes on an 18mm white leather strap with contrast stitching, with a West End presentation box.

Brand heritage

The West End Watch Co. was founded in 1886 in Bombay as a Swiss firm, selling watches made in the Swiss Jura to India and the East. Its robust watches were worn by soldiers, railwaymen and civil servants across India, the Gulf and beyond. The Sowar line (the word means a cavalryman) dates back to the First World War, and the Sowar Prima became the brand's best-known water-resistant everyday watch.
