About this watch

A compact West End Watch Co. Sowar Prima in a 29mm "boy size" steel case. The deep black military-style dial has luminous triangle markers with 12, 3, 6 and 9, luminous dots, a red centre seconds hand, the "West End Watch Co Sowar" script and the Prima cross logo.

The case has a decagonal screw-down caseback and is sharp and unpolished. Inside is a Swiss 17-jewel manual-wind movement. It comes on a period stainless steel expansion bracelet, 16mm wide, with a West End box.

Brand heritage

The West End Watch Co. was founded in 1886 in Bombay as a Swiss firm, selling watches made in the Swiss Jura to India and the East. Its robust watches were worn by soldiers, railwaymen and civil servants across India, the Gulf and beyond. The Sowar line (the word means a cavalryman) dates back to the First World War, and the Sowar Prima became the brand's best-known water-resistant everyday watch.
