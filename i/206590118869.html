About this watch

A 1980s Swiss Grovana in an all-black finish. The black dial has bold white Arabic numerals at 12, 6 and 9, white baton markers, a vivid orange minute track and a framed date window at 3 o'clock, and is signed "5 ATM Tested", "Shockresistant" and "Swiss Made". It is framed by a black rotating diver-style bezel.

The black PVD-coated case measures approx. 39mm excluding the crown. Inside is a Swiss automatic movement with date. It comes on an integrated black rubber strap, 19mm wide, and with a presentation box.

Brand heritage

Grovana was founded in 1924 in Tenniken, in the Swiss canton of Basel-Landschaft, by the Gröflin brothers. In 1970 the business was acquired by entrepreneur Werner Bitterli, and it is still family owned, run today by his son Christopher Bitterli. Grovana is one of the few watch companies that still develops and produces its own watches in Baselland.
