About this watch

A commemorative quartz watch marking the liberation of Kuwait. The black dial carries a waving Kuwaiti flag in green, white, red and black, with the inscriptions "Le Koweït Libre" and "Free Kuwait" around the edge and an Arabic inscription at the lower left.

The slim black-finish case measures approximately 35mm across and 6mm thick, with a snap caseback marked "Made in France" and "3 ATM". It comes on a black leather strap with white edging, 17mm wide.

Brand heritage

Kuwait was invaded and occupied by Iraq in August 1990 and was liberated in February 1991 by an international coalition in the Gulf War. "Free Kuwait" became the slogan of the campaign for the country's liberation, carried on badges, posters, clothing and souvenirs around the world. Commemorative watches like this one were made at the time and are collected today as Gulf War and Kuwaiti memorabilia. This example is unbranded and bears the slogan in French and English, consistent with its French manufacture.
