About this watch

An elegant 1990s ladies dress watch with a slim rectangular steel case, a champagne centre framed by a black surround with gold-tone indices, a date at 6 o'clock and the Olma signature with Swiss Made.

It sits on an integrated two-tone steel and gold-plated bracelet and comes with an Olma presentation box.

Brand heritage

Olma traces back to 1906, when the Numa Jeannin company of Fleurier, Switzerland, began producing quality watches under the OLMA name. The brand became known for practical, reliable Swiss watches at accessible prices, and later for slim quartz dress models like this one.
