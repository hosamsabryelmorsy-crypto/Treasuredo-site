About this watch

The 35mm stainless-steel case has a screw-down caseback stamped "Seiko 6119-6000 Water Proof". Inside is the robust Seiko calibre 6119 automatic. It is fitted on a grey suede strap and comes with a Seiko box from the dealer Al-Hussaini & Co.

Brand heritage

Seiko was founded in Tokyo in 1881 by Kintaro Hattori and grew into one of the few fully integrated watch manufacturers in the world, making its own movements, hairsprings and even crystals. It produced Japan's first wristwatch in 1913 and the world's first quartz wristwatch, the Astron, in 1969.

The Seiko 5 line was launched in 1963 around five core features: automatic winding, day-date display, water resistance, a recessed crown at 4 o'clock and a durable case and bracelet. The 6119 is one of Seiko's most reliable 21-jewel automatic calibres.
