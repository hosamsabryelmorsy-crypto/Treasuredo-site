About this watch

A space-age 1970s Felca. The silver-white textured dial carries the red Felca dot logo, "Cosmo 2001", "Automatic 25 Jewels" and "Swiss Made", with black-filled applied markers, gold-tone hands and a day-date window at 3 o'clock, framed by a black inner ring.

The gold-plated cushion case measures approximately 38mm and 11mm thick, with a stainless-steel screw-down back. Inside is a 25-jewel Felca automatic movement, signed "Felca 25 Jewels Swiss Made" on the rotor and shown in the listing photos. It is fitted with a brown leather strap.

Brand heritage

Felca was founded in 1919 by watchmaker Fritz Schluep in Grenchen, Switzerland. The company sold early on in Germany, America and Japan, and in the 1950s began using the Titoni name for its export watches, especially in Asia; the firm later became Titoni Ltd, which is still run by the founding family today.
