About this watch

A striking two-tone sector dial: a silver centre with a black outer ring, bold Arabic numerals, luminous-style hands and a small-seconds register marked "Ancre 15 Rubis, Antimagnetic". The dial is signed Harema Luxury with a star emblem.

The 35mm round stainless-steel case has a screw-down back and is worn on a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Harema is a little-documented mid-century label. The French wording on the dial, "Ancre 15 Rubis" (lever escapement, 15 jewels), is typical of watches made for French-speaking markets, where Besançon was the historic centre of watchmaking. Surviving examples of small names like this are uncommon today.
