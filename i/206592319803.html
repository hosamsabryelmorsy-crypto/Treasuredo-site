About this watch

A bold, oversized chronograph from Ferre Milano. The black dial carries large Arabic numerals, three sub-dials, a date window at 4 to 5 o'clock and luminous hands, signed "FERRE Milano". The polished steel bezel is decorated with "F" monograms at the hour positions.

The 46mm stainless-steel case is about 14mm thick, with chronograph pushers and a four-screw back marked "All Stainless Steel". It sits on a heavy matching stainless-steel bracelet, 24mm wide.

Brand heritage

Ferre Milano carries the name of Gianfranco Ferré, the Milanese designer often called the "architect of fashion". Trained as an architect, Ferré founded his own fashion house in Milan in 1978 and from 1989 to 1996 was artistic director of Christian Dior. Watches under the Ferre Milano name translate his structured, sculptural style into bold Italian-designed timepieces.
