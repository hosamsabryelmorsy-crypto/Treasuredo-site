About this watch

A dependable Seiko 5 Sports automatic with a sculpted 40mm stainless-steel case, black dial with applied markers, luminous hands and a day-date window at 3 o'clock. The dial reads Water 100m Resist and Automatic.

The exhibition caseback shows the Seiko calibre 7S26A automatic movement, signed Twenty-One Jewels, Seiko Time Corp, Japan. It is worn on a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Seiko launched the Seiko 5 range in 1963, built around five attributes including automatic winding, day-date display, water resistance and a durable case. The 21-jewel calibre 7S26 is one of Seiko's best-known workhorse automatic movements, celebrated for its longevity and ease of servicing.
