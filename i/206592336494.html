About this watch

A collectible Saudi military emblem watch. The white dial carries the full-colour emblem of the Royal Saudi Land Forces with its Arabic inscription, framed by applied gold-tone dot markers, gold-tone hands and the Cherry Bravo signature. The dial is marked Quartz and Japan Movt.

The 34mm two-tone case combines brushed steel with a polished gold-plated bezel and sits on a matching two-tone link bracelet with fold-over clasp. At only about 5mm thick it wears slim and light.

Brand heritage

Cherry Bravo is a little-documented watch label; the real interest of this piece lies in its dial. It carries the emblem of the Royal Saudi Land Forces, the army branch of the Saudi armed forces, with the force's name written in Arabic beneath the crest. Emblem dials like this are collected today as pieces of Gulf military memorabilia.
