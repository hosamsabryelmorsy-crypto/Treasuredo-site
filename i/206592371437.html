About this watch

A collectible Saudi football commemorative watch. The gold sunburst dial carries the green emblem of the Saudi Arabian Football Federation with its Arabic and English name, alongside the palm-and-swords national symbol, with twelve stone-set hour markers and the Sirgint logo.

The slim 33mm gold-plated case has sculpted bar lugs and a steel snap back marked "Sirgint Swiss Quartz 50290M, Water Resistant 3 ATM". It is fitted on a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Sirgint is a small, little-documented Swiss-made label; its watches are mostly found with Gulf presentation dials like this one, commissioned with national emblems or institutional logos.

Logo watches like this one date from the 1990s, the era when the Saudi national football team won Asian titles and reached its first FIFA World Cup in 1994.
