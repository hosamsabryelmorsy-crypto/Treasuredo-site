About this watch

An elegant two-tone Nivada automatic. The white dial carries the Nivada shield logo, black Roman numerals, slim blued-style hands, a date window at 3 o'clock and the "Flatline Automatic" signature.

The 39mm steel case has a gold-tone bezel engraved with numerals, gold-tone crown and an exhibition caseback that shows the 21-jewel Swiss automatic movement, signed Nivada. It is fitted on a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Nivada was founded in Grenchen, Switzerland, in 1926 and became one of the best-known mid-range Swiss makers. Its Antarctic was worn on the US Navy's Operation Deep Freeze expedition in the 1950s, and the brand later produced the celebrated Chronomaster Aviator Sea Diver and Depthmaster.
