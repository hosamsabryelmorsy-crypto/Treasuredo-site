About this watch

A bold 1970s Swiss automatic from Sandoz. The striking honey-yellow to brown gradient sunburst dial carries applied markers, red accents at the quarters, an orange seconds hand and a day-date window at 3 o'clock, signed "Sandoz Automatic 25 Jewels Swiss Made".

The wide brushed stainless steel cushion case measures approx. 38mm and has a screw-down caseback. Inside is a 25-jewel Swiss automatic movement with day and date. It is fitted to a black leather strap, 18mm.

Brand heritage

The Sandoz name has deep roots in Swiss watchmaking: the Sandoz family is recorded in Le Locle from the 16th century, and Henry Sandoz established himself as a watchmaker in 1895, specialising in thin watches, repeaters and chronographs. The firm began making wristwatches in 1914, moved to Peseux in 1919 and became Henry Sandoz & Fils in 1920. In the 1960s and 1970s Sandoz was widely known for good-value Swiss automatic watches, many of them with 25-jewel movements.
