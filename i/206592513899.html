About this watch

A charming post-war French dress watch. The silver dial has stylised Art Deco Arabic numerals alternating with dagger batons, blued-style hands and a small-seconds sub-dial at 6 o'clock, signed ELIX and Incabloc.

The 35mm round stainless-steel case has a screw-down back and slim lugs, on a tan leather strap. The movement is signed ELIX with calibre number 233, as shown in the photos.

Brand heritage

Elix was founded in 1852 and produced complete watches in Besançon, the historic capital of French watchmaking near the Swiss border, making it one of the oldest names in the Franche-Comté. In 1927 the firm was acquired by Ernest and Paul Mougin-Piquard, who continued making Elix watches alongside the Mougin-Piquard name. The Incabloc shock-protection system signed on the dial was developed in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, and introduced in 1934; it protects the delicate balance pivots if the watch is knocked or dropped.
