About this watch

A bold piece of 1970s-style Swiss design. The green gradient dial has applied baton markers, the Jovial winged emblem and a date window at 3 o'clock, inside a sculpted stainless-steel case that flows into an integrated wave-link bracelet.

Inside is a Swiss manual-wind movement. It comes with a Jovial Swiss Made box, as shown in the photos.

Brand heritage

Jovial was founded in 1929 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, during the Great Depression, and later moved its headquarters to Bienne. From the start the brand specialised in the Middle Eastern market, where it remains a familiar name, and it still produces Swiss Made watches today.
