About this watch

An elegant ladies dress watch with a deep blue sunburst dial, applied silver batons and the Felca, 17 Jewels and Swiss Made signatures, in a polished cushion-square case.

It sits on a textured steel mesh bracelet and is powered by a Swiss 17-jewel manual-wind movement.

Brand heritage

Felca was founded in 1919 in Grenchen, Switzerland, by watchmaker Fritz Schluep. In the 1950s the company began selling watches in Asia under the name Titoni, which later became the name of the whole company. It is still owned and run by the Schluep family today.
