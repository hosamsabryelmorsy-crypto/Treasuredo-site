About this watch

A fun 1970s pop-art design: a bold dark-brown square case framing a round cream dial with black Arabic numerals and slim hands, signed Lucerne and Swiss.

The translucent display back shows the Swiss 17-jewel manual-wind movement, and the watch is worn on a matching brown perforated rally-style strap.

Brand heritage

Lucerne, named after the Swiss lakeside city, was a Swiss watch label known for affordable mechanical watches during the 1960s and 1970s. Its playful designs of the period, with new case materials, shapes and colours, are now appreciated by collectors of authentic, accessible vintage Swiss watches.
