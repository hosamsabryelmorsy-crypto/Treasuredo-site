About this watch

A bold cushion-square gold-plated case frames a silver dial with faceted gold baton markers, red-framed numerals at 12, 6 and 9, and a date window at 3 o'clock. The brand name appears in both Arabic script and Latin letters, with "21 Jewels, Waterproof" and Incabloc.

Inside is a 21-jewel manual-wind mechanical movement, shown in the photos, and the case has a stainless-steel screw-down back. It is worn on a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Nobellux was one of the many small mid-century labels that sold mechanical watches through regional distributors. Its Arabic-script signed dial marks it as a watch made for Middle Eastern markets, a practice many Swiss-movement brands followed in the period. The Incabloc shock-protection system signed on the dial was developed in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, and introduced in 1934; it protects the delicate balance pivots if the watch is knocked or dropped.
