Brand heritage

Cartier was founded in Paris in 1847 by Louis-François Cartier and became known as the "jeweller of kings", supplying royal courts across Europe. Alongside its jewellery and watches such as the Santos and the Tank, the house developed a celebrated range of accessories and leather goods.

Collection history

In the 1970s, Robert Hocq and Alain-Dominique Perrin set out to bring Cartier to a new generation. The result was "Les Must de Cartier", a line of lighters, pens, watches and leather goods that turned Cartier into a lifestyle brand. Its signature became deep Bordeaux leather, embossed with the interlocking double-C logo and finished with gold-tone corner guards. Made in France, these pieces are sought after today as an accessible way to own a part of the history of the house.
