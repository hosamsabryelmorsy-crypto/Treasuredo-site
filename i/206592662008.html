About this watch

A big, bold Japanese diver from Orient's golden era. The brown-to-gold gradient dial carries applied steel markers with lume inserts, a day-date window at 3 o'clock, the Orient crest at 12 and the "KD 21 Jewels" signature with three stars below the centre.

The jumbo 45mm stainless-steel case has a coin-edge bezel and two crowns: the upper crown turns the internal rotating dive bezel, the lower one sets the time. It is worn on a stainless-steel bracelet and comes with an Orient presentation box.

Brand heritage

Orient was founded in Tokyo in 1950, with roots in the watch business started by Shogoro Yoshida in 1901. The company built its reputation on in-house automatic movements at honest prices and is counted among Japan's major watchmakers alongside Seiko and Citizen.

The King Diver line was introduced in the 1960s as a robust dive-style automatic with a crown-operated internal bezel, kept safely under the crystal. Big "jumbo" cases like this one are among the most characterful vintage Orients.
