About this watch

A classic round stainless steel Mido with a warm champagne dial, applied baton markers (doubled at 12, 6 and 9) and a framed date window at 3 o'clock, signed Mido Ocean Star and powerwind. The dial has developed a natural speckled patina.

The 35mm case has a caseback engraved Mido Ocean Star, Selfwinding, Antimagnetic, Stainless Steel, Waterproof, Shockproof, Switzerland. It is fitted with a tan leather strap.

Brand heritage

Mido was founded in 1918 in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland, by Georges Schaeren; the name comes from the Spanish "Yo mido", meaning "I measure". In 1954 Mido introduced its efficient Powerwind self-winding system, and in 1959 it launched the Ocean Star, whose single-piece case, loaded through the front, removed the joint between case and caseback to keep water out.
