About this watch

A lightweight Seiko perpetual calendar in titanium. The blue sunburst dial carries gold-framed luminous markers, luminous hands, a minute track ring, a date window at 3 o'clock and the "Perpetual Calendar Titanium" signature.

The sculpted titanium case and bracelet are water resistant to 10 bar (100 m). The caseback is signed Seiko Titanium, reference 8F32-004A, Made in Japan, with a serial number that dates it to 1999. It comes with a Seiko box.

Brand heritage

Seiko was founded in Tokyo in 1881 by Kintaro Hattori and grew into one of the few fully integrated watch manufacturers in the world, making its own movements, hairsprings and even crystals. It produced Japan's first wristwatch in 1913 and the world's first quartz wristwatch, the Astron, in 1969.

The 8F calibres are among Seiko's most advanced analog quartz movements: the 8F32 is a high-accuracy perpetual calendar that automatically accounts for month lengths and leap years until 28 February 2100.
