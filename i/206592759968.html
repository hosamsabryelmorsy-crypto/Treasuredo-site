About this watch

An elegant slim dress watch from Edox. The silver dial is set with sparkling stone clusters between black baton markers, and carries the Edox hourglass logo, the "Les Genevez" script, a date window at 6 o'clock and Swiss Made.

The 34mm black case and matching black link bracelet give it a sleek, modern look. The caseback is marked reference 27004 and Sapphire Crystal. It comes with an Edox presentation box.

Brand heritage

Edox was founded in 1884 in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland, by watchmaker Christian Ruefli-Flury; the name comes from ancient Greek and refers to the measuring of time, and the hourglass logo has been its symbol since early on. The brand created the 1961 Delfin with its double O-ring and double caseback, and in 1998 set a record with a 1.4mm calendar movement for its ultra-slim watches. The "Les Genevez" name honours the Jura village where Edox assembles its watches.
