About this watch

A ladies' Zodiac automatic with a white dial featuring a colour portrait of Muammar Gaddafi in his red beret above a colourful commemorative emblem, black Roman numerals, gold-tone markers, the Zodiac logo and a date window at 3 o'clock. The 28mm stainless-steel case, approx. 9mm thick, has a gold-tone bezel and a screw-down back, and flows into an integrated stainless-steel bracelet with Zodiac-signed folding clasp.

Muammar Gaddafi ruled Libya from 1969 until 2011. During his decades in power, watches bearing his portrait were made by several well-known Swiss houses, usually in small numbers and associated with presentation pieces, which gives them a unique place where watch collecting meets 20th-century political history. Portrait-dial watches of this kind are rare today and are sought after by collectors of Middle Eastern and North African historical pieces.

Brand heritage

Zodiac was founded in Le Locle, Switzerland, in 1882 by Ariste Calame. The firm became known for robust, well-built sports watches, launching the Sea Wolf in 1953 as one of the first purpose-built dive watches offered to the public, and in the late 1960s the space-age Astrographic. Zodiac's reputation for reliable Swiss automatic watches made it a natural choice for special-order dials in the Middle East and North Africa.
