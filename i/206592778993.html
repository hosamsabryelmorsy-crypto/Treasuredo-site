About this watch

A striking black dial crossed by a cream diagonal band, with an outer railway-style minute track and 24-hour numerals 13 to 24, baton hands, the Omax logo, "17 Jewels" and a red date window at 3 o'clock. Swiss Made.

The chunky 39mm stainless-steel cushion case is about 12mm thick, with a screw-down caseback bearing the Omax logo. It sits on a steel bracelet with bold red inserts, 22mm wide.

Brand heritage

Omax was founded in Geneva in 1946 as a family business making mechanical watches for the Middle East and the Indian subcontinent. By the 1960s it was among the largest Swiss watch exporters to the Indian subcontinent, and through the 1970s it spread across Africa and the Middle East. For many people in these regions an Omax was their first Swiss watch.
