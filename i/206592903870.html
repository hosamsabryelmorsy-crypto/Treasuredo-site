About this watch

A ladies'-size gold-tone Raymond Weil Genève watch with a crisp white dial featuring a colour portrait of Saddam Hussein, gold-tone baton markers, the Raymond Weil Genève signature and a date window at 3 o'clock. The 25mm case, approx. 7mm thick, has a snap-on caseback stamped with reference 5339. It is worn on a brown crocodile-pattern leather strap and comes in its Raymond Weil Genève presentation box.

Saddam Hussein was President of Iraq from 1979 until 2003. During his rule, watches bearing his portrait were made by respected Swiss houses, usually in small numbers and associated with presentation gifts, which is why they have become sought-after pieces for collectors of Middle Eastern political history. Finding such a dial from a well-known Geneva maker, complete with its box, is unusual.

Brand heritage

Raymond Weil was founded in Geneva in 1976 by Raymond Weil and Simone Bédat, at the height of the quartz crisis. The house helped revive Swiss watchmaking by presenting the Swiss watch as an elegant, emotional luxury object, and is known for naming its collections after music and the arts. It has remained an independent, family-owned company.
