Brand heritage

Buren was founded in 1873 in Büren an der Aare, a small medieval town on the river Aare in Switzerland. The company became known for technical innovation: in 1954 it patented a micro-rotor automatic movement, one of the first to set a small rotor inside the movement so that self-winding watches could be made much thinner.

In 1966 Buren was bought by the American Hamilton Watch Company, and in 1969 Hamilton-Buren joined Breitling, Heuer-Leonidas and Dubois Dépraz to launch the Calibre 11 "Chronomatic", among the world's first automatic chronographs. The Buren factory closed in 1972, which makes the brand's vintage watches a small and interesting chapter of Swiss watchmaking history.
