Brand heritage

Mido was founded in 1918 in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland, by Georges G. Schaeren. The name comes from the Spanish "yo mido", meaning "I measure". Mido built its reputation on practical, robust watches: the Multifort of 1934 was one of the first watches that was self-winding, shock-resistant, anti-magnetic and water-resistant all at once.

In 1954 Mido introduced its Powerwind self-winding system, and in 1959 it launched the Ocean Star, whose single-piece case, loaded through the front, removed the joint between case and case back to keep water out.
