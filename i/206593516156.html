About this watch

A ladies sports-elegant watch from Raymond Weil. The white dial has gold-tone round and baton markers, a triangle at 12, a framed date window at 3 o'clock and the Raymond Weil Genève and Flamenco signatures.

The 30mm gold-plated case has a riveted-style bezel and screw-down caseback engraved with reference 5370 and Swiss Made, on an integrated gold-plated link bracelet.

Brand heritage

Raymond Weil Genève was founded in Geneva in 1976 by Raymond Weil and Simone Bédat, in the middle of the quartz crisis, and is still an independent family-run Swiss watchmaker. A lifelong music lover, Raymond Weil gave many collections musical names, such as Amadeus, Fidelio and Traviata; the Flamenco name follows that tradition.
