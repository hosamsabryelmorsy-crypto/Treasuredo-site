About this watch

The Rado Florence is one of Rado's classic dress watches. This example has a silver and gold-tone striped dial with two applied Rado anchor emblems, slim gold-tone hands and "Rado Quartz Florence" signatures, framed by a dark bezel with fine gold lines.

The rectangular case measures approx. 25 x 30mm and 6mm thick, with a screw-secured Rado back. It is powered by a Swiss quartz movement and worn on a tan leather strap.

Brand heritage

Rado traces its history to 1917, when the brothers Fritz, Ernst and Werner Schlup founded Schlup & Co. in Lengnau, Switzerland, initially producing movements. Watches under the Rado name followed in the 1950s, and in 1962 the DiaStar was launched as "the world's first scratchproof watch", setting the brand's direction in innovative, hard-wearing materials. Rado is today part of the Swatch Group.
