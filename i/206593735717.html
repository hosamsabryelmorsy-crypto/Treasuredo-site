It has a screw-down caseback and is worn on a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Barlux was a small independent Swiss-movement label of the 1960s and 1970s. Like many small independent labels of the post-war decades, it built its watches around movements supplied by specialist Swiss makers and sold them mainly through export distributors. Little documented history of the brand survives today, and most such names disappeared during the quartz crisis of the 1970s and 1980s, which makes surviving examples an honest, affordable corner of vintage collecting. The Incabloc shock-protection system signed on the dial was developed in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, and introduced in 1934; it protects the delicate balance pivots if the watch is knocked or dropped.
