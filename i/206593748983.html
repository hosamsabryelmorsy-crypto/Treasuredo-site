About this watch

A gold-plated round case with a striking dial that fades from vivid red at the top to blue at the bottom. It has applied baton markers, gold-tone hands, a framed date window at 3 o'clock and the Nino, 17 Jewels and Shockprotected signatures.

The 34mm case has a screw-down back and is worn on a brown leather strap.

Brand heritage

Nino was a small Swiss-movement label of the 1970s, the most colourful decade in watch design, when bold gradient dials broke with the plain silver dials of earlier years. Like many small independent labels of the post-war decades, it built its watches around movements supplied by specialist Swiss makers and sold them mainly through export distributors. Little documented history of the brand survives today, and most such names disappeared during the quartz crisis of the 1970s and 1980s, which makes surviving examples an honest, affordable corner of vintage collecting.
