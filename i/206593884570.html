About this watch

A classic Rado everyday automatic. The champagne dial has applied baton markers, the red Rado anchor emblem, "Voyager" signature and a framed day-date window at 3 o'clock.

The 37mm stainless-steel case is framed by a textured gold-tone bezel for a refined two-tone look, with a screw-down caseback stamped with the Rado seahorse emblem. Inside is a Rado-signed 17-jewel Swiss automatic movement. It comes on a brown leather strap with a Rado box and cloth.

Brand heritage

Rado traces its origins to the Schlup & Co. movement workshop founded in Lengnau, Switzerland, in 1917, and began selling watches under the Rado name in the 1950s. The brand became known for bold design and innovative materials, culminating in the scratch-resistant DiaStar of 1962. The red anchor on the dial is traditionally used on Rado automatic models.

The Voyager was one of Rado's popular automatic day-date lines of the 1970s and 1980s, especially well loved in the Middle East and Asia.
