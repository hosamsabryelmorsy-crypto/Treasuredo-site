About this watch

A head-turning piece of Space Age design. Instead of hands, this Basis "Digital" shows the hours and minutes on rotating discs in a wide black window, with red pointers and the words "Swiss" and "17 Jewels" on the dial.

The 40mm faceted, highly polished case sits on a black leather strap. The steel caseback is marked "Automatic Shockproof All Stainless Steel"; the watch is listed as manual wind.

Brand heritage

Basis Watch was founded in 1918 by Adolf Thommen in Tecknau, Switzerland, and built its business on affordable pin-lever (Roskopf) watches. In 1967 Basis became a founding member of Economic Swiss Time Holding together with Buler, Agon, Ferex and Continental, directed by Max Thommen of Basis and Walter Rüfli of Buler. In the 1970s Basis produced bold mechanical "digital" jump hour watches, a futuristic answer to the coming LED era.
