Brand heritage

Hanhart was founded in 1882 by Johann A. Hanhart in Diessenhofen, Switzerland, and moved its production to Schwenningen in the Black Forest region of Germany in 1902. The company became one of the leading makers of mechanical stopwatches, used widely in sport, science and industry, and later gained fame for its pilot chronographs of the late 1930s.

Model history

In 1924 Hanhart introduced the first affordable mechanical stopwatch, which made accurate timing available to schools, clubs and workshops. Three-pusher models such as this one, with separate controls for start, stop and reset, allow timing to be interrupted and resumed, and remain respected for their robust construction and precise 1/5-second timing.
