About this watch

A Gulf collectible: the dial shows the golden falcon emblem of the United Arab Emirates, framed by an arc in the red, green, white and black of the national flag and an Arabic inscription naming the Ministry. A wide black ring surrounds the centre, and the slim gold-plated round case has ribbed gold-tone hood lugs.

The stainless steel snap back is signed Westar, "Water Resistant" and "Swiss". It is fitted on a dark blue Hirsch leather strap.

Brand heritage

Westar was launched in the early 1980s by the Al-Futtaim group of Dubai as a Swiss-made watch brand offering stylish watches at accessible prices, and became a familiar name across the Gulf. After the founding of the UAE in 1971, ministries and institutions commissioned watches bearing the national emblem as official gifts and awards, and these are sought after today by collectors of Gulf and commemorative timepieces.
