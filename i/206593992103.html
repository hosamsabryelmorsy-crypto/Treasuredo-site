Brand heritage

Seiko was founded in Tokyo in 1881, when Kintaro Hattori opened a shop selling and repairing watches and clocks. The company produced Japan's first wristwatch, the Laurel, in 1913, and in 1969 launched the Astron, the world's first quartz wristwatch, a milestone that changed the watch industry. Through the following decades Seiko's slim quartz dress watches became a byword for accuracy and quality at an accessible price.
