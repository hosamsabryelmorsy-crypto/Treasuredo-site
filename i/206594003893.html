About this watch

A confident 1970s design from Jovial. The oval silver-champagne dial is signed with the winged Jovial "J" logo, "Renaissance", "17 Jewels" and "Swiss Made", with applied pyramid markers, gold-tone baton hands and a date window at about 5 o'clock.

The broad gold-plated case measures about 37mm and has a steel screw-down back marked "Steel Back Swiss Made Waterproof Shockprotected", number 56.1623. It is fitted on a brown croco-pattern leather strap and comes with a Jovial box.

Brand heritage

Jovial was formed in 1929 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, and later moved its headquarters to Bienne. From the beginning it specialised in the Middle Eastern market, where it became one of the leading mid-range Swiss brands.
