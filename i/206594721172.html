About this watch

A classic 1980s diver look: the black dial has large round luminous markers, a triangle at 12, gold-tone printing, a day-date window at 3 o'clock and is marked "Deep Sea Diver 660ft/200m" and "Quartz".

The 38mm stainless-steel case is topped with a blue and red "Pepsi" rotating bezel and fitted to a stainless-steel jubilee-style bracelet with fold-over clasp.

Brand heritage

The dive watch became one of the defining watch styles of the 20th century, and the red-and-blue "Pepsi" bezel, first used on travel watches to separate day and night hours, became a favourite colour scheme for sporty divers. In the 1980s many brands paired this look with accurate quartz movements, creating rugged everyday sports watches. Prestige de Geneve is a smaller name from this period, and its watches offer the classic diver style at an accessible price.
