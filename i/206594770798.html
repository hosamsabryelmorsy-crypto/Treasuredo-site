About this watch

This Grandlux has a cream dial with gold-tone Arabic numerals, luminous-style hands, a small seconds sub-dial at 6 o'clock and the markings "17 Jewels", "Waterproof" and "Antimagnetic".

The round 30mm case has distinctive sculpted lugs and a screw-down back, and houses a 17-jewel Swiss manual-wind movement. It is fitted to a grey leather strap.

Brand heritage

Grandlux was one of the many smaller Swiss brands of the post-war years. In that period, features developed for wartime service, such as bold Arabic numerals, luminous hands, shock and magnetic protection and water-resistant cases, became popular with civilians, while designers added elegant touches like sculpted "fancy" lugs. These charming pieces are a favourite with collectors of affordable vintage Swiss watches.
