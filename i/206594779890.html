About this watch

This Tiara Viceroy has a deep blue sunburst dial with fine white baton markers, the Tiara crown logo with "of Switzerland" and "Viceroy" script, and slim white hands.

The wide TV-style cushion case (approx. 33mm) combines a brushed top with polished flanks and has a snap-on back marked "Steel Back, Incabloc, 17 Jewels, Swiss Made". It houses a 17-jewel Swiss manual-wind movement and is fitted to a blue croco-pattern leather strap.

Brand heritage

Tiara of Switzerland was one of the smaller Swiss watch brands active in the 1970s, a bold decade for Swiss design when makers embraced wide cushion and "TV" cases and vivid sunburst dials around dependable Swiss mechanical movements. Today these pieces are popular with collectors who love authentic 1970s character.
