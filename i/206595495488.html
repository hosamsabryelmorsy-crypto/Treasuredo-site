About this watch

Instead of hands, rotating discs display the hours and minutes in small windows: a jumping hour on the left and a rotating minute disc on the right, both flagged by red pointers, across a black brushed dial marked "Basis", "Swiss", "17 Jewels" and "Digital". It is a futuristic look achieved entirely with traditional Swiss mechanics.

The sculpted, highly polished stainless steel case measures about 40mm and 14mm thick, with a screw-down caseback stamped "All Stainless Steel", "Shockproof" and "Automatic". The seller lists the 17-jewel Swiss movement as manual wind. It is fitted on a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Basis was one of the Swiss economic-watch brands that joined Buler, Agon and Continental in 1967 to found Economic Swiss Time Holding, a group of Roskopf watch makers headquartered in Basel and directed by Max Thommen of Basis and Walter Rüfli of Buler. Buler itself was founded in 1945 in Lengnau and became a pioneer of digital-display watches in the 1970s.
