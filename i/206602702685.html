About this watch

A great example of the short-lived mechanical "digital" era. The black dial with grey frame is signed CRONEL with its crown emblem, Shockprotected and Swiss Made; the hour is shown on a jumping disc and the minutes on a rotating disc, read against a yellow pointer.

The wide brushed cushion "TV" case measures approx. 38mm across and 14mm thick, with a stainless steel snap-on back marked CRONEL 504, Waterresistant, Shockprotected, Swiss Made. Inside is a Swiss Buler manual-wind movement. It is fitted with a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Cronel was one of the smaller Swiss brands of the period, marked with a stylised crown emblem. In the 1970s it was one of the brands of Camy Watch Co. of Geneva, the company Raymond Weil directed before founding his own brand. Mechanical "digital" watches like this one were a short-lived Swiss answer to the electronic age: before quartz and LED displays took over in the later 1970s, makers housed simple mechanical movements in futuristic cases and showed the time through windows, with the hour on a jumping disc and the minutes on a rotating disc.
