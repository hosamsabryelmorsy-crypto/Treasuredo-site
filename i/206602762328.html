About this watch

A compact 1960s Swiss skin diver with plenty of character. The bronze-brown dial has aged to a warm tropical tone and carries bold luminous Arabic numerals and batons, signed "Verbel" and "Ancre 17 Rubis Antichoc". A black bidirectional timing bezel and an oversized knurled crown complete the classic sports look.

The approx. 28mm stainless-steel case has a screw-down caseback stamped "Tested 20 Atmospheres, Stainless Steel, 100% Waterproof". Inside is a Swiss 17-jewel lever manual-wind movement with Antichoc shock protection. It comes on a black leather two-piece strap with buckle.

Brand heritage

Verbel is one of the many smaller Swiss watch labels of the 1960s whose company history is not well documented. Labels like this bought complete Swiss lever movements and cases from specialist suppliers, which is why they could offer a real 17-jewel shock-protected diver at an accessible price. Skin divers of this kind, made for swimmers and holidaymakers rather than professional divers, were among the most popular sports watches of the decade and are now collected for their variety and charm.
