About this watch

A classic 1950s gents dress watch with lovely proportions. The silvered dial, signed Avia and 15 JEWELS, carries applied gilt Arabic numerals at 12, 2, 4, 8 and 10, gilt dart markers, slim gilt hands and a recessed subsidiary seconds dial at 6 o'clock. The dial has developed a gentle, even patina.

The 35mm round case has elegant flared lugs and a snap-on back stamped FOND ACIER INOXYDABLE (stainless steel back). Inside is a Swiss 15-jewel hand-wound movement. It is fitted with a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Avia was the main brand of Degoumois & Cie., a watch company founded by Henri-Victor Degoumois in La Chaux-de-Fonds in 1887 that later moved to Neuchâtel. Launched in association with the British firm Louis Newmark, Avia covered the whole market, from inexpensive watches to fine lever movements in gold and silver cases, and by the 1950s it was a familiar name in jewellers' windows, especially in Britain.
