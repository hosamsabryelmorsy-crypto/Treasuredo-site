About this watch

A neat ladies' Roamer with a silvered sunburst dial, slim black and gilt baton markers, an applied gold 12, gilt hands and a date window at 3 o'clock, a less common feature on a ladies' watch of this size. The dial is signed ROAMER and Swiss Made.

The round gold-plated case measures approximately 25mm without crown and 6mm thick, with a stainless-steel back engraved "Roamer Swiss Made Brevete" with patent numbers and "Waterproof". It is powered by a Swiss mechanical hand-wound movement and fitted with a black lizard-pattern leather strap.

Brand heritage

Roamer was founded in 1888 in Solothurn, Switzerland, by Fritz Meyer, who began with a small workshop making cylinder escapements. The Roamer name was registered in 1908 and acquired by Meyer and his partner Johann Studeli, whose Meyer & Studeli (MST) movements earned a reputation for precision and reliability. Through the 1950s and 1960s Roamer sold dependable, well-finished Swiss watches around the world.
