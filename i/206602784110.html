About this watch

This watch carries the emblem of the United Arab Emirates, the golden falcon, printed in colour on a white dial signed Pierre Lannier Paris and Quartz. Black Arabic numerals, a fine minute track and gilt hands give it a clean, legible look.

The 38mm round stainless-steel case has a screw-detailed bezel and flows into an integrated steel bracelet with folding clasp. The caseback is marked All Stainless Steel and Water Proof 3ATM. Emblem-dial watches like this were commissioned across the Gulf from the 1970s onward and often presented as official gifts, which is why collectors look for them today.

Brand heritage

Pierre Lannier was founded in Alsace, France, in 1977 by the Burgun family, and its watches have been designed and assembled in Ernolsheim-lès-Saverne ever since. Still family-run, now by the second generation, it is one of the best-known French watch houses.
