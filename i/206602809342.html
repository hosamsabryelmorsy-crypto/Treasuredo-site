About this watch

A characterful Art Deco era watch signed Genie, with the brand name in both Latin and Arabic script and a red emblem beneath 12. The two-tone sector dial has Arabic numerals, a sub-seconds register at 6 o'clock and copper-tone hands, and has aged heavily into a strong, unrestored patina.

The 34mm case has distinctive flared teardrop lugs and a snap back marked Swiss Made, and houses a Swiss mechanical manual-wind movement. Fitted with a brown leather strap.

Brand heritage

Genie has been producing Swiss-made watches since 1919, and its watches with dual-script dials were sold widely in the Middle East. Today the brand is based in Grenchen, Switzerland, where it celebrated its 100-year anniversary in 2019.
