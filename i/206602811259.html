About this watch

A well-built 1990s Seiko SQ50 multi-function quartz. The silver-white dial has applied baton markers, baton hands with a slim gold-tone seconds hand, and a window at 3 o'clock showing the mode and date. It is signed Seiko Auto Calendar, Alarm SQ50.

The 38mm stainless-steel case has two pushers on the left and the crown on the right, and sits on a stainless-steel bracelet. The screw-down caseback reads Seiko Stainless Steel, Water Resistant 5 Bar, 6M26-8030, Japan. Comes with a Seiko box.

Brand heritage

Seiko began in 1881 when Kintaro Hattori opened a watch shop in Tokyo, and in 1969 it introduced the Astron, the world's first quartz wristwatch. In the following decades Seiko developed sophisticated analogue quartz calibres such as the 6M26, which combines an auto calendar that adjusts itself for the length of each month with a 12-hour alarm.
