About this watch

A 1970s Kienzle Swiss Cosmopolitan skin diver with a vertically brushed silver dial, applied luminous baton markers, luminous baton hands and a date window at 3 o'clock, signed Kienzle Swiss, Cosmopolitan, Automatic and T Swiss. An inner 12-hour ring sits inside a black rotating bezel, a combination that suits a watch named for the international traveller.

The 38mm cushion-shaped stainless steel case has a screw-down case back marked 20 ATM. It comes on its Kienzle-signed stainless steel bracelet with its original white Kienzle box.

Brand heritage

Kienzle is one of the oldest names in German timekeeping. The business began in 1822 in Schwenningen, in the Black Forest, and took its name from Jakob Kienzle, who joined the family firm in the 1880s and built it into one of the largest clock and watch makers in Europe.

For its better wristwatches the company turned to Switzerland, and pieces signed Kienzle Swiss combine the German brand with Swiss-made automatic movements and cases. The Cosmopolitan belongs to the golden age of the skin diver, the late 1960s and 1970s.
