About this watch

A slim square Tissot dress watch from the Six-T line. The 24mm gold-tone case frames a genuine white mother-of-pearl dial whose colour shifts with the light, with gold hands and baton markers, a sunburst minute ring, Tissot 1853 signature and a date window at 6 o'clock.

It sits on the signature six-row gold-tone link bracelet. The caseback reads Tissot 1853, Water Resistant 30M, Sapphire Crystal, Stainless Steel, L910. Comes with its Tissot box.

Brand heritage

Tissot was founded in 1853 in Le Locle, in the Swiss Jura, by Charles-Félicien Tissot and his son Charles-Émile. It has long been known for well-made Swiss watches at sensible prices, and alongside its sports models has always offered elegant jewellery watches for women, such as the Six-T with its supple six-row bracelet.
