About this watch

The Gucci Dive is the house's playful take on the classic steel sports watch. Instead of plain markers, the silver guilloché dial carries luminous stars, dots and triangles, with the Gucci bee near 10 o'clock, a green-tipped seconds hand and a date window between 4 and 5 o'clock. It is framed by a white unidirectional dive bezel with Gucci's green and red accents.

The 40mm stainless-steel case has a screwed caseback signed GUCCI, Water Resistant 20 ATM, Swiss Made, 136.3, Stainless Steel. It is fitted with its stainless-steel bracelet with Gucci push-button deployant clasp and comes with inner and outer Gucci presentation boxes.

Brand heritage

Gucci was founded in Florence in 1921 by Guccio Gucci as a maker of leather goods and luggage. In the early 1970s it became one of the first fashion houses to sell its own Swiss-made watches, and its watches are still produced in Switzerland today. The bee, a motif from the house's archive, was revived as one of Gucci's signatures in recent years and appears on the Dive dial.
