About this watch

A handsome mid-century Swiss dress watch. The silver sunburst dial is signed "Agon 17 Rubis Calendrier" and "Antimagnetic", with applied gold-tone dart markers, dauphine hands, a date window at 3 o'clock and a small-seconds register with crosshair at 6.

The 36mm gold-plated case has a stainless-steel snap caseback stamped "Fond Acier Inoxydable, Swiss Made" and 10400. It runs on a 17-jewel Swiss manual-wind movement and is fitted to a brown leather strap.

Brand heritage

Agon was based in Mumpf, Switzerland. The firm was founded by Robert Triebold in 1937, took the Agon name in 1953 and grew into a large producer, making more than 80,000 watches a month by the 1960s in a modern factory. Agon was a founding member of Economic Swiss Time Holding in 1967, which merged with SSIH in 1971. Its jewelled-lever models like this Calendrier sat at the upper end of its range.
