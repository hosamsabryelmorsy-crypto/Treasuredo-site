About this watch

A large-for-its-era 1950s Delbana with oversized Arabic numerals, slender dark hands and a small-seconds register at 6 o'clock. The warm ivory dial, signed "Delbana", "15 Jewels" and "Antimagnetic", has developed an even, characterful vintage patina.

The round 37mm steel-tone case has a snap-on stainless steel back and is fitted with a period-style expanding metal bracelet. Inside is a Swiss hand-wound movement signed Delbana and marked 15 Rubis, shown in the photographs.

Brand heritage

Delbana was founded in 1931 in Grenchen, Switzerland, by Goliardo Della Balda, a watchmaker originally from San Marino; the name is a contraction of his surname. By the 1950s the company was producing over 100,000 watches a year and exporting solid, well-priced Swiss watches worldwide. Delbana later merged with Wega in 1976, and in 2001 joined Delma and moved to Lengnau, where the name continues today.
