About this watch

One of the most dramatic Japanese sports watches of the 1970s, in the sought-after black and orange colourway. The big cushion case frames a busy dial with a black centre, orange and white segmented scales, luminous markers, the Orient logo, three stars and "KD 21 Jewels", plus an internal rotating ring operated by the second crown.

The stainless-steel case measures approx. 45.5mm and has a screw-down caseback with the Orient emblem. Inside is an Orient 21-jewel automatic movement with day and date (bilingual Arabic/English day). It is fitted to a steel bracelet.

Brand heritage

Orient traces its roots to 1901, when Shogoro Yoshida opened the Yoshida Watch Shop in Tokyo. The company resumed watch production in 1950 and took the Orient Watch name in 1951, building its reputation on in-house mechanical movements. The original King Diver of 1965 combined a compressor-type case with a self-winding movement, and the oversized 1970s KD became an Orient icon. Orient became part of Seiko Epson in 2009.
