About this watch

The case back is signed RADO 711.2011.4 WATERSEALED and secured with four screws. The watch sits on its integrated stainless-steel bracelet with a Rado-signed clasp and comes with a vintage Rado presentation box.

Brand heritage

Rado grew out of Schlup & Co., the movement maker founded in 1917 in Lengnau, Switzerland, by the brothers Fritz, Ernst and Werner Schlup. The company began selling watches under the Rado name in the 1950s, and in 1962 launched the DiaStar, marketed as the world's first scratchproof watch thanks to its hard-metal case and sapphire crystal. That focus on hard materials earned Rado its name as the "master of materials".
