About this watch

An uncommon 1970s skin-diver style automatic signed "Navy" beneath a small crown logo. The graphic black dial has an oversized luminous 12, aged baton markers, a red outer chapter ring, broad red-tipped hands, sweep seconds and a framed date at 3 o'clock under a magnifier.

The 38mm cushion case is framed by a blue and silver rotating bezel and closed by a stainless steel screw back stamped 1470 and marked Dustproof, Stainless Steel Back and Antimagnetic. It is fitted with a bright blue leather strap.

Brand heritage

"Navy" is one of the many small private-label names that appeared on European sports watches of the 1960s and 1970s. Watches like this were typically assembled from bought-in cases and movements and sold through jewellers and department stores. Little documentation survives on the label itself, and no military-issue provenance is claimed.
