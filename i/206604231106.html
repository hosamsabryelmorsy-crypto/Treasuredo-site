About this watch

A dressy Nivada Antarctic with a gunmetal-grey sunburst dial crossed by fine crosshair lines. The Nivada name and flowing Antarctic script sit left of centre, balanced by a framed day-date window at 3 o'clock with an English weekday, applied rectangular markers and polished baton hands.

The approx. 36mm stainless steel case has a screw-down case back with the Antarctic penguin medallion. Inside is a 25-jewel Swiss automatic movement with a Nivada-signed rotor. It is fitted on a black crocodile-pattern leather strap.

Brand heritage

Nivada was founded in Grenchen, Switzerland, in 1926 and was an early producer of waterproof automatic watches. In the United States its watches were sold as Nivada Grenchen and through Croton. The brand is best known among collectors for the Antarctic, the Chronomaster and the Depthomatic.

The Antarctic was launched in 1950 and earned its name when Nivada watches accompanied members of the U.S. Navy's Operation Deep Freeze to Antarctica in the mid-1950s; the penguin emblem on the case back recalls that story.
