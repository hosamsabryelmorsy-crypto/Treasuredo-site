About this watch

A compact Omega De Ville quartz dress watch with a softly faceted cushion case and a minimalist champagne dial: applied gold baton markers, slender gold hands and a central seconds hand, with no date. Decades of ageing have given the dial a heavily spotted, warm patina.

The inner caseback is signed Omega and marked "LL 195.0032" and "Lunette plaqué or G 20 microns, fond acier inoxydable" (20-micron gold-plated bezel, stainless steel back). Inside is the Omega Swiss quartz calibre 1417, marked Seven Jewels. The watch is fitted with a brown leather strap with a vintage Omega-signed buckle.

Brand heritage

Omega traces its history to 1848, when Louis Brandt set up his workshop in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland; the Omega name comes from a celebrated movement of 1894. The company has been Official Timekeeper of the Olympic Games many times since 1932, its Speedmaster was worn on the Moon in 1969, and it was among the Swiss pioneers of quartz watchmaking. The De Ville name first appeared in 1960 on dressier Seamaster models and became a collection in its own right in 1967.
