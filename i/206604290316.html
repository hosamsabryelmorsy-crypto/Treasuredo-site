Vintage Edox Hydromatic Automatic, 25 Jewels, Midnight Blue Dial, Date, 33mm, late 1960s–early 1970s

Midnight blue with one well-judged splash of colour: a vivid orange centre seconds hand. Applied silver-tone baton markers, slender matching hands and a framed date window at 3 o’clock complete a compact, sharply lugged 33mm watch on a navy leather strap.

Brand heritage

Edox was founded in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland, in 1884 by watchmaker Christian Rüefli-Flury. The brand made its name in water-resistant watches, notably the Delfin of 1961 with its double-gasket crown and the Hydro-Sub diver of 1965, and later for inventive pieces such as the Geoscope world timer of 1970.

Model history

Hydromatic was the name Edox gave to its water-protected automatic watches of the 1960s. This example carries an Edox-signed 25-jewel self-winding movement, shown in the photographs, in a screw-back case, and dates to roughly the late 1960s or early 1970s.
