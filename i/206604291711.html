About this watch

A 1980s Mido Ocean Star with a warm ivory dial carrying a crowned eagle insignia of the Royal Saudi Air Force, Arabic lettering and the number 13. Slender baton markers, gold-tone hands and a date window at three sit in an angular stainless-steel case with a decorative gold-tone screwed bezel and an integrated two-tone bracelet with Mido-signed folding clasp.

The Swiss quartz movement keeps the watch easy to wear, and the case back still carries a surviving Mido Aquadura label.

Brand heritage

Mido was founded in 1918 in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland, by Georges G. Schaeren; the name comes from the Spanish "yo mido", meaning "I measure". The brand built its reputation on practical innovation, from the tough Multifort of 1934 to its Aquadura crown-sealing system using specially treated cork. Now part of the Swatch Group, Mido has always been especially strong in the Middle East, Latin America and Asia.

The Ocean Star name became Mido's flagship water-resistant line from 1959. Emblem-dial watches of this kind were commissioned in the Gulf region as presentation and commemorative pieces.
