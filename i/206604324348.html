Sultanate of Oman 2005 Commemorative Watch, Khanjar National Emblem Dial, Quartz, 34mm, Date

An Oman-themed commemorative watch with an elaborate silver-tone coin-style dial carrying the “Sultanate of Oman 2005” inscription, Arabic script and the national emblem of a khanjar dagger over crossed swords. A framed date window sits near 6 o’clock and the bezel is finished with a ring of clear decorative accents and a finely ridged edge.

Background

The khanjar and crossed swords is the national emblem of Oman and the badge of the ruling Al Said dynasty. Sultan Qaboos bin Said ruled Oman from 1970 to 2020, and 2005 marked thirty-five years of his reign, an occasion celebrated with the country’s 35th National Day. The watch carries no maker’s brand name.

About this piece

Emblem and commemorative watches have long been produced in the Gulf states for national occasions and given as official gifts or sold as souvenirs. This piece belongs to that tradition, with the 2005 date and Omani symbols as the entire focus of the dial.
