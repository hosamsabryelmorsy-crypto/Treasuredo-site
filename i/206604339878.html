About this watch

West End Watch Co. is a Swiss watchmaker founded in 1886, famous across India and the Middle East. Its rugged "Sowar Prima" line was the soldier's watch of choice, and special dials were made for the Sultanate of Oman bearing the red national Khanjar emblem. This example has the rare cream-yellow dial with the red Khanjar, luminous sword hands, and a day-date window. Supplied in its West End Watch Co. presentation box.
