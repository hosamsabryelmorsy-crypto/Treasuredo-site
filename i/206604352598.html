About this watch

A vintage Ebel 1911 ladies watch, reference 188901, with a cream dial bearing the Arabic name "Khalifa" above the Ebel signature and the historic State of Qatar emblem in the lower half. The dial has eleven diamond hour markers, slender baton hands and a date window at 3 o'clock.

The approximately 26mm stainless-steel case carries the 1911's signature screwed 18K yellow gold bezel, as confirmed by the caseback inscription ("Stainless Steel Case, Bezel 18K Gold, Swiss"), and flows into Ebel's integrated two-tone bracelet. The name on the dial evokes Sheikh Khalifa bin Hamad Al Thani, Emir of Qatar from 1972 to 1995.

Brand heritage

Ebel was founded in 1911 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, by Eugène Blum and Alice Lévy; the name is formed from their initials, Eugène Blum Et Lévy. Known as "The Architects of Time", the house became famous for its fluid case shapes and integrated wave-link bracelets. The 1911 collection was introduced in 1986 to mark the company's 75th anniversary and takes its name from the founding year; it is recognised by its softly contoured case, five-screw bezel and integrated bracelet. In the 1980s and 1990s several Swiss houses produced dials personalised for Gulf states for presentation purposes.
