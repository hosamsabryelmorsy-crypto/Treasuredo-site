Brand heritage

Camy was a Geneva watch company known for reliable, well-priced Swiss mechanical watches, sold widely across Europe, Asia and the Middle East from the 1950s through the 1970s. It also holds an interesting place in Swiss watchmaking history: Raymond Weil joined the company in 1949 and later became its director, before establishing his own brand in 1976.

Model history

Portrait and emblem dials were commissioned from Swiss makers in large numbers during the 1970s and 1980s for governments and institutions in the Middle East, and were typically given as presentation pieces. Watches with Iraqi portrait dials from this period are now collected as historical and political memorabilia.
