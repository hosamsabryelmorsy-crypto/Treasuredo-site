About this watch

A vintage Citizen automatic from circa 1977, reference 71-0423, with an unusual silver-toned dial of crystalline, stone-like texture. Irregular frosted areas alternate with broader reflective patches, set off by raised gold-tone hour markers. The dial is marked Automatic and 21 Jewels, and the calendar shows the date and an Arabic day display.

The faceted, octagonal gold-plated case measures approximately 37mm and has a stainless-steel back marked Citizen Watch Co., 4-164024 SMT, GN-4, 71-0423, Japan, G.P., with serial 71012504, and a CTZ-signed crown. It comes on a matching gold-tone bracelet with Citizen-signed clasp and with the Citizen presentation box shown.

Brand heritage

Citizen began in 1918 as the Shokosha Watch Research Institute in Tokyo and took the Citizen name in 1930, after its first pocket watch of 1924. It grew into one of the largest watch manufacturers in the world, producing its movements in-house and later pioneering light-powered Eco-Drive technology. During the 1970s Citizen produced a very wide range of 21-jewel automatic watches, many of them for the Middle East market with Arabic and English day wheels, and experimented with faceted cases and decorative dials in unusual textures.
