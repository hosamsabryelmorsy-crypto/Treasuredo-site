Brand heritage

Girard-Perregaux traces its history to 1791, when Jean-François Bautte began making watches in Geneva, and takes its name from Constant Girard and Marie Perregaux, who established the firm in La Chaux-de-Fonds in 1856. It is one of the oldest Swiss manufactures, celebrated for the Tourbillon with Three Gold Bridges and for pioneering high-frequency movements in the 1960s. In the 1950s and 1960s the house produced a broad range of slim, understated dress watches with hand-wound and Gyromatic automatic movements, and manual-wind models such as this one represent its classic mid-century style.
