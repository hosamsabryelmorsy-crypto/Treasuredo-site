Vintage Monceau Dress Watch – Octagonal Case – Swiss Manual Wind – Cream Dial – Circa 1970s

A vintage Monceau dress watch from the 1970s, with an octagonal silver-tone case and broad bevelled edges framing a warm cream dial. The dial is deliberately minimal, with four applied baton markers, a discreet script signature and a two-hand display without date. The case measures approximately 35mm across and 8mm thick, with a stainless steel back marked "FOND ACIER INOXYDABLE". It is fitted with a brown leather strap, approximately 18mm wide, with silver-tone pin buckle.

Brand heritage

Monceau traces its beginnings to a family-owned watch and jewellery boutique in Lausanne, Switzerland, in the early 1950s. During the 1960s the brand expanded into international distribution, becoming known for elegant Swiss-made dress watches and jewellery pieces.

Model history

This watch belongs to Monceau's earlier production era. The octagonal case with bevelled edges reflects the geometric styling that became fashionable in Swiss watchmaking during the 1970s, paired here with a slim hand-wound movement and a clean, uncluttered dial in the tradition of the classic dress watch.
