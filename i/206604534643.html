Bernhard H. Mayer Hugo Chronograph – Swiss Quartz – Black PVD Steel – Sapphire Crystal – 44mm – With Box

A Bernhard H. Mayer "Hugo" chronograph in black PVD-coated stainless steel. The black textured dial has vivid red Arabic numerals, red chronograph hands, two sub-registers and a date window, framed by a red-on-black rotating countdown bezel with a luminous triangle at 12. The 44mm case is 15mm thick, with dual pushers and a screw-down caseback engraved "All Stainless Steel / Sapphire Crystal / Swiss Made / 10 ATM Water Resistant". It comes on a 22mm black rubber strap with signed buckle and with the branded presentation box.

Brand heritage

Bernhard H. Mayer was founded in 1871 in Pforzheim, Germany, the historic centre of the German jewellery industry, as an engraving workshop and mint producing fine medals and coins. The house later extended its name to jewellery and to a line of Swiss-made watches.

Model history

The Hugo is one of the sports chronographs in the Bernhard H. Mayer watch collection, built around a Swiss quartz chronograph movement in a large dive-style case with sapphire crystal, rotating bezel and 10 ATM rating.
