About this watch

An unused Raymond Weil Tango, reference 5599, with a dial bearing the full-colour crest of the King Khalid Military Academy of Saudi Arabia on a white chevron-guilloché centre, with gold Roman numerals and a date window at 3 o'clock.

The 39mm two-tone case has the signature Tango bezel with six gold-tone stud motifs and a sapphire crystal; the caseback is marked Sapphire Crystal, W.R. 50m, Swiss Made, Stainless Steel. The watch remains in new old stock condition, with the protective wrapping still in place on the two-tone bracelet and caseback. The Swiss quartz movement is running and the quick-set date works properly.

Brand heritage

Raymond Weil was founded in Geneva in 1976 by Raymond Weil, at the height of the quartz crisis, and remains one of the few independent, family-owned Swiss watch houses. The brand is closely associated with music and the arts, and many of its collections, including Tango, Toccata, Parsifal and Maestro, take their names from that world.

The King Khalid Military Academy in Riyadh is the officer academy of the Saudi Arabian National Guard. Swiss watches with institutional crest dials were commissioned for presentation, and examples rarely reach the open market.
