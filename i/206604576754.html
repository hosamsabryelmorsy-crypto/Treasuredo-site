About this watch

An unusual Longines dress watch whose gold dial is covered with a fine repeating pattern of the Longines winged-hourglass emblem. Slim applied baton markers with black inlay, two slender hands and the Longines signature with its winged hourglass complete a very elegant, understated design.

The compact square-cushion gold-plated case measures approximately 30mm and has a stainless-steel caseback with a battery hatch engraved with a month-reminder ring. It is fitted with a dark brown lizard-grain leather strap with Longines-signed gold-tone buckle, and comes with a vintage Longines presentation box.

Brand heritage

Longines was founded in Saint-Imier, Switzerland, in 1832 by Auguste Agassiz. Its winged-hourglass logo, registered in 1889, is regarded as the oldest registered trademark still in use in its original form. The company built its reputation on precision timing for sport and aviation, including the Hour Angle watch developed with Charles Lindbergh, and was an early mover in quartz watchmaking at the end of the 1960s.
