About this watch

A striking early-1990s sports watch from Marco Valentino, signed Swiss Made on the caseback with reference 91.005M. The black dial carries an unusual set of nautical indications: a tide subdial (HIGH / LOW) at 3 o'clock, a SPRING / NEAP tide range subdial at 9 o'clock, a moon / sun phase aperture at 6 o'clock and a date window at 12. Red accents, gold-tone luminous dot markers and gold-tone hands contrast with a brushed steel rotating bezel graduated 0-60. The case is fitted with a bracelet of brushed steel links with black rubber inserts, ending in a black rubber strap with a steel pin buckle.

Brand heritage

Marco Valentino signed Swiss-made fashion watches, mostly quartz, sold in the 1980s and 1990s. Its catalogue was mainly dress watches, so a tide and moonphase sports model like this one turns up much less often.
