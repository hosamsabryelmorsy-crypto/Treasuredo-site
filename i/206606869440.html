About this watch

A bold vintage Orient King Diver-style automatic from Orient's classic "Crystal 21 Jewels" three-star line. The big 45mm cushion steel case carries two crowns: the lower one sets the time and the day-date, the upper one turns the internal rotating diving bezel with its red-orange first-15-minute section and white 5-minute scale. The black dial has applied gold-tone baton markers with lume dots, gold-tone hands, the gold Orient crown logo and a day-date window at 3 o'clock. Caseback stamped "Water Resistant - All Stainless Steel" with the Orient lion crest and case number T 469643-4B. Supplied on a steel link bracelet with an Orient box.

Brand heritage

Orient Watch Co. was founded in Tokyo in 1950 and is known for building its own mechanical movements in-house, offering automatic watches at honest prices for decades. Since 2009 Orient has been part of Seiko Epson. The King Diver name is one of Orient's best-loved vintage dive watch lines, and Orient brought it back as a modern reissue in 2020.
