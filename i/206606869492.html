About this watch

A rare piece of 20th-century Middle Eastern history: a petite Eterna ladies' wristwatch whose silver-white dial carries a printed colour portrait of Iraqi president Saddam Hussein in a blue suit. Watches bearing his portrait were made for Iraq as commemorative / presentation pieces, and Swiss brands such as Eterna supplied the watches. This example has a crisp silver dial with applied silver baton markers, the ETERNA signature, "T SWISS T" at the bottom, polished steel case and knurled crown, fitted on a grey leather Eterna-signed strap with a gold-tone buckle, and comes in its original white Eterna presentation box.

Brand heritage

Eterna was founded in 1856 in Grenchen, Switzerland, by Josef Girard and Urs Schild. The company is credited with one of the first alarm wristwatches (patented 1908) and, in 1948, the Eterna-Matic with its ball-bearing-mounted rotor, which inspired the five-dot logo still used today. Eterna watches also accompanied Thor Heyerdahl on the 1947 Kon-Tiki expedition, and the movement maker ETA was originally Eterna's own movement division.
