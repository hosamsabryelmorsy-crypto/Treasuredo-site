About this watch

A genuine piece of Middle Eastern political and horological history: a Swiss-made Cauny wristwatch with a full-colour printed portrait of Iraqi President Saddam Hussein in a blue suit on a golden sunburst dial. The dial is signed "Cauny" with the brand's emblem, "Waterproof 17 Jewels" and "T Swiss Made T", with applied gold-tone Arabic numerals 12, 6 and 9, black-inlaid baton markers, lume-filled baton hands, a red-tipped seconds hand and a date window at 3 o'clock. The 1970s-style cushion case carries a stainless steel caseback stamped "Swiss Made - Shockprotected - Stainless Steel Back", case number 61004.

Brand heritage

Cauny is a Swiss watch brand founded in 1927, with production in La Chaux-de-Fonds and strong Portuguese ties. Its "Prima" line, registered in 1953, made Cauny one of the best-selling watch brands in Portugal and Spain through the mid-20th century. During the 1970s and 1980s the Iraqi government commissioned a number of Swiss watchmakers to print Saddam Hussein's portrait on their dials; these watches were given to officers, officials and guests, and today they are sought after as historical collectibles.
