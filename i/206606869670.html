About this watch

A bold 1970s Swiss automatic from Louis Rossel, reference 1624. The sculpted gold-plated tonneau case flows straight into an integrated gold-tone bracelet, framing a sunburst dial that fades from deep brown at the edge to warm gold at the centre. The dial is set with amber-coloured stones at the hour positions and faceted baguette-style markers at 12, 6 and 9, with a white day-date window at 3 o'clock. The "T Swiss Made T" signature marks it as a 1970s tritium-era dial. Supplied in a wooden presentation box.

Brand heritage

Louis Rossel was a Swiss watch brand best known for its expressive 1970s designs, offering mechanical automatic and day-date models in gold-plated cases and colourful dials. Its watches are signed Swiss Made, and the bracelet clasp on this example carries the brand's "LR" monogram.
