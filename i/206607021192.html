About this watch

A vintage Zodiac Astrographic, one of the most recognisable designs of the space-age era. Its "mystery dial" has no markers at all: the hands are printed on transparent discs, so they appear to float over the matte black dial with nothing holding them in place. This example has silver baton hour and minute hands, a slim seconds hand with a red arrow tip, a framed date window, and is signed "Zodiac", "Astrographic Automatic" and "Swiss Made". The round 36mm stainless steel case has a black cabochon crown and a caseback stamped with the Zodiac logo, and the watch is fitted on a black leather rally-style strap with round holes.

Brand heritage

Zodiac was founded in Le Locle, Switzerland, in 1882 by Ariste Calame, and is best known for the Sea Wolf dive watch of 1953. The Astrographic was introduced in 1969, the year of the first Moon landing, as a tribute to the space age; its floating, disc-mounted hands made it one of the most distinctive watches of its time.
