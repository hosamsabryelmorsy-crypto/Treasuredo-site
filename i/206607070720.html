This vintage Favre-Leuba Genève dress watch is built around a striking mid-century design. A soft salmon dial with a fine minute track and slim gold-tone hands sits at the center, framed by an unusually wide brushed gold-tone bezel engraved with baton hour markers, so the dial appears to float inside a broad golden ring. Sharply faceted lugs complete a clean, architectural look that wears with real presence despite its understated dial.


Favre-Leuba, founded in 1737, is one of the oldest Swiss watchmaking houses.


Case & Movement:

- Case: Gold-tone round case with wide brushed bezel and faceted lugs, stainless steel snap-on caseback marked "Modèle Déposé"

- Movement: Swiss mechanical hand-wound (manual winding), 17 jewels, signed "Favre-Leuba Genève – Seventeen 17 Jewels – Swiss"

- Dial: Salmon, signed "Favre-Leuba Genève," with center seconds

- Strap: Black lizard-embossed leather strap (not original)

- Included: Favre-Leuba Genève presentation box (not confirmed original to this watch)


Condition:
