About this watch

A charming vintage Grovana "secret" cocktail watch from the 1970s. The small round watch head sits under a hinged, spring-loaded lid set with clear and red rhinestones: closed, it reads as a jewelled bangle; flip the lid open and the off-white dial appears, signed "Grovana", "17 Jewels" and "Swiss Made", with gold-tone Arabic numerals and gold-tone hands. The gold-tone case flows into a rigid fluted bangle set with small stones on the shoulders, and a fine safety chain is fitted for extra security.

Brand heritage

Grovana's roots go back to 1924, when the brothers Hans and Walter Gröflin founded a movement factory in Tenniken, near Basel, Switzerland. In 1970 Werner Bitterli took over the factory and gave it the Grovana name, launching the first Grovana watch collection the following year. The company is still family owned and still makes its watches in Tenniken.
