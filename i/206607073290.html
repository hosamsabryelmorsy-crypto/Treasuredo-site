About this watch

A vintage Swiss Nivada Automatic in a stainless steel case with a wide, polished bezel. The cream-white dial carries bold black Roman numerals, the black Nivada "N" shield logo, a framed date window at 3 o'clock and "Swiss Made" at the bottom. At 6 o'clock it has a light-blue shield emblem with the Arabic word "كاظمة" (Kazma) above a red flaming torch, a dial made for Kazma in Kuwait. It is worn on a steel bar-link bracelet and comes in a wooden presentation box.

Brand heritage

Nivada was founded in 1926 in Grenchen, in the Swiss Jura, and became known in the mid-20th century for robust automatic and sports watches such as the Antarctic, the Depthmaster and the Chronomaster. Nivada also supplied many Middle Eastern markets, where dials with local emblems and logos were ordered as presentation pieces.
