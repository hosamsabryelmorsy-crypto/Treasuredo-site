About this watch

A striking West End Watch Co. Sowar wearing the full colours of the Sultanate of Oman. The dial is printed with the Omani national flag set at a bold diagonal: a white field with black minute markers carrying the "West End Watch Co" and red script "Sowar" signatures, a red band bearing the white Khanjar national emblem, and a green field in the lower right. Gilt sword hands with luminous inserts and a slim red seconds hand complete the look. The stainless steel case has the classic Sowar shape with integrated lug ends and a screw-down caseback signed "West End Watch Co - Everbright - Automatic". Worn on an expanding stainless steel bracelet and supplied in a brown West End Watch Co. box signed "by Aubry of Switzerland".

Brand heritage

West End Watch Co. was founded in 1886 and built its name supplying robust Swiss watches to India, where its watches became a staple of the army and the railways. The Sowar ("cavalryman") is the brand's best-known model, registered as a sub-brand in 1917 and still produced today. In 1973 the company passed to the Aubry Brothers of Noirmont, Switzerland, which dates this "by Aubry of Switzerland" box to the 1970s or later. Thanks to long trading links between India and the Gulf, West End watches have been popular across the Arabian Gulf, and Omani-themed dials are sought after by collectors.
