About this watch

An elegant vintage dress watch from Giorgio Beverly Hills, Swiss made. It has a clean white dial with gold-tone Roman numerals at XII, III and IX, slim gold-tone leaf hands, a printed outer minute track and a small seconds sub-dial at 6 o'clock. The rounded gold-tone bezel sits on a stainless steel case with a hooded lug design that flows into the matching two-tone steel and gold-tone link bracelet. The screw-down caseback is engraved with the Giorgio crest, "Beverly Hills", "Water Resist. 3ATM. Swiss Watch" and number 930572.

Brand heritage

Giorgio Beverly Hills started as a boutique on Rodeo Drive in Beverly Hills, opened by Fred Hayman in 1961. It became one of the best-known names in Beverly Hills luxury and is famous for its yellow-and-white striped Giorgio fragrance, launched in 1981. Under the Giorgio crest the house also sold a line of Swiss made watches.
