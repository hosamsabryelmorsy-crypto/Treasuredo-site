About this watch

A slim vintage Swiss Edox dress watch from around the 1970s-1980s. The gold-coloured dial carries black Roman numerals, slim black hour and minute hands, a framed date window at 6 o'clock, the Edox name below 12 and "Swiss Made" at the bottom. The gold-tone case has tapered lugs and a contrasting steel-coloured fluted (coin-edge) bezel, and the crown is set with a dark blue cabochon. The steel caseback is stamped with the Edox hourglass emblem, "Dustproof" and the reference 410 5014. It is worn on a brown lizard-pattern leather strap with a gold-tone buckle and comes in a wooden presentation box.

Brand heritage

Edox was founded in 1884 by Christian Rüefli-Flury in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland. The name comes from the Greek word for "hour", and the brand's hourglass emblem, seen on this caseback, reflects it. Edox is best known for its water-resistant watches, most famously the Delfin of 1961 with its double O-ring caseback, and it is still an independent Swiss watchmaker today.
