About this watch

A charming vintage ladies' tank-style dress watch signed ORNATA, Swiss made. The rectangular gold-tone case with elongated side rails frames a striking Art Deco dial: a black diamond (rotated square) panel outlined in gold, set against vivid emerald-green corners under a domed crystal. Two slim gold hands and a gold "ORNATA" signature keep the design clean, and the gold crown is set with a black cabochon. Fitted with a black leather strap and presented in a wooden-look presentation box.

Brand heritage

Ornata is a small Swiss-made watch name that appeared on mid-century dress and pendant watches. Like many independent Swiss labels of the era, it produced fashion-led designs rather than mass-market models, which makes pieces like this uncommon today.
