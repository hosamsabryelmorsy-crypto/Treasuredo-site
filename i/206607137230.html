About this watch

A vintage presentation-style wristwatch made for King Khalid University Hospital (K.K.U.H.) of King Saud University in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. The black dial carries the King Saud University crest in gold (palm tree, crossed swords and open book) with "King Saud University" and its Arabic name around it, the founding year 1957, the hospital's name in Arabic calligraphy and "K.K.U.H." below. The same crest, text and "K.K.U.H." are engraved on the caseback and on the bracelet clasp. Octagonal steel case, gold two-hand dial with four gold square markers, on a matching steel mesh-style bracelet. The dial carries no watchmaker's name.

Brand heritage

King Saud University, founded in 1957 (1377 AH), is Saudi Arabia's first university. Its teaching hospital, King Khalid University Hospital in Riyadh, was inaugurated by King Khalid in 1982, in the university's 25th anniversary year. Watches with institutional Saudi crests were given as gifts and souvenirs to staff and are collected today as pieces of Saudi history.
