About this watch

A bold, fashion-forward ladies' watch from the Italian house Iceberg. The polished stainless steel rectangular case sits on sculpted H-shaped lugs that flow straight into a chunky open-link steel bracelet, giving the piece the look of a statement bracelet as much as a watch. The silver dial is covered with a glittering pavé-style texture that catches the light from every angle, finished simply with the ICEBERG signature, two slim hands and "Swiss" at the base. The bracelet clasp is signed ICEBERG.

Brand heritage

Iceberg is an Italian fashion house founded in 1974 by Silvano Gerani and Giuliana Marchini, famous for its playful, pop-art inspired knitwear and confident Italian style. Its watches carry the same spirit: design-led pieces with strong shapes and a touch of glamour.
