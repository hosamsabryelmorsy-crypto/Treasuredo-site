About this watch

A slim and elegant vintage dress watch from Catena, Switzerland. The brushed silver dial carries a ribbed gold-tone inner ring, twelve applied gold dot markers and a pair of gold-tone hands, signed "CATENA", "QUARTZ" and "SWISS MADE". The round 26mm steel case wears a polished gold-tone bezel and gold crown, and flows into a two-tone integrated link bracelet with hinged lugs and gold-tone accents. The caseback is engraved "D-886-M", "3 ATU", "Modele Depose" and "Swiss". Presented in a wooden-style watch box.

Brand heritage

Catena is a Swiss watch brand. In the 1970s and 1980s it was known for accessible, Swiss-made dress watches with clean, design-led dials, and the name continues today with Swiss-made mechanical watches.
