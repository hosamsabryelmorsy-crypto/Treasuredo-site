About this watch

A classic piece of 1970s Swiss design: the Edox Bluebird 28800. The big polished cushion-shaped steel case frames a brown-to-black gradient sunburst dial with a silver segmented chapter ring and applied baton markers. At 3 o'clock is a framed day-date window with the day in Arabic script and the date on a red disc. The dial is signed "EDOX automatic - bluebird 28800 - T Swiss Made T" with the Edox hourglass logo. "28800" refers to the high-beat 28,800 vibrations-per-hour automatic movement. It comes on its steel multi-link bracelet with an Edox hourglass-logo clasp.

Brand heritage

Edox was founded in 1884 in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland, by Christian Rüefli-Flury. Its name comes from the Greek word for "hour", and its logo is a stylised hourglass. Edox is known for robust, water-resistant sports watches like the Delfin and Hydro-Sub. In the 1970s the Bluebird line paired bold, sculpted cases with fast-beating automatic movements.
