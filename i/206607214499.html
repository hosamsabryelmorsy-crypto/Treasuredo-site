About this watch

A charming 1970s Zodiac automatic for ladies. The dark blue dial carries the Zodiac crosshair emblem and signature, "AUTOMATIC" and "SWISS MADE", with applied silver baton markers, silver baton hands and a framed date window at 3 o'clock. The round 28mm stainless steel case has a wide brushed bezel and a crown at 4 o'clock, and the snap caseback is engraved with the Zodiac name and logo, "Automatic", "Water", "Shockresistant", "Antimagnetic", "Swiss" and Zodiac's dolphin emblem. It is worn on a stainless steel link bracelet with an NSA Swiss-made "Steel Inox" fold-over clasp. Presented in a wooden watch box.

Brand heritage

Zodiac traces its roots to 1882, when Ariste Calame founded his watchmaking business in Le Locle, Switzerland; the Zodiac name was registered in 1908. The brand is best known for the 1953 Sea Wolf, one of the first purpose-built dive watches, and for its 1960s and 1970s automatic sports and dress watches such as the Astrographic and Olympos.
