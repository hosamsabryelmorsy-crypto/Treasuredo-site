About this watch

A vintage Rado Florence dress watch with a striking architectural design: a gold-tone case framed by a black 12-sided bezel and a polished gold-tone inner ring. The champagne-gold dial has a diagonal ribbed (striped) pattern, signed "RADO QUARTZ" and "FLORENCE", with set stone markers at 12 (double), 3, 6 and 9 and slim dark hands. Fitted with a black leather strap with pin buckle and supplied with a Rado presentation box.

Brand heritage

Rado was founded in 1917 in Lengnau, Switzerland, by the Schlup brothers, and became known for scratch-resistant materials such as hardmetal and high-tech ceramic. The Florence line is one of Rado's long-running classic dress collections, still in the catalogue today.
