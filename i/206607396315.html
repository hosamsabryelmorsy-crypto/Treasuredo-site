About this watch

A bold piece of 1970s Swiss design from Omax. The black dial is crossed by a cream horizontal band carrying the Omax logo and "17 Jewels", with printed 13-24 hour numerals around the dial, a grey minute track, skeleton-tipped baton hands and a red date at 3 o'clock. It sits in a wide cushion/tonneau case and comes on its original-style steel bracelet with red inlays at the lugs, a true space-age look. Supplied in an Omax "Since 1946" presentation box.

Brand heritage

Omax was founded in Switzerland in 1946. Through the 1960s and 1970s the brand built a strong following for its Swiss mechanical watches, which were exported widely to the Middle East and the Indian subcontinent.
