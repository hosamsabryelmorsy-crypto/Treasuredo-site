About this watch

A colourful Gulf commemorative piece from West End Watch Co. The green dial carries the full State of Qatar coat of arms: a white ring with the Arabic inscription "Dawlat Qatar", an olive centre showing a dhow under sail, a palm tree and two crossed curved swords, and a maroon band lettered "STATE OF QATAR". The dial is signed "West End Watch Co" at the upper left, with white baton markers, a date window at 3 o'clock, dark leaf-shaped hands and a slim gilt seconds hand. The compact 29mm steel case has a knurled crown and a snap-on caseback stamped with the West End Watch Co. "Everbright" seal, "Swiss Made" and the numbers D 3152 / 1692. Worn on an expanding steel bracelet and supplied in a brown West End Watch Co. box signed "by Aubry of Switzerland".

Brand heritage

West End Watch Co. was founded in 1886 and built its name supplying robust Swiss watches to India, where its watches became a staple of the army and the railways; its Sowar model is still produced today. In 1973 the company passed to the Aubry Brothers of Noirmont, Switzerland, which dates this "by Aubry of Switzerland" box to the 1970s or later. Thanks to long trading links between India and the Gulf, West End watches have been popular across the Arabian Gulf, and dials bearing Gulf state emblems are sought after by collectors.
