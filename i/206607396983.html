About this watch

A genuine 1970s Candino from the height of the space-age design era. The faceted, cushion-shaped "TV" steel case frames a silver-grey dial signed with the Candino logo and name, with a recessed dark blue sparkle centre carrying the script "Galaxy" signature, applied stone-set and gilt markers, gilt baton hands and a framed date window at 3 o'clock. Worn on a black leather strap with gold-tone buckle and supplied in a Candino Swiss Watch presentation box.

Brand heritage

Candino is a Swiss watch brand founded in 1947 and registered under the Candino name in 1953. In the 1960s and 1970s it produced affordable Swiss-made mechanical watches with bold, fashionable designs, including the angular "TV" and space-age cases that collectors look for today.
