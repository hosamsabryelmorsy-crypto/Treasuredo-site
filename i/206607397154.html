About this watch

A slim 1960s Swiss dress watch by Camy. The round case carries a wide stepped bezel with a textured (florentine-finish) inner ring framing a silver-champagne dial decorated with fine vertical lines, applied faceted baton markers at 12, 3, 6 and 9, and slim silver hands. The dial is signed "Camy 17 Jewels", "Incabloc" and "Swiss Made". The angular wire-style lugs and gold-tone crown give it a distinctive mid-century design. Fitted on a black leather strap with pin buckle.

Brand heritage

Camy Watch Co. SA was a Geneva-based Swiss watch company founded in 1917. From 1949 to 1975 the firm was managed by Raymond Weil, before he founded his own brand, and by the mid-1960s Camy was producing around 1.5 million watches a year.
