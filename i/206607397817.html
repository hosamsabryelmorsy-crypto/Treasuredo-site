About marcasite jewellery

Marcasite jewellery uses small faceted stones (in fact cut pyrite) set in silver to give a soft, antique sparkle. The style was popular in Georgian and Victorian times and again in the Art Deco era of the 1920s and 1930s, and marcasite set silver watches remain a classic, feminine look today.
