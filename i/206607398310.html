About this watch

An elegant Art Deco style ladies' bracelet watch in 925 sterling silver, richly set with faceted marcasite stones. The 29 mm case frames a small round white dial with black Arabic numerals, a minute track, black hands and a diamond emblem above the "Quartz" signature. The case flows into a matching sterling silver link bracelet, also set with marcasites, finished with a fold-over clasp. It comes in a vintage presentation box.
