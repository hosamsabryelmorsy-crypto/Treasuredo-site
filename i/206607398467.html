About this watch

A classic vintage Favre-Leuba Sea King dress watch from the brand's Geneva era. The silver sunburst dial carries applied gold Arabic numerals, gold dot minute markers, gold baton hands and a slim gold seconds hand, signed "Favre-Leuba Geneve" with the crest logo, "Sea King" and "Swiss Made". The round gold-plated case has a polished bezel, angular lugs and a signed stainless steel snap caseback engraved "Water-Resistant" and "Stainless Steel Back" (case number 4510-51). Supplied on a burgundy brown leather strap with a gold-tone buckle, in a presentation box.

Brand heritage

Favre-Leuba traces its origins to 1737, when Abraham Favre was registered as a watchmaker in Le Locle, making it one of the oldest Swiss watch brands. The company moved to Geneva in 1896. In the mid-1950s it introduced its own calibres, used in mid-century lines such as the Sea Chief, Sea Raider and the Sea King, a robust everyday watch with a water-resistant case.
