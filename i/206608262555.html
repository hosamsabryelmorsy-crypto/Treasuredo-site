About this watch

A vintage Swiss-made Radstar quartz watch, reference 9525, from the quartz era of the late 1970s to 1980s. The black dial carries a gold double-line octagonal track, applied-style gold Roman numerals at XII, IX and VI, gold sword hands and a gold-framed date window at 3 o'clock, signed "Radstar", "Quartz" and "Swiss Made". It sits in a 35mm tonneau-shaped case on an integrated bar-link bracelet stamped "Stainless Steel", and comes in a Radstar Swiss presentation box.

Brand heritage

Radstar is a small, little-documented Swiss-made watch label of the 1970s and 1980s. This example is signed "Swiss Made" on both the dial and the caseback.
