About this watch

A vintage Westar Swiss quartz triple-calendar with moonphase, case reference 8858 engraved on the caseback. The white dial carries three champagne sub-dials for month (12 o'clock), day (3 o'clock) and date (9 o'clock), and a midnight-blue moonphase disc with gold moon and stars at 6 o'clock. The hour markers are applied gold-tone Roman numerals and batons, matched by slim gold-tone hands. It has a sculpted stainless steel case with a gold-tone crown on a matching integrated steel bracelet with gold-tone arch accents on each link. It comes in its original Westar presentation box and outer box.

Brand heritage

Westar was launched in the early 1980s by the Al-Futtaim group as a Swiss-made fashion watch brand. It became well known across the Middle East and is now sold in more than 45 countries.
