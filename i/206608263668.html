About this watch

The Invicta Speedway ref. 9224 is one of the brand's best-known racing-inspired chronographs. This is the earlier "Professional 200M" dial version: a black textured dial with three gold-ringed chronograph subdials (minutes, 24-hour, seconds), applied luminous baton markers, luminous hands and a date window at 4:30. It sits under a gold-tone tachymeter bezel ("Units per Hour"), with screw-down gold-tone crown flanked by gold-tone pushers, on a two-tone stainless steel bracelet with a signed fold-over safety clasp. Supplied in its yellow Invicta box with the warranty booklet and card.

Brand heritage

Invicta was founded in 1837 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, by Raphael Picard; the name means "invincible" in Latin. Today the brand is run by Invicta Watch Group, headquartered in Florida, USA, and is known for bold, well-equipped watches at accessible prices. The Speedway line, launched in the 1990s, is its classic motorsport-styled chronograph collection.
