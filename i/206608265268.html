About this watch

An Aigner Genua Due, reference A31600, with the brand's signature horseshoe-shaped case. The dark mother-of-pearl dial shimmers in blue and purple tones and carries the silver Aigner horseshoe "A" logo at 12, two applied silver dot markers at 3 and 9, black hands and the script "Etienne Aigner" signature above "Swiss Made". The glossy black case and link bracelet have polished steel accents and close with a stainless steel butterfly deployant clasp. The caseback is decorated with the Aigner "A" pattern and engraved "Aigner Genua Due, Swiss Made, All Stainless Steel, 3ATM Water Resistant, A31600". Supplied in an Aigner box.

Brand heritage

Aigner was founded in 1965 by the Hungarian-born designer Etienne Aigner and is based in Munich, Germany. The brand is known for its leather goods and its horseshoe "A" emblem, which also shapes the case of its Genua watches.
