About this watch

A sporty diver-style Bernhard H. Mayer Force in stainless steel. The black dial has a fine pyramid-pattern texture with large luminous baton hour markers, a white minute track, a framed date window at 3 o'clock and the "FORCE" name above 6. The luminous hands include a distinctive arrow-shaped hour hand. Around the dial is a coin-edge bezel with a black insert and red 60-minute scale. The case has a signed crown with the Bernhard H. Mayer logo, and the caseback is engraved "Bernhard H. Mayer, Stainless Steel, Sapphire Crystal, 41701.644.6, Swiss Made, Water Resistant 5 ATM". It is fitted on a stainless steel link bracelet with a signed fold-over clasp with push-button release, and comes in a Bernhard H. Mayer box with a care leaflet and polishing cloth.

Brand heritage

Bernhard H. Mayer dates its history to 1871, when the company was founded in Pforzheim, Germany, by a metal engraver; "Depuis 1871" is printed on the dial. Today the brand makes Swiss-made watches.
