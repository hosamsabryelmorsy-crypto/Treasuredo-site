About this watch

A collectible 1970s Gulf sporting watch. The champagne dial carries the green-and-gold emblem of Al-Arabi Sporting Club with the Arabic inscription "النادي العربي الرياضي الكويت" (Al-Arabi Sporting Club, Kuwait), signed Tressa and Swiss Made, with baton markers and gold-tone hands.

The slim gold-plated round case measures about 35mm excluding the crown and has a steel snap back marked "Tressa Swiss Antimagnetic Stainless Steel Back". Inside is a 17-jewel Swiss manual-wind movement, shown in the photos. It is fitted on a dark brown leather strap.

Brand heritage

Tressa was a Swiss watch brand active from roughly the early 1960s to the early 1970s and was part of Zeno Watches. It specialised in the Arab markets, with colourful dials and period cases, and disappeared during the quartz crisis.

Al-Arabi Sporting Club is one of Kuwait's oldest and most successful sports clubs, known for its green colours.
