About this watch

The cream dial is signed "BONORA De Luxe, Ancre 17 Rubis" and "Antimagnetic", with stylised black Arabic numerals at the even hours, long baton markers, small red and green accent dots on the outer track, slim gold-tone hands and a small seconds sub-dial at 6 o'clock.

The round stainless-steel case measures approximately 33mm excluding crown and 12mm thick, with a screw-down back marked "Antimagnetic, Stainless Steel Back, Waterprotected". It houses a 17-jewel lever (ancre) manual-wind movement and is fitted with a black leather strap.

Brand heritage

Bonora is a little-documented brand. The French "Ancre 17 Rubis" wording on the dial reflects the French-speaking watch industry of the Franco-Swiss Jura, centred on Besançon and the neighbouring Swiss watchmaking towns, where many small houses made charming everyday mechanical watches in the 1950s and 1960s.
