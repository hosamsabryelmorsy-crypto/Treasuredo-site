About this watch

A classic Japan-made Seiko 5 automatic in a compact 34 mm stainless steel case. The deep navy blue dial has applied silver Arabic numerals at 12, 6 and 9, polished applied baton markers, a white minute track, the applied "SEIKO" name and the "5" shield, and a framed day-date window at 3 o'clock. It has silver-tone sword-style hands with a red-tipped seconds hand, and "Made in Japan" and the dial code 7S26-2272 are printed at 6. Inside is Seiko's self-winding calibre 7S26 with 21 jewels. The caseback is engraved "Seiko, Water Resistant, Made in Japan, ST. Steel, 7S26-0400" and the serial number. It is fitted on a Seiko stainless steel multi-link bracelet with a signed fold-over clasp and comes in a Seiko box.

Brand heritage

Seiko traces its history to 1881, when Kintaro Hattori opened his shop in Tokyo, and it is one of the few watchmakers that makes its own movements. The Seiko 5 line, launched in the 1960s, was built around five features: automatic winding, a day-date display, water resistance, a durable case and a durable bracelet. The calibre 7S26, introduced in the mid-1990s, became one of the best known Seiko automatic movements.
