About this watch

A rare piece of 20th-century Middle Eastern history: a ladies' Eterna-Matic KonTiki automatic whose silver dial carries a printed colour portrait of Iraqi president Saddam Hussein in a blue suit. Watches bearing his portrait were supplied to Iraq by Swiss and Japanese makers as commemorative and presentation pieces. This one has a cushion-shaped two-tone case with a gold-tone fluted bezel, gold-tone baton markers and hands, the Eterna five-dot logo, "ETERNA-MATIC" and "KonTiki" signatures, "SWISS" at 6 o'clock and a day-date window at 3 o'clock (Spanish day disc). It is fitted on a two-tone steel and gold-tone jubilee-style bracelet and comes in an Eterna presentation box.

Brand heritage

Eterna was founded in 1856 in Grenchen, Switzerland, by Josef Girard and Urs Schild. In 1948 it introduced the Eterna-Matic, whose ball-bearing-mounted rotor inspired the five-dot logo still used today. Eterna watches accompanied Thor Heyerdahl on the 1947 Kon-Tiki raft expedition, which gave the KonTiki line its name, and the movement maker ETA was originally Eterna's own movement division.
