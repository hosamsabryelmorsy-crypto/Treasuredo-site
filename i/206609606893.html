About this watch

A bold 1970s Octo automatic. The brown-to-black gradient dial is signed "Octo", "25 Jewels", "Incabloc Automatic" and "Swiss Made", with applied gold-tone batons, gold-tone hands and a framed day-date window at 3 o'clock.

The oval stainless-steel case is framed by a wide gold-tone bezel, measures about 38mm excluding the crown and has a screw-down back. The 25-jewel Swiss automatic movement is shown in the photos. It comes on a stainless steel bracelet with a signed Octo clasp.

Brand heritage

Octo was a Swiss watch brand from La Chaux-de-Fonds, documented from at least 1904. It was best known for its 8-day movements (hence the name), later became Montres Octo SA and relocated to Bienne during the Second World War. In the 1960s Octo also produced the SkyNavy, a watch-compass hybrid.
