Brand heritage

Omega's story began in 1848, when Louis Brandt opened a watch assembly workshop in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland. The company moved to Biel/Bienne in 1880, adopted the Omega name in 1903, and became famous for precision, Olympic timekeeping and the Speedmaster worn on the Moon in 1969.

The Dynamic family was launched in 1968. Designed around the way a watch actually sits on the wrist, it combined an elliptical case with a round dial, a monocoque construction and easily interchangeable straps, and it became one of the most recognizable Omega designs of the era.
