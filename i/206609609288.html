About this watch

A clean, minimalist white dial with the Garuda Pancasila emblem in gold and colour, slim gold-tone hands and the inscription "Istana Presiden Republik Indonesia" (Presidential Palace of the Republic of Indonesia) along the right-hand edge.

The stainless-steel caseback is engraved with the Garuda emblem and the words "Kenang-Kenangan Dari Presiden Republik Indonesia" (a souvenir from the President of the Republic of Indonesia). The slim, round gold-tone case with a faceted bezel houses a quartz movement and is fitted to a dark brown leather strap, and the watch comes with its presentation box.

Brand heritage

The Garuda Pancasila, a golden mythical eagle bearing a shield with the five principles of Pancasila and the motto "Bhinneka Tunggal Ika" (Unity in Diversity), was designed by Sultan Hamid II under President Sukarno's supervision and adopted as Indonesia's national emblem on 11 February 1950. Watches engraved as gifts from the President are collected today as presidential and diplomatic memorabilia.
