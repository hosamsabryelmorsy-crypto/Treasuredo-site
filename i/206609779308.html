About this watch

A 1970s Longines Automatic with a distinctive design: a round, stepped polished bezel set into a brushed stainless steel case with wide integrated shoulders that flow into the strap. The silver dial has a fine linen texture with thin black-lined baton markers, black baton hands, a central seconds hand and a framed date window at 6 o'clock. It is signed with the Longines winged hourglass logo under 12, "Automatic" above the date and "T Swiss Made T" at the bottom edge. Inside is the Longines automatic calibre L633.1 with 25 jewels and a signed Longines rotor. The inside of the caseback is stamped "Longines Swiss Made 4818" together with "633", matching the calibre. It comes on a black croc-grain leather strap and with a Longines box.

Brand heritage

Longines was founded in 1832 in Saint-Imier, Switzerland, and its winged hourglass logo is one of the oldest registered trademarks still in use. In the 1970s Longines built many of its automatic models around the calibre L633 family, a 25-jewel automatic with date.
