About this item

A refined pair of men's rimless sunglasses by FRED Paris. The lenses float on a slim gold-tone bridge with square end caps, and the gold-tone hinge blocks carry the FRED name. Each temple is set with dark brown twisted marine-cable inlays, the nautical cable motif FRED uses throughout its jewellery and eyewear, and ends in dark brown temple tips fixed with small slotted screws. Blue-grey tinted lenses give a cool, understated look.

Brand heritage

FRED was founded in Paris in 1936 by jeweller Fred Samuel. Its best-known design is the Force 10 marine cable, created in 1966, and FRED launched its first eyewear collection, Force 10, in 1988, bringing the cable motif to frames and sunglasses.
