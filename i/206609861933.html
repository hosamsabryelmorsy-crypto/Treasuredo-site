About this item

A pair of vintage S.T. Dupont rimless sunglasses, made in Austria by Estede, the licensed maker of S.T. Dupont eyewear. The 23 KT gold-plated frame has a finely engraved, textured bridge and lens end pieces, and each temple carries a row of diagonal brown tortoise-effect lacquer inlays next to the engraved script "S.T. Dupont" signature. The black temple tips are marked with the gold script Dupont "D". The oval-rectangular brown tinted lenses suit both men and women.

Brand heritage

S.T. Dupont was founded in Paris in 1872 and is known for its luxury lighters, pens and leather goods, and for its Chinese lacquer work. Its vintage eyewear was produced under licence by Estede in Austria, and frames were often finished in 23 KT gold plating with lacquered details, like this pair.
