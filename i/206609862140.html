About this item

A pair of vintage Christian Dior butane pocket lighters offered together as one set, presented in a grey Christian Dior box. The first lighter has a vertically fluted silver-tone body with a ribbed gold-tone collar and gold-tone "Swiss Made" insert; its base is signed "Christian Dior Paris" and stamped "PLAQUE OR G". The second lighter is engraved all over with the Christian Dior "CD" monogram on a finely lined silver-tone ground, with a ribbed silver-tone collar and gold-tone insert; its base is signed "Christian Dior Paris" with a maker's mark. Both have the classic flip-top lid and thumb-wheel flint ignition.

Brand heritage

Christian Dior founded his Paris couture house in 1946. From the 1970s and 1980s the house licensed a range of men's accessories, including Swiss-made gas lighters signed "Christian Dior Paris", decorated with fluted (godron) finishes and the CD monogram that became the brand's signature motif.
