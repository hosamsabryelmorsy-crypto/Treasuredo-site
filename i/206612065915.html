This vintage Eterna-Matic pairs a classic 1970s cushion-shaped steel case with a rich blue sunburst dial that deepens toward the edges and lightens at the center as it catches the light. Applied baton markers, slim steel hands, the Eterna five-dot emblem and a framed date window at 3 o'clock keep the layout clean and balanced. The case combines brushed flanks with polished bevels for a sharp, period look on the wrist.


Inside is an automatic movement stamped 2824, with the rotor signed "Eterna-Matic 25 Jewels." ETA, maker of the 2824 caliber, began as Eterna's own movement division, which gives these watches a direct link to one of Switzerland's great movement makers.


Case & Movement:

- Case: Stainless steel, cushion shape, 34mm, brushed and polished finish

- Movement: Swiss automatic, 25 jewels, caliber 2824, rotor signed "Eterna-Matic 25 Jewels"

- Caseback: Stainless steel, inner side stamped "Eterna Watch Co – Swiss – Stainless Steel" and "633 2013 41"

- Dial: Blue sunburst, signed "Eterna-Matic," with date window

- Strap: Dark blue crocodile-embossed leather strap, Eterna-signed on the underside, with Eterna-logo steel buckle

- Included: Eterna presentation box (not confirmed original to this watch)


Condition:

Genuine vintage piece in pre-owned condition consistent with its age. The blue dial presents well, with a few small spots on the left side near the Eterna emblem. The case shows scratches and wear from use, and the inner case ring around the movement shows some dark marks. The box shows wear on the corners. The watch is shown running in the photos, but no service history is available, so it is sold as-is; current timekeeping accuracy is not guaranteed.
