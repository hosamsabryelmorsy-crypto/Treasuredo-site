A bold 1970s-style Swiss automatic from Sandoz of Switzerland, with a sculpted all-stainless-steel cushion case that has real wrist presence.


The dial is the highlight: a brushed silver finish that shifts with the light, applied polished baton markers with doubled black-filled markers at the cardinal positions, small lume dots, gold-tone lumed hands and a red-tipped centre seconds hand. A date window at 3 o'clock and the "Automatic 25 Jewels" signature complete a clean, confident design.


Details:

- Brand: Sandoz

- Movement: Swiss automatic, 25 jewels, Incabloc

- Case: All stainless steel cushion case, approx. 36 mm

- Case back: Screw-down, signed Sandoz with crest, marked "All Stainless Steel", "Automatic", "Tropicalized", "Incabloc", "Waterresistant", "Antimagnetic", "Swiss Made", and "1782 Z-84-8"

- Dial: Brushed silver with applied markers and date at 3

- Strap: Black leather strap (not original)


Condition:

Pre-owned vintage condition with wear consistent with age. The crystal shows scratches and scuffs, and the polished case has light scratches from use. No water resistance is implied despite the case back marking. As with all vintage automatic watches, accuracy may vary. Please view all photos carefully, as they form part of the description.
