A charming mid-century Swiss dress watch from Cauny Prima, the brand best known for its popularity across Spain and Portugal, in its "Calendario" date version.


The dial is the highlight: a silvered face with raised gold-tone Arabic numerals in a distinctive stylised font, a date window at 3 o'clock with red numerals, and an offset small seconds sub-dial. Slim gold-tone hands and the script "Calendario" signature complete a warm, characterful vintage design.


Details:

- Brand: Cauny Prima

- Model: Calendario

- Movement: Swiss mechanical, manual wind, 15 jewels ("Ancre 15 Rubis"), antimagnetic

- Case: 10-micron gold-plated bezel and case with stainless steel back, approx. 37 mm

- Case back marked: Cauny Prima fleur-de-lis logo, "De Luxe", "Lunette-Carrure Plaqué Or G. 10 Microns", "Swiss Made", "Fond Acier Inoxydable", "93-1071", "376"

- Dial: Silvered with applied gold-tone Arabic numerals, date at 3 and small seconds

- Strap: Red croc-embossed leather strap (not original)


Condition:

Pre-owned vintage condition with wear consistent with age. The dial shows noticeable spotting and patina across its surface, including around the numerals. The gold plating shows light wear from use.
