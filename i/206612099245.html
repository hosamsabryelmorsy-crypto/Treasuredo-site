A handsome vintage Swiss automatic from Mido's celebrated Ocean Star line, in its self-winding "Powerwind" version with date.


The dial is the highlight: a silver sunburst finish with clusters of four small sparkling stone-set markers at 12, 9 and 6, a date window at 3 o'clock, and the script Mido signature with "Ocean Star" and "Powerwind". The stones have not been tested, and the dial and hands are not confirmed as factory original. Silver-tone hands and a centre seconds hand keep the design clean and elegant.


Details:

- Brand: Mido

- Model: Ocean Star Powerwind

- Movement: Swiss automatic (self-winding)

- Complications: Date at 3

- Case: Stainless steel, approx. 35 mm

- Case back marked: "Mido Ocean Star" with star logo, "Shockresistant", "Selfwinding", "Waterproof", "Switzerland", "Stainless Steel", 27063007

- Dial: Silver sunburst with stone-set markers

- Strap: Brown croc-embossed leather strap (not original)


Condition:

Pre-owned vintage condition with visible wear consistent with age. The dial shows noticeable patina, spotting and wear, particularly towards the centre and outer edge. The hour hand shows surface wear. The case back shows heavy wear and scratches, with some markings faded. No current water resistance is implied.
