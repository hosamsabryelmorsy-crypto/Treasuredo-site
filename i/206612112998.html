A characterful mid-century dress watch from Olma, with a generous 37 mm case and a heavily patinated dial full of vintage charm.


The dial shows raised gold-tone Arabic numerals, a small seconds sub-dial at 6 o'clock and gold-tone hands, under a domed crystal with a decorative scalloped inner edge. Long, tapered lugs give the case an elegant profile.


Please note: the dial carries an Arabic-script logo reading "كوكا كولا" (Coca-Cola). The logo appears noticeably crisper and darker than the faded Olma signature and the surrounding patina, and it is likely a later addition rather than a factory print. This is not presented as an official Coca-Cola product.


Details:

- Brand: Olma

- Movement: Mechanical, manual wind

- Case: Gold-tone round case, approx. 37 mm

- Dial: Patinated silver-tone dial with gold-tone Arabic numerals and small seconds

- Crystal: Domed, with decorative scalloped edge

- Strap: Dark brown textured leather strap (not original)


Condition:

Pre-owned vintage condition with heavy wear consistent with age. The dial shows strong patina and spotting, and the Olma signature is heavily faded. The case shows wear, with some tone difference on the lugs. No water resistance is implied. As with all vintage mechanical watches, accuracy may vary.
