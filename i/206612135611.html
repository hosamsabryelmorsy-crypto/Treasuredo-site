A miniature tapestry of light, ornament and Parisian elegance.


The dial is the defining beauty of this Pierre Balmain. Delicate arabesque leaves and curling tendrils unfold across a luminous silver-toned centre, revealing soft pearlescent flashes as the light changes. Warm gold-tone ornament contrasts with the dark olive-toned Roman numeral chapter ring, giving the composition both richness and clarity.


The effect recalls fine metallic embroidery on silk—a fitting connection to Balmain’s couture heritage. The arabesque motif became a signature of the watch brand, inspired by the elaborate embroidery of Pierre Balmain’s fashion creations.


An engraved “PIERRE BALMAIN / PARIS” bezel frames the dial, while the sculptural, interlocking bracelet carries its decorative character around the wrist. At 33mm, this is an elegant men’s dress watch with restrained proportions: visually distinctive without relying on an oversized case.
