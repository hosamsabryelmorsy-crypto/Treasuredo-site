Vintage Mido Multifort Grand Luxe Super-Automatic, a characterful Swiss wristwatch with compact 28mm proportions and a distinctive aged black dial.


Estimated to date from the 1940s, this example features outlined Arabic numerals, gold-tone hands and central sweep seconds beneath a prominently domed crystal. The dial carries the Mido signature, Multifort Grand Luxe designation and curved Super-Automatic inscription.


Inside is the signed Mido calibre 817, a 17-jewel bumper automatic movement. Its winding weight travels through a limited arc between spring stops, representing an earlier generation of Swiss self-winding watchmaking.


Specifications:

• Brand: Mido

• Model: Multifort Grand Luxe Super-Automatic

• Estimated period: Circa 1940s

• Country of manufacture: Switzerland

• Case diameter: 28mm

• Case: Stainless steel

• Movement: Mechanical bumper automatic

• Calibre: 817

• Jewels: 17

• Dial: Black with pronounced mottled patina

• Numerals: Arabic

• Functions: Hours, minutes and central seconds

• Strap: Black textured leather


Condition:

Visible age-related wear includes scratches to the case and caseback, marks on the domed crystal, and substantial dial patina. The hour and minute hands appear to have lost their luminous infill. The leather strap shows creasing and wear. Please examine the original close-up photographs for the watch’s cosmetic condition.
