Vintage 1960s Invicta Men's Dress Watch – Swiss Manual Wind 17 Jewels, Silver Sunburst Dial, 33mm Steel Case

The Story Behind the Brand
Invicta traces its origins to 1837, when Raphael Picard founded the company in La Chaux-de-Fonds, the heart of Swiss watchmaking. The name means "invincible" in Latin. For more than a century, Invicta produced reliable Swiss mechanical watches that were sold around the world, and its mid-century dress watches are appreciated today for their clean design and dependable movements.

About This Piece
This 1960s Invicta features a silver sunburst dial with applied baton markers and Arabic numerals at 12, 3, 6 and 9, the Invicta logo and slim dauphine-style hands. The round stainless steel case has a screw-down back and houses a Swiss manual-wind movement with 17 jewels. It is fitted to a black leather strap.
