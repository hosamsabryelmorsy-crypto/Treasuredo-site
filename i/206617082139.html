About this watch

A vintage Waltham automatic with a strong 1970s look: a gold-tone case with sweeping, horn-shaped lugs and a fluted (coin-edge) bezel framing a silver-blue brushed dial. The dial has applied gold-tone double baton markers at 12, 3 (date), 6 and 9, gold-tone hour markers on a dark chapter ring, gold-tone dauphine-style hands and a slim central seconds hand. It is signed with the red Waltham crown logo, "Waltham 17 Jewels" and "Self-Winding Shockresistant", with a date window at 3 o'clock. Inside is a Waltham Watch Co. automatic movement signed "Seventeen 17 Jewels, Waltham Watch Co., Unadjusted, Japan" with the calibre mark S-36C. The steel caseback is signed Waltham, "Antimagnetic, Shockresistant, Waterproof" and "Base metal bezel, St. steel back"; inside it is stamped "Case Hong Kong" with case number B-278B. It is fitted with a gold-tone Speidel expanding bracelet.

Brand heritage

The Waltham Watch Company, based in Waltham, Massachusetts, was one of the great American watchmakers of the 19th and 20th centuries and a pioneer of making watches with interchangeable, machine-made parts. After American production ended, the Waltham name continued on wristwatches made with movements and cases sourced abroad, such as this self-winding model with a Japanese movement and a Hong Kong case.
