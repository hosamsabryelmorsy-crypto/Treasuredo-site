About this watch

A classic Art Deco style Longines dress watch from around 1950. The rectangular 10K yellow gold-filled case has stepped, hooded lugs and a gently curved profile, and the dial sits under a high dome crystal that gives the watch its vintage look from every angle. The silver-cream dial has an applied gold 12, gold diamond and arrowhead markers, the Longines name with its winged hourglass logo, gold dauphine-style hands and a square small seconds subdial at 6. Inside is the Swiss Longines calibre 23Z, a 17-jewel manual-wind movement. The case was made for the US market by Longines-Wittnauer Watch Co., New York. The watch comes with its original red Longines presentation box and a Longines-signed gold-tone buckle. Art Deco Longines watches with a high dome crystal, hooded lugs and their original Longines box are hard to find complete today, which makes this a rare chance for a collector of 1940s-50s Longines.

Brand heritage

Longines was founded in 1832 in Saint-Imier, Switzerland. Its winged hourglass logo was registered in 1889 and is one of the oldest registered trademarks still in use. In the United States, Longines watches were imported and sold by the Longines-Wittnauer Watch Co. of New York. Many American-market Longines of the 1940s and 1950s, like this one, combine a Swiss Longines movement with a 10K gold-filled case made in the USA.
