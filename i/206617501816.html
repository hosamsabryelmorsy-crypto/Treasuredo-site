About this watch

A 1990s Seiko analog-digital ("ana-digi") world timer, reference H021-8040, made in Japan in July 1996 (serial 670808). It combines classic hands with three LCD windows on a cream dial: the time in up to 28 world cities, a stopwatch timing to 1/1000 of a second, and dual alarms. The steel case has gold-tone accents on the flanks, four gold-tipped pushers, a fixed bezel printed with world city codes and a tachymeter scale on the dial edge. The blue-filled, gold-framed baton markers, blue and gold hands and the red aeroplane-tipped seconds hand give it a strong 90s aviation look. It comes in a grey Seiko capsule-style presentation case.

Brand heritage

Seiko has led quartz and digital watchmaking since it launched the world's first quartz wristwatch, the Astron, in 1969, and its early LCD and ana-digi models of the 1970s to 1990s are now popular collector pieces. The H021 calibre was one of Seiko's multi-function ana-digi movements of the 1990s, with world time, a 1/1000 second chronograph and a two-channel alarm.
