About this watch

A vintage Citizen Crystal Seven automatic from 1969 (serial 90714413, case number 61-5013). The black dial carries applied rectangular silver markers, polished baton hands, the "CITIZEN Crystal Seven 21 Jewels" signature, a day window at 9 o'clock and a framed date window at 3 o'clock. The large round stainless steel case has a wide polished bezel that sweeps out over the lugs, with the crown at 3 o'clock, giving the watch a bold late-60s space-age look on the wrist. Inside is Citizen's 21-jewel automatic day-date calibre with a black "CITIZEN 21 JEWELS" rotor. It comes on a steel multi-link bracelet with a Citizen-signed fold-over clasp, with a Citizen box and guarantee booklet.

Brand heritage

Citizen has made watches in Tokyo since 1924, when its first pocket watch bearing the Citizen name was produced. The Crystal Seven line, launched in 1965, was one of Citizen's key automatic collections of the era: a 21-jewel self-winding movement with day and date, offered in many case designs through the late 1960s and 1970s. Citizen later revived the name with its modern C7 automatic series, a sign of how important the original line is to the brand.
