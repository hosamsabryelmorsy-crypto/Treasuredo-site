About this watch

A Seiko Arctura Kinetic Chronograph, case reference 7L22-0AF0, powered by Seiko's 7L22 Kinetic chronograph calibre. The pale champagne sunray dial carries the distinctive Arctura layout: an offset time subdial at 6 with applied 12 and 6 numerals, a running seconds subdial at 10, a 45-minute chronograph counter at 2 with a blue accent, and a date window at 4. Navy luminous hands, a polished steel bezel engraved with a 0-55 minute scale, sapphire crystal and an exhibition caseback showing the Kinetic rotor. It comes as a full set with the Seiko Kinetic box, booklet and the original warranty card filled in with the watch serial and case number.

Brand heritage

Seiko introduced its automatic-quartz technology in 1988 and launched it under the Kinetic name in 1991: a rotor driven by the movement of the wrist charges the watch, combining quartz accuracy with no battery changes. The Arctura line of the 2000s brought Seiko's sculpted, integrated-bracelet sports design to the Kinetic range, and the 7L22 Kinetic chronograph is one of its signature calibres.
