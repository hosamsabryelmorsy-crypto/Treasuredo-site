Fitted on a burgundy leather strap with red stitching.

Brand heritage

CYMA traces its roots to 1862, when the Schwob brothers began making watches in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland; the company later became CYMA Watch Co. based in Le Locle. The CYMA name was introduced in the early 20th century and the brand worked closely with the Tavannes Watch Co. CYMA is well known to collectors for its Cymaflex shock-protected movements and as one of the twelve makers of the British Army W.W.W. "Dirty Dozen" watches of the Second World War.
