About this watch

A bold rectangular dress watch from BLADE's Intrepid line, reference 2681GSS. The deep black dial carries three square sub-dials framed in gold print: day of the week at 9, date at 3 and a 24-hour indicator at 6 marked "Multifunction". Two-tone applied baton markers and gold dauphine hands with white luminous inserts make it easy to read. The stainless steel case has a rose gold tone finish and sits on a matching rose gold tone link bracelet.

Brand heritage

BLADE is a fashion watch brand whose Intrepid collection focuses on multifunction quartz designs. This piece is signed "BLADE Intrepid" on the dial and caseback, with "Swiss Design" printed on the dial.
