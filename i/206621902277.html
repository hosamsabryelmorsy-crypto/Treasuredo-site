About this watch

A vintage ladies' Sindaco from the sporty-luxury era of the late 1970s and 1980s. The case carries a twelve-sided gold-tone bezel set with eight screw heads, framed by a brushed case and an integrated two-tone bracelet of flat brushed bars, each fitted with two gold-tone screw heads. The champagne dial has a fine "clous de Paris" pyramid texture, black Roman numerals at XII, IX and VI, gold applied baton markers, black baton hands, a gold seconds hand and a framed date window at 3 o'clock. Signed SINDACO with the brand emblem, QUARTZ WATERRESISTANT and SWISS MADE on the dial.

Brand heritage

Sindaco (Italian for "mayor") is a Swiss watch brand that produced Swiss Made mechanical and, later, quartz watches for decades. Its vintage pieces, from divers to jump-hour models, are collected today for their Swiss build and distinctive designs.
