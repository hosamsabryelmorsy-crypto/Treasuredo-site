About this watch

Bold, graphic and pure 1970s: the dial is split into a vivid red centre stripe framed by fine gold lines and crisp cream-white side panels, signed with the Titus laurel-wreath emblem, "TITUS", "17 JEWELS" and "GERMAN MVT". Two slim gold-tone hands, no markers, nothing to distract from the design. The elongated, faceted gold-plated tank case has clipped corners and a gold crown set with a blue cabochon. The stainless steel caseback is signed Solvil et Titus Genève and still carries remnants of its factory protective film. Fitted with a dark green crocodile-embossed Hirsch Duke leather strap (14 mm) with a gold-tone buckle.

Brand heritage

Solvil et Titus traces its origins to Paul Ditisheim, the celebrated watchmaker from La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, and gives 1887 as its founding year. Through the 20th century Titus became known for stylish, well-priced dress watches sold across Europe, the Middle East and Asia; the brand is today part of Hong Kong's Stelux group.
