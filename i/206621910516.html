About this watch

A classic Japanese dress watch from the golden era of Seiko mechanical watchmaking. This reference 66-7970 is powered by Seiko's hand-wound calibre 66B with 17 jewels and Diashock shock protection, housed in a polished stainless steel case with a narrow bezel and a slightly domed crystal. The silver sunburst dial carries applied steel baton markers with luminous dots, black SEIKO lettering, "17 JEWELS" with the Diashock emblem, and lumed baton hands. It is worn on a stainless steel expansion bracelet with a finely engraved grid pattern and a SEIKO-signed clasp section. The caseback serial 372639 dates it to July 1973.

Brand heritage

Seiko traces its roots to 1881, when Kintaro Hattori opened his watch and clock shop in Tokyo. In 1913 the company produced Japan's first wristwatch, the Laurel, and through the 1960s and 1970s its mechanical watches set the standard for precision and value. Simple, well-built hand-wound pieces like this 66-7970 are today valued by collectors as honest, wearable vintage Seiko.
