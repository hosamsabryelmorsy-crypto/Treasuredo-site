About this watch

A distinctive Swiss-made dress watch signed Georges Claude. The round 34 mm case is sculpted in two tones: the upper section and top lug are gold-tone with three vertical ribs, flowing into a polished steel-tone body around the rest of the case. The glossy black dial is deliberately minimal, with no hour markers, just the brand's gold three-horse-heads emblem below 12, the gold script Georges Claude signature at 4, a framed date window at 6 and "Swiss Made" at the edge. Gold hour and minute hands with open circle details and a slim gold seconds hand complete the look. It comes on a black leather strap with gold-tone buckle and a Jacques Farel Collection velvet pouch.

Brand heritage

Georges Claude was a Swiss designer watch label whose name appears on watches made in cooperation with established Swiss houses such as Revue, Vulcain and Wittnauer, typically recognised by unusual sculpted case designs. The "Georges Claude" mark was registered for watches in the 1970s, and the caseback of this piece is marked "Des. Reg." (registered design), with the same three-horse emblem engraved as on the dial.
