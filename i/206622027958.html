About this watch

A classic early-1970s Seiko 5 automatic, reference 6119-8400, in the distinctive cushion ("TV") shaped stainless steel case with the crown tucked away at 4 o'clock. The deep navy-blue dial has a faceted cross pattern that catches the light from every angle, with applied silver markers with lume inserts, silver baton hands and a framed day-date window at 3 o'clock. Signed "Water 70M Resist, Automatic, 21 Jewels" on the dial. The caseback serial 104203 dates it to 1971.

Brand heritage

Seiko introduced the Seiko 5 line in 1963 around five defining features: automatic winding, day-date display, water resistance, a recessed crown at 4 o'clock and a durable case and bracelet. The in-house calibre 6119 is one of the best-known Seiko 5 day-date automatic movements of the late 1960s and 1970s.
