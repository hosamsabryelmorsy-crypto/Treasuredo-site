About this watch

A colourful 1970s ladies' watch signed Olano, with a Swiss movement (dial signed "Swiss Movt"). The cushion-shaped dial pairs a sky-blue centre with a deep navy outer ring and fine white minute track, applied gold baton markers (doubled at 12, 3, 6 and 9), gold baton hands with luminous inserts, a red seconds hand and a red-numeral date window between 1 and 2 o'clock. It sits in a period gold-plated tonneau case with a stainless steel screw-down caseback marked "Shockproof - Waterproof - Stainless Steel Back" and the Olano logo, on a matching gold-tone textured link bracelet.

Why this one is special

  • Typical 1970s design: TV/cushion dial in two shades of blue inside a gold tonneau case
  • Applied gold markers and logo, red date and red seconds hand for a pop of colour
  • Date function, which is uncommon on small ladies' watches of the period
  • Original-style gold-tone link bracelet, ready to wear
