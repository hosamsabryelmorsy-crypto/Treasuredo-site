About this watch

A charming 1970s ladies' wristwatch from Ricoh of Japan. The dial is a deep green gradient, almost black at the top and fading to a vivid green towards 6 o'clock, with applied gold baton markers, slim gold hands, a gold "R" logo and RICOH signature, "17 JEWELS" above 6, and a white date window with red numerals at 3 o'clock. It sits in a faceted, polished stainless steel cushion case measuring 27mm across (excluding crown) and 31mm lug to lug, and is worn on its stainless steel link bracelet with a fold-over clasp signed "Ricoh". The caseback is signed with the Ricoh logo, "Water Resistant - Shock Resistant - All Stainless Steel" and the reference 216005A.

Brand heritage

Ricoh's watchmaking goes back to the Nagoya firm Takano, which made wristwatches in the 1950s. In 1962 Takano was taken over by the Ricoh company and became Ricoh Watch Co., which went on to build its own mechanical movements and sold watches across Japan and abroad through the 1960s and 1970s. The watch business later became Ricoh Elemex.
