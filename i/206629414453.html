About this watch

A vintage Raymond Weil Geneve dress watch, reference 5352. A deep navy blue dial is framed by a full ring of small clear stones, with the RW monogram and "Raymond Weil Geneve" in gold below 12, slim gold leaf hands and a round date window at 6 o'clock. The round 18K gold electroplated case has a wide polished bezel and distinctive hooded lugs at 12 and 6, with the crown at 3 o'clock. Worn on a blue leather strap with contrast stitching and a gold-tone buckle.

Brand heritage

Raymond Weil was founded in Geneva in 1976 by Raymond Weil and has remained an independent, family-run Swiss watchmaker, well known for its elegant, music-inspired dress watches.
