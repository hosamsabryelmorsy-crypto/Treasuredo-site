About this item

A Ratti-era Persol 6200 with a bold, wide rectangular front in thick black acetate, the brand's silver arrow ornaments on the front corners and wide black Meflecto temples with the characteristic groove pattern. Fitted with brown gradient lenses and supplied with a brown Persol case.

Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by Giuseppe Ratti; its early wearers were pilots and sports drivers, and the name comes from "per il sole" (for the sun). In the late 1930s Persol developed the patented Meflecto system, flexible temples that adapt to the wearer's head, and the silver arrow became the brand's signature. Ratti-era frames are prized by collectors.
