About this item

An original vintage optical frame by the Parisian jeweller FRED: the Cythere, size 57 mm. The gold-plated full-rim frame has a soft geometric lens shape and a two-tone twisted marine cable set into the upper brow, echoing the cable-and-shackle language of Fred's famous Force 10 jewellery. Gold shackle-style hinges with ribbed collars, thin gold temples and black ear tips complete the look. The frame still wears its original clear FRED PARIS LUNETTES demo lenses and comes with the original blue Fred presentation case, white Fred Lunettes outer box and paper sleeve.

Brand heritage

FRED was founded in Paris in 1936 by Fred Samuel, who opened his first boutique at 6 rue Royale. In 1966 the house created the Force 10 bracelet, with its twisted marine cable and shackle clasp, which became the signature of the Maison. Fred later carried the same nautical motifs into its eyewear, sold as FRED Lunettes. Fred has been part of the LVMH group since the mid-1990s.
