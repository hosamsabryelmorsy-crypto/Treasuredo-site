Brand heritage

Ted Lapidus (1929–2008) was a Paris couturier known for bringing tailored, ready-to-wear elegance to French fashion. His label extended into accessories and eyewear, and Ted Lapidus Paris sunglasses made in France are now sought after by collectors of vintage French designer frames.
