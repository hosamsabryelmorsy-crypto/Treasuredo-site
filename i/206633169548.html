About this item

Authentic Chopard rimless sunglasses with a clean, low-profile rectangular silhouette. Purple-grey gradient lenses, fading to soft lavender at the bottom, are held by gold-tone hardware: a slim straight bridge bar fixed with screws and squared gold hinge brackets at the outer corners. The black temples carry a fine embossed texture and a gold CHOPARD plaque on the outside, with a signed oval Chopard insert at each ear end. The serial CH 1381117 is printed on the inner temple tip. Supplied with the original Chopard blue hard case.

Brand heritage

Chopard was founded in 1860 by Louis-Ulysse Chopard in Sonvilier, Switzerland, and has been owned by the Scheufele family since 1963. Known for fine watches and high jewellery, the Geneva house extends the same taste for gold and precise detailing to its eyewear, made in Italy under licence by De Rigo.
