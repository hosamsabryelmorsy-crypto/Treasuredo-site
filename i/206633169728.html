Brand heritage

FRED is a Paris jewellery house founded in 1936 by Fred Samuel, who opened his first shop on Rue Royale. The house has been part of the LVMH group since the mid-1990s. FRED Lunettes eyewear carries the house's jewellery-inspired design into precision French-made frames.
