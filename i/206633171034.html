Brand heritage

Ted Lapidus (1929–2008) trained with Dior and launched his own Paris label in 1951. He joined the Chambre Syndicale de la Haute Couture in 1963 and dressed stars such as Brigitte Bardot and Alain Delon. From the 1970s the house licensed sunglasses and perfumes; this frame's tag names Lamy Jeune Fils of France as maker.
