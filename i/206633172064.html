Brand heritage

Fred was founded in Paris in 1936 by jeweller Fred Samuel. In 1966 his son Henri Samuel, a keen sailor, combined marine steel cable with gold to create the Force 10 bracelet, named after the Beaufort storm scale. The nautical cable motif became the house signature and was carried into Fred's luxury eyewear made in France, of which the Ocean is one of the best-known 1980s models.
