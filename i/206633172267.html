About this item

An original 1980s Cazal 902 from the TARGA design series, made in West Germany. The oversized geometric aviator has thin gold rims with stud rivets, the raised trapezoid wire bridge typical of the 902, a translucent amber-yellow nose saddle and smoke-grey gradient lenses with the original white "TARGA design" and Cazal signature print. Supplied with the original tan suede Cazal pouch.

Brand heritage

Cazal was founded in 1975 in Germany by designer Cari Zalloni, whose sculptural, architectural frames became icons of 1980s style and hip-hop culture. The 902, made in West Germany before reunification, is one of the original Zalloni designs and remains one of the most collected vintage Cazal models.
