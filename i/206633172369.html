Brand heritage

Louis Féraud opened his first boutique in Cannes in 1950 and later moved his couture house to Paris, where he became known for joyful colour and artistic prints. Féraud licensed eyewear in the 1980s and 1990s, made in France, which carried the same bold, colourful spirit as his fashion.
