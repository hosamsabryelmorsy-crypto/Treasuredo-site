Brand heritage

CAZAL takes its name from its founder and designer Cari Zalloni (CA-ZAL), who started the company in Germany in 1975. His bold, architectural frames became icons of 1980s style and of early hip-hop culture, and original West German production from that decade is among the most collected vintage eyewear today.
