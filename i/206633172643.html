About this item

A new old stock Christian Dior Monsieur 2248 in colour 94, the large 63-17 size, with the frame made in Austria in the 1980s. The teardrop pilot frame has black metal rims and temples, a pale-gold ribbed brow bar and bridge, gold bead details on top of the rims and ribbed gold end pieces, fitted with solid brown lenses. It still carries the original Christian Dior paper hang tag and comes with the Dior pouch, cloth, case and boxes.

Brand heritage

Christian Dior founded his Paris couture house in 1946. "Monsieur" was the house's menswear line, and in the 1970s and 1980s Dior eyewear was produced in Austria, home of some of Europe's finest frame makers. The Monsieur aviators of the 1980s are among the most wanted vintage Dior frames today.
