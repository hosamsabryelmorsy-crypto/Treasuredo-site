About this item

A genuine mid-century French frame stamped MESSIDOR II. A slim gold-filled brow bar sweeps across the top and curves gracefully over the nose, holding honey-amber acetate lower rims and brown glass lenses in a soft upswept browline / cat-eye shape. Fine gold-filled skull temples complete the light, elegant construction. The brow bar is stamped with the MESSIDOR II name between French maker and gold-filled (doublé or) hallmark squares, and the temples carry matching hallmarks.

About the maker

Little is documented about the Messidor name today. The frame is a typical example of mid-century French optical craftsmanship, when gold-filled (doublé or) frames, a thick layer of gold bonded over a base metal, were struck with small square hallmarks identifying the maker and the gold content. That makes these frames far more durable than ordinary plated metal.
