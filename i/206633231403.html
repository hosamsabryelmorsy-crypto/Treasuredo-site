Brand heritage

Persol takes its name from the Italian per il sole ("for the sun"). The company was founded in Turin in 1917 by optician and photographer Giuseppe Ratti, first making protective eyewear for pilots and sportsmen. In the late 1930s Persol introduced the Meflecto system, flexible temples built around inserted cylinders that let the arm adapt to the wearer's head without pressure – still one of the brand's signatures today.
