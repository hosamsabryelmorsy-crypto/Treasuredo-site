About this item

An original Persol Ratti 6201 from the pre-Luxottica Ratti era, made in Italy. The thick gloss black acetate front has a soft rectangular shape with gently lifted outer corners, finished with the silver Persol arrows on each side. The temples carry Persol's Meflecto flexible system with four metal inserts. The inner temples are stamped 6201, the Persol script logo and the oval RATTI mark. Fitted with brown gradient lenses, dark at the top and lighter toward the bottom.

Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by optician Giuseppe Ratti; the name comes from "per il sole" (for the sun). The company began with protective goggles for pilots and racing drivers. Its patented Meflecto system, flexible inserts in the temples that let the arms adapt to the head, dates from 1938, and the Supreme silver arrow became the brand's signature. Frames from the pre-Luxottica era (Persol was acquired by Luxottica in 1995) stamped RATTI are especially valued by collectors.
