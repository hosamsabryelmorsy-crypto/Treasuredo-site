About this item

A bold, sporty Ratti-era Persol: model 6626 in glossy black acetate, with a thick straight brow, a deep wraparound front and solid warm brown lenses. The broad, tapered temples carry Persol's Meflecto construction, marked by two fine cuts with small metal pins just behind the hinges. The inside of the temples is stamped 6626, Persol in script, RATTI, and HASSAN, the name of the optician who originally retailed the frame. It comes with a brown leather-look case embossed with the Persol logo.

Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by Giuseppe Ratti, who began by making protective glasses for pilots and sportsmen; the name comes from the Italian per il sole, "for the sun". In the late 1930s the company introduced its patented Meflecto flexible temple system, still a Persol hallmark today. Frames signed "Ratti" were made before the brand was acquired by Luxottica in 1995 and are the ones vintage collectors look for.
