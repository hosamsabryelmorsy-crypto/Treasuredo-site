About this item

A sharp 1960s Persol in thick glossy black acetate, with an upswept rectangular cat-eye front, brown gradient lenses and Persol's silver arrow on each front corner. The temples have the Meflecto flexible section, marked by four grooves with small silver pins on the top edge, and Persol is signed in script on the temple.

Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by Giuseppe Ratti, who began by making protective glasses for pilots and sportsmen; the name comes from the Italian per il sole, "for the sun". In the late 1930s the company introduced its patented Meflecto flexible temple system, still a Persol hallmark today. Frames signed "Ratti" were made before the brand was acquired by Luxottica in 1995 and are the ones vintage collectors look for.
