About this item

A rare, sculptural Persol: model 6650 in thick glossy black acetate, with a flat heavy brow, deep shield-aviator rims and very wide temples that work almost like side shields. A small silver Persol arrow sits on the outside of each temple, and the hinges are mounted on riveted metal plates. Three Meflecto cuts with small metal pins run across each temple. Inside, the temple is stamped 6650, Persol, the Meflecto logo and RATTI.

Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by Giuseppe Ratti, who began by making protective glasses for pilots and sportsmen; the name comes from the Italian per il sole, "for the sun". In the late 1930s the company introduced its patented Meflecto flexible temple system, still a Persol hallmark today. Frames signed "Ratti" were made before the brand was acquired by Luxottica in 1995 and are the ones vintage collectors look for.
