Brand heritage

Persol takes its name from the Italian per il sole ("for the sun"). The company was founded in Turin in 1917 by optician and photographer Giuseppe Ratti, first making protective eyewear for pilots and sportsmen. In the late 1930s Persol introduced the Meflecto flexible temple and the Silver Arrow, which remain the brand's signatures. Frames from the Ratti era are prized by collectors for their build quality.
