About this watch

A men's Movado SE (Sports Edition), reference 84.G1.1892, on its integrated stainless steel bracelet. The silver sunray dial carries the MOVADO wordmark below 12, large dark grey Arabic numerals at 12, 9 and 6, polished dot markers at the other hours, black baton hands and a framed date window at 3 o'clock. The sculpted round case has the SE line's signature polished bezel with four raised rivet studs, a protected crown at 3 o'clock and a brushed bracelet with a polished stud on every link, closing with a signed Movado fold-over clasp. Supplied in a Movado box.

Brand heritage

Movado was founded in 1881 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, by Achille Ditesheim. The name Movado, Esperanto for "always in motion", was adopted in 1905. The brand is best known for the Museum Watch, designed by Nathan George Horwitt, whose dial is held in the collection of the Museum of Modern Art in New York.
