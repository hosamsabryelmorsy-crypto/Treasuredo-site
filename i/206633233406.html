About this item

An original Persol Ratti 6286 T, made in Italy in the Ratti era. The bold flat-top rectangular front in black acetate has a straight brow line and a keyhole-style bridge, fitted with brown gradient lenses. The temples use Persol's Meflecto flexible system with four metal inserts. The inner temples are stamped 6286 T, the Persol script logo, the oval RATTI mark and a second rectangular stamp that appears to read HASSAN (a Middle East market mark seen on some Ratti frames).

Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by optician Giuseppe Ratti; the name comes from "per il sole" (for the sun). The company began with protective goggles for pilots and racing drivers. Its patented Meflecto system, flexible inserts in the temples that let the arms adapt to the head, dates from 1938, and the Supreme silver arrow became the brand's signature. Frames from the pre-Luxottica era (Persol was acquired by Luxottica in 1995) stamped RATTI are especially valued by collectors.
