About the maker

Tiffany Lunettes was an Italian luxury eyewear label of the 1980s and 1990s, known for 23K gold-plated and platinum-finished frames with jewellery-inspired details and fine hand finishing. It is a separate eyewear brand and is not affiliated with Tiffany & Co.
