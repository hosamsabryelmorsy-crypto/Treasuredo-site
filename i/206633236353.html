Brand heritage

Karl Lagerfeld (1933–2019) was one of the most influential designers of the 20th century: creative director of Fendi from 1965 and of Chanel from 1983, and founder of his own Karl Lagerfeld label in 1984. Licensed Lagerfeld eyewear of the 1980s and 1990s, much of it made in France, is collected today for its bold, architectural designs.
