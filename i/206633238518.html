About this item

The Persol 6201 is a 1960s classic: a thick black acetate square front with softly upswept outer corners, the signature silver Persol arrow on each front corner, and Meflecto flexible temples marked by the grooved band behind the hinges. Fitted with brown lenses. The temple is stamped with the Persol script logo, alongside an additional imprint in Arabic script from the original retailer.

Brand heritage

Persol takes its name from the Italian per il sole ("for the sun"). The company was founded in Turin in 1917 by optician and photographer Giuseppe Ratti, first making protective eyewear for pilots and sportsmen. In the late 1930s Persol introduced the Meflecto flexible temple and the Silver Arrow, which remain the brand's signatures. Frames from the Ratti era are prized by collectors for their build quality.
