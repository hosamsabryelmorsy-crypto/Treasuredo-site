About this item

A mid-century pair of Persol Ratti sunglasses in thick, glossy black acetate. The rounded panto front sweeps up at the outer corners into a soft cat-eye line, with a sculpted keyhole bridge and the silver Persol arrow plaques on both front corners. The temples carry the Meflecto flexible-stem system, visible as the grooved metal-insert section behind each hinge, and the lenses are a warm solid brown.

Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by optician Giuseppe Ratti, who first made protective eyewear for pilots and racing drivers; the name comes from the Italian "per il sole" ("for the sun"). In the 1930s the company developed the patented Meflecto flexible-temple system, and its Supreme Silver Arrow hinge became the brand's signature. Frames from the Ratti family era, before the brand joined Luxottica in 1995, are prized by collectors for their dense hand-finished acetate and classic Italian lines.
