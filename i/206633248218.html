Brand heritage

Christian Dior founded his Paris couture house in 1946 and changed post-war fashion with the New Look of 1947. From the late 1960s Dior eyewear was made under licence by Optyl, whose lightweight Optyl material and precise metalwork produced some of the most collected designer sunglasses of the 1970s and 1980s.
