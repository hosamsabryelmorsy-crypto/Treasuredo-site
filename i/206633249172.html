About this item

A substantial pair of mid-century sunglasses, hand-shaped from thick tortoise acetate with bold amber and dark brown tiger-stripe patterning. The wide rectangular front has softly rounded corners and a deep, sculpted rim, joined to straight temples by riveted pin hinges marked with two small metal dots. The lenses are a dark grey-green tint. There is no maker's name on the frame; we sourced it as Italian-made, as stated in our earlier listing.

Background

Thick hand-cut acetate frames like this were typical of European, and especially Italian, sunglasses of the 1950s and 1960s, when the bold rectangular "Riviera" look was at its height. Unsigned workshop frames of this period are now collected for their material quality and strong shapes.
