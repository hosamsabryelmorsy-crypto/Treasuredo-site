Elegant 1980s Helena Rubinstein Paris sunglasses, model HR 2-09, made in France. The softly squared silver-tone metal frame has engraved, striped side panels on the outer rims, a slim bridge with adjustable nose pads, and brown marbled temples. Fitted with grey-green tinted lenses.

Brand heritage

Helena Rubinstein, born in Krakow in 1872, opened her first beauty salon in Melbourne and went on to London, Paris and New York, building one of the great cosmetics houses of the 20th century. Her name also appeared on fashion accessories, including fine eyewear made in France.
