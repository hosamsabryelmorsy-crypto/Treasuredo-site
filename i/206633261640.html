Brand heritage

TAG Heuer traces its roots to the watch company Edouard Heuer founded in Saint-Imier, Switzerland, in 1860, a name long tied to motor racing and sports timing. After the TAG group acquired Heuer in 1985 the brand became TAG Heuer, and its eyewear range carried the same sport-performance thinking, with light rimless and flexible designs produced in France.
