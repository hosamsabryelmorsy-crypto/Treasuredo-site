Brand heritage

Jean Lempereur launched his French eyewear collection in 1973; according to trade sources the line was later continued under the Karavan name from 1993. Lempereur frames carry the "JL" monogram and the "Frame France" mark, and the "création" models of the 1970s and 1980s are collected today for their colourful acetates and strong period shapes.
