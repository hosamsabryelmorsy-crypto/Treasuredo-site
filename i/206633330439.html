About this item

A bold pair of vintage browline sunglasses made in Italy. The thick, flat-top brow bar is black acetate with a matte finish on the front and a glossy finish on the inside, with squared outer corners and small silver bow-tie shaped decorative inlays at each upper corner. The lenses sit in fine gold-tone metal lower rims, giving the classic half-rim "clubmaster" look in a heavier, more architectural mid-century style. Brown gradient lenses, darker at the top and lighter toward the bottom. The temples are fitted with riveted metal hinges and carry gold-tone metal plaques, one stamped "MADE IN ITALY".
