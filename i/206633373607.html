Brand heritage

The house of Rochas was founded in Paris in 1925 by couturier Marcel Rochas, known for elegant, feminine fashion and later for celebrated fragrances such as Femme and Eau de Rochas. Rochas eyewear of the late 20th century was made in France and is collected today for its decorative, gold-detailed designs.
