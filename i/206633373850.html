About this item

A rare vintage Persol Ratti 6294/T in rich havana tortoise acetate. The flat-top navigator front has a double bridge with an open keyhole cut-out, and the temples use Persol's Meflecto flexible system with four metal rings. The inner temple is stamped 6294/T. Fitted with solid brown lenses.

Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by optician Giuseppe Ratti, whose first products were protective glasses for aviators and sportsmen; the name comes from "per il sole" (for the sun). In the late 1930s the company introduced the Meflecto flexible-temple system, with small metal cylinders set into the acetate temple to reduce pressure on the head, and the silver arrow became the brand's signature. Frames made in the Ratti era are sought after by collectors for their Italian acetate work and engineering.
