About this item

Striking vintage French sunglasses with large round-oval lenses. The amber tortoise acetate front and temples are decorated with a dark swirl pattern outlined in fine metallic lines, an Art Deco look with a stained-glass effect. Each temple ends the inlay in a round medallion, and the inner temple is stamped "BREVET N. 71.12199", a diamond maker's mark and "FRANCE". Light yellow-amber lenses complete the look. These glasses were offered as unused.

Brand heritage

"Brevet" is the French word for patent. Frames of this kind are sold among collectors under the Brevet (often "Michelle Brevet") name, but the stamp on this pair shows a patent number, not a maker's name, so the exact workshop is not confirmed. The patent number "71.12199" points to the early 1970s. French frame makers of the period were known for decorative, hand-finished acetate like this pair.
