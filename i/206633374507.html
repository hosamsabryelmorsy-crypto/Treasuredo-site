About this item

A vintage Persol P013 made from Optyl, the light, heat-mouldable material that was the high-tech frame material of the 1970s and 80s. The aviator-style front is translucent honey-brown, darker at the top and lighter at the bottom, with a sculpted bridge and brown lenses. Each temple carries an oval gold-tone Persol "P" badge on the outside; inside, the temples are marked with the Optyl logo and Persol script, and "P013 10 52.70".

Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by Giuseppe Ratti, first making protective goggles for pilots and racing drivers; the name comes from "per il sole" (for the sun). Before Luxottica bought the company in 1995, Persol frames were made under the Ratti family and are now known to collectors as "Persol Ratti". Optyl frames from this period are valued for their light weight and comfort.
