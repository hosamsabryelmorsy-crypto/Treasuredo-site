Brand heritage

The house of Jean Patou was founded in Paris by Jean Patou in 1914 and is credited with shaping the sporty, modern look of 1920s fashion. Patou produced couture, ready-to-wear and perfume, and its licensed accessories, including eyewear, carried the name through the following decades.
