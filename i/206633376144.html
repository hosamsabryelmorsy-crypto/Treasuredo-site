About this item

Mid-century Italian sunglasses by Samco in striped "Havana" tortoise acetate - warm amber and brown stripes with a clear crystal edge. The front has a strong, upswept rectangular shape with pointed outer corners, and the thick temples carry a grooved section with a flexible metal core. The original red "Samco" label is still on the left lens, "SAMCO" is etched into the lens, and the inner temple has the metal "Samco" plaque.

Brand heritage

Samco was an Italian sunglass maker whose acetate frames, often marked "Mod. Dep." (registered design), were popular in the 1950s and 60s. Today they are collected for their bold mid-century shapes and Italian acetate.
