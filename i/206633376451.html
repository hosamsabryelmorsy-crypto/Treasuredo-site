Brand heritage

Christian Dior founded his couture house in Paris in 1946. From the late 1960s Dior sunglasses were produced under licence in Germany and Austria by Optyl, the company of Wilhelm Anger, which used its lightweight Optyl material; by the 1970s and 1980s these frames were among the best-known designer eyewear in the world.
