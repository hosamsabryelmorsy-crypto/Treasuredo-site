About this item

Glamorous oversized Nina Ricci sunglasses with large, softly squared lenses. Gold-tone "N" emblems sit at the bridge and on the temple fronts, and the slim translucent arms are signed "NINA RICCI PARIS". Brown gradient lenses complete the warm colour palette.

Brand heritage

The fashion house Nina Ricci was founded in Paris in 1932 by Maria "Nina" Ricci and her son Robert, and became famous for refined, feminine couture and for the perfume L'Air du Temps. Vintage Nina Ricci eyewear from the 1970s-80s, with its signature gold "N" logo, is sought after for its elegant oversized shapes.
