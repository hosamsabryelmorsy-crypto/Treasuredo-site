The cable-style gold temples end in honey tortoise acetate tips. Grey-violet gradient lenses. New old stock, still with the original Henry Jullien France hang tag.

Brand heritage

Henry Jullien is one of the historic names of French eyewear, founded in 1921 in the Jura region of France, the heartland of French spectacle making. The house is known for its gold-filled ("doublé or") metal frames and traditional craftsmanship, and holds France's Entreprise du Patrimoine Vivant label for exceptional know-how.
