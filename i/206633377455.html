About this item

Glamorous oversized Jacques Fath sunglasses, hand made in France. The square-butterfly front shades from amber-brown along the brow to clear crystal at the bottom, with gold ornaments at the hinges. The gold temples are sculpted in a twisted, rope-like pattern, carry the FATH logo on the outside and end in black tips. Inner temple stamps: "JACQUES FATH", a maker's mark, "HAND MADE IN FRANCE" and "90430 JF/DZ 54 x 19". Dark grey lenses.

Brand heritage

Jacques Fath opened his Paris couture house in 1937 and became one of the most influential designers of the post-war period. Eyewear made under the Jacques Fath name in France from the 1970s to the 1990s is collected for its couture details and quality.
