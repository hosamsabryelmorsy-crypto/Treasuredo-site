About this item

An original Ratti-era Persol 6201 in glossy black acetate, made in Italy. The slightly upswept rectangular front carries the silver Persol Supreme Arrow on each side, and the temples have the four-cylinder Meflecto flex system. Fitted with brown gradient lenses. Medium size with 48 mm lenses.

Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by Giuseppe Ratti, who first made protective eyewear for pilots and sportsmen. The brand patented the Meflecto flexible temple system in the late 1930s and is known for its silver Supreme Arrow hinge emblem. Frames made before the Luxottica takeover in 1995 are known to collectors as Ratti-era Persol.
