Brand heritage

Morel is a family-owned French eyewear maker from the Jura mountains, the historic heart of French spectacle making. Its story starts in 1880, when Jules Morel made his first pince-nez; in the 1930s Marius Morel grew the business into large-scale frame production. The company is still run by the family today.
