About this item

An original Christian Dior Monsieur reference 2115 in translucent olive-green Optyl with darker smoky contours, made in Austria around the late 1970s to early 1980s. Wide sculpted temples with the gold-tone CD emblem and solid dark green lenses give it a strong, masculine look that also works as a unisex statement frame.

Brand heritage

Dior eyewear was produced under licence by Wilhelm Anger, the inventor of the heat-moldable Optyl material, from the late 1960s, and his Austrian-made Dior frames led the designer eyewear market through the 1970s and 1980s. The Monsieur line was Dior's menswear range of that era.
