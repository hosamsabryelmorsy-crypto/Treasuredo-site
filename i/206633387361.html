About this item

A wonderfully expressive 1950s cat-eye frame made in Italy. The black frame is faced with a pale yellow decorative surface printed with a repeating tiny dark star motif, and the upswept outer corners carry small metal rivet plaques. Black inner temples and brown lenses complete the classic rockabilly / pin-up look. This is a genuine period frame, not a modern retro copy.

Brand heritage

Italy was one of the great centres of post-war European eyewear, and Italian cat-eye frames of the 1950s are known for their playful decoration and bold upswept shapes. This frame is stamped MADE IN ITALY; the small maker plaque on the temple reads approximately "ADOC" but we could not confirm the maker, so we do not attribute it to a specific brand.
