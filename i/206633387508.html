About this item

A genuine circa-1950s men's sunglass frame in substantial dark-brown acetate, stamped "MADE ITALY" inside the temple. The acetate looks almost black in low light and glows warm chocolate and mahogany when lit. Large soft-square shape with a strong brow, rounded lower rims, flared endpieces and a low sculpted bridge, plus small diamond-shaped metal rivets on the front and temples. Fitted with brown-tinted lenses.

Brand heritage

Post-war Italy was one of the great centres of acetate eyewear making, and 1950s frames like this were cut from thick sheet acetate and finished by hand. No maker's name is visible on this pair; its appeal is the real Italian manufacture and the period rockabilly silhouette.
