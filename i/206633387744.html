New old stock Asdor Mistral sunglasses from the 1980s, unworn. Glossy black frame with a softly squared, oversized shape and a sculpted double-rim effect around the lenses. Brand heritage

Asdor was an Italian eyewear maker active from the 1970s into the 1980s, known for well-made acetate frames such as the Patent, Oasis and Mistral models. Its frames are now sought after by collectors of Italian vintage sunglasses.
