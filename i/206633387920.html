About this item

A classic 1970s Italian aviator in glossy black acetate, with a double bridge (top brow bar) and large teardrop lenses in dark green glass. Each temple has a pair of silver metal bands and a small silver rivet plate at the hinge. The temple is stamped "BREV. LASTES ITALY" and "M 255C", and the ear tip carries the size 52-18.

Brand heritage

Lastes was an Italian eyewear maker; "BREV." on the temple is short for "brevetto", Italian for patent. Lastes frames were made in Italy in the 1970s-1990s, including the later Swing by Lastes line. We found no reliable source linking Lastes to Persol, so we list this pair only under its own name.
