About this item

An unused pair of 1980s Giorgio Beverly Hills GS 601 sunglasses. The oversized cream-white acetate frame has gold geometric cut-out inlays at the upper outer corners, and the wide temples carry the famous Giorgio yellow-and-white stripes with a gold crest emblem. Light blue lenses finish a bright, sunny Beverly Hills look.

Brand heritage

Giorgio Beverly Hills was the celebrated boutique at 273 Rodeo Drive, run by Fred Hayman from 1962 and known for its yellow-and-white striped awnings. The stripes became the brand's signature on its famous 1981 fragrance and on accessories such as these sunglasses.
