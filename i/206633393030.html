Brand heritage

Metzler is a long-established German optical maker that moved to Stuttgart in 1911 and became known for solid, optician-quality frames rather than fashion gimmicks. In the 1970s and 1980s many Metzler sunglasses were fitted with Carl Zeiss lenses.
