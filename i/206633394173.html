Brand heritage

Balenciaga was founded by Spanish couturier Cristobal Balenciaga, who opened his first house in San Sebastian in 1917 and his Paris house in 1937. Known as "the master" of couture, he shaped fashion for decades. Vintage French-made Balenciaga eyewear with the BB logo is now sought after by collectors.
