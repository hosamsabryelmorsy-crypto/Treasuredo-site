Authentic vintage Jacques Fath sunglasses made in France. Brand heritage

Jacques Fath founded his Paris couture house in 1937 and was one of the leading names of post-war French fashion before his death in 1954. The house name lived on through licensed accessories and fragrances, including French-made eyewear.
