Brand heritage

Viennaline was founded in Austria in the 1950s by Wilhelm Anger, who also founded Carrera and invented Optyl, a light, heat-stable epoxy-resin frame material. Many of its designs were by Udo Proksch, and the brand helped popularise the cat-eye and butterfly shapes of the period.
