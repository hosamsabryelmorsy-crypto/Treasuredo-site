About this item

A vintage pair of Zeiss sunglasses from the Carat line, model 6845, made in Germany in the 1980s. The thin light-gold metal frame has large cat-eye / butterfly shaped lenses with a straight top line, and a slim top bar that dips into an elegant V-shaped point above the bridge. Each outer end piece is decorated with a vertical row of four prong-set stones, alternating clear and red. The frame is fitted with amber-brown gradient lenses (darker at the top, lighter at the bottom), adjustable nose pads and thin stepped double-wire temples with dark brown tips. A glamorous, feminine 80s design.

Brand heritage

Zeiss is the famous German optics company founded by Carl Zeiss in Jena in 1846, known worldwide for its lenses for cameras, microscopes and eyeglasses. In the 1970s and 1980s Zeiss also produced fashion frames and sunglasses in West Germany, and the Carat line was its upmarket range of dressy metal frames. Vintage Zeiss sunglasses are collected today for their German build quality and bold period designs.
