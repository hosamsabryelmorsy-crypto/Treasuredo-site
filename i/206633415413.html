About this item

A vintage pair of Christian Dior sunglasses, model 2742 in colour 41, with a polished gold metal front, large rounded lenses and brown gradient lenses (dark brown at the top fading to light at the bottom). The signature detail is at each side: the frame opens into a sculpted V-shaped wing decorated with inset brown enamel panels, leading to a gold hinge plate with the engraved "CD" monogram and stepped chevrons. The temples are havana tortoiseshell with gold rivet dots. A glamorous, feminine shape typical of Dior eyewear of the period.

Brand heritage

The House of Dior was founded in Paris by Christian Dior in 1946 and changed fashion with the 1947 "New Look". Dior eyewear was produced under licence in Austria for many years, and these Austrian-made frames from the 1980s and 1990s, with their jewellery-like gold metalwork and CD monograms, are now widely collected as vintage designer sunglasses.
