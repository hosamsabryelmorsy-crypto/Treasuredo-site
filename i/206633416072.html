About this item

A vintage pair of Longines sunglasses made under licence by Metzler in Germany. The temples are stamped "LONGINES BY METZLER" with the Metzler spectacles logo and "GERMANY", and the inside of the other temple reads "Mod. 4525/47.. 815 54□22 140" (the end of the model suffix is partly worn). Each temple carries the gold Longines winged-hourglass emblem near the hinge.

The design is a slim horizontal oval in gold-tone metal, with a red-and-black marbled lacquer inlay around each lens, a matte black brow bar across the top and a separate gold inverted-U bridge below it. The temples are gold at the hinge and matte black along their length, ending in black coated ear tips. Solid warm brown lenses.

Brand heritage

Longines, founded in 1832 in Saint-Imier, Switzerland, is one of the oldest Swiss watch brands, and its winged-hourglass emblem is among the oldest registered trademarks still in use. Metzler, the German eyewear maker, produced Longines-branded frames and sunglasses under licence; these Longines by Metzler pieces are now sought after by vintage eyewear collectors.
