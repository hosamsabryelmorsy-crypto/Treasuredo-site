About this item

An unused pair of 1980s Cazal Mod. 101 sunglasses in translucent striped acetate - swirls of teal, amber, cream and brown - with a soft butterfly-shaped front and solid brown lenses. The signature detail is the sculpted wavy temples, a playful Cari Zalloni touch. The temple is stamped "Mod. 101 56□14" and the inner temple carries the Cazal logo.

Brand heritage

Cazal was founded in Germany in 1975 by designer Cari Zalloni with manufacturer Günter Böttcher; the name comes from the first syllables of CAri ZALloni. In the 1980s Cazal frames became icons of early hip-hop style and are now highly collectible.
