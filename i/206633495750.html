About this item

Electric blue meets Parisian glamour. These vintage Rochas Paris sunglasses pair a sculpted, glossy black wraparound frame with vivid blue mirrored lenses. The standout detail is the broad gold-tone plaque on each temple with a raised sunburst rosette and a blue stone centre - a jewellery-like finish. Inner temples are stamped "ROCHAS PARIS 9031 42" and "C4 MADE IN FRANCE CE".

Brand heritage

Rochas is the Paris fashion house founded by couturier Marcel Rochas in 1925, famous for its elegant couture and perfumes such as Femme. Its licensed eyewear, made in France, carried the house's taste for jewellery-like gold detailing onto bold acetate frames.
