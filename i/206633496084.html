About this item

A small, perfectly round Oliver by Valentino frame from the 1990s. Matte brushed silver metal rims are paired with a warm red-tortoise acetate saddle-bridge insert and matching tortoise temples with raised metal Oliver plaques, fitted with grey gradient lenses. Inner temples are stamped "OLIVER MADE IN ITALY" and "1301 905 42-23 140".

Brand heritage

Oliver was a diffusion line of the Italian fashion house Valentino, founded in Rome by Valentino Garavani; its Italian-made eyewear of the 1990s is collected today for its unusual, design-led shapes.
