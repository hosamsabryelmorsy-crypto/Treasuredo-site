About this item

A spectacular statement piece by France Look: the oversized aviator-style MASTER FL 10 in glossy black acetate with brown gradient lenses. The temples are architectural gold-tone openwork metal frames holding black inlay panels with exposed screw details. The inlays are printed "FRANCE LOOK Hand made in France" and "MASTER FL 10".

Brand heritage

France Look was a French eyewear label whose bold, often oversized frames were made in France; its 1970s-80s designs with elaborate metal temples are now hard to find.
