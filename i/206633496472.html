Brand heritage

Optyl is a heat-mouldable epoxy material developed in Austria by Wilhelm Anger in 1964: lighter than acetate, hypoallergenic, and able to return to its original shape when warmed. Through the 1970s and 1980s Austrian and German makers used Optyl for bold sculpted frames; Cobra Optyl frames from Austria are a collector favourite from that era.
