Brand heritage

Silhouette was founded in 1964 in Linz, Austria, by Anneliese and Arnold Schmied, who set out to turn eyewear from a simple visual aid into a fashion accessory. The company has remained an independent, family-run Austrian business and is known worldwide for precise, lightweight frames made in Austria.
