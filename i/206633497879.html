Brand heritage

Cazal was launched in Germany in 1975 by designer Cari Zalloni together with entrepreneur Günter Böttcher; the name joins the first syllables of CAri ZALloni. Cazal's bold German-made frames became cult objects in the 1980s, and vintage Cazal is now among the most sought-after designer eyewear.
