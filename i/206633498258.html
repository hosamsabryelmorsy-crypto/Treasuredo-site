About this item

A large 1970s Yves Saint Laurent frame, model 7759, in translucent olive-honey crystal acetate with a solid black brow bar across the top and a straight double bridge. White rectangular YSL logo plaques sit on the outer temples near the hinges.

The lenses are a soft brown-to-pink gradient. Inside the temples the frame is stamped YVES SAINT LAURENT and FRANCE 7759.

Brand heritage

The Yves Saint Laurent couture house was founded in Paris in 1961 by Yves Saint Laurent and his partner Pierre Bergé. Through the 1970s the YSL name was extended under licence to accessories including eyewear, and these early French-made frames are now prized by collectors.
