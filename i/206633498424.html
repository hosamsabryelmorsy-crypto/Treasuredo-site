About this item

An unused 1980s Jacques Bogart Paris frame with a squared panto front in gold-plated metal. The brow line and the outer temples are decorated with multicolour enamel (teal, grey and violet), set off by finely textured gold panels at the hinges; the black temple tips carry the J. Bogart logo, and the lenses are a warm reddish brown.

Inside the temples the frame is marked JACQUES BOGART PARIS, HAND MADE IN FRANCE, and 9133x JB/CI 51 X 21 (last digit of the model number partly worn).

Brand heritage

Jacques Bogart is a French house founded in Paris in 1975, best known for men's fragrances such as Bogart pour Homme and One Man Show (1981). In the 1980s the name also appeared on fine accessories, including hand-made French eyewear like this frame.
