About this item

A classic Ratti-era Persol aviator in glossy black acetate, with a keyhole double bridge, teardrop lenses in solid light brown and the signature Persol silver arrow hinges. Three silver metal inlay bands decorate each temple near the hinge.

The inside temple is stamped 58200 above PATENT (the number is faint with age).

Brand heritage

Persol was founded in 1917 in Turin by optician and photographer Giuseppe Ratti, whose first Protector goggles were made for aviators and racing drivers. The name comes from the Italian per il sole, for the sun. Frames from the Ratti era are among the most collected Italian sunglasses.
