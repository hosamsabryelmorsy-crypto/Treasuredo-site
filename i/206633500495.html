Brand heritage

Optyl, a lightweight heat-memory material, was developed in Austria in 1964 by the Anger company (later Carrera). In the 1970s it allowed designers to make large, sculptural frames that stay light on the face, and Cobra Optyl frames of the period are now collected for their bold shapes and colours.
