About this item

A dramatic 1970s Persol-Ratti oversized round butterfly frame, model P203-10, moulded in Optyl. The translucent front fades from cognac amber across the brow to olive-honey at the bottom of the rims and along the temples, and carries the original rose/plum tinted lenses. The inner temples are printed "Persol-RATTI" with the optyl logo on one side and "ITALY P203-10" on the other; the outer temples carry a small oval badge.

Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by Giuseppe Ratti; the name comes from "per il sole" (for the sun). In the 1960s-70s some Persol-Ratti frames were made in Optyl, the lightweight, hypoallergenic thermoset material patented by Austrian Wilhelm Anger in 1964.
