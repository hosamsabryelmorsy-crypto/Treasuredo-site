About this item

Unused vintage Viennaline sunglasses in Optyl: a bold oversized soft-square frame in translucent olive green fading from deep olive at the brow to light khaki at the bottom, with thick sculpted temples and grey gradient lenses. The inner temple carries the moulded "VIENNALINE" name and the Optyl badge.

Brand heritage

Viennaline is an Austrian brand founded by Wilhelm Anger, the founder of Carrera and inventor of Optyl, a lightweight epoxy-resin frame material patented in 1964 that keeps its shape and is hypoallergenic.
