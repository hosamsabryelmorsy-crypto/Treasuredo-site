Brand heritage

Cazal was founded in Germany in 1975 by designer Cari Zalloni and his partner Günter Böttcher; the name joins the first syllables of Zalloni's names. Its bold frames became icons of 1980s street style and hip-hop culture, and vintage German-made Cazal is keenly collected today.
