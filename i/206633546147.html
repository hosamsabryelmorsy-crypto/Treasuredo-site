About this item

An original mid-century French oval frame marked "SOLARITE FRANCE" on the inner temple: thick black acetate with a heavy hooded brow that gives the famous "sleepy" look, small rivet details at the hinges and solid blue lenses.

Brand heritage

France has a long eyewear tradition centred on the Jura town of Oyonnax, which became the country's frame-making capital. By the 1950s French workshops were producing hand-finished acetate frames in bold shapes, and original mid-century French frames are prized today for their material quality and design.
