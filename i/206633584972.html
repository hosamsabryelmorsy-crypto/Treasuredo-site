About this item

An unused pair of vintage Samco oversized square sunglasses, still carrying the original red Samco seal hang tag and the factory sticker on the lens. The translucent acetate front melts from soft mint green across the bridge into blush pink along the top and bottom rims, and the sculpted green temple shields fade to pink toward the tips. Warm brown gradient lenses complete a true 1970s glamour look.

Brand heritage

Samco was an Italian eyewear maker whose acetate sunglasses were sold widely from the mid-century through the 1970s and 1980s; its frames are known to collectors for bold colours and playful shapes, often marked with the red Samco seal tag seen here.
