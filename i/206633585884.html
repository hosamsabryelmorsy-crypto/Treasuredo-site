About this item

A large 1980s Daytona aviator in gold-tone metal with a double bridge whose top bar is enamelled burgundy, matching the burgundy enamel inlays on the temples. Brand heritage

Daytona was an Italian-made eyewear label of the 1980s and 1990s known for sporty, motoring-inspired metal frames; this pair is clearly marked "FRAME ITALY".
