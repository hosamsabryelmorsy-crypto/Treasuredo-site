About this item

A petite, sharply upswept 1950s French cat-eye in warm honey tortoise acetate, trimmed with two small star-shaped metal rivets at each upper corner and fitted with light amber lenses. The inner temples are stamped "L&S" and "Frame France".

Brand heritage

"Frame France" was the export marking used on French-made acetate frames in the 1950s, when the workshops of the Jura region supplied cat-eye frames around the world; this pair carries the L&S maker's stamp.
