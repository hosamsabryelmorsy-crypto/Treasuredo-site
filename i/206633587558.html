About this item

An unworn, new-old-stock pair of ModyLook Paris sunglasses with a huge sculpted square-butterfly front. Brand heritage

ModyLook was a Paris fashion-eyewear label whose frames were made in France during the era of bold oversized acetate sunglasses. We could not find a documented company history, so we only state what the frame itself shows: Paris branding and French manufacture.
