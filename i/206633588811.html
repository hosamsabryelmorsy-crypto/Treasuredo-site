About this item

An unused 1970s Jacques Fath "Vanoise/7" sunglass with large round lavender-purple lenses in a thick olive-green, khaki and brown marbled acetate front. The wide sculpted temples are translucent honey-amber marbled acetate, each with a gold-tone "JF" monogram on the outside. Inner temples are stamped "JACQUES FATH" and "VANOISE/7 FRAME FRANCE".

Brand heritage

Jacques Fath (1912-1954) was a Paris couturier whose house is considered, with Dior and Balmain, one of the three dominant influences on post-war haute couture. The house name later appeared on licensed accessories, including French-made eyewear like this pair.
