About this item

An unused Asdor Patent sunglass with large softly squared lenses in solid dark grey. The translucent acetate front fades from smoky brown at the brow to pale olive at the base; the translucent grey temples show the wire core and carry engraved rings near the hinge. The temple is stamped with the Asdor spade logo, "ASDOR PATENT" and "52 18".

Brand heritage

Asdor Patent frames were made in Italy in the 1970s and 1980s and are far less common today than the big Italian names of the period. Each frame carries the brand's small spade logo.
