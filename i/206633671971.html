About this item

A true mid-century cat eye frame in glossy black acetate, crowned with polished silver-tone metal brow caps that are hand-engraved with leaf scrollwork and set with small decorative rivets. The slim silver-tone metal temples carry matching engraving and finish in translucent amber-yellow tips. Fitted with soft rose-pink tinted lenses for a feminine, retro "librarian" look.

Brand heritage

This frame comes from the golden age of cat eye eyewear in the 1950s, when black acetate fronts with engraved metal brows and temples were the height of fashion. The inner temple is signed in script (the mark is small and partly worn; it appears to read "Montex"), and the front is stamped with the size 46/20 in an oval cartouche, typical of the period.
