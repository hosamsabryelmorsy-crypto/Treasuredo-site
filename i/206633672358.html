About this item

A big, bold Samco statement frame in transparent acetate that fades from soft pink at the centre to pale champagne at the outer rims. A cut-out keyhole detail sits above the bridge, and the dramatic wavy temples curve down in pink and sweep up to grey-green tips, with an open loop near the hinge. Dark grey lenses complete a true Mod / jet-set look for men or women.

Brand heritage

Samco was an Italian eyewear maker whose acetate sunglasses were sold widely from the mid-century through the 1970s; its frames are known to collectors for bold colours and playful, sculptural shapes like this one.
