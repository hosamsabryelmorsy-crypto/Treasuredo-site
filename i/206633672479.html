Brand heritage

Cobra frames were made in Austria in Optyl, the lightweight heat-cured material patented in 1964 by Wilhelm Anger, founder of Carrera. Optyl is about 20% lighter than acetate, hypoallergenic and keeps its shape, which is why Austrian Optyl frames of the 1970s are prized by collectors today.
