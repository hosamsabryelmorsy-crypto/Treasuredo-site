About this item

An unused pair of Samco oversized square sunglasses that still carries the original red Samco seal hang tag. Light brown gradient lenses complete a glamorous Mod / jet-set look for men or women.

Brand heritage

Samco was an Italian eyewear maker whose acetate sunglasses were sold widely from the mid-century through the 1970s; its frames are known to collectors for bold colours and playful, sculptural shapes, often marked with the red Samco seal tag seen here.
