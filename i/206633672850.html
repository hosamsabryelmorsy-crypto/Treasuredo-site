About this item

A striking 1980s Nazareno Gabrielli design: a flat, straight brow bar in brown and taupe mottled pattern sits over thin gold-tone metal half-moon rims, with slim gold-tone metal temples and adjustable nose pads. The lens rims are engraved with the Nazareno Gabrielli name, and the brown lenses complete a refined, distinctive Italian look.

Brand heritage

Nazareno Gabrielli is an Italian fashion house founded in 1907 in Tolentino, in the Marche region, originally known for fine leather goods. In the 1980s the brand extended into accessories, including sunglasses made in Italy that combined classic shapes with refined materials.
