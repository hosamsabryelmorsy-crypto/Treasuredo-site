About this item

A rare, unused pair of 1950s French cat eye sunglasses in warm honey tortoise acetate with a clear crystal inner layer. The long, narrow upswept lenses in smoky gray and the slim tapered temples, stamped FRANCE with a gold-tone wire core, give a sleek, glamorous mid-century silhouette.

Brand heritage

France, and above all the Jura frame-making town of Oyonnax, was the heart of European acetate eyewear in the 1950s. Unsigned French workshop frames of this era, marked only 'Frame France' or 'France', are prized today for their thick hand-finished acetate and bold mid-century shapes.
