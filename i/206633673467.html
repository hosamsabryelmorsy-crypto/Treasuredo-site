Brand heritage

Helena Rubinstein (1872-1965) built one of the first great international beauty houses, with salons in London, Paris and New York. The house name also appeared on French-made eyewear in the 1970s and 1980s; this pair is signed 'Helena Rubinstein PARIS' and 'MADE IN FRANCE' on the temples.
