Brand heritage

Charles Jourdan founded his house in Romans-sur-Isere, France, in 1921 and it became one of the great names in French luxury footwear, opening its Paris boutique in 1957. At its peak around 1979 the brand extended into accessories including sunglasses, sold under the Charles Jourdan Paris name.
