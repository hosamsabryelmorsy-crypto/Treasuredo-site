Brand heritage

Carrera was founded in Austria in 1956 by Wilhelm Anger and named after the Carrera Panamericana road race. From 1978 it produced the Porsche Design eyewear collection with F. A. Porsche, designer of the original Porsche 911, bringing sports-car engineering to sunglasses. Frames from this era are now sought after by collectors.
