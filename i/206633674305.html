About this item

A bold, unused pair of 1950s French cat eye sunglasses carved from thick glossy black acetate, with sharply swept-up outer wings and violet-purple lenses. Two small gold-tone rivets decorate each outer corner. A striking and rare mid-century design.

Brand heritage

The upswept cat eye shape, often credited to designer Altina Schinasi in the late 1930s, defined women's eyewear in the 1950s. France, and above all the Jura frame-making town of Oyonnax, was the heart of European acetate eyewear in the 1950s. Unsigned French workshop frames of this era, marked only 'Frame France' or 'France', are prized today for their thick hand-finished acetate and bold mid-century shapes.
