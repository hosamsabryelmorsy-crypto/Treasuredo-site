About this item

An authentic pair of vintage Paloma Picasso model 3845 colour 31 sunglasses, made in Austria from lightweight Optyl. The translucent honey-amber oval front has a smooth moulded bridge, and the temples are decorated with a red and gold marbled pattern and the gold Paloma Picasso signature. Dark gray-green lenses.

Brand heritage

Paloma Picasso, the daughter of Pablo Picasso and Francoise Gilot, became a celebrated designer in her own right, best known for her jewellery for Tiffany & Co. from 1980. Her name also appeared on a line of designer eyewear in the 1980s and 1990s, many of the frames made in Austria from Optyl.
