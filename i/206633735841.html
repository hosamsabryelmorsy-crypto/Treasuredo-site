Brand heritage

Germano Gambini comes from a family of opticians whose first shop opened in Bologna in 1912. He joined the business in the early 1960s, was one of the first in Italy to treat glasses as a fashion item, imported Pierre Cardin designer eyewear and designed many Cardin models before building his own Made in Italy collections.
