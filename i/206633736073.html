About this item

A deadstock 1960s combination frame sold under the IOM name, model No. 917. The soft oval front is mottled brown tortoise acetate, joined by broad gold-plated end pieces that wrap around the rims and flow into flat, finely textured gold-plated temples with black acetate tips. Warm amber lenses give the classic mid-century driving-glass look.

Brand heritage

IOM sunglasses were made in Hong Kong during the 1960s eyewear boom and are found today as unworn old stock in metal and combination styles. Little documented company history survives, which makes surviving NOS pairs a niche collector find.
