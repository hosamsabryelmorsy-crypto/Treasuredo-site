About this item

A refined 1970s aviator from Rodenstock's Junge Linie (young line) range, model 137. The thin gold-tone metal frame carries a pale mint-green enamel trim along the upper rims, with a straight double bridge, slim wire temples ending in cream tips, and clear adjustable nose pads. Solid brown lenses.

Brand heritage

Rodenstock was founded in Würzburg in 1877 by Josef Rodenstock and moved to Munich in 1883. It is one of Germany's oldest optical houses and introduced its 'Florida' aviator-shaped glasses in the 1930s, making the aviator part of its heritage.
