About this item

A glamorous oversized square frame from Ted Lapidus Paris, model TL 1501. Glossy black acetate is set with a fine gold glitter border inside each rim, hammered gold-tone bars and a column of rhinestones on each end piece, and four rhinestones on the bridge. Grey gradient lenses. Temples signed TED LAPIDUS PARIS, MADE IN FRANCE and TL 1501.

Brand heritage

Edmond 'Ted' Lapidus (1929-2008), son of a Paris tailor, opened his own fashion house in 1951 and became one of the innovators of 1960s-70s fashion, known for bringing safari and military details and unisex tailoring into couture. His house later expanded into accessories such as these French-made sunglasses.
