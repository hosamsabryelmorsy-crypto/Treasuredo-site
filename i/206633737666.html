About this item

A classic round panto frame by F.O.C.A., model 684, in translucent amber-honey tortoise acetate with a keyhole-style bridge and two silver chevron inlays on each temple. Brown gradient lenses. Temples stamped FOCA-684, MADE IN ITALY and 50-22/3615.

Brand heritage

F.O.C.A. was founded in 1946 in Cadore, the historic heart of Italian eyewear, and was known for hand-finished frames made from acetate aged for months for density and durability. The company closed in 1990, and its remaining deadstock is now sought after by collectors.
