Brand heritage

Viennaline was founded in Austria by Wilhelm Anger, the founder of Carrera and inventor of Optyl - a heat-hardened frame material lighter than acetate with a memory effect that helps frames keep their shape. Viennaline frames were among the best-selling European fashion eyewear of their time.
