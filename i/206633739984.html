About this item

A characterful round panto frame by George Hogg, model 04-85, in a matte translucent mustard-yellow acetate with a keyhole bridge, two small rivet dots at each front corner and an oval HOG logo plaque on each temple. Brown lenses give a warm, intellectual 1980s look. It comes on its original George Hogg display card printed with the model and size.

Brand heritage

George Hogg was a 1980s fashion eyewear label whose frames were made in Italy; the inner temples read GEORGE HOGG FRAME ITALY together with the model and size.
