About this item

An unused pair of vintage IOM Solex No. 912 sunglasses. The oversized butterfly front is cut from translucent striped smoke-brown and cream acetate with flared lower outer corners, set on wide flat brushed silver metal temples with black tips. Classic green lenses complete a true jet-set look of the late 1960s and early 1970s. The temple carries the script Solex signature and the tip is marked No. 912.

Brand heritage

IOM was a Hong Kong eyewear maker active in the 1960s and 1970s whose unworn old-stock frames are now sought after by vintage collectors; Solex was the model line name used on this pair.
