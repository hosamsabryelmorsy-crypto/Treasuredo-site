Brand heritage

L&S is a French frame mark found on mid-century acetate frames stamped Frame France. French acetate eyewear has been made for generations in the Jura region of eastern France, around Oyonnax and Morez, where mid-century workshops cut and polished frames by hand and stamped them simply "Made in France" or "Frame France".
