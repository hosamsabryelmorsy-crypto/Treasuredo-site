Warm champagne-gold metal, generous oval lenses and tortoiseshell-pattern temple tips give these Nazareno Gabrielli sunglasses a distinctive Italian elegance for men and women. Brand heritage

Nazareno Gabrielli is an Italian leather-goods house founded in 1907 in Tolentino, in the Marche region; in the 1980s and 1990s its name also appeared on licensed Italian-made eyewear.
