About this item

A clean American half-rim aviator by Shuron from its Facemaker line. A silver-tone metal chassis with a double bridge and adjustable nose pads carries dark brown brow rims over the top of each lens, while the lower edge is held by a fine wire for a light, rimless look. Slim dark brown temples are stamped "Facemaker by Shuron 140 U.S.A." on both sides. Solid brown lenses.

Brand heritage

Shuron is one of America's oldest eyewear makers, with roots in 1865, and is best known for the Ronsir browline frame of 1947, worn across the 1950s and 1960s. Its frames were made in the USA and remain sought after by collectors of American optical history.
