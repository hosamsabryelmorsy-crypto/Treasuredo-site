Brand heritage

Longines was founded in 1832 in Saint-Imier, Switzerland. Its winged hourglass emblem, registered in 1889, is one of the oldest registered trademarks still in use. In the 1980s and 1990s Longines-branded eyewear was produced in Germany by Metzler. The titanium "Titan" frames were the top of that line.
