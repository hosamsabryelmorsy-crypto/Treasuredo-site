About this watch

A vintage Alfred Dunhill Millennium in two-tone steel and gold tone. The round brushed steel case with its integrated hooded lugs and protected crown is the signature Millennium shape, framed by a slim gold-tone bezel ring and finished with a fluted gold-tone crown. The dial is the clean, less common version without numerals: a gold sunburst (radiating guilloche) dial carrying only the boxed Dunhill "d-bar" emblem at 12 and a framed date window at 6, with slim gold baton hands. It sits on the matching Dunhill two-tone bar-link bracelet with gold-tone stripes and a clasp signed with the Dunhill "d" medallion, and comes in its original Dunhill box.

Brand heritage

Alfred Dunhill built his London house in the early twentieth century on fine smoking accessories, lighters and gentlemen's goods, and the "d-bar" logo became one of the best known marks of British luxury. The Millennium collection, with its distinctive round case and integrated lugs, was one of Dunhill's most recognisable watch designs of the 1980s and 1990s, with Swiss-made movements.
