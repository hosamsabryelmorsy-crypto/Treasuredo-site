About this watch

A vintage Seiko 5 automatic from the 6309 family, case reference 6309-6240 (stamped on the caseback). It is fitted with an eye-catching silver dial with a radial grey-and-silver checkerboard ("target") pattern framed by gold chapter rings, applied gold SEIKO logo and Seiko 5 shield, gold faceted marker at 12, gold batons at 6 and 9, lumed gold hands and a blue/black day-date window at 3 o'clock. The cushion-shaped steel case wears on an integrated Seiko stainless steel bracelet with a signed clasp.

Brand heritage

Seiko launched the Seiko 5 line in 1963 as an affordable, robust automatic watch built around five key features: automatic winding, day-date display, water resistance, a recessed crown at 4 o'clock and a durable case and bracelet. The 6309 automatic calibre family, introduced in the mid-1970s, powered a huge range of Seiko 5 models and is known for its reliability.
