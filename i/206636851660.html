About this watch

A charming mid-century Swiss complete-calendar watch signed Arly. The cream dial, now warmed by an even, honest patina, shows the day (THU) and month (MAY) in two windows beneath the script Arly logo, the date on an outer 1–31 track read by a central pointer hand with a blue arrow tip, and a moonphase with starry blue disc at 6 o'clock combined with the small seconds. Copper-brown Arabic numerals, gold leaf hands and the printed “17 JEWELS” complete the classic 1950s look. The 33.5mm gold plated case has a stainless steel screw-down back, with small recessed correctors on the case sides for setting the calendar, and comes on a gold-tone Fixoflex expanding bracelet.

Brand heritage

Arly was one of the many independent Swiss watch brands of the post-war era that built affordable, well-made mechanical watches around Swiss ebauche movements. The triple calendar with moonphase (day, month, pointer date and lunar cycle) was the showpiece complication of the 1940s and 1950s: famous houses such as Patek Philippe, Universal Genève and Movado used it in their prestige models, while smaller Swiss brands such as Arly brought the same “complete calendar” layout within reach of everyday buyers. Today these period moonphase calendars are sought after for their busy, beautiful dials and the charm of a mechanical calendar that you set by hand.
