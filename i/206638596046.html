About this item

A bold single-lens shield (mask) sunglass from Ted Lapidus Paris, model TL202 in colour 1020. The green-grey shield lenses, divided by a slim black centre bar, sit under a thin brow bar finished in a marbled blue, purple and pink pattern, framed at each end by open gold-tone loops engraved "TED LAPIDUS". The thick glossy black temples carry "TED LAPIDUS PARIS" on the outside and "TL202 1020" and "MADE IN FRANCE CE" on the inside.

Brand heritage

Ted Lapidus (1929-2008) was a Paris couturier who opened his fashion house in the 1950s and became known in the 1960s and 1970s for bringing couture ideas into ready-to-wear, including the safari jacket and unisex styles. His name was later licensed for accessories, fragrance and eyewear, with frames made in France.
