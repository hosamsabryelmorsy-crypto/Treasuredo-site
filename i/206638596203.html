A vintage pair of Emmanuelle Khanh Paris sunglasses in a dramatic cat-eye shape. The glossy black acetate front has a broad, sculpted brow with two parallel grooves running along the top and sharply upswept wing tips, a keyhole-free saddle bridge and almond-shaped lower rims. It is fitted with solid grey-green (G-15 style) lenses. Each temple carries the silver "EK" monogram near the hinge, and one temple is printed "EMMANUELLE KHANH PARIS" on the inside. A bold, feminine shape with a true 1950s-inspired silhouette.

Brand heritage

Emmanuelle Khanh (1937-2017) was a French fashion designer and a pioneer of French ready-to-wear in the 1960s. She later became famous for her eyewear: from the 1970s her oversized, sculptural acetate frames, many handmade in France, were worn by celebrities and became icons of Parisian style. Vintage Emmanuelle Khanh frames, recognised by the EK monogram, are sought after by eyewear collectors.
