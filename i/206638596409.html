About this item

A genuine pair of mid-century French sunglasses with a bold oval shape. The frame is a warm, translucent honey and amber tortoiseshell acetate with deep striping, thick rounded rims and a sculpted acetate bridge. The dark grey-green lenses still carry the original small triangular black and silver sticker reading "VERRES Bi-Filtrants", the label French opticians used on period sun lenses. The temples are reinforced with a visible metal core and open on riveted metal hinges, with two pin rivets showing on each end piece, a construction typical of French frames of the 1950s and 1960s.

Brand heritage

France, and especially the Jura region around Oyonnax and Morez, was the centre of European eyewear making in the mid-20th century. Many fine acetate frames of that era were made without a maker's name on the frame, and the "Verres Bi-Filtrants" lens sticker is a period French marking. This pair carries no maker's name, so it is offered honestly as unbranded vintage French eyewear.
