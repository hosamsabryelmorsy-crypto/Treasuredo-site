About this item

Elegant women's sunglasses by Asdor of Italy, model 9902. A feminine oval front in translucent champagne-yellow crystal acetate with fine amber and green flecks at the outer corners, finished at each hinge with an open-work gold-tone ornament of stacked loops and polished beads. Slim gold-tone metal temples with a lattice detail near the hinge end in striped honey acetate tips, each set with a small heart emblem. Dark blue-grey lenses complete the look. Inner temples marked "52□18-135 9902" and "By Italy ASDOR", next to a worn script signature.

Brand heritage

Asdor was an Italian eyewear maker, known to collectors mainly for its "Asdor Patent" frames of the 1970s and 1980s. Its decorative Italian sunglasses are hard to find today.
