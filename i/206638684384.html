About this item

A vintage pair of Christian Dior women's sunglasses, model 2486 in colour 41, with a frame made in Germany. The large soft-octagonal metal front has thin rims finished in brown havana tortoise enamel, and a raised polished gold rope-twist decoration that runs along the top outer corner of each lens and down the outer edges to the hinges. A single straight gold bridge, clear nose pads on adjustable pad arms, slim gold temples with the CD logo near the hinge and tortoise temple tips complete the look. Fitted with olive-brown gradient lenses, dark at the top and fading to pale at the bottom.

Brand heritage

Christian Dior founded his couture house in Paris in 1946 and changed post-war fashion with the "New Look" of 1947. Dior eyewear followed from the late 1960s and became one of the best-known designer eyewear lines of the 1970s and 1980s, with many of its metal and Optyl frames produced in Germany. Vintage German-made Dior sunglasses are widely collected today for their quality and glamorous designs.
