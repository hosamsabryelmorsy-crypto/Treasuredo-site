About this item

A bold, wide pair of vintage browline sunglasses made in Italy. The thick flat-top brow bar is glossy black acetate with squared, slightly upswept outer corners, each set with a small silver-tone arrowhead inlay. The lenses sit in fine pale gold-tone metal lower rims for the classic half-rim "clubmaster" look, in a heavier mid-century Italian style. Solid neutral grey lenses. The thick black acetate temples are fitted with riveted metal hinges, two silver pin-dot rivets on each end piece, and gold-tone metal plaques on the inside: one stamped "MADE IN ITALY", the other stamped with the number "600/53".

Brand heritage

Italy has been a centre of fine acetate eyewear making since the mid-20th century, with small workshops in the Belluno and Cadore region producing hand-finished frames for many labels. This frame carries no maker's name, only the "MADE IN ITALY" plaque, so it is offered honestly as unbranded vintage Italian eyewear.
