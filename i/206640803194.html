About this item

A bold, wide pair of vintage browline sunglasses made in Italy. The thick flat-top brow bar is black acetate with a matte finish on the front, with squared, slightly upswept outer corners, each set with a small silver-tone arrowhead inlay. The lenses sit in fine pale gold-tone metal lower rims for the classic half-rim "clubmaster" look, in a heavier mid-century Italian style. Warm brown gradient lenses, darker at the top and fading to a light peach at the bottom. The thick black acetate temples have riveted metal hinges, two silver pin-dot rivets on each end piece, and gold-tone metal plaques on the inside stamped "MADE IN ITALY".

Brand heritage

Italy has been a centre of fine acetate eyewear making since the mid-20th century, with small workshops in the Belluno and Cadore region producing hand-finished frames for many labels. This frame carries no maker's name, only the "MADE IN ITALY" plaques, so it is offered honestly as unbranded vintage Italian eyewear.
