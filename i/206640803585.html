Brand heritage

Tiffany Lunettes was a luxury eyewear line made in Italy in the late 1980s and 1990s. It is known for heavy 23K gold-plated and platinum-plated metal frames with jewellery-style details, and today it is a sought-after name among collectors of vintage designer eyewear.
