About this item

A glamorous pair of 1970s women's oversized butterfly sunglasses. The clear frame fades from pale champagne at the bridge to translucent amber-brown at the outer edges, where the flared end pieces carry engraved gold-tone leaf plates, a row of tiny gold dots and a small iridescent rhinestone on each side. Thin textured silver-tone metal temples with brown tips and solid dark grey lenses complete the look.
