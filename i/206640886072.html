About this item

Statement 1970s oversized sunglasses by Samco of Italy, never worn and still carrying the original red Samco sunburst seal hang tag and the "Cristallo Filtrante / Filterglass / Filtering Glass" lens sticker. The large rounded-square front is in translucent striped acetate, warm amber bands over pale ice-blue, with a keyhole bridge featuring an open cut-out above the nose. Wide sculpted temples with a rectangular open slot at the hinge sweep into dramatic curved arms. Flat light olive-brown glass lenses. Inner temple stamped "ITALY".

Brand heritage
