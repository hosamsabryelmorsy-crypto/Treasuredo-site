About this item

A refined pair of vintage Jacques Fath Paris sunglasses in glossy black acetate, made in France. The frame has a flat, straight brow line that dips into a shallow V at the bridge, with large squared lenses in a soft brown-mauve gradient tint (darker at the top, lighter at the bottom). Each outer front corner carries a rectangular gold-tone metal plaque engraved with a script monogram, joined to the black acetate temples by metal hinges. The left temple is printed "JACQUES FATH Paris"; the right temple reads "MADE IN FRANCE 3020-O SH 07" with the maker's logo.

Brand heritage

Jacques Fath (1912-1954) was one of the leading Paris couturiers of the post-war years, celebrated for his glamorous, feminine designs alongside Christian Dior and Pierre Balmain. After his death the Jacques Fath name continued on licensed accessories, including eyewear made in France, which is now sought after by collectors of vintage French designer frames.
