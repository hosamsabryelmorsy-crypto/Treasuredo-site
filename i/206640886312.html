About this item

A striking pair of oversized eight-sided sunglasses, the bold geometric shape that defined late 1960s and 1970s glamour. The thick acetate frame is cut with faceted octagonal rims and shades from deep tortoise brown along the top and outer edges to a clear pale champagne at the bottom and across the straight bridge. The solid brown lenses fill the large 58 mm octagons. The temples carry the same brown-to-clear fade, are reinforced with a visible metal core, and open on riveted metal hinges, with two pin rivets showing on each front corner. The inside of one temple is stamped "MADE IN FRANCE".

Brand heritage

France, and especially the Jura region around Oyonnax and Morez, was the centre of European acetate eyewear making in the 20th century. Many fine frames of that era left the workshops without a maker's name on them. This pair carries only the "MADE IN FRANCE" stamp and no maker's name, so it is offered honestly as unbranded vintage French eyewear.
