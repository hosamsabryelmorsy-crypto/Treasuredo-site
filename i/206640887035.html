About this item

A glamorous pair of vintage Nina Ricci Paris sunglasses, model 1735, handmade in France. The large rounded oval lenses sit in a clear champagne-crystal acetate frame, accented along the upper outer rim by a red band set with a row of sparkling clear rhinestones and fine gold stripes. Each hinge carries the gold Nina Ricci "N" logo and a small cluster of rhinestones, and the red temples continue the line of stones. Solid rose-brown tinted lenses.

Brand heritage

Nina Ricci is a Parisian couture house founded in 1932 by Maria "Nina" Ricci and her son Robert Ricci. Known for its feminine elegance and for the fragrance L'Air du Temps, the house also produced fine eyewear handmade in France, which is now sought after by collectors of vintage designer sunglasses.
