About this item

A glamorous pair of true mid-century cat eye sunglasses in glossy solid black acetate. The upswept outer corners of the front and the front of each temple are decorated with silver-tone metal inlay panels set with clear rhinestones and framed by a gold-tone dotted, scalloped border, so the sparkle continues across the hinge when the glasses are worn. Oval brown tinted lenses, a softly shaped keyhole-style bridge and thick black temples. The temples are fitted with riveted metal hinge plates (two pin rivets on each side), a construction typical of 1950s European frames. The inside of one temple still carries an old paper shop label handwritten "2300/177 S".

Brand heritage

The jewelled cat eye frame is the defining eyewear look of the 1950s, when opticians and frame makers on both sides of the Atlantic decorated acetate frames with rhinestones, metal filigree and inlays. This frame carries no maker's name or country stamp, so it is offered honestly as an unsigned vintage piece.
