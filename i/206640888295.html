About this item

A classic mid-century pair of French cat eye sunglasses. The glossy solid black acetate frame sweeps up into pointed wing tips, each upper corner set with two small silver rivet dots, and the sculpted bridge has moulded acetate nose pads. The flat lenses carry a rich rose-red tint, a striking period colour. The temples open on riveted metal hinges and are stamped "MADE IN FRANCE" on the inside.

Brand heritage

France, and especially the Jura region around Oyonnax and Morez, was the centre of European eyewear making in the mid-20th century, and many fine acetate frames of that era were made without a maker's name on the frame. This pair is stamped only "MADE IN FRANCE", so it is offered honestly as unbranded vintage French eyewear.
