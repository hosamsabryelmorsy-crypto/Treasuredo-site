About this item

A handsome pair of vintage German rectangular frames by OWP, in thick honey-brown striped tortoise acetate with darker brown temples. The squared, slightly upswept front has a sculpted keyhole-style bridge, and each end piece carries a pale gold-tone metal plaque engraved with a bold black geometric pattern that continues across the hinge onto the temple. Fitted with light blue tinted lenses. The inside of one temple is stamped with the boxed "OWP" maker's mark, the other with "50/20" and "Made in Germany".

Brand heritage

OWP takes its initials from Optische Werke Passau, the eyewear manufacturer founded in 1947 in Passau, Bavaria, which still designs and makes spectacle frames and sunglasses in Germany today.
