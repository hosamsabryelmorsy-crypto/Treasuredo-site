About this item

A classic square trapezoid sunglass by INDO of Spain, model Darling, colour 150 Negro. The thick glossy black acetate frame has the bold flared brow line of the great mid-century American square frames, finished with two silver lozenge rivets on each side of the front and on the temples, and fitted with dark grey lenses. Stamped inside the temples "DARLING 150 NEGRO" and "INDO FRAME SPAIN".

Brand heritage

INDO is a Spanish optical company founded in Barcelona in the 1930s and grew into one of Spain's best-known makers of lenses and frames. Its vintage acetate frames, stamped "INDO Frame Spain", are collected today for their solid build and classic shapes.
