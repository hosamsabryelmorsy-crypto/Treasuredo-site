About this item

A genuine pair of vintage Paco Rabanne Paris women's sunglasses, model 111. The large oval frame is made of clear crystal (champagne-tinted) plastic, shading to brown along the upper brow line, with a dark top rim punctuated by small gold-tone bars and striped brown and burgundy accents on the outer corners. The pale light-blue lenses give a soft 1980s look. The crystal temples show their gold-tone metal core and carry an inset burgundy panel with the cream Paco Rabanne script signature on the outside. Inner temples are printed "paco rabanne - 111 Paris" and "135 marro".

Brand heritage

Paco Rabanne (Francisco Rabaneda Cuervo, 1934-2023) was a Spanish-born designer who founded his Paris fashion house in 1966, famous for his space-age dresses made of metal and plastic discs. The house expanded into fragrance and accessories, and vintage Paco Rabanne Paris eyewear from the 1970s and 1980s is collected for its bold, architectural designs.
