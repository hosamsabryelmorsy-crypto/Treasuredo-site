About this item

A vintage pair of Persol Ratti sunglasses from the "Manager" line, stamped "Manager 102". The bold flat-top navigator front is cut from translucent amber / cognac tortoise acetate with a straight brow line and a sculpted bridge, fitted with brown gradient lenses that are darker at the top and fade lighter towards the bottom. The temples carry a gold-tone hinge plate with a black inlay and arrow-shaped tip, and the outer temple is stamped "PERSOL" in the classic block lettering. A strong 1970s / 1980s Italian design in a generous 56 mm size.

Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by Giuseppe Ratti, and its name comes from "per il sole" (for the sun). The brand began by making protective eyewear for pilots and sports drivers and became one of the most collected names in Italian eyewear. Frames signed "Persol Ratti" date from the period when the company was still run by the Ratti family, which makes them especially sought after by vintage collectors.
