About this item

A bold 1970s-style men's navigator from Maison De Frame Ltd, model "Extravagant". The squared lenses sit in thin satin silver-tone metal rims under a flat double brow bar, joined by a sculpted H-shaped bridge with a wide grooved centre post. The flat stepped temples with their zig-zag notch are a signature 70s detail, finished with black temple tips. Light grey tinted lenses, comfortable for everyday wear.

Brand heritage

Maison De Frame Ltd (also seen as Maison De Frames) marketed eyewear frames made by factories in Japan, Italy and Hong Kong; its name is found on many vintage metal and acetate frames from the 1970s and 1980s. This pair is stamped "Frame Japan", from the period when Japanese makers were known for precise metal frame work.
