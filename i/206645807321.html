About this item

A refined vintage optical frame by Neostyle, the German eyewear house; this frame's lenses still carry the original "NEOSTYLE® Personality Design" demo print with the brand's bust emblem. The small oval full-rim front is finished in a soft matte gold tone, with a raised keyhole-style bridge and amber translucent nose pads on adjustable pad arms. The real charm is in the temples: a pointed oval cut-out framing a cross-hatched gold insert near the hinge, a fine engraved wave pattern running along the arm, and round engraved medallion temple tips. A delicate, jewellery-like frame from the classic Neostyle era.

Brand heritage

Neostyle is a German eyewear brand, well known at opticians through the 1980s and 1990s for fashionable, high-quality metal and acetate frames sold by opticians worldwide under its "Personality Design" signature, with collections such as College, Academic, Boutique and Dynasty.
