About this item

A genuine vintage pair of French sunglasses with a soft, rounded cat eye silhouette, cut from thick light blonde honey tortoise acetate with warm amber mottling. Straight, solid temples with a fine wire core, riveted metal hinges and two silver pin rivets at each front corner show the hand-finished construction of classic French eyewear. Brand heritage

The frame carries no maker's name, only the "MADE IN FRANCE" stamp, so it is sold as unbranded. France, and the Jura region around Oyonnax and Morez in particular, has been a centre of handmade acetate eyewear for generations; the wire-cored temples, riveted hinges and pin-rivet details are typical of French frames of the 1950s and 1960s.
