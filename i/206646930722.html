About this item

A striking pair of vintage sunglasses from Ted Lapidus Paris, model TL 38 in colour 03. The thin gold-tone metal front has a flat-top brow bar with a finely ribbed double bridge and angular, cut-corner geometric lenses in a brown gradient tint. Open rectangular gold-tone hinge blocks lead into thick acetate temples in a marbled burgundy-red and teal mosaic pattern with gold veining, a gold wire core and a small square gold-tone plaque near the hinge. The temples are printed "TED LAPIDUS PARIS" on the outside and "TL 38 03" on the inside.

Brand heritage

Ted Lapidus (1929-2008) was a Paris couturier who opened his fashion house in the 1950s and became known in the 1960s and 1970s for bringing couture ideas into ready-to-wear, including the safari jacket and unisex styles. His name was later licensed for accessories, fragrance and eyewear, and Ted Lapidus Paris sunglasses are now sought-after vintage designer pieces.
