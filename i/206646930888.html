Brand heritage

Marc by Marc Jacobs was the younger, more playful diffusion line of American designer Marc Jacobs, launched in 2001 and folded into the main Marc Jacobs label in 2015. Its eyewear was produced under licence by Safilo and is known for bright colours and bold acetate shapes.
