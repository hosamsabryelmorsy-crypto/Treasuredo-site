Brand heritage

Silhouette was founded in 1964 in Linz, Austria, by Anneliese and Arnold Schmied, and still designs and makes its eyewear in Linz. The brand became known for light, fashion-forward frames and in the 1970s and 1980s produced some of the most distinctive metal and aviator styles of the era.
