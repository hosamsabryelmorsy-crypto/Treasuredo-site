About this item

A classic pair of vintage men's pilot sunglasses by Cottet, model 36740, made in France. The full-rim frame is shiny gold-tone metal with squared, slightly octagonal lenses, a flat top bar with a brown inlay and a lower bridge (double-bridge look) and a translucent peach-amber saddle nose bridge. Solid brown lenses give an even, warm tint. Thin straight gold-tone temples finish in black tips. The pair comes on its original Cottet store tag card (printed "36-740", size "54", "MADE IN FRANCE") and with a burgundy Cottet hard case.

Brand heritage

Cottet is a historic eyewear name with French roots in Morez, the Jura town at the heart of French spectacle making. Its metal pilot frames of the 1970s and 1980s, stamped "Cottet FRAME FRANCE", are well known to vintage eyewear collectors for their clean geometric lines and quality construction.
