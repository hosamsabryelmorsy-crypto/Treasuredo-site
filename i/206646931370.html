Each hinge carries a silver temple bar with the black and white Playboy rabbit-head emblem. A bold 1970s-80s look that is still very wearable today.

Brand heritage

In the 1970s and 1980s Playboy licensed its eyewear to Optyl of Austria, the firm founded by Wilhelm Anger, who developed Optyl: a lightweight heat-cured epoxy material that holds its shape and is comfortable to wear. Optyl also made Carrera and Viennaline frames, and its Playboy line is now collected for its bold designs and Austrian build quality.
