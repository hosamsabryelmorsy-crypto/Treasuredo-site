About this item

A striking pair of mid-century French sunglasses with a long, sleek almond cat eye shape that sweeps to sharp pointed outer corners. The frame is a warm, translucent reddish-brown tortoiseshell acetate with thick, sculpted rims and a moulded acetate bridge. The wide 66 mm lenses are a solid dark grey-green. The temples open on riveted metal hinges, with two pin rivets showing on each end piece, and one temple is stamped "MADE IN FRANCE", a construction typical of French frames of the 1950s and 1960s.

Brand heritage

France, and especially the Jura region around Oyonnax and Morez, was the centre of European eyewear making in the mid-20th century. Many fine acetate frames of that era were made without a maker's name on the frame. This pair is stamped only "MADE IN FRANCE" and carries no maker's name, so it is offered honestly as unbranded vintage French eyewear.
