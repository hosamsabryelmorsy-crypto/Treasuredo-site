About this item

An original pair of Alitalia "Classic 500" aviator sunglasses from the 1980s, made in Italy. The frame has a pale gold metal outer rim with a translucent red inner rim around each lens, a flat top brow bar marked "CLASSIC 500" and the line's signature short vertical centre post between the brow bar and the bridge. The lenses are an amber-brown gradient, darker at the top and fading lighter toward the bottom. Clear plastic saddle nose pads sit on the bridge, and the thin gold temples have black ribbed grip sleeves near the hinges with the small "A" logo on the end pieces and clear plastic temple tips.

Brand heritage

In the early 1980s the Italian national airline Alitalia launched its own line of sports and aviator sunglasses, made in Italy and sold under the Alitalia name. Models such as the Classic 500, Sport 550 and Professional 560 have since become sought-after collector pieces, helped by Kanye West wearing Alitalia aviators in the late 2000s.
