About this item

An elegant pair of vintage women's sunglasses by Sferoflex, Italy. The thin pale gold-tone metal frame has large, soft-square oversized lenses, with the front rims finished in a speckled brown "turtle" (tortoise) enamel. The temples are joined to the front by open double-loop gold ornamental end pieces, a refined decorative detail typical of 1970s and 80s ladies' eyewear. The lenses have a brown gradient tint, dark at the top fading to light amber at the bottom. The frame still carries its original white Sferoflex shop tags on both temples.

Brand heritage

Sferoflex is an Italian eyewear brand known for its patented flexible spring hinge, which lets the temples flex outward and return to shape for a comfortable fit. The brand later became part of the Luxottica group and is still produced today. The temple stamps "SFEROFLEX PAT" refer to this patented hinge system.
