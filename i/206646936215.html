About this item

A striking pair of 1980s Silhouette sunglasses, model M 3088 /20, colour C 622. The oversized, angular shield-style front is finished in glossy cream white, with graphic black-and-silver diagonal accents at the upper outer corners and along the lower inner rims. Grey gradient lenses fade from smoke at the top to almost clear at the bottom. The wide, sculpted temples carry the small gold oval Silhouette script plaque by the hinges and are stamped "Made in Austria" with the Silhouette logo.

Brand heritage

Silhouette was founded in Linz, Austria, in 1964 by Anneliese and Arnold Schmied, and still designs and makes its eyewear in Linz. In the 1970s and 1980s the brand became known for bold, fashion-forward plastic sunglasses alongside its later famous rimless frames.
