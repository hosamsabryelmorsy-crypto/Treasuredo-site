About this item

A vintage pair of Atelier sunglasses made in Austria, model 2004, size 58-18. The large squared front is made of brushed pale-gold metal with a translucent amber-brown acetate brow line over each lens, a brushed gold bridge and fixed honey-yellow nose pads. The flat brushed gold temples carry a thin black inlaid line and a black "G" emblem at the hinge, ending in translucent brown acetate temple tips. Fitted with brown gradient lenses (dark at the top, lighter towards the bottom).

Brand heritage

Atelier was an eyewear line from Austria associated with the Wilhelm Anger optical factory, the Austrian maker also known for producing the Niki Lauda Atelier and Carrera-era frames. Atelier frames of the 1970s and 1980s are recognised for their precise metalwork and bold, architectural shapes.
