About this item

A vintage pair of OWP design sunglasses made in Germany, model 7564, colour 167. The small octagonal front combines translucent brown (amber-tortoise) acetate rims with a thin gold-tone metal brow and a satin gold metal bridge, set with solid brown lenses. The temples feature a distinctive gold triple-rod section at the hinge, flowing into black acetate temple tips with rounded ball ends. Adjustable clear nose pads on gold pad arms.

Brand heritage

OWP is a German eyewear maker whose frames, stamped "OWP design - Germany", were widely sold through European opticians in the 1970s to 1990s. Its vintage frames are known for German workmanship and inventive metal and acetate combinations.
