The temples have the classic three raised ridges near the hinge, and the inner temple is stamped "642". Supplied with a brown leather Persol slip case with snap closure.

Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by Giuseppe Ratti, and its name comes from "per il sole" (for the sun). The silver arrow on the hinge, inspired by the ancient sword, became the brand's emblem and remains one of the most recognisable details in eyewear. Vintage Persol frames are collected worldwide.
