About this watch

A classic early-1960s Omega Seamaster 30 in stainless steel, reference 135.003-62 SC, powered by Omega's in-house manual-wind calibre 286 with 17 jewels. The silvery-champagne linen (cross-hatch) textured dial carries an applied Omega symbol, applied rose-gold-tone baton markers with a double baton at 12, slim rose-gold-tone baton hands and a centre seconds hand. The clean 35mm case with tapered lugs and the Seamaster seahorse caseback make it an elegant, very wearable dress watch. Supplied with an Omega "Precision Watch" box.

Brand heritage

Omega introduced the Seamaster line in 1948. The Seamaster 30 takes its name from Omega's famous 30mm family of manual-wind calibres, renowned for their robustness and accuracy, with the calibre 286 being one of the later and most refined versions found in these 1960s references.
