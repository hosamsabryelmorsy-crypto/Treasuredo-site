About this item

A distinctive pair of vintage Stepstar browline sunglasses from the brand's Country Collection USA, model "Oklahoma" 6004/S, colour 07F. The warm brown havana tortoise acetate brow sweeps up into softly pointed cat-eye corners, each set with a small silver arrow-shaped rivet. Above the nose sits a black hexagonal bridge plate with a silver chain-link inlay, and tortoise acetate nose-pad arms rest the frame comfortably on the face. Thin black metal lower rims hold solid dark grey lenses. The real showpiece is the temples: gold-tone metal with a deeply engraved, black-filled western scene of an eagle in flight over mountains, finished with black temple tips.

Brand heritage

Stepstar was an eyewear label of the 1980s and 1990s known for its themed lines, such as the City Collection USA and the Country Collection USA, whose models were named after American cities and states (Dallas, Chicago, Manhattan, Ranger, Oklahoma). This pair is stamped "stepstar COUNTRY COLLECTION USA" on the inside of one temple and "OKLAHOMA 6004/S USA COL. 07F 140" on the other.
