About this item

A bold pair of 1960s oversized round sunglasses in the French style. The thick front is translucent cherry-amber acetate with a rounded, slightly upswept cat-eye brow and wide flared end pieces, each set with two small silver pin-dot rivets. The temples are a striking brown and cream diagonal striped horn-effect acetate with a metal core wire running inside, joined to the front with riveted metal hinges. Solid dark green-grey (G-15 type) lenses. The original small triangular "VERRES Bi-Filtrants" lens sticker is still in place on the left lens, a French lens label from the period.

Brand heritage

France, and in particular the Jura region around Oyonnax and Morez, was the heart of European acetate eyewear making in the 1950s and 1960s, with many small workshops cutting and hand-finishing thick acetate frames. This frame carries no maker's name; the only marking is the French "VERRES Bi-Filtrants" lens sticker, so it is offered honestly as unbranded vintage eyewear.
