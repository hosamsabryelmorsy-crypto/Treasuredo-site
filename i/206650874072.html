About this item

A vintage pair of Nazareno Gabrielli women's sunglasses, model MOD 043, with the frame made in Italy. The large, softly squared butterfly front is in translucent honey/champagne plastic that shades from warm amber at the top to pale clear yellow at the bottom. A fine red-orange line is inlaid along the top of each brow. Round hinge medallions sit on the end pieces (the left one shows the red NG monogram), joined by short gold-tone metal rods to slim gold-tone temples with translucent amber sleeves. Fitted with brown gradient lenses, dark at the top and fading to light brown at the bottom.

Brand heritage

Nazareno Gabrielli is an Italian leather goods house from Tolentino in the Marche region, founded in 1907. During the 1980s and 1990s the brand also produced a line of accessories and eyewear under the Nazareno Gabrielli name, typically marked with the NG monogram and made in Italy.
