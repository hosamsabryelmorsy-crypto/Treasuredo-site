About this item

Vintage women's sunglasses from Jean Lempereur's Boutique line, model 49 in colour 957. The large, soft-hexagonal oversized front is a three-layer acetate: a brick red face outlined in black, over a clear champagne inner layer, with a black back. It is fitted with flat, light powder-blue lenses. The black temples carry the gold-tone "JL" monogram near the hinges, and the gold-tone hinges show through the clear end pieces. Inner temples are stamped "BOUTIQUE 49 56□14", "957" and "jean lempereur MADE IN FRANCE FRANCE 140".

Brand heritage

Jean Lempereur was a French eyewear designer whose own collection was launched in 1973; his acetate frames, made in France, are known for bold graphic colour work and are sought after by collectors of 1970s and 1980s French eyewear.
