About this item

A pair of vintage women's sunglasses by Lancel Paris, stamped 671 999 54/20. The full-rim frame has a soft square, slightly butterfly shape with gently raised upper corners, in clear champagne / pale-yellow crystal acetate. Across the top of the whole front runs an inlaid brow stripe in teal green and white that dips in a soft V over the bridge. The temples have an ivory / cream panel near the hinge set with a small rectangular gold-tone plaque, then continue as clear crystal shafts with a gold glitter inlay on the inside. The lenses are solid medium grey.

Brand heritage

Lancel is a Paris luxury house founded in 1876 by Angèle and Alphonse Lancel, best known for its leather goods and accessories. Its eyewear line put the house's Parisian style into sunglasses and optical frames, and this pair carries the "LANCEL PARIS" name on the inside of the temple.
