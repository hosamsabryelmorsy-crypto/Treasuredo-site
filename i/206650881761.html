About this item

A bold pair of vintage Guy Laroche Paris sunglasses, model GL Mars 3506, hand made in France. The thick, sculpted front has softly rounded oval / cat-eye lens openings and wide flared end pieces that flow into broad flat temples, all cut from a rich mottled brown and amber tortoiseshell acetate. The lenses are a warm brown gradient, darker at the top and fading lighter towards the bottom. Gold-tone metal hinges, clear nose pads, and "Guy Laroche Paris" printed in silver on the outside of each temple.

Brand heritage

Guy Laroche (1921-1989) was a French couturier who opened his Paris fashion house in 1957 and became known for elegant, modern women's fashion and the famous Fidji and Drakkar Noir fragrances. Guy Laroche eyewear carried the house's Parisian style into frames made in France.
