About this item

An elegant pair of vintage Zeiss sunglasses made in West Germany, model 1589, colour 3141. The large rounded butterfly / cat-eye front is cut from a translucent honey-amber acetate that deepens to a warm orange-brown at the lower rims. Each end piece carries a finely engraved striped metal inlay in copper and dark blue tones, and the clear amber temples show textured metal cores inside. The lenses are solid brown glass, giving a classic warm tint. The Zeiss logo and "West Germany" are printed on the temple tip.

Brand heritage

Carl Zeiss, founded in Jena in 1846, is one of the most famous names in optics. After the Second World War the company was re-established in Oberkochen, West Germany, and its eyewear division produced high quality frames and sunglasses with Zeiss mineral lenses through the 1970s and 1980s. Frames marked "West Germany" date from before German reunification in 1990.
