About this item

A genuine vintage pair of Nina Ricci Paris sunglasses from the 1970s to early 1980s. The oversized soft-aviator front is cut from honey tortoise acetate laminated over a clear crystal inner layer, with a fine gold-tone line running along the top of each lens rim, the signature detail of this Nina Ricci series. The temples are honey and crystal acetate with a gold-tone "N" logo plaque at each hinge. Gradient grey-brown lenses, darker at the top and fading towards the bottom. Stamped "NINA RICCI PARIS" and "HANDMADE FRANCE" on the temples, with the frame code "113-M053" on the inside of the temple.

Brand heritage

Nina Ricci is the Paris couture house founded in 1932 by Maria "Nina" Ricci and her son Robert Ricci. From the 1960s and 1970s the house extended its name into licensed accessories, and its eyewear of that period was produced in France, the home of fine handmade acetate frames.
