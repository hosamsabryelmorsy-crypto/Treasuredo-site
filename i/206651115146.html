About this item

A striking pair of vintage Cazal Mod. 301 women's sunglasses. The oversized butterfly front is cut from translucent green acetate that fades to a streaked champagne/clear tone along the lower rims and at the bridge, where the rims cross over in Cazal's sculpted X-shaped bridge. Black acetate inserts sit at the outer lower corners, finished with ribbed silver metal end pieces. The temples are flat polished metal with the dramatic stepped dog-leg curve and a rectangular cut-out window near the hinge, ending in black acetate tips. Solid dark green-grey lenses. Comes with an original Cazal suede pouch.

Brand heritage

Cazal was founded in Germany in 1975 by the Greek-born designer Cari Zalloni; the name is formed from the first letters of his name (CAri ZALloni). His sculptural, architectural frames made Cazal one of the most recognisable eyewear brands of the late 1970s and 1980s, and vintage Cazal frames remain highly collected today.
