Brand heritage

Rodenstock was founded in 1877 by Josef Rodenstock and is one of Germany's oldest makers of spectacle lenses and frames, based in Munich. In the 1970s and 1980s its premium "Exclusiv" frame line combined quality acetate and metal work with fashionable designs, and these frames are collected today by vintage eyewear enthusiasts.
