About this item

A rare early pair of vintage Cazal sunglasses, model Mod. 304. The oversized square-butterfly front is cut from a translucent marbled acetate swirling with amber, caramel, turquoise and soft pink, with a sculpted double-bar bridge and two small cut-outs between the bars. Polished chrome block hinges lead into Cazal's signature flat silver metal temples with the dramatic zigzag "Z" step, finished with dark brown acetate tips. The lenses are a solid dark grey-green. Comes with a Cazal suede pouch.

Brand heritage

Cazal was founded in Germany in 1975 by the designer Cari Zalloni; the name comes from the first letters of his names (CAri ZALloni). His sculptural, architectural frames became design icons of the late 1970s and 1980s and remain highly collected today.
