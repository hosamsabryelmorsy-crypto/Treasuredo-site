About this item

A pair of vintage Jean Lempereur sunglasses from the Boutique line, model Boutique 90, colour 383. The angular cat-eye front is cut from layered acetate: a glossy red top layer over blonde/amber tortoise, so the tortoise shows as a fine inner rim around each lens and along the edges. Each end-piece carries three sculpted horizontal ridges, and the blonde tortoise temples are finished with a small gold-tone rivet ornament. Fitted with flat light-blue tinted lenses.

Brand heritage

Jean Lempereur is a French eyewear label. Its acetate frames, signed "Jean LEMPEREUR - Made in France" on the temple, are collected today for their bold French designs of the 1970s and 1980s.
