A striking pair of vintage Nina Ricci sunglasses, reference NR0114-RB, size 54-20. A true statement frame with strong 1970s glamour.

Brand heritage

Nina Ricci is the Paris couture house founded in 1932 by Maria "Nina" Ricci and her son Robert Ricci, famous for its romantic, feminine fashion and for the fragrance L'Air du Temps (1948). Vintage Nina Ricci eyewear carries the house's Parisian elegance and its signature "N" emblem.
