About this item

A vintage pair of Cartier Paris rimless sunglasses with a gold-plated metal frame, the signature C-shaped Cartier hinges with gold screws, a thin arched bridge, gold nose pad arms and slim gold temples finished with black tips. The rectangular rimless lenses have a pink / fuchsia gradient tint, darker at the top and lighter towards the bottom. The pair comes as a complete Must de Cartier set with outer box, red presentation box, booklets and leather pouch.

Brand heritage

Cartier, founded in Paris in 1847, entered eyewear in 1983 under the "Les Must de Cartier" line. Its gold-plated rimless frames with the C-shaped hinge, made in France, became some of the most recognised luxury eyewear of the 1990s and are still highly sought after by collectors today.
