Brand heritage

The House of Balmain was founded in Paris in 1945 by couturier Pierre Balmain, whose elegant post-war collections made him one of the great names of French haute couture. Licensed Pierre Balmain eyewear, made in France, was produced in the 1980s and 1990s with distinctive textured acetates and signature-script temples.
