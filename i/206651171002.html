About this item

A vintage pair of Henry Jullien "Keano" aviator sunglasses from the 1980s. The gold-tone metal front has geometric, angled lens rims, a flat engraved brow bar stamped "HENRY JULLIEN" and a twisted rope-style bridge. The rope-twist temples run from an open-loop hinge carrying a square plaque with tortoiseshell-brown enamel and the two-bar emblem, and end in tortoiseshell acetate tips. Brown-to-amber gradient lenses and clear amber nose pads complete the look.

Brand heritage

Henry Jullien is a French eyewear house from Oyonnax, the historic centre of French spectacle making in the Jura, renowned since the 1920s for its finely finished gold-filled metal frames.
