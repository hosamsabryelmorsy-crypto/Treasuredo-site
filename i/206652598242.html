About this item

A vintage pair of early Silhouette sunglasses, stamped Mod 48, Col 141. The bold oversized square front is cut from translucent clear-amber acetate with irregular caramel, brown and smoky grey mottling, with a small notched keyhole bridge and thick rounded rims. The translucent temples show their gold-tone metal core wire, carry a silver-tone angular hook ornament on the outside near the hinge and the white script "Silhouette" logo on the inner metal plate. A true 1970s statement shape.

Brand heritage

Silhouette was founded in Linz, Austria, in 1964 by Anneliese and Arnold Schmied and still designs and makes its eyewear in Linz. The brand became known worldwide for fashionable 1970s and 1980s sunglasses and later for its ultra-light rimless Titan Minimal Art frames. Early two-digit Silhouette models from the 1970s are sought after by eyewear collectors.
