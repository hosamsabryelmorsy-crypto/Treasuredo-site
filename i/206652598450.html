About this item

Classic men's sunglasses by Asdor of Italy, marked "EXCLUSIVE 55". A large soft-square front in solid black acetate with a straight, sculpted bridge, fitted with bottle-green tinted lenses. The black acetate temples are set at the hinge end with gold-tone metal sleeves and, on the outside, a gold-framed plaque with a dark burgundy-brown inlay and a small gold "ASDOR" label. Screw-fixed hinges throughout. Inner temples marked "EXCLUSIVE 55" and "MADE IN ITALY" with the Asdor logo; inner rim marked "54-20".

Brand heritage

Asdor was an Italian eyewear maker, known to collectors mainly for its "Asdor Patent" frames of the 1970s and 1980s. Its Italian-made sunglasses are hard to find today.
