About this watch

A vintage Seiko Automatic, case reference 7009-8081, powered by Seiko's self-winding calibre 7009 with 17 jewels. The deep navy blue dial carries white serif Arabic numerals with small luminous dots, an applied SEIKO logo with "Automatic" below it, the 17 Jewels lightning-bolt signature above 6 and a day-date window at 3 showing day and date in English. It sits in Seiko's distinctive 1970s cushion "TV" style stainless steel case with wide polished flanks, on a stainless steel multi-link bracelet. The caseback serial 653427 dates it to 1976.

Brand heritage

Seiko, founded in Tokyo in 1881, became one of the great names of Japanese watchmaking, and in the 1960s and 1970s built a reputation for robust, affordable automatic watches. The calibre 7009, introduced in the 1970s, is one of Seiko's longest-serving automatic day-date movements, used for decades and known for its durability and easy servicing.
