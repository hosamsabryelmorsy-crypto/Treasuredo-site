About this watch

A handsome Jovial moonphase calendar dress watch, reference 1218G in the two-tone white-dial version (2T-WH). The silver-white dial has a fish-scale guilloche pattern, applied gold baton markers with a double marker at 12, open square markers and a black minute track. Two silvered sub-dials show the date (left) and the day of the week (right), and a moonphase window at 6 shows a gold moon and stars on a dark blue sky with a 0 to 29½ day scale. Gold-tone leaf hands with luminous inserts, a polished stainless steel case with a gold-tone inner bezel ring, a gold-tone crown and a matching two-tone five-link bracelet with a fold-over clasp. Supplied in a Jovial Swiss Made box.

Brand heritage

Jovial is a Swiss watch brand founded in 1929, known for honest, well-made Swiss watches and, for generations, one of the best-loved Swiss names across Egypt and the Middle East.
