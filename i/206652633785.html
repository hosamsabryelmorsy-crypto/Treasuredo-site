About this item

A classic pair of vintage Metzler aviator sunglasses, model 7501, colour 024. The thin matte black metal full-rim frame has the traditional teardrop aviator lens shape with a straight black brow bar across the top, paired with a translucent amber / honey saddle bridge with integrated nose pads, a typical German design of the period. Solid smoky grey lenses, slim black metal temples with black teardrop temple tips. The inside of the temple is stamped "FRAME GERMANY BY METZLER 7501 024 140" and the bridge "58□18".

Brand heritage

Metzler is a German eyewear maker, best known in the 1970s and 1980s for its quality metal frames and for producing eyewear under licence for names such as Longines and Zeiss. Vintage Metzler frames are sought after today for their German build quality and classic designs.
