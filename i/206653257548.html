About this watch

A vintage Rado Conway from the late 1960s, in a sculpted stainless steel cushion (tonneau) case with a wide polished bezel and short integrated lugs. The silver brushed dial carries the red Rado anchor disc logo above the RADO wordmark, "Conway" signed below centre, applied two-tone baton markers with black inlay, a red-numeral date window at 3 o'clock and "Swiss Made" at 6. The screw-down caseback is engraved "Conway", carries Rado's famous seahorse emblem with "Water-Sealed" and the reference 11680. It comes on a Rado steel link bracelet with a Rado-signed fold-over clasp and in a vintage Rado presentation box.

Brand heritage

Rado traces its roots to the Schlup & Co. watch factory founded in 1917 in Lengnau, Switzerland, and adopted the Rado name in the 1950s. Through the 1960s and 1970s Rado built a strong following with robust, stylish automatic and mechanical models such as the Golden Horse, Green Horse and Conway, marked by the seahorse "Water-Sealed" caseback, and in 1962 it launched the scratch-resistant DiaStar.
