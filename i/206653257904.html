About this item

Large square metal sunglasses by Eschenbach, typical of the 1970s. The brushed silver metal front carries a thin peach/salmon brow line along the top of each lens, a flat silver bridge with a clear one-piece saddle nose pad, and broad brushed end pieces. Slim silver temples end in dark brown plastic tips. Fitted with brown gradient lenses, dark at the top fading to light at the bottom. One temple is stamped with the Eschenbach crown logo and "ESCHENBACH"; the inside of the other temple is stamped "20 04 56/18".

Brand heritage

Eschenbach Optik is a German optical company founded in Nuremberg in 1913, known for magnifiers, optical instruments and spectacle and sunglass frames.
