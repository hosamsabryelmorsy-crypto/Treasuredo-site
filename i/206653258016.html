About this item

A bold pair of oversized eight-sided sunglasses, the geometric shape that defined late 1960s and 1970s glamour. The thick acetate front is cut with faceted octagonal rims and shades from deep amber brown along the top and outer edges to clear pale champagne at the lower inner corners and across the short straight bridge. Solid brown tinted lenses fill the large 54 mm octagons. The temples carry the same brown-to-clear fade, are reinforced with a visible gold-tone wire core and open on riveted metal hinges, with two pin rivets showing on each front corner. The inside of the temples is stamped "MADE IN FRANCE".

Brand heritage

France, and especially the Jura region around Oyonnax and Morez, was the centre of European acetate eyewear making in the 20th century. Many frames of that era left the workshops without a maker's name on them. This pair carries only the "MADE IN FRANCE" stamp and no maker's name, so it is offered honestly as unbranded vintage French eyewear.
