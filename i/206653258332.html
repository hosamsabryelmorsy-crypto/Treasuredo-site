Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by Giuseppe Ratti; the name comes from "per il sole" (for the sun). Its sunglasses are recognised by the Supreme Arrow on the front of the temples, a design inspired by ancient warriors' swords that also serves as the hinge cover. Persol frames are hand-finished in Italy and have been worn by screen icons for decades.
