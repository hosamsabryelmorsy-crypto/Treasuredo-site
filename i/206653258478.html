Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by Giuseppe Ratti, and its name comes from "per il sole" (for the sun). Frames from the Ratti era, signed "Persol-Ratti", are sought after by collectors of vintage Italian eyewear. Optyl is a lightweight, heat-moulded material developed in the late 1960s, prized for its comfort and its deep, glossy colour fades.
