About this item

A large, softly rounded pilot-style acetate frame from the 1970s, stamped "Bravo" in gold script and "Adensco" on the inside of the temple and "FRAME FRANCE 016 361" on the other temple. The translucent acetate fades from clear honey at the bridge and upper inner rims to a darker striated brown at the outer and lower rims, a typical seventies two-tone look. The temples follow the same fade, with gold-tone wire cores visible through the acetate and a small silver-tone square plaque with a black "L"-shaped emblem on the outside near each hinge. Riveted five-barrel metal hinges. Fitted with brown gradient lenses.

Brand heritage

Adensco is a long-established eyewear label whose frames were made in France and Italy; it later became a house brand of the Italian eyewear group Safilo. French-made Adensco acetate frames from the 1960s and 1970s are much less common than the later Italian metal frames.
