About this item

Vintage sunglasses by the German eyewear label Actuell, model 92. The soft, rounded-octagonal front is made of translucent peach-pink acetate that deepens to a warmer orange-pink at the outer edges, with a flat angular keyhole bridge and small round metal rivets at the corners. It holds solid brown tinted lenses. The temples are flat brushed silver-tone metal with two long cut-out slots and black plastic ear tips. The temples are stamped "actuell 92" and "Made in Germany"; the ear tip is marked "54□18".

Brand heritage

Actuell was a German eyewear label; its frames, including the later Actuell Couture line, are sought after today by collectors of vintage German eyewear.
