About this item

Vintage men's sunglasses by Lozza of Italy, model 848. A large angular navigator front in bright silver-tone chrome metal with a straight flat brow bar and a double bridge, fitted with grey-green gradient lenses that are darker at the top and fade to almost clear at the bottom. Thin silver metal temples with dark brown plastic tips, adjustable clear nose pads on metal pad arms. Inner temples stamped with the maker's logo, "LOZZA" and "MADE IN ITALY" on one side and "848", "52□17" and "130" on the other.

Brand heritage

Lozza is one of Italy's oldest eyewear houses, founded in 1878 in the Cadore area of the Veneto, the cradle of Italian eyewear. Its metal frames of the 1970s and 1980s are sought after by collectors of vintage Italian sunglasses.
