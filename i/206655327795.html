About this item

Women's sunglasses from the French couture house Jean Patou, Paris. An oversized soft-square front in translucent champagne / pale yellow crystal acetate with a straight sculpted bridge, fitted with solid brown tinted lenses. Silver-tone metal hinge blocks on the outer temples carry the engraved interlocking JP monogram. One temple is marked "JEAN PATOU PARIS"; the other is stamped "MADE IN FRANCE RIVA 52 18 28".

Brand heritage

The house of Jean Patou was founded in Paris by Jean Patou in 1914 and is credited with shaping the sporty, modern look of 1920s fashion. Patou produced couture, ready-to-wear and perfume, and its licensed accessories, including eyewear, carried the name through the following decades.
