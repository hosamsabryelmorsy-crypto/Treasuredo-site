About this item

A bold oversized square sunglass from the French maker Essel, from its Boutique fashion line. The thick front is translucent honey and champagne-brown acetate with darker horizontal horn-like striations, cut with very wide, flat square rims and softly rounded corners, a low straight bridge and small block end pieces. The straight temples are matching translucent honey acetate with a visible metal core wire and silver multi-barrel hinges. Solid dark grey-green glass lenses, the left lens etched "ST". The inner temple is stamped "ESSEL BOUTIQUE" and "130".

Brand heritage

Essel was one of the major French optical companies. On 1 January 1972 it merged with its rival Silor to form Essilor, today part of EssilorLuxottica. Frames signed Essel therefore date from before or around that merger, which places this pair in the late 1960s or early 1970s.
