About this item

A striking pair of vintage Nina Ricci Paris women's sunglasses with a bold eight-sided (octagonal) front. The translucent champagne acetate frame is decorated around each lens with four black inlay bars, each fixed by small gold-tone screw heads, giving a jewel-like architectural look. The bridge carries the gold Nina Ricci "N" emblem outlined in black, and a gold "N" plaque sits at each hinge. The temples are translucent with a gold-tone metal core and a fine black and amber stripe. The lenses are a soft pink-mauve gradient fading lighter toward the bottom.

Brand heritage

Nina Ricci is a Paris couture house founded in 1932 by Maria "Nina" Ricci and her son Robert Ricci. Its vintage eyewear, produced under licence in France, is known for refined acetates and gold detailing, and frames like this one are stamped "Handmade France".
