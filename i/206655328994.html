Brand heritage

Bausch & Lomb was founded in Rochester, New York in 1853 and became one of America's great optical companies. In 1937 it introduced the Ray-Ban brand and for decades produced its sunglasses and frames in the USA, until the eyewear business was sold to Luxottica in 1999. Vintage B&L USA frames are prized by collectors for their quality acetate and timeless shapes.
