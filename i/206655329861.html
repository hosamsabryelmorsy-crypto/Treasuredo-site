About this item

A vintage pair of semi-rimless sunglasses from the Welling "English Leather" eyewear line, model "In The Groove 201". The silver-tone metal brow bar has a brushed finish with fine engraved grooves along its length and dips into a low curved bridge, with a thin wire bridge below. The wide soft-square lenses are held at the bottom by a fine nylon line (half-rim / nylor construction) and have a gray-violet gradient tint, darker at the top and fading to nearly clear at the bottom. Thin straight silver wire temples end in black plastic ear tips. Adjustable nose pads on silver pad arms.

Brand heritage

English Leather is the classic American men's grooming and fragrance brand, and its name was also used on a licensed line of eyewear distributed by Welling. This frame was made in Japan, where much of the finest metal eyewear of the period was produced.
