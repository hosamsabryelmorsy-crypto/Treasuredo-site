About this item

A low, sculpted flat-top rectangular sport frame from the golden age of Italian motoring style. Layered acetate: a warm striated brown and amber tortoise front over a crystal-clear back layer, so each lens is outlined by a fine clear rim and the frame fades to crystal along the lower edge. Twin metal rivet bars decorate the outer front corners, and the lenses are a soft brown gradient. Inner temple stamped "SAMCO MOD. DER", temple tip stamped "ITALY".

Brand heritage

Samco was an Italian eyewear maker whose hand-finished acetate sunglasses were sold widely from the mid-century onward; collectors look for its bold layered acetates and sculpted shapes, typically stamped "Samco" with a "Mod." model mark and "Italy".
