About this watch

A collectible piece of Iraqi military and political history: a Ulystar wristwatch in an all olive-green military style, with a small full-colour portrait of Iraqi President Saddam Hussein printed on the left of the dark olive-green dial. The dial is signed "ULYSTAR", "QUARTZ 5 ATU" and "SWISS MADE", with large white Arabic numerals 1 to 12, cream luminous dots, a white minute track, a framed date window at 3 o'clock, white sword hands and a thin red seconds hand. The olive-green case has a unidirectional-style rotating diver bezel with white 20, 40 and 50 numerals, white bars and a red first-quarter section, a black knurled crown at 3 o'clock, and a stainless steel caseback held by four screws. It is fitted on a matching olive-green link bracelet with a pin buckle and comes in a green "Luxury Box" presentation box.

Brand heritage

During the 1970s, 1980s and 1990s, many Swiss and Japanese watch brands made watches with Saddam Hussein's portrait on the dial for Iraq; these were often given to officers, soldiers, officials and guests, and today they are sought after as historical collectibles. Military-style versions such as this green Ulystar are associated with the Iraqi armed forces of that era.
