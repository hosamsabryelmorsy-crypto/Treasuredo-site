About this watch

A genuine 1980s Seiko ana-digi from the golden age of Japanese quartz: the Sports 100 Alarm-Chronograph reference H557-5330. It combines a classic analog display with an LCD digital window at the top of the dial, giving digital time, alarm and chronograph functions alongside traditional hands. The champagne-gold dial carries applied gold baton markers and gold hands, set in a squared, faceted-corner stainless steel case with gold-plated accents and a matching two-tone integrated bracelet. The caseback serial dates it to 1984. Supplied in a Seiko presentation box.

Brand heritage

Seiko launched the world's first quartz wristwatch, the Astron, in 1969, and in the late 1970s and early 1980s led the way with hybrid analog-digital ("ana-digi") watches. The H557 calibre powered a large family of Seiko Sports 100 and Silver Wave ana-digi models, now popular with collectors of 1980s design.
