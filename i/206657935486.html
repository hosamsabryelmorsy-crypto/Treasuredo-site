About this item

Large vintage aviator sunglasses by Jacques Bogart Paris, stamped "3026" on the temple. The teardrop front has glossy black inner rims set inside a thin gold-tone metal outer rim, a straight double brow bar (a fine silver-tone wire above a flat gold-tone bar) and a black keyhole-style centre bridge with two slots. Fine gold-tone wire temples end in dark brown tips. Fitted with brown gradient lenses, dark at the top and fading to light tan, and adjustable nose pads. A bold 1980s statement frame.

Brand heritage

Jacques Bogart is a Paris fashion and fragrance house founded in the 1970s, best known for its men's fragrances. In the 1980s the name also appeared on a line of designer eyewear, and these vintage Jacques Bogart Paris frames are now sought after by collectors of 80s sunglasses.
