About this watch

A 1970s Swiss-made Sandoz self-winding watch with a striking royal blue dial. The dial carries applied silver baton markers with black inlay, a cream minute track with lume dots, and a black inner flange printed with 13 to 24 hour numerals for a sporty, world-time look. Silver baton hands, a framed date window at 3 o'clock, and "SANDOZ / AUTOMATIC 25 JEWELS / SWISS MADE" signatures complete it. The polished stainless steel cushion (TV-style) case has wide flat flanks with notched lugs, measures about 37 mm across excluding the crown and 39.8 mm lug to lug (caliper measured), and is fitted on a navy blue leather strap.

Brand heritage

Sandoz is a Swiss watch brand from the Jura watchmaking region. Through the 1960s and 1970s it produced robust, well-finished Swiss automatic watches with jewelled movements and bold period designs, sold widely in Europe and the Middle East. Vintage Sandoz automatics are appreciated today for their Swiss build quality and distinctive 1970s styling.
