About this watch

A vintage 1970s Rado NCC 444 for ladies, the asymmetric "Cobra head" design from Rado's NCC family. The gold-plated case has a sculpted cushion/TV shape with the crown set at the lower right, framing a champagne gold sunburst dial with the applied RADO logo, the red Rado anchor emblem, "NCC 444" signature, double-faceted baton markers with black inlay and a date window above 6 o'clock. Steel screw-down caseback engraved with case reference 558.3018.2, the Rado "Water Sealed" emblem and serial number. Supplied with a gold-tone link bracelet and a Rado box.

Brand heritage

Rado was founded in 1917 in Lengnau, Switzerland, by the Schlup brothers as Schlup & Co., and the Rado name was introduced in 1957. Known for bold, design-led cases in the 1960s and 1970s, Rado went on to pioneer scratch-resistant hard metal with the DiaStar in 1962. The moving red anchor emblem on the dial is one of the brand's best-known signatures.
