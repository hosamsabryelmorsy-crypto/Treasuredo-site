About this item

A dramatic pair of vintage French sunglasses with a sculpted wraparound design. The front is cut from translucent smoky charcoal / olive-grey acetate with natural darker and lighter streaks, and it sweeps out into very wide, flared wing panels that wrap around the sides of the face, a striking mid-century "side shield" cat eye silhouette. The almond-shaped lenses have a soft lavender / light violet tint. Glossy solid black temples open on riveted metal hinges, with two small pin-rivet dots on each front corner. Small amber translucent nose pads sit inside the bridge. The inside of the temple is stamped FRANCE.

Brand heritage

France was a leading centre of handmade acetate eyewear in the 1950s and 1960s, with the Jura region (around Oyonnax and Morez) producing frames for Paris fashion houses and opticians. This pair is stamped only "FRANCE" and carries no maker's name or model number, so it is offered honestly as unbranded French vintage eyewear.
