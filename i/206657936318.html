About this item

A classic Italian double-bridge aviator from Lastes in translucent honey-amber acetate, with a darker brown streaky grain running along the brow bar and the outer rims. Large teardrop lenses in a solid olive-brown tint. Each temple carries two silver-tone metal bands and a riveted silver hinge plate. One temple is stamped "BREV. (LASTES)" followed by the model number (partly worn, it appears to read "M 255C"), the other is stamped "MADE IN ITALY A.M.", and the ear tip carries the size "52 18".

Brand heritage

Lastes was an Italian eyewear maker active from roughly the 1970s to the 1990s, later known for its "Swing by Lastes" and "Finissimo by Lastes" lines. "BREV." on the temple is short for "brevetto", Italian for patent. We list this pair only under its own stamped name.
