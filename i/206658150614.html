Brand heritage

Christian Dior founded his couture house in Paris in 1946 and changed post-war fashion with the "New Look" of 1947. Dior eyewear followed from the late 1960s and became one of the best-known designer eyewear lines of the 1970s and 1980s, with many of its Optyl frames produced in Germany. Optyl, a lightweight heat-mouldable material, was used for many of the classic Dior frames of that period, and these German-made pairs are widely collected today.
