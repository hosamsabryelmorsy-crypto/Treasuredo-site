About this watch

A classic mid-century Swiss dress watch signed Spera De Luxe, with the brand's anchor emblem above the name. The two-tone champagne dial carries applied gold Arabic numerals at 12, 3, 6 and 9 with applied dagger markers, a beaded dot minute ring, gold leaf hands and a guilloche sub-seconds register at 6 o'clock, signed "17 Rubis" and "Swiss". The gold-plated round case has long, sharply faceted lugs typical of 1950s Swiss design. Fitted on a black lizard-pattern leather strap and supplied in a presentation box.

Brand heritage

Spera was a Swiss watch brand of the mid-20th century, best known today for its manual-wind dress watches and its rare Spera diver and chronograph models. Like many smaller Swiss houses of the era, Spera used reliable Swiss ebauche movements, here a 17-jewel ("17 Rubis") hand-wound calibre.
