About this watch

A classic vintage Swiss dress watch signed "CX Charmex of Switzerland". The white dial carries bold black Roman numerals, a fine gold inner ring, black sword hands with a slim seconds hand, a script "Q" for quartz, "Swiss Made" at the bottom and a gold-framed date window at 5 o'clock. The slim round gold-plated case is finished with a gold crown set with a dark blue cabochon, and the watch is fitted on a tan leather strap with cream stitching. Supplied in a presentation box.

Brand heritage

Charmex of Switzerland is a Swiss watch brand known for well-priced Swiss Made quartz and mechanical watches. Its quartz dress watches of the 1980s and 1990s, like this one, followed the classic Roman-numeral styling popular in that era.
