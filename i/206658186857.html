About this watch

A classic 1960s-1970s Jovial Slim Line dress watch. The silver sunburst dial carries applied gold faceted markers, applied gold 12, 9 and 6 numerals, the winged Jovial emblem and the signature "Slim Line" script, with a framed date window at 3 o'clock showing red numerals. Gold baton hands with luminous inserts and a red centre seconds hand complete the look. Housed in a slim gold-plated round case with a domed crystal and a fluted gold crown, on a black leather strap with a gold-tone buckle, and supplied in a Jovial box.

Brand heritage

Jovial is a Swiss watch brand established in 1929 (as stated on the box, "Jovial since 1929"). In the 1960s and 1970s Jovial built a strong following, especially across the Middle East, for well-made Swiss mechanical watches with distinctive dials at accessible prices.
