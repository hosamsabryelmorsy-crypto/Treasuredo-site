About this watch

A 1960s Swiss-made men's dress watch from Cornavin of Geneva. The champagne dial has aged into a warm, uneven tropical patina with orange-brown toning and speckling, giving it real vintage character. It carries applied gold-tone markers with distinctive teardrop/comma markers at 12, 6 and 9, tapered dagger batons at the other hours, dauphine hands and a date window at 3 with red numerals. Signed "Cornavin Geneve 17 Jewels", "Incabloc" and "Swiss Made". Inside is a Swiss 17-jewel hand-wound movement with the bridge signed "Seventeen 17 Jewels, Unadjusted, Swiss, Gigantic S.A." and marked "4017 N". The screw-down caseback is signed "Stainless Steel Back", "Waterproof", "Antimagnetic", "Incabloc" and "Swiss".

Brand heritage

Cornavin was a Geneva watch brand, named after the city's Cornavin quarter around its main railway station. In the 1950s and 1960s it sold Swiss-made mechanical watches like this one. Later it became better known for marketing Soviet-made watches in Western markets.
