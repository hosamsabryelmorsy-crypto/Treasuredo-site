About this watch

A slim, understated Swiss dress watch from Longines' Presence line, also sold as La Grande Classique. The 33mm polished stainless steel case carries a silver sunburst dial with thin applied baton markers, a date window at 3 o'clock and slender baton hands. It runs on a Swiss quartz movement and comes on a black crocodile-grain leather strap, presented in a Longines box.

Brand heritage

Longines was founded in 1832 in Saint-Imier, Switzerland, and its winged hourglass emblem is one of the oldest registered trademarks still in use. The Presence collection is the brand's classic dress line, known for clean dials and thin, elegant cases.
