About this watch

A vintage Seiko 5 automatic, reference 6119-7083, powered by the in-house 21-jewel calibre 6119 with day-date. The caseback serial 155613 dates it to May 1971. The 36mm cushion (TV-shaped) stainless steel case has brushed surfaces with polished bevelled edges and the crown at 4 o'clock. The charcoal grey sunburst dial (code 6119-7080T AD) has fine silver crosshair lines, applied silver four-square hour markers, silver baton hands with lume inlays and a framed MON/5 day-date window at 3 o'clock. It is fitted on a dark navy-grey leather strap.

Brand heritage

Seiko introduced the Seiko 5 line in 1963: affordable, durable automatic watches defined by five features: automatic winding, day-date display, water resistance, a recessed crown at 4 o'clock (on many models) and a durable case and band. The calibre 6119, introduced in the late 1960s, became one of the most widely used Seiko 5 day-date movements of the 1970s and is known for its robustness.
