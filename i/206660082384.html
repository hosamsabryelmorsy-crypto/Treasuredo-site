About this watch

A charming ladies dress watch by Les Bergues Geneve, reference 14067T. The gold-tone dial is struck in relief as a replica of the reverse of the classic US $5 "Half Eagle" gold coin: the heraldic eagle with shield, olive branch and arrows, the "In God We Trust" banner, "United States of America" around the edge and "Five" at the bottom. The 25mm gold-tone case has a milled coin-edge bezel that completes the coin look, with slim dark hands, a fine seconds hand and a reeded crown. Fitted to a black lizard-grain leather strap.

Brand heritage

Les Bergues Geneve is a Geneva-signed watch brand known for elegant gold-tone quartz dress and presentation watches from the late 20th century.
