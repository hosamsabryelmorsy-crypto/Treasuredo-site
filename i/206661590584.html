About this item

Vintage 1990s sunglasses by Daytona, made by Safilo in Italy, model DA 900/S in colour 033. A small rounded-rectangle gold-tone metal frame with an engraved bridge, gold-tone nose pads and bottle-green tinted lenses. The open-work gold-tone temples have a ribbed grille detail and a cut-out window, and finish in mottled tortoiseshell acetate tips. The left lens still carries the original winged DAYTONA lens sticker, and the right lens has a small DAYTONA etching.

Brand heritage

Daytona was an eyewear line produced by Safilo, the Italian eyewear maker from the Veneto region, in the late 1980s and 1990s. Daytona frames are known for their bold metal work and racing-inspired styling, and are collected today as authentic Italian vintage eyewear.
