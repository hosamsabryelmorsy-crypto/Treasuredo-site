About this item

A striking pair of vintage Yves Saint Laurent aviator sunglasses, model 8359-O in colour Y63, made in France in the 1980s. The large one-piece shield-style aviator front has a dark brown, almost black, textured frame. Across the top runs a brown leather-covered brow bar with a single row of white saddle stitching, and above it a thin double-bar wire sweeps out to the outer corners. The flat temples are also covered in brown textured leather with white stitching along the top edge and curve down behind the ears. Silver-tone hinges, brown gradient lenses (dark at the top, fading to pale at the bottom).

Brand heritage

Yves Saint Laurent founded his Paris couture house in 1961 and went on to shape modern fashion with designs such as Le Smoking tuxedo for women. In the 1970s and 1980s the house licensed eyewear made in France and Italy, and the French-made YSL frames of that period, with their bold shapes and luxury materials, are now sought after by collectors of vintage designer eyewear.
