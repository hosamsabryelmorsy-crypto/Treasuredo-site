About this item

A bold pair of vintage Playboy sunglasses, model 4515 in colour 50, made in Austria from Optyl. The large octagonal butterfly front is in translucent honey-olive Optyl that darkens towards the top edge. A slate blue-grey band runs across the outer top corners and along the first part of each temple, where a small Playboy rabbit-head logo sits on each side. The temples drop down, then sweep back into long honey-coloured ear pieces. Uniform light brown lenses, silver-tone hinges.

Brand heritage

Playboy eyewear was made under licence in the 1970s and 1980s by Optyl, the Austrian company that developed Optyl, a lightweight heat-shaped epoxy resin frame material. The oversized Optyl frames of this period, signed "PLAYBOY" with the optyl mark and stamped "MADE IN AUSTRIA", are popular with collectors of 70s designer eyewear.
