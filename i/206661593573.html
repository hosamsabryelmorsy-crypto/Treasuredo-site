About this item

Vintage 1990s oval sunglasses by Chagall, made in Italy by Visibilia, model LL 45052 in colour 059. A gold-tone thin metal oval front with a faceted bridge and amber nose pads, sculpted gold-tone end pieces that wrap around the lenses, and open-work square hinge blocks. The glossy black temples carry a brushed silver-tone plate engraved with the script "Chagall" logo and a large brushed silver-tone "C" emblem. Solid dark grey lenses, with the gold script "Chagall" logo on the left lens.

Brand heritage

Chagall was an Italian eyewear line produced in the 1990s by Visibilia, an eyewear maker in Italy that also made frames under licence for fashion houses. Chagall frames are known for their bold metal work and jewellery-like temple details, and are collected today as authentic Italian 90s vintage eyewear.
