About this item

A striking pair of vintage Sting women's sunglasses made in Italy, model MF70, colour 700. The glossy black acetate front has a feminine oval cat-eye shape, with ribbed gold-tone metal bars wrapping each outer corner. The open, double-bar gold-tone temples carry a small black square plaque set with a raised gold heraldic crest, a bold baroque detail typical of 1990s Italian fashion eyewear, and finish in black acetate tips. Fitted with dark brown-grey sun lenses, the left lens marked with the small "★ STING ★" logo.

Brand heritage

Sting is an Italian eyewear brand produced by De Rigo, the eyewear group founded in Belluno, Italy, in 1978. Sting became one of the best-known Italian fashion eyewear lines of the 1980s and 1990s, and its vintage frames made in Italy are collected today for their bold designs.
