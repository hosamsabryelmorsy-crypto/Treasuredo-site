About this item

Vintage 1990s Police sunglasses, model 2243 in colour 120, made in Italy. A small round gold-tone metal frame with a short bridge bar decorated with a dark brown enamel band and a row of tiny gold dots, adjustable nose pads, and wide gold-tone end-pieces wrapped in dark brown enamel with ribbed gold lines and the same dotted detail. The thick havana tortoiseshell acetate temples carry the inner stamps "MOD. POLICE 2243 COL. 120" and "POLICE C MADE IN ITALY CE". Brown tinted lenses.

Brand heritage

Police is an Italian eyewear brand launched in 1983 by De Rigo, the eyewear maker from Belluno in the Veneto region. Its bold metal frames of the late 1980s and 1990s, made in Italy, are sought after today as authentic vintage Italian designer eyewear.
