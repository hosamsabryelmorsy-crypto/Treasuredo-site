About this item

Vintage women's sunglasses by Jean de Paris, model 674/2, made in France. A large oversized square frame in crystal champagne acetate that warms to honey at the lower rims, with a black and gold striped brow line across the top of each lens. Small ridged black-and-gold end-pieces at the hinges and slim gold-tone metal temples with a black inlay stripe and a monogram plaque. The inner temples are stamped "JEAN de PARIS" and "674/2 MADE IN FRANCE". Pink-brown gradient lenses, darker at the top and fading to almost clear at the bottom.

Brand heritage

Jean de Paris is a French eyewear label whose frames were made in France and sold through opticians in the late 1970s and 1980s, known for large, feminine acetate and metal shapes typical of Parisian eyewear of that era.
