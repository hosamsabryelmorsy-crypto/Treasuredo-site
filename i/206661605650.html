About this item

A bold pair of vintage French sunglasses from the 1970s. The large soft-square lenses sit in a thick, sculpted gold-tone metal full rim with a rounded bridge. The flat gold-tone temples have a long open slot and two small rivets at the hinge, and finish in black temple tips; the inner tip is stamped "FRANCE". Solid grey (smoke) glass lenses, with the original vintage "VERRES TREMPES / TEMPERED LENSES" sticker still on one lens. No maker's name is marked on the frame, so it is sold as an unsigned French frame.

Brand heritage

France has a long tradition of eyewear making, centred on the Jura region around Morez and Oyonnax, where frames have been made by hand since the 18th and 19th centuries. Gold-tone metal oversized frames like this one were a signature look of 1970s French and European fashion.
