Brand heritage

Metzler was one of Germany's leading eyewear makers, based in Bavaria. In the 1970s and 1980s it produced frames under its own name and made eyewear for names such as Longines and Paloma Picasso. Its well-built metal frames of that era are sought after today as vintage German eyewear.
