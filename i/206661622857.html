Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by Giuseppe Ratti, and its name comes from "per il sole" (for the sun). The brand is known for its patented Meflecto system, flexible temple stems that adapt to the shape of the head for a more comfortable fit, and for the silver Supreme arrow hinge, which became the Persol signature. Vintage Ratti-era Meflecto Persols are collected worldwide.
