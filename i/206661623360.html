About this item

A stylish pair of vintage Von Furstenberg sunglasses made in Italy, model MF 68, colour 201. The slim oval metal front pairs a black-enamelled lens rim with a gold-tone upper brow and a sculpted gold-tone bridge. Each outer corner carries a bold ribbed five-bar gold-tone end piece, and the open-cut gold-tone temples are set with a small round crest emblem and finish in black tips. Fitted with dark smoke-grey sun lenses and adjustable clear nose pads, a typical piece of 1990s Italian luxury eyewear design.

Brand heritage

Von Furstenberg eyewear was the Italian-made line carrying the name and crest of the Von Furstenberg family, sold under the MF model series in the 1980s and 1990s. These frames, made in Italy with bold gold-tone metalwork, are now sought after by collectors of vintage designer sunglasses.
