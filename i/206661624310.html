Brand heritage

Alpina is a German eyewear and sports-optics company founded in 1980 and based in Friedberg, Bavaria. In the 1980s and 1990s it made its name with high-quality Made in Germany sunglasses, including the collectible M1 series, and today it is best known for ski goggles, helmets and sport glasses.
