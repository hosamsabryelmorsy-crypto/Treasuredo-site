Brand heritage

Longines was founded in 1832 in Saint-Imier, Switzerland, and registered its winged hourglass logo in 1889, one of the oldest trademarks still in use. The brand was a pioneer of aviation and sports timing, and its vintage steel watches of the 1960s and 1970s are widely collected today.
