Brand heritage

Cartier was founded in Paris in 1847 by Louis-François Cartier. The Panthère de Cartier watch, launched in 1983, is known for its supple brick-link bracelet, and the two-tone steel and gold version became one of the defining luxury watches of the 1980s and 1990s. Original Cartier service links are the correct way to lengthen a Panthère bracelet.
