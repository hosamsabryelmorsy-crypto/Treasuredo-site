About this item

An original vintage Omega stainless steel bracelet, reference 1442/420 (bracelet only, no watch). The outer rows are polished steel and the centre row is black, giving the two-tone look used on Seamaster and Constellation models of the 1970s and 1980s. The inside of the folding clasp is clearly stamped "STAINLESS STEEL 1442/420", the Omega logo and maker number (32).

Brand heritage

Omega was founded in 1848 in La Chaux-de-Fonds and is one of the most famous Swiss watchmakers: the Speedmaster went to the Moon in 1969, and the Seamaster and Constellation lines defined Omega's sports and dress watches for decades. Original signed Omega bracelets are sought after by collectors restoring vintage pieces.
