About this item

A set of original Corum Lion Heart parts for restoring or completing a Lion Heart watch: the 12-sided stainless steel case middle with lugs, the 12-sided two-tone caseback (gold-tone edge, brushed steel centre) stamped "64 801 21" with its serial number, the silver-white dial signed CORUM and LION HEART with gold-tone indices, and the gold-tone movement spacer ring.

Brand heritage

Corum was founded in 1955 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, by Gaston Ries and René Bannwart. It is known for bold designs such as the Coin Watch, the Golden Bridge and the Admiral's Cup with its twelve-sided case. The Lion Heart uses the same twelve-sided case shape in a dress watch.
