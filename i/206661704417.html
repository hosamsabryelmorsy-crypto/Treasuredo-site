Brand heritage

Zodiac was founded in 1882 in Le Locle, Switzerland, by Ariste Calame. Its Sea Wolf of 1953 was one of the first dive watches sold to the public. In the 1970s Zodiac produced some of its boldest designs, including the Olympos with its asymmetric "manta ray" case and the high-beat SST models.
