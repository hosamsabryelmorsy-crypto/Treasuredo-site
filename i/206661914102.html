Brand heritage

Fendi was founded in Rome in 1925 by Adele and Edoardo Fendi. The double-F logo, designed by Karl Lagerfeld after he joined the house in 1965, became one of the best known luxury monograms, and the house's 1990s eyewear is known for bold gold metalwork and logo medallions.
