About this item

A genuine 1960s pair of French women's sunglasses in the bold, rounded oval shape of the mod era. The thick front is cut from translucent amber-red tortoise acetate with darker brown and lighter honey streaks running through it. The bridge is sculpted into a high keyhole with moulded acetate nose pads on the inside. Each outer end piece carries two small silver rivets, and the plain translucent amber temples are joined with brass barrel hinges and finish in flat teardrop tips. The flat lenses are tinted olive-brown, with no gradient.

The inner temples are stamped "MADE IN FRANCE". There is no maker's name or model number on the frame, so it is sold as an unbranded French frame.
