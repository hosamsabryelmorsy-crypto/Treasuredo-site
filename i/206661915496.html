About this item

A genuine mid-century pair of women's cat eye sunglasses. The narrow, rectangular front sweeps up into sharp pointed outer corners, each set with a pair of small silver rivets. The frame is laminated acetate: a satin pearlescent olive-gold face over a black back layer, which shows as a fine dark outline along the top edge and as solid black on the inside of the frame. The temples carry the same olive-gold colour on the outside, black on the inside, with a gently wavy profile typical of the period, and are joined to the front with riveted metal hinges. Fitted with warm amber-brown tinted sun lenses.
