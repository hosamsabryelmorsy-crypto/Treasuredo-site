About this item

An original 1980s pair of Christian Dior sunglasses, model 2535 in colour 43. Seen from the front, each lens sits in a rose-bronze metal rim that rises at the outer corner into a small pointed cat-eye wing, and a fine pale-gold wire floats above each lens as a second brow line, the "double wire" look this model is known for. A slim gold bridge joins the lenses. The temples are split gold wires with the interlocking CD logo near the hinges, ending in translucent pink tips signed "Christian Dior". The lenses are a warm brown gradient that fades to light peach at the bottom.

Brand heritage

Christian Dior opened his Paris couture house in 1946 and launched the "New Look" in 1947. From 1970 Dior sunglasses were made under licence by Optyl, with frames produced in Austria and Germany. These 1970s and 80s Dior frames are collected today for their sculptural designs and quality.
