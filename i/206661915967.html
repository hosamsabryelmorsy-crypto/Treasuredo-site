Brand heritage

Viennaline was an Austrian eyewear brand closely tied to Optyl, the light, heat-mouldable material developed in Austria in the 1960s and used for many of the best-known fashion frames of the 1970s and 1980s. Austrian-made Viennaline Optyl frames are sought after for their bold shapes, rich translucent colours and light weight.
