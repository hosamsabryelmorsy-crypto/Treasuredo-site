About this item

A chunky vintage pair of Italian sunglasses in thick black acetate. The front has a straight flat top, a keyhole-style bridge and wide, rounded rectangular lenses in a solid brown tint. Each end-piece is decorated with small white inlaid stars, two on the frame corner and two on the temple, and the front section of each temple is ribbed with deep grooves. Riveted multi-barrel metal hinges. The inside of the temple is stamped "ITALY" and carries a small plaque reading "SUNVIOL EXTRA".

About the maker

The frame is marked "Sunviol Extra" and "Italy". We could not find reliable published history for this name, so we describe only what is marked on the frame. The thick acetate, riveted hinges and star inlays are typical of Italian sunglasses of the 1960s and 1970s.
