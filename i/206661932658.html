Brand heritage

FRED is the Parisian jeweller founded in 1936 by Fred Samuel. Its eyewear line, FRED Lunettes, is made in France and takes its design cues from the house's nautical jewellery. Fred Samuel was a keen sailor, and this inspired the Force 10 cable bracelet.
