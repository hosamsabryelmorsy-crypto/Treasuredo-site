Brand heritage

Cartier was founded in Paris in 1847 by Louis-François Cartier. In 1904 Louis Cartier designed the Santos wristwatch for the aviator Alberto Santos-Dumont, and its exposed screws became one of the house's best known motifs. Cartier launched its first eyewear collection in 1983, and the Vendome Santos aviator, with screw-decorated bridge and hinges, became one of its signature designs of the 1980s.
