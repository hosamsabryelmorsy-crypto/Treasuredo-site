About this item

An Italian-made square sunglass by Lastes, cut from warm Havana tortoiseshell acetate with a gently rounded square front, a sculpted saddle bridge and solid green (G-15 style) sun lenses. The thick acetate temples carry a rectangular silver-tone plate at the hinge and two slanted silver bands just behind it, and the frame is joined with sturdy multi-barrel riveted hinges. One temple is stamped "BREV. (LASTES) M. 6008", the other "MADE IN ITALY A.M.", and one ear tip is stamped with the size "50.22".

Brand heritage

Lastes was an Italian eyewear maker of the 1970s to 1990s, also known for its "Swing by Lastes" and "Finissimo by Lastes" lines. "BREV." is short for "brevetto", Italian for patent, and appears on many Lastes frames of the period.
