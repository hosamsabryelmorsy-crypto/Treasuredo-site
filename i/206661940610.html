Brand heritage

Yves Saint Laurent founded his Paris couture house with Pierre Bergé in 1961. In the 1960s and 1970s he introduced the women's tuxedo "Le Smoking", the safari jacket and the Rive Gauche ready-to-wear boutiques, and YSL accessories, eyewear included, carried that Parisian style worldwide. French-made YSL frames from the 1970s are collected today.
