About this item

A glamorous original 1950s cat eye by Nilsol of Italy: translucent honey-yellow acetate with sparkling clear rhinestone clusters at the upswept corners and soft pale green lenses. The inner temple is stamped "NILSOL-ITALY".

Brand heritage

Nilsol was an Italian sunglasses brand of the Sordelli family firm, a maker whose eyewear roots go back to 1921; the "NIL SOL" name was registered for sunglasses in the late 1950s. Its mid-century frames are known for playful colours and jewelled details.
