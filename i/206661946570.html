About this item

A genuine vintage Cazal 901 from the "TARGA design" series, the large teardrop aviator that defined 1980s Cazal metal frames. It comes with its original grey Cazal case, a spare pair of solid brown TARGA design lenses and the original "création Cazal" leaflet.

The frame has a silver/gunmetal-tone rim with the signature ball-stud details, a gold-tone double bar bridge with the sculpted T centre piece, a clear amber nose bridge and silver temples with black tips. It is fitted with large brown-to-clear gradient lenses. The right lens (as you look at it) carries the original white "TARGA design Cazal" print.

Brand heritage

Cazal was founded in Germany in 1975 by designer Cari Zalloni, whose name gives the brand its name (CA-ri ZAL-loni). His sculptural, architectural frames became icons of 1980s style and street culture, and original West German Cazals are now sought-after collector pieces.
