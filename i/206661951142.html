About this item

A genuine vintage Carrera Porsche Design 5652 optical frame in colour 40, a bright gold tone, made in Germany in the 1980s. The slim round/panto full-rim wire front sweeps into a fine sculpted bridge with integrated black nose pads, finished with straight fine temples and black temple tips. The outer temple is signed "CARRERA PORSCHE DESIGN" with the Porsche Design emblem, the inner temple carries the full factory stamps and serial number, and the right lens has the printed "PORSCHE DESIGN" logo.

It comes with a vintage Carrera Porsche Design box (Carrera International Austria), a Carrera Porsche Design "The Original" authenticity certificate card and a Porsche Design booklet.

Brand heritage

Porsche Design was founded in 1972 by Ferdinand Alexander Porsche, designer of the Porsche 911. From 1978 its eyewear was made under licence by the Austrian maker Carrera, and the Carrera Porsche Design frames of the late 1970s and 1980s, with their clean engineered lines, are now sought-after collector pieces.
