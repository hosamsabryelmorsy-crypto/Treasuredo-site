Brand heritage

Silhouette was founded in Linz, Austria, in 1964 by Anneliese and Arnold Schmied and still designs and makes its eyewear in Linz. The brand became known worldwide for fashionable 1970s and 1980s sunglasses and later for its ultra-light rimless Titan Minimal Art frames. Vintage Silhouette frames made in Austria are sought after by eyewear collectors.
