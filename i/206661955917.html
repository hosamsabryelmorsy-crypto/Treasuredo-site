Brand heritage

Carrera was founded in 1956 by Wilhelm Anger in Austria. Famous for its lightweight Optyl frames and sporty, technical designs, Carrera became one of the defining eyewear brands of the 1970s and 1980s, and its vintage aviators are today sought-after collector pieces.
