Created during Carrera’s celebrated collaboration with Porsche Design, this German-made optical frame translates precision-led industrial design into an elegant, oversized silhouette. Its polished gold-tone cast-metal construction surrounds generously proportioned soft-square butterfly lenses, finished with subtly winged outer corners, a sculpted bridge, dark nose pads and slender temples with black tips.


The factory markings remain clearly visible:


* Model: 5652

* Colour code: 40

* Lens width: 58mm

* Bridge width: 15mm

* Temple length: 125mm

* Marked: FRAME GERMANY


The frame retains clear Porsche Design-branded presentation lenses. Their prescription and protective properties are not confirmed, so they should be assessed or replaced by an optician before regular use.


Exceptionally well preserved for its age, the frame presents with a bright, even finish and only light storage or handling wear visible. No obvious break or repair can be seen in the photographs. The lenses may display minor handling marks.
