About this item

A vintage pair of Cottet "Fidia" pilot sunglasses in slim gold-tone metal. Cottet is a historic eyewear name with roots in the French spectacle-making tradition of Morez in the Jura, and its vintage metal frames are collected for their fine finishing. This model is one of the more decorative designs: the front has a straight top bar above a straight lower bridge, and the wide lenses have a flat top line and sharply angled outer corners, with an open triangular wire detail at each hinge. The upper inner corner of each rim carries a fine textured white-lilac inlay, and the temples are finished in a white and lilac diagonal crosshatch pattern, ending in ivory-colour tips. The lenses are a brown gradient that fades from rich brown at the top to almost clear at the bottom.

Brand and markings

The frame is signed with the Cottet logo, "COTTET® FIDIA 56□15", on the inside of one temple and "1740 130" on the other, and the Cottet logo is printed in blue on the nose pads.
