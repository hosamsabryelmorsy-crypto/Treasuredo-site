About this item

A true vintage pair of women's cat-eye sunglasses in the classic mid-century style. The front is cut from dark chocolate-brown acetate laminated over a translucent honey-amber layer, so a fine golden line glows around each lens and along the edges of the frame. The brows sweep up into sharp, pointed wings, and the outer side of each temple carries a small silver-tone fin-shaped ornament near the hinge. The temples are reinforced with a metal core wire, a hallmark of quality frames of the period, and the lenses are a warm solid brown tint.
