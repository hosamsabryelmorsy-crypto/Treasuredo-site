About this item

A new old stock (deadstock) pair of 1960s IOM men's sunglasses, stored and never worn. The thick, glossy black acetate rectangular front has the classic mid-century "wayfarer" look, with solid brown sun lenses and a small silver-tone lightning-bolt (zig-zag) metal inlay at each outer corner. Riveted metal hinges join the front to flat gold-filled temples with a fine diamond (checkered) engraved pattern on the outer face and a reeded block at the hinge end, finished with black acetate earpiece tips. The front is stamped "50-20" inside, one temple is stamped "140-5 1/2" (140 mm / 5 1/2 inch), and one earpiece tip carries the stamped IOM emblem.

Brand heritage

IOM frames were made in Hong Kong in the 1960s and are among the high-quality frames of that era, built with solid acetate fronts and gold-filled metal parts. The IOM emblem on the earpiece tip shows the letters I and M in small boxes either side of an O with a diamond. This pair carries the emblem and its sizes but no model number. Dual metric/inch temple sizing such as "140-5 1/2" and riveted acetate fronts with engraved gold-filled temples are typical of the best frames of the period.
