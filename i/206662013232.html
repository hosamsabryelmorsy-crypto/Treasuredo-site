Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by Giuseppe Ratti, whose company made protective goggles for pilots and racing drivers. The name comes from "per il sole" (for the sun). The silver arrow on the temples, first used in the 1930s, became the brand's signature. Eyewear marked "Persol Ratti" comes from the decades when the company was still run by the Ratti family, before Luxottica acquired Persol in 1995, which is why "Ratti" pieces are prized by collectors.
