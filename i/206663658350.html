About this watch

A new old stock Vostok Amphibia from the first years of the Russian Federation, never worn and still with its factory hang tag. The off-white dial carries the double-headed eagle, the waving Russian tricolour and the banner "РОССИЯ" (Rossiya / Russia), a commemorative design made by the Chistopol watch factory after the end of the Soviet Union. It sits in the classic round Amphibia case with a rotating red and black bezel, a large knurled crown and the famous screw-down caseback with the floating crown system, rated to 200 m. The original passport is stamped with a release date of 13.02.93 (13 February 1993).

Brand heritage

Vostok (Восток, "East") has made watches in Chistopol, Tatarstan, since 1942. In 1967 it introduced the Amphibia, a mechanical dive watch rated to 200 m that used an inventive compressible caseback and floating crown seal instead of expensive machining. The Amphibia was issued to Soviet military and navy personnel and remains one of the most collected Russian watches today.
