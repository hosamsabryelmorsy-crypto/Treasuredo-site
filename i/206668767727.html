About this watch

A vintage French Mortima mechanical wristwatch with a striking two-tone dial: a black centre, a matte gold chapter ring with silver Arabic 12, 6 and 9 and silver baton markers, and an outer black minute track printed in gold from 5 to 60. The dial is signed "MORTIMA WATCH 21 JEWELS" under 12 and "CALENDAR" with the circled-cross emblem above 6, with a date window at 3 o'clock (red numerals). Gold dauphine-style hands with luminous inserts, a domed crystal and a fluted gold-tone crown complete the military-inspired look.

Brand heritage

Mortima was a French watch brand associated with Cattin & Cie of Morteau, in the Franche-Comté watchmaking region on the Swiss border. It was at its peak in the 1950s and 1960s and is best known today for its Super Datomatic and Super De Luxe models.
