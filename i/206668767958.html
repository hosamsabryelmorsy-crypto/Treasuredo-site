A spare or donor movement for a Baumatic watch of the 1970s, or a display piece for collectors.

Brand heritage

Baume & Mercier traces its roots to the Baume family workshop founded in 1830 in the Swiss Jura; the Baume & Mercier partnership was formed in Geneva in 1918. The Baumatic name was used on the brand's automatic watches, and the BM 12820 is documented in auction catalogues as the 30-jewel micro-rotor automatic calibre of 1970s Baumatic models.
