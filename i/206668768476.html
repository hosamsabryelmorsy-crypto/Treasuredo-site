About this item

A complete Hamilton-signed Swiss automatic wristwatch movement, removed from a watch and offered on its own. The plate is stamped "2825-2", the ETA calibre based on the workhorse ETA 2824-2 that adds an offset small-seconds hand. The automatic rotor is signed "HAMILTON" with the H logo cut-out and "TWENTY-FIVE 25 JEWELS - SWISS". It comes with its white date disc (1-31), black luminous hour and minute hands, small-seconds hand, stem and crown fitted, ready for a project or as a replacement for a Hamilton watch with this calibre.

Brand heritage

Hamilton was founded in 1892 in Lancaster, Pennsylvania, made its name with railroad-grade pocket watches and military watches, and is today part of the Swatch Group, producing its watches in Switzerland with ETA-based movements.
