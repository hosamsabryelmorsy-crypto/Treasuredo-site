About this watch

A true 1970s design piece: the NINO Swiss Automatic reference N-205, in a bold TV-screen (cushion) stainless steel case with a brushed top and polished bevels. The grey-anthracite sunburst dial carries the NINO star logo, applied silver baton markers with lume inserts, matching baton hands and a white date window at 3 o'clock. The caseback is signed "NINO SWISS", "AUTOMATIC ALL STAINLESS STEEL WATER RESISTANT" and engraved with the reference N-205.

Brand heritage

Montres Nino SA was a Swiss watch company that opened at the end of the 1960s. Nino designed its own cases and dials and fitted movements sourced from established Swiss movement makers, producing the distinctive, boldly shaped automatics typical of 1970s Swiss design.
