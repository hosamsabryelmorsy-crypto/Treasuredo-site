About this item

An uncased Longines manual-wind wristwatch movement, complete with its original signed dial and gold-tone hands, held in a steel movement spacer ring. The movement bridge is signed "LONGINES" and numbered 6861288, which by Longines serial dating places it around 1944. The dial is a classic 1940s Longines design: champagne-silver with printed Arabic numerals 12, 2, 4, 8 and 10, small gold dot hour markers, a dot minute track and a recessed sub-seconds dial at 6 o'clock. An ideal base for a restoration or custom case project.

Brand heritage

Longines was founded in 1832 in Saint-Imier, Switzerland, and its winged hourglass logo, registered in 1889, is among the oldest registered trademarks still in use. In the 1930s and 1940s Longines built its own in-house manual-wind calibres, used in its dress watches and in military watches of the era.
