About this watch

A 1940s Longines men's wristwatch head in a round stainless steel case with a stepped bezel and long, angular straight lugs. The aged cream-grey dial carries black painted Arabic numerals, a faded Longines signature below 12, blued steel hands and a small seconds subdial at 6. Inside is a signed Longines manual-wind movement, serial 7002319, which dates it to around 1945. The case back is stamped inside "ACIER INOX", the winged hourglass "EFCo Longines" mark and case number 6145. Sold as a restoration project: the crown and stem are missing and the movement is shown removed from the case.

Brand heritage

Longines was founded in 1832 in Saint-Imier, Switzerland, and its winged hourglass is one of the oldest registered trademarks still in use. EFCo stands for Ernest Francillon & Co, the family firm behind Longines; the EFCo mark is found inside genuine Longines cases of this period.
