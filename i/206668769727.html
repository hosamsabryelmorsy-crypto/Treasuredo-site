About this watch

A 1970s Omega De Ville ladies' automatic in the clean square design that defined the collection in that decade. The deep navy blue dial (it reads almost black in shade) carries the applied Omega symbol, "OMEGA" and "DE VILLE AUTOMATIC" in gold, slim gold baton markers and gold stick hands. The gold-plated case has a thin bezel and a faceted, bevel-cut square crystal, a stainless steel caseback and an Omega-signed crown. It sits on a gold-tone Omega mesh bracelet with an Omega-signed fold-over clasp, and comes in a red Omega presentation box.

Brand heritage

Omega was founded in 1848 by Louis Brandt in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland. The De Ville name first appeared in 1960 as a dress line within the Seamaster family and became its own collection in 1967, known for elegant, design-led cases such as this square model.
