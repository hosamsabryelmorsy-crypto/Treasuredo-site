About this watch

A vintage Edox men's quartz watch from the Iraq of the 1980s, with a full-colour portrait of Saddam Hussein in beret and green military uniform printed on the lower left of a cream dial. Watches with the president's portrait on the dial were made by many Swiss and Japanese brands for the Iraqi market in that period and are now sought-after historical collectibles. This one has a signed Edox dial with the applied Edox emblem at 12, thin silver baton markers, date at 3 and "Swiss Made" at 6, in a brushed steel barrel-shaped case on an integrated brick-link steel bracelet. It comes in a vintage blue Edox box, signed EDOX on the lid.

Brand heritage

Edox was founded in 1884 by Christian Rüefli-Flury in Biel, Switzerland, and is today based in Les Genevez in the Swiss Jura. The name comes from the Greek word for "hour", and the brand is best known for the Delfin (1961) and Hydro-Sub (1965) dive watches.
