About this watch

A genuine 1970s Seiko Quartz QZ, reference 0923-5000, from the first generation of Seiko's mass-produced quartz watches. The cushion-shaped "TV" case in stainless steel frames a deep navy-blue sunburst dial with applied silver baton markers (double baton at 12), silver baton hands, a gold-tone SEIKO logo, "QUARTZ QZ" script, the gold-tone hexagonal quartz emblem and a day-date window at 3 o'clock. It is worn on its integrated stainless steel bracelet with Seiko-signed fold-over clasp and comes with a Seiko presentation box.

Brand heritage

Seiko launched the Quartz Astron in 1969, the world's first quartz wristwatch. Within a few years it brought quartz accuracy to a wider public with lines such as the QZ series of the mid-1970s, powered by the calibre 0923 day-date quartz movement. The serial number on this caseback (532862) dates it to March 1975.
