About this watch

A classic 1960s Enicar Ultrasonic dress watch. The champagne sunburst dial carries the Enicar Saturn logo, "ENICAR ultrasonic" and "INCABLOC 17 JEWELS", with applied gold Arabic 12, 9 and 6, applied gold batons, gold dauphine hands and a framed date window at 3 o'clock. The 34mm gold-plated case has a slim bezel and faceted lugs, closed by a stainless steel screw-down caseback engraved with the Enicar logo, "Ultrasonic", "Waterproof", "Incabloc", "Swiss" and reference 100/183. Worn on a brown textured leather strap with gold-tone buckle and supplied in a vintage presentation box.

Brand heritage

Enicar was founded in 1914 by Ariste Racine in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland; the brand name is "Racine" spelled backwards. Enicar became famous for its Sherpa line and for equipping the 1956 Swiss Everest-Lhotse expedition. "Ultrasonic" marked Enicar's 1950s-60s movements cleaned with ultrasonic technology, a selling point of the era.
