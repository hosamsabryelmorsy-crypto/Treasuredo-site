About this watch

A 1970s Ginsbo-Matic Coastguard 200 automatic in a cushion-shaped stainless steel case. The dial has a gold-to-olive gradient with fine vertical stripes, applied silver baton markers with textured tops, baton hands with lume strips, the green G emblem and a day-date window at 3 o'clock. The caseback shows the Coastguard submarine emblem and is signed "Ginsbo-Matic Swiss" and "Coastguard". It comes on its signed Ginsbo stainless steel brick-link bracelet and in a presentation box.

Brand heritage

Ginsbo is a Swiss watch brand, best known for its automatic "Ginsbo-Matic" watches of the 1960s and 1970s. The Coastguard line, with its submarine caseback emblem, was among the brand's sportier designs and was made in several versions, including the Coastguard 100, 200 and 300.
