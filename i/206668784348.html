About Catena

Catena is a Swiss watch brand (Catena SA) with roots in the early 20th century. In the 1970s and 1980s Catena became known worldwide for affordable, glamorous gold-tone dress watches with textured and basket-weave bracelets, slim quartz cases and decorative dials, sold widely in Europe and the Middle East. This piece is a typical example of that era: a Catena-signed dial paired with a reliable Japanese quartz movement, as marked "JAPAN MOVT" on the dial. Today these vintage Catena pieces are sought after for their bold 70s/80s style.
