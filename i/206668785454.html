About this watch

A vintage Roven Dino Swiss quartz watch, reference 8501T, with a printed portrait of Muammar Gaddafi (also spelled Qaddafi, Gadhafi or Kadhafi), the former leader of Libya, on the lower left of a white vertically-striped dial. The dial is signed Roven Dino with "QUARTZ" and "Origin Swiss Made", applied gold Roman numerals at XII, IX and VI, gold baton markers, a gold-framed date window at 3 and slim gold dauphine hands. It sits in a round polished stainless steel case with a gold-tone bezel ring and gold-tone crown, on a two-tone steel and gold multi-link bracelet with an RD-signed fold-over clasp. It comes in a grey "Swiss Quartz" presentation box.

Collector context

Muammar Gaddafi led Libya from 1969 until 2011. During that period, wristwatches with his portrait on the dial were produced by a number of Swiss and other watch brands and circulated in Libya and the wider region as presentation, commemorative and souvenir pieces. They are now collected as historical memorabilia of that era, alongside similar portrait-dial watches made for other Middle Eastern heads of state. At the top end, special-order pieces made for Libya by houses such as Patek Philippe and Chopard have appeared at major auction houses. Signed Swiss-movement examples like this one, with sapphire crystal and steel case, are less commonly seen than the many unsigned and brass souvenir versions.

This watch is offered purely as a historical collectible. No specific provenance, recipient or presentation history is claimed beyond what is visible on the watch itself.

Brand heritage

Roven Dino is a watch brand using Swiss quartz movements, known for dress watches and sport models such as the Talon and the Mariana diver, typically fitted with sapphire crystals.
