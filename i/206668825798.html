Brand heritage

Silhouette was founded in Linz, Austria, in 1964 by Arnold and Anneliese Schmied and still makes its frames in Linz. The brand is known for lightweight eyewear, and its titanium frames from the 1980s and 1990s are popular with collectors of vintage designer sunglasses.
