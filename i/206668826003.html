About this item

A vintage pair of Lanvin Paris sunglasses, model 713, colour 60 "Noir Arpege / Arpege Black", made in France. The oval front is clear crystal acetate with black inlays at the outer corners. Twin gold wires sweep across the outer ends of the front, leaving open cut-outs between them. The slim gold metal temples have diamond-shaped hinge ornaments and black tips. The lenses are a grey-violet gradient. The frame comes with its original labelled Lanvin box, a Lanvin leather-look pouch and the original Lanvin lens template disc.

Brand heritage

Lanvin is the oldest French couture house still in operation. Jeanne Lanvin founded it in Paris in 1889. Its logo shows Jeanne and her daughter Marguerite and is based on a 1907 drawing by Paul Iribe. The same mother-and-daughter emblem appears on the box of this pair. The colour name "Arpege" refers to Lanvin's famous 1927 perfume.
