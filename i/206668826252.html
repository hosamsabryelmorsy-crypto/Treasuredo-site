About this item

A 1990s pair of Polo by Ralph Lauren "Classic VI Flex" metal sunglasses. The round panto front is gold-tone metal with a brown tortoiseshell-pattern enamel wrap on the rims, a stepped keyhole bridge with a beaded top edge, ornate engraved end pieces, slim gold temples with a tortoise-pattern band near the hinges and amber tortoise temple tips. Fitted with grey gradient lenses (dark at the top, fading to clear at the bottom) and clear adjustable nose pads.

Brand heritage

Ralph Lauren founded Polo in New York in 1967 and built it into one of the best-known names in classic American style. The "Polo Classic" eyewear line of the 1980s and 1990s, with its numbered models such as the Classic VI, is now sought after by vintage eyewear collectors for its traditional round and panto shapes and detailed metalwork.
