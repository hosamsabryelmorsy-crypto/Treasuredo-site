Brand heritage

Gianfranco Ferre (1944-2007) trained as an architect in Milan, which earned him the nickname "the architect of fashion". He founded his own house in 1978 and was artistic director of Christian Dior from 1989 to 1996. The GFF eyewear line from that era is known for clean, structured metal frames made in Italy.
