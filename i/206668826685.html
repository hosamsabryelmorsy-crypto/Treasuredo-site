Brand heritage

Gianfranco Ferré (1944–2007), trained as an architect and known as "the architect of fashion", founded his Milan house in 1978 and was artistic director of Christian Dior from 1989 to 1996. His GFF eyewear of the late 1980s and 1990s was made in Italy and is collected today for its clean architectural lines and fine metal detailing.
