Brand heritage

Gianfranco Ferré (1944-2007) trained as an architect in Milan and became known as "the architect of fashion". He founded his own label in Milan in 1978 and was artistic director of Christian Dior from 1989 to 1996. His eyewear, Gianfranco Ferré Lunettes, was produced under licence by Safilo, the Italian eyewear maker.
