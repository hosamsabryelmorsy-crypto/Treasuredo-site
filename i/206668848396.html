About this item

An original vintage Omega caseback for case reference 166.0174, sold on its own as a spare part. The outside carries the engraved Omega symbol and "OMEGA" inside a circle, with six notches around the edge for a caseback opener (screw-down type). The inside is stamped "ACIER INOXYDABLE", the Omega Watch Co. triangle with "FAB. SUISSE SWISS MADE", a case-maker hallmark, and the numbers "166 0174" and "366 0833". A black gasket is fitted on the rim. Measured diameter: 33.3mm.

Brand heritage

Omega was founded by Louis Brandt in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, in 1848 and moved to Biel/Bienne in 1880. The seven-digit 166.xxxx reference format used here identifies Omega's steel automatic cases of the late 1960s and 1970s.
