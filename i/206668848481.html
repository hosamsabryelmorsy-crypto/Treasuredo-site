Brand heritage

Omega was founded in 1848 by Louis Brandt in La Chaux-de-Fonds and is based in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland. The De Ville name first appeared in 1960 within the Seamaster family and became its own collection in 1967, Omega's line of slim, elegant dress watches. In the late 1970s and 1980s many De Ville models were fitted with Omega's own Swiss quartz calibres and the distinctive skyline case back seen here.
