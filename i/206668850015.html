About this item

An original Omega Constellation snap-on case back in stainless steel, with the famous Geneva Observatory medallion (observatory dome under eight stars) at the centre. The inside is signed with the Omega Watch Co. triangle logo and "Acier Inoxydable", with the references 198.0129 and 398.0858, and the outside is stamped 1333. The 198.01xx Constellation quartz references of the late 1970s and 1980s use Omega's calibre 1333. A correct replacement part for restoring a Constellation of this reference.

Brand heritage

Omega launched the Constellation in 1952 to celebrate its chronometer precision records. The observatory medallion on the case back became the collection's signature and is one of the most recognised details in vintage watch collecting.
