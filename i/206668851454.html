Brand heritage

Omega was founded in 1848 by Louis Brandt in La Chaux-de-Fonds and is based in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland. It is known for the Speedmaster, worn on the Moon in 1969, and for the Seamaster and Constellation lines. Original Omega bracelets are sought after by collectors and owners restoring their watches.
