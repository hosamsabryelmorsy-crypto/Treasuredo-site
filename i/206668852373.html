About this item

A vintage Omega "bumper" automatic movement, calibre 351, still mounted in a 34mm stainless steel wristwatch case with elegant hooded, curved lugs and its crown. The movement bridge is signed "OMEGA WATCH Co. SWISS", "SEVENTEEN 17 JEWELS", serial 11876451, and the calibre number 351 with the Omega symbol is stamped under the barrel wheel. The serial number dates it to around 1948. Sold as a project base: the dial, hands, oscillating weight (bumper rotor), caseback and crystal are not included.

Brand heritage

Omega was founded by Louis Brandt in La Chaux-de-Fonds in 1848 and moved to Biel/Bienne, Switzerland, in 1880. In the 1940s Omega built its 28.10 RA / 330-350 series "bumper" automatics, in which the half-rotor swings between two springs, before the full-rotor 470/500 series of the 1950s. The cal. 351 is the 17-jewel, centre-seconds member of this family, used in Omega and early Seamaster automatics.
