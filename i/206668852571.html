Brand heritage

Omega was founded in 1848 by Louis Brandt in La Chaux-de-Fonds and is based in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland. The Seamaster line was introduced in 1948 and quickly became one of Omega's best-known families; the hippocampus case-back medallion has been its emblem since the late 1950s.
