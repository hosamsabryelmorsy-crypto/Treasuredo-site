About this item

An original Omega snap-on caseback for the reference 136.0099, the 35 mm manual-wind Omega Geneve dress watch of the early-to-mid 1970s (calibre 1030 family). The outside carries the embossed Omega logo medallion; the inside is stamped with the Omega Watch Co. triangle hallmark, "ACIER INOXYDABLE", "FAB. SUISSE / SWISS MADE" and the case reference "136 0099". A correct replacement part for a 136.0099 that is missing its back or has a damaged one.

Brand heritage

Omega was founded in 1848 in La Chaux-de-Fonds by Louis Brandt and has been based in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland since 1880. The Geneve line was Omega's everyday dress range of the 1950s to 1970s, and the 136.0099 is one of its best-known manual-wind references.
