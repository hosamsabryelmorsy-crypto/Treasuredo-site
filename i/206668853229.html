About this item

An original vintage Omega case back in stainless steel, sold on its own as a spare part. It is the back for the Omega Geneve Megaquartz 32 KHz Day-Date, case reference 196.0030 (calibre 1310), from the early-to-mid 1970s. The outside carries the raised Omega symbol and "OMEGA" medallion; the rim has six small notches. The inside is engraved with the Omega Watch Co. "Fab. Suisse / Swiss Made" triangle, "CARRURE - LUNETTE PLAQUÉ OR G 20 MICRONS", "FOND ACIER INOXYDABLE", the reference "1960030" and a case maker's mark. It measures 33.7 mm across (caliper).

Brand heritage

Omega was founded by Louis Brandt in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, in 1848 and moved to Biel/Bienne in 1880. In the early 1970s Omega launched the Megaquartz 32 KHz, powered by calibre 1310, its first in-house quartz movement, produced from about 1973 to 1978.
