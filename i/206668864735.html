About this watch

A rare piece of 1980s Iraqi history: a Swiss-made Oris Quartz dress watch with a colour portrait of Saddam Hussein in olive military uniform printed on its champagne-gold dial. Watches with his portrait were ordered in quantity from Swiss and Japanese makers and handed out as official presentation gifts in Iraq during the 1980s, which makes them sought-after historical collectibles today. This example carries the full Oris signature on both dial and caseback.

Brand heritage

Oris was founded in 1904 in Hölstein, Switzerland, and remains one of the few independent Swiss watchmakers. In the 1980s it produced slim quartz dress watches like this one alongside its mechanical models.
