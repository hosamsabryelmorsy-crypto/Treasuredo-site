About this item

An original Omega cushion-shaped caseback for the case reference 162.025, the vintage Omega De Ville automatic of the late 1960s (calibre 565 family). The outside has a brushed finish with a polished round centre; the inside is stamped with the Omega Watch Co. triangle hallmark, "FAB. SUISSE / SWISS MADE", "LUNETTE PLAQUE OR G 20 MICRONS", the Omega logo, the Swiss bear-head control mark, "FOND ACIER INOXYDABLE" and the case reference "162.025". A correct replacement back for a 162.025 that is missing its back or has a damaged one.

Brand heritage

Omega was founded in 1848 in La Chaux-de-Fonds by Louis Brandt and has been based in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland since 1880. The De Ville line began in the 1960s as Omega's dress range, and the 162.025 cushion case with the automatic calibre 565 is one of its classic references of that era.
