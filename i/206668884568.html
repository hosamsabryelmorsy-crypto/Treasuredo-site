About this item

An original Omega caseback from a vintage Seamaster Quartz, reference 196.0239 / 396.0936 (Omega's quartz Seamaster of the late 1970s and early 1980s, calibre 1430 family). The outside carries the raised Seamaster hippocampus (seahorse) medallion with the word SEAMASTER and the Omega symbol. The inside is stamped OMEGA WATCH Co., FAB. SUISSE, SWISS MADE, the Omega symbol and the reference numbers 196.0239 and 396.0936. A good replacement part for a Seamaster with a damaged or missing back.

Brand heritage

Omega, founded in 1848 in Switzerland, introduced the Seamaster line in 1948. The hippocampus emblem on the caseback has identified Seamaster models for decades.
