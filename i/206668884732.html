It also carries old hand-scratched watchmaker service marks. The outside is a plain, smooth domed steel back. A correct original part for restoring or completing a vintage Universal Genève watch.

Brand heritage

Universal Watch was founded in 1894 in Le Locle by Numa-Emile Descombes and Ulysse Georges Perret and later moved to Geneva, becoming Universal Genève. It is known for its Compax chronographs and for the Polerouter, launched in 1954 for SAS polar flights.
