Brand heritage

Omega was founded by Louis Brandt in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, in 1848 and moved to Biel/Bienne in 1880. In the 1970s and 1980s Omega built a range of slim quartz dress watches, many in the De Ville family, around in-house based quartz calibres such as the 1365.
