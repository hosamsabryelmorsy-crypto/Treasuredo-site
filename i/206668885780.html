About this item

An original vintage Omega stainless steel caseback for the case reference 135.041, the late-1960s Omega Genève manual-wind (calibre 601 family), sold on its own as a spare part. The inside is stamped "ACIER INOXYDABLE", the Omega Watch Co. triangle hallmark, "FAB. SUISSE / SWISS MADE", the case reference "135.041" and a "CB" case-maker mark. The brushed outside is engraved "WATERPROOF" with a small engraved motif, and the rim has small locking tabs. It measures 32.6 mm across.

Brand heritage

Omega was founded by Louis Brandt in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, in 1848 and has been based in Biel/Bienne since 1880. The Genève line was Omega's everyday collection of the 1950s to 1970s, and the steel 135.041 with the manual-wind calibre 601 is one of its best-known references from the late 1960s.
