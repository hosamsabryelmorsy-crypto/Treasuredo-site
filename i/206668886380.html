About this item

Four connected genuine Concord Saratoga bracelet links in stainless steel, for extending or repairing a Concord Saratoga bracelet. They show the Saratoga's signature integrated design: brushed centre bars crossed by polished, bevelled cross-pieces, joined with their original pins.

Brand heritage

Concord was founded in 1908 in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland. Introduced in the late 1970s and early 1980s, the Saratoga became one of the house's best-known collections, prized for its sculpted, fully integrated bracelet. Original Saratoga links are no longer easy to find, and they are the only correct way to lengthen a Saratoga bracelet.
