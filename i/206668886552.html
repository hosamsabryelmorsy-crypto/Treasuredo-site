It comes with its two-tone silver dial signed "CYMA" with the C logo, "CYMAFLEX" and "SWISS MADE", applied gold arrowhead and baton hour markers (per seller) and a dotted minute track, gold leaf hands (per seller) with a black centre seconds hand, a gold fluted crown with stem (per seller), and a gold-tone movement ring stamped "METAL".

Brand heritage

Cyma was founded in 1862 by the Schwob brothers in Switzerland and was closely tied to the Tavannes Watch Co., whose factory produced Cyma movements. "Cymaflex" was the brand's shock-protection system for the balance, featured on its mid-century calibres.
