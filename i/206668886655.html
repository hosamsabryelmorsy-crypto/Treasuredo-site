Inside, it is stamped with the Omega Watch Co. triangle, the Omega symbol, "BREVET + 508925", the case reference 166.0136 and the number 366.0826, plus "CARRURE-LUNETTE PLAQUE OR L 80 MICRONS / FOND ACIER INOXYDABLE" (case middle and bezel gold-plated, back in stainless steel).

Brand heritage

Omega introduced the Seamaster in 1948, and the hippocampus seahorse emblem on the caseback became the hallmark of the line. Vintage Seamaster references with 166.xxxx numbers were the automatic models of the 1960s and 1970s.
