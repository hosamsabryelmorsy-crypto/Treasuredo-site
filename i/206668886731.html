About this item

A genuine vintage Zenith wristwatch case back (spare part), stamped "ZENITH PILOT - SWISS MADE" across the centre with the case serial 9035461, inside the classic ring legend "WATERPROOF * SHOCKPROOF * NON-MAGNETIC * STAINLESS STEEL". The inside carries a decorative guilloché (sunburst/cell) finish and is stamped "MODELE DEPOSE". The faceted (multi-sided) centre section is typical of vintage Zenith steel cases. An ideal replacement or restoration part for a vintage Zenith Pilot.

Brand heritage

Zenith was founded in 1865 by Georges Favre-Jacot in Le Locle, Switzerland, and is one of the oldest Swiss manufactures. The "Pilot" name has been associated with Zenith since the early 20th century, and vintage steel Zenith Pilot wristwatches are sought after by collectors today.
