About this item

An original Omega case back from a vintage Omega De Ville quartz dress watch, reference 195.0075 (1980s). The outside has a brushed finish and is signed with the Omega symbol and "De Ville". The inside is stamped with the Omega Watch Co. Swiss Made triangle hallmark, "Lunette Plaqué Or G 20 Microns, Fond Acier Inoxydable" (20-micron gold-plated bezel, stainless steel back) and the case reference 195 0075.2. It is a good replacement part for a 195.0075 De Ville that has a damaged or missing back.

Brand heritage

Omega was founded in 1848 in La Chaux-de-Fonds and is now based in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland. It introduced the De Ville name in 1960 for its elegant dress watches, and De Ville became a collection of its own in 1967.
