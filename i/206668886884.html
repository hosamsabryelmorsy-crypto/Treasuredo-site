Brand heritage

Zodiac was founded in 1882 by Ariste Calame in Le Locle, Switzerland. The brand is best known for the Sea Wolf, introduced in 1953 as one of the first purpose-built dive watches for the public, and for its automatic models of the 1960s and 70s. Vintage Zodiac parts are in demand among collectors and watchmakers restoring these watches.
