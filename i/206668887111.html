Brand heritage

Founded in 1848 by Louis Brandt in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Omega became one of Switzerland's best-known watchmakers. The De Ville line, introduced in the 1960s as Omega's dress collection, received many slim quartz models in the 1970s and 1980s, including the 192 series.
