Brand heritage

Ebel was founded in 1911 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, by Eugene Blum and Alice Levy; the name comes from their initials (Eugene Blum et Levy). Its Sport Classic, introduced in 1977, with its screwed bezel and wave-link bracelet, became one of the house's best-known designs.
