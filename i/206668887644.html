About this watch

A genuine 1970s Rado NCC 101 in stainless steel, caseback stamped REF 11944 and NCC 101 with the Rado "Water Sealed" emblem. The striking brown dial has a natural tiger-eye / wood-grain pattern with darker streaks, framed by a pale inner rim, applied steel baton markers, a white Rado anchor logo and a framed day-date window at 3 o'clock. It sits in the iconic wide "TV-screen" cushion case on its integrated brushed steel bracelet with Rado-signed clasp, and comes in a Rado presentation box.

Brand heritage

Rado was founded in 1917 in Lengnau, Switzerland, by the Schlup brothers and has used the Rado name since the 1950s. The anchor on the dial marks Rado's automatic watches. In 1962 Rado launched the DiaStar, the first scratch-resistant watch, and during the late 1960s and 1970s the brand became known for bold, futuristic case designs such as the NCC series.
