About this watch

A striking vintage Nivada "Boutique" ladies' tank watch. The dial pairs an orange-red centre panel with a black border set with a frame of small clear stones, signed "Nivada", "BOUTIQUE" and "SWISS", with gold hands and no hour markers. The rectangular gold-plated tank case has the classic rounded side bars and a ribbed crown with a black cabochon, on a steel snap-on caseback stamped with the Nivada "N" shield logo. Inside is a Swiss hand-wound mechanical movement signed "Seventeen 17 Jewels Swiss". Circa 1970s.

Brand heritage

Nivada Grenchen was founded in Grenchen, Switzerland, in 1926 and became known for robust everyday watches such as the Antarctic, the Depthmaster and the Chronoking, as well as elegant dress watches for men and women. The brand was revived in 2020 and its vintage pieces are collected again today.
