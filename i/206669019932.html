About this watch

An elegant Rado Florence ladies' dress watch, reference 111.3872.2 (engraved on the caseback). A deep black dial carries an applied gold RADO logo and twelve faceted gold pyramid hour markers, with slim gold baton hands, a gold seconds hand and a discreet date window near 5 o'clock. The 28mm round gold-tone case with its wide polished bezel sits on Rado's gold-tone five-row brick-link bracelet with fold-over clasp. Supplied with a Rado box.

Brand heritage

Rado is a Swiss watchmaker based in Lengnau, Switzerland, part of the Swatch Group and known for its scratch-resistant materials and clean, modern design. The Florence line is one of Rado's long-running classic dress collections.
