About this watch

A vintage Nina Ricci Paris dress watch with a striking two-tone dial: a champagne-gold centre with fine concentric guilloche, framed by a deep royal-blue chapter ring with gold Roman numerals. Slim gold dauphine hands, "NINA RICCI" and "Paris" printed on the dial. The 34mm round yellow-gold-plated case has a slim polished bezel, sharp faceted lugs and a fluted crown at 3 o'clock. Powered by a 17-jewel mechanical hand-wound movement. Supplied as a full set with the Nina Ricci box and guarantee booklets.

Brand heritage

Nina Ricci is the Paris fashion house founded in 1932 by Maria "Nina" Ricci and her son Robert Ricci. Famous for haute couture and fragrances such as L'Air du Temps, the house also offered watches and accessories under the "Montres Nina Ricci" name.
