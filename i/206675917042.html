About this watch

A bold 1970s Swiss automatic from Sandoz in the classic "TV" cushion case. The smoky brown-grey gradient dial (darker towards the edge) has twelve applied block markers inlaid with a red stripe, silver baton hands with red inlay, a red central seconds hand and a framed day-date window at 3 o'clock with the day in Arabic and the date in numerals. "SANDOZ" sits beside 9 o'clock, "AUTOMATIC 25 JEWELS" above 6 and "SWISS MADE" at the bottom edge. The polished stainless steel case measures 38 mm across and 39 mm lug to lug; the caseback is signed SANDOZ with the lion crest, "Swiss Made", "Automatic", "Incabloc", "All Stainless Steel", "Water Resistant" and reference 1839Z-84-8.

Brand heritage

Sandoz is a Swiss watch brand with roots in the Sandoz watchmaking family of the Jura; in the 1960s and 1970s it produced a wide range of mechanical and automatic watches that were especially popular in the Middle East, which is why many of its day-date models carry Arabic day wheels.
