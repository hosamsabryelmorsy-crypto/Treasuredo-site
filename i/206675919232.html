About this watch

A smart Swiss-made dress-sport watch by Grovana, reference 1519.1. The deep blue dial has a wave-guilloche centre surrounded by a sunburst outer ring, applied steel baton markers, a Roman XII at 12, a white date window at 3 and "SWISS MADE" at 6. The polished steel case has a distinctive bezel set with decorative rivets, and it sits on a matching stainless steel link bracelet with a folding clasp. The caseback is engraved "Grovana of Switzerland", "All Stainless Steel", "Water Resistant", "Swiss Made" and "1519.1".

Brand heritage

Grovana is a Swiss watch company founded in 1924 in Tenniken, in the canton of Basel-Land, Switzerland. The family-run firm has produced Swiss-made watches for almost a century and also makes the Swiss Alpine Military line.
