About this watch

A classic mid-century Swiss automatic from Alpha. The two-zone silver dial has a finely turned (concentric) centre framed by a gold dotted ring, applied gold Arabic numerals at the even hours alternating with gold dagger markers, and gold dauphine hands with a sweep seconds hand. The dial is signed with the Alpha logo, "ALPHA 25 JEWELS" and "AUTOMATIC ALPHAFLEX", with "SWISS" at the bottom. The gold plated case has a stainless steel screw-down back engraved "Incabloc Swiss", "Waterproof", "Antimagnetic", "Stainless Steel Back", "Alpha", "Automatic" and the numbers "57 93 14". Fitted on a black croc-pattern leather strap.

Brand heritage

Alpha was a Swiss watch brand of the mid-20th century, marked by its Greek letter alpha logo; its watches were made in Switzerland and sold widely in export markets.
