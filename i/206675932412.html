About this watch

A 1970s ladies' Rado Conway in the classic cushion "TV" case. The grey sunburst dial carries the red Rado anchor logo above the applied gold RADO wordmark, gold applied baton markers, gold "Conway" script above 6 and "Swiss Made" at the bottom edge. Gold-tone baton hands with white inlay, a gold central seconds hand and a gold-framed day-date window at 3 (English day). The polished stainless steel case measures 25.6 mm across and 29.7 mm lug to lug, with a gold-tone crown. It is fitted on a two-tone steel and gold-plated link bracelet with a fold-over clasp signed RADO with the anchor logo, and comes with a Rado presentation case and Rado outer box.

Brand heritage

Rado was founded in 1917 in Lengnau, Switzerland, as Schlup & Co. and took the Rado name in the 1950s. Its red anchor logo and 1960s-70s models such as the Conway, Green Horse and Golden Horse made it one of the most popular Swiss brands of the era, especially in the Middle East and Asia.
