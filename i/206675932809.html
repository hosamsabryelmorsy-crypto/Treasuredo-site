About this watch

A big early-1970s Seiko 5 automatic, reference 6119-8273, in the wide cushion-shaped stainless steel case collectors call the "jumbo". The silver sunburst dial has applied faceted baton markers, a printed minute track, the raised SEIKO 5 shield emblem, "AUTOMATIC 21 JEWELS" with the Diashock symbol and a framed day-date window with an Arabic day wheel. It runs on the in-house Seiko calibre 6119 automatic with 21 jewels and is fitted to a Seiko steel bracelet signed on the clasp.

Brand heritage

Seiko launched the Seiko 5 line in 1963 around five promises: automatic winding, day-date display, water resistance, a recessed crown and a durable case. The calibre 6119 was one of the workhorse movements of the line from the late 1960s through the 1970s.
