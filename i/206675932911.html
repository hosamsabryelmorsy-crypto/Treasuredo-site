About this watch

A vintage Orient King Diver from the AAA era, with one of the most striking dials of the line: a grey dial crossed by a light blue diagonal band that carries both the day window (at 8) and the date window (at 2). Large applied rectangular luminous markers, the crowned Orient emblem, three stars and the "King Diver 21 Jewels" signature complete the dial. A dark grey inner rotating dive bezel marked 15 / 30 / 45 is turned by the upper crown, with the winding crown below it, inside a coin-edge steel bezel ring. Fitted to a heavy steel link bracelet and supplied in an Orient box with an Orient booklet.

Brand heritage

Orient Watch Company was founded in Tokyo in 1950. Its first King Diver of 1965 introduced an internal rotating bezel operated by a second crown, and the AAA King Diver that followed became one of Orient's best-known vintage sports watches.
