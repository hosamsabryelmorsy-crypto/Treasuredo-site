Brand heritage

Cartier was founded in Paris in 1847 by Louis-François Cartier. The house launched its first eyewear collection, Vendome, in 1983 under the "Les Must de Cartier" line. The Vendome aviator was offered in two main decors: Santos, with exposed screws, and Louis Cartier, with the elegant "C" decor hinges seen here. Both became icons of 1980s and 1990s luxury eyewear and remain highly collected today.
