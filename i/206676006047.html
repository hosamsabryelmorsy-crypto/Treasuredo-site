About this item

A classic vintage Persol model 6201 in glossy black acetate, a compact, bold frame with gently upswept outer corners and a keyhole-style bridge. The front carries Persol's signature silver Supreme Arrow at each hinge, and the temples feature the patented Meflecto flexible system, recognisable by the grooved segments and silver pins near the hinges. Fitted with brown lenses and supplied with a brown leather Persol slip case stamped "Made in Italy".

Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by Giuseppe Ratti, whose name ("per il sole", for the sun) became a byword for Italian sunglasses. The brand is known for its Supreme Arrow hinge emblem and for the Meflecto system, flexible temples designed to adapt comfortably to the shape of the head. Vintage Persol frames made in Italy are sought after by collectors worldwide.
