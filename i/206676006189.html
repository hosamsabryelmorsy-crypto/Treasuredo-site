About this item

A vintage Cartier aviator from the Vendome collection in the Louis Cartier ("C decor") design: a gold-plated metal teardrop frame with a straight double top bar, burgundy (bordeaux) lacquer inlays on the lower bridge and on both hinge end-pieces, the signature open "C" shaped decor at the front of each temple, and slim gold temples finished with burgundy-brown tips carrying a small gold plaque. Clear nose pads sit in gold C-shaped pad arms. The lenses are a solid rose-brown tint.

Stamped "Cartier PARIS 59 [] 14" inside the bridge, "Cartier PARIS MADE IN FRANCE 140" on the inner temple, and serial number 815611 on the top bar. It comes as a set with the red Cartier outer box, the red "must de Cartier Paris" presentation case (underside marked "must de Cartier Paris, Marques et Modele Deposes, Registered Trademarks and Design, santos de Cartier Paris 701") and a burgundy leather Cartier pouch with gold corners.

Brand heritage

Cartier, founded in Paris in 1847, launched its first eyewear in 1983 with the Vendome collection under "Les Must de Cartier". The Vendome Louis Cartier version, with its "C" decor temples, and the Santos version, with its screws, became the best-known luxury aviators of the 1980s and 1990s and remain sought after by collectors today.
