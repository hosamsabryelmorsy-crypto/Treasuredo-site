About this item

The famous Christian Dior 2056 "butterfly", one of the defining women's sunglasses of the late 1970s and 1980s. Two large rounded-diamond lenses meet at a single crossing point in the centre, framed by thin pale-gold metal rims with an extra open wire loop sweeping around the outer side of each lens. An oval gold and black enamel CD logo medallion sits at each hinge, and the temples are dark brown Optyl fixed with two screws. Fitted with solid brown tinted lenses.

The temples are marked "optyl" with the Optyl logo and "(C) Christian Dior" on one side, and "MADE IN AUSTRIA 2056 41" on the other.

Brand heritage

Christian Dior eyewear was produced under licence from 1970 by the Austrian maker Wilhelm Anger (Optyl), whose heat-mouldable Optyl material made the light, sculpted Dior frames of the era possible. The 2056 butterfly is one of the most recognised and collected designs of that partnership.
