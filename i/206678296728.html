The fold-over clasp cover is signed with the Rado anchor logo, and the inside of the clasp is stamped "N S A", the NSA triangle logo, "SWISS MADE" and "STEEL INOX". Each end has a riveted fitting plate with a notched tab, made to fit the case directly. Bracelet only, no watch.

Brand heritage

Rado was founded in 1917 in Lengnau, Switzerland, as Schlup & Co., and took the Rado name in 1957. Its anchor logo is famous from models such as the Golden Horse and the DiaStar of 1962. NSA (Noble Swiss Alliance) made original bracelets for Rado and other Swiss brands in the 1960s and 1970s.
