Brand heritage

Zodiac was founded in 1882 by Ariste Calame in Le Locle, Switzerland, and is best known for the Sea Wolf dive watch of 1953. Original bracelets signed with the Zodiac emblem are wanted for restoring and wearing vintage Zodiac watches.
