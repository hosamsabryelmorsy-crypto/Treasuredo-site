Brand heritage

Ebel was founded in 1911 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland. Its Sport Classic, launched in 1977, is known for the integrated wave-link bracelet and the five-screw bezel. Original Ebel wave bracelets are wanted to restore or complete vintage Sport Classic watches.
