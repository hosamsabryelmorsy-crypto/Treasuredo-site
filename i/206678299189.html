About this item

An original vintage Certina bracelet in brushed stainless steel. It is built from flat, wide horizontal links joined by small screws, giving the clean, architectural look of 1970s and 1980s Certina sports and dress watches. The fold-over clasp cover is engraved with the Certina logo and the word "CERTINA", and the inside of the clasp is stamped with the number "11446". The ends are straight. Bracelet only, no watch.

Brand heritage

Certina was founded in 1888 in Grenchen, Switzerland, by the brothers Adolf and Alfred Kurth, and is known for its DS ("Double Security") concept of robust, shock-resistant watches. Original signed Certina bracelets from the vintage era are sought after for restoring and completing vintage Certina watches.
