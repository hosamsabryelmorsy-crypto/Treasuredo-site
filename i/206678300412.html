Brand heritage

Ebel was founded in 1911 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, by Eugène Blum and Alice Lévy (the name comes from "Eugène Blum Et Lévy"). The Sport Classic, launched in 1977, with its screwed bezel and integrated wave bracelet, became the brand's signature design.
