About this watch

A classic character watch from the 1970s: Mickey Mouse stands at the centre of the white Disney-signed dial, his arms acting as the hour and minute hands, with bold black Arabic numerals around the edge and a small red-tipped seconds disc. The dial is marked "17 Jewels".

The 36mm cushion-shaped case is stainless steel with broad polished flanks and a screw-down caseback. Inside is a Swiss mechanical manual-wind movement; the movement itself is marked "Twenty One 21 Jewels" (see photo), while the dial reads 17 Jewels. It comes on a black leather strap with a Jovial "Since 1929" wooden box.

Brand heritage

Jovial was founded in 1929 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, and later moved its headquarters to Biel/Bienne. From its earliest years the brand concentrated on the Middle Eastern market, where its dependable Swiss mechanical watches became widely known, a focus it has kept to this day.

Mickey Mouse first appeared on a wristwatch in 1933, when Ingersoll launched its famous Mickey watch at the Chicago World's Fair. Licensed Mickey watches by Swiss makers followed in the 1960s and 1970s and are popular with collectors of both watches and Disney memorabilia.
