A stitched strap closure and gold-tone coronet buckle give this vintage Rolex presentation box a distinctive, luggage-inspired character. Its deep burgundy-brown exterior opens to a warm wood-grain interior, with the gilt “Création Genève ROLEX” signature positioned inside the lid.


The design is consistent with Rolex’s vintage buckle-style presentation boxes of approximately the 1970s–1980s, although the exact production date and original watch pairing of this example are not documented. The shaped flap, contrasting stitching and crown-detail buckle make it an appealing collectible in its own right, beyond its original role as watch packaging.


The box is offered empty, without a fitted watch insert, cushion or watch holder. The photographs show the complete contents included in this sale; no watch or documentation accompanies it.


Condition shows substantial age-related wear. The exterior has numerous scratches, scuffs and rubbed edges, with creasing, surface wear and fraying around the strap and stitched sections. The buckle also shows surface marks. Inside, the wooden surfaces display uneven discoloration, staining and scattered dark marks, while the material along the hinge area is visibly worn. The interior Rolex signature remains clearly legible.


An interesting addition to a vintage Rolex accessories collection, particularly for someone who appreciates the combination of burgundy colour, wood-grain detailing and a distinctive coronet-buckle closure. It is offered in its photographed condition, rather than as a pristine or complete presentation set.
