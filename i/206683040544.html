Brand heritage

Fendi was founded in Rome in 1925 by Adele and Edoardo Fendi as a fur and leather workshop, and was built into an international fashion house by their five daughters. Karl Lagerfeld joined in 1965 and created the double-F "FF" logo, which became one of the most recognisable monograms in fashion. Fendi Orologi, the watch line, was launched in 1988 and is Swiss made.

Model history

The 4000 series is the best-known Fendi watch design, a square case whose bezel is formed by the interlocking FF logo. It was produced from the 1990s in gentlemen's (4000G) and ladies (4000L) sizes with Swiss quartz movements, and was offered on a wide range of coloured straps and bracelets.
