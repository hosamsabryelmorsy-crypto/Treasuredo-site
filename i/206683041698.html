About this watch

A dressier take on a sports watch: a sculpted two-tone bezel, a silver-white sunburst dial, bold applied 12 and 6 numerals, gold-tone hands and markers and a magnified date at three o'clock.

The approximately 43mm stainless-steel case with gold-plated details has a sapphire crystal and an exhibition case back showing the 26-jewel Swiss automatic movement and its striped rotor; the back is marked Swiss Made, Sapphire Crystal and 10 ATM. A matching two-tone bracelet with folding safety clasp completes the watch, which comes with its presentation box, outer packaging and red reference tag.

Brand heritage

Hanowa was founded in Switzerland in 1963 by Hans Noll, and its Swiss Military Hanowa line, launched around 1990, is produced under licence from the Swiss Confederation. The brand is known for solid Swiss-made sports watches with military-inspired styling at accessible prices.

Nautica is one of the brand's marine-themed collections; reference 05-5184.55.001 is the two-tone automatic version on bracelet, documented in the 2013 collection.
