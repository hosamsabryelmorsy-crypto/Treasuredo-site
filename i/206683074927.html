Brand heritage

Chopard was founded by Louis-Ulysse Chopard in 1860 in Sonvilier, Switzerland, and has been owned by the Scheufele family since 1963. The house is known for the Happy Diamonds, Mille Miglia and L.U.C collections. An original box completes a Chopard watch for collectors and resale.
