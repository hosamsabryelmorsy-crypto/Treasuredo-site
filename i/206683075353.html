About this item

A collectible Seiko watch presentation box made for the Barcelona 1992 Olympic Games, when Seiko was the Official Timer. The rounded grey shell has a glossy black top printed with the Barcelona '92 emblem, the Olympic rings, "OFFICIAL TIMER SEIKO" and a "SEIKO" logo on the front. The lid opens to show the same Barcelona '92 / Olympic rings / Seiko Official Timer print on its translucent panel, and the dark grey base has a slot to hold a watch and a "SEIKO" logo. The underside is moulded with the code "E-CM34H". Box only, no watch.

Brand heritage

Seiko has been an Olympic timekeeper several times since the Tokyo 1964 Games. Its Barcelona 1992 Official Timer boxes were made for the watches sold for the Games and are a nice piece of Olympic and Seiko memorabilia.
