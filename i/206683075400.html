Brand heritage

Boucheron was founded by Frédéric Boucheron in Paris in 1858 and was the first of the great jewellers to open on the Place Vendôme, in 1893. Its emerald-cut logo recalls the house's gem-cutting tradition. An original Boucheron box completes a Boucheron watch.
