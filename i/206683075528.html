About this item

An original vintage Christian Dior presentation box for a Dior lighter. The grey-taupe lid is printed in gold "Christian Dior PARIS" inside a gold border line. Inside, the lid is lined in grey suede-feel fabric stamped in gold "Christian Dior PARIS", and the base has a matching grey insert with a rectangular recess to hold the lighter. It comes with the original Christian Dior Paris "briquets - lighters" instruction booklet. Box and booklet only, no lighter.

Brand heritage

The House of Christian Dior was founded in Paris in 1946. In the 1970s and 1980s Dior lent its name to a line of Swiss-made luxury gas lighters, now sought after by collectors. An original box and booklet complete a vintage Dior lighter.
