About this item

An original Cartier Parfums presentation box made for the 50 ml (1.6 fl. oz.) Must de Cartier Eau de Toilette natural spray. The hinged lid is printed in gold with the classic Cartier garland border and reads "Eau de Toilette - must de Cartier Paris - Vaporisateur - Natural Spray". Inside, an ivory lining is stamped "Parfums Cartier Paris" in the lid, with a fitted ivory satin-style cradle for the bottle. The box is sold EMPTY: no bottle and no fragrance are included.

Brand heritage

Must de Cartier was launched in 1981 as the first women's fragrance from Cartier, part of the "Les Must de Cartier" line that brought the Paris jeweller's style to a wider range of accessories. Its bordeaux-red packaging with gold garlands mirrors the famous Cartier red jewellery and watch boxes.
