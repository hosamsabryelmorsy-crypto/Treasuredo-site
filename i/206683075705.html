About this item

An original Cartier Paris presentation box from the Panthère de Cartier line. The hinged hard box has the classic Cartier bordeaux-red grained covering with the gold garland border on the lid, gold-tooled bands around the sides and canted corners. The ivory interior is stamped "Cartier Paris" in gold in the lid, and the ivory cushioned insert has a slot to hold a bracelet watch. The base is printed "Cartier Paris - Marques et Modèle Déposés - Registered Trademarks and Design - Panthère de Cartier - 525". Sold EMPTY: no watch included.

Brand heritage

The Panthère de Cartier watch was introduced in 1983 and became one of the defining Cartier designs of the 1980s and 1990s, named after the panther emblem associated with the house since the early 20th century. The red box with gold garlands is Cartier's signature presentation packaging.
