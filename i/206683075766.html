About this item

A vintage Les Must de Cartier presentation case in the classic Cartier bordeaux-red grained covering, with the gold garland border on the lid and gold-tooled bands around the sides. It closes with two brass catches at the front and has two brass shell-shaped lid stays at the hinge. The ivory lid lining is stamped "les must de Cartier Paris" in gold and the base is signed "Cartier" in gold. The ivory insert has four rectangular recesses, the kind of format Cartier used to present Les Must accessories such as lighters (the case is sold empty).

Brand heritage

Les Must de Cartier was launched in the 1970s to bring Cartier design to a wider range of accessories: lighters, pens, leather goods and watches. Presentation and display cases like this one carry the same red-and-gold livery as the famous Cartier jewellery boxes.
