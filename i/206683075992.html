Brand heritage

Tudor was registered in 1926 and established as Montres Tudor S.A. by Hans Wilsdorf, the founder of Rolex, to offer Rolex-grade reliability at a more accessible price. The shield logo shown on this box was used by Tudor from the late 1960s onwards.
