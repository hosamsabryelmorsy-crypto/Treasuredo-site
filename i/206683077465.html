About this item

An original vintage Omega watch box in burgundy red leather. The two side doors open outward from the centre and are held closed by a stitched leather strap with a keeper embossed with the Omega symbol. Inside, a burgundy velvet-type tray holds a C-shaped watch cushion, with moiré-lined doors. The base is embossed "MADE IN SWITZERLAND". Box only, no watch.

Brand heritage

Omega was founded in 1848 by Louis Brandt in La Chaux-de-Fonds and is based in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland. Famous for the Speedmaster worn on the Moon and the Seamaster, Omega's burgundy red boxes are recognised by collectors worldwide.
