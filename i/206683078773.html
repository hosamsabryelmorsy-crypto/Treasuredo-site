About this item

An original vintage Ebel single-watch presentation box from the 1980s era. The square burgundy case carries Ebel's signature front medallion, styled like a screw-down caseback and reading "EBEL - Les Architectes du Temps - Water Resistant" in gold on black. Inside, the burgundy suede-style lining is printed with the Ebel logo and "The Architects of Time", and the base has a burgundy fitted watch holder.

Brand heritage

Ebel was founded in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, in 1911 by Eugene Blum and Alice Levy (EBEL = Eugene Blum Et Levy). Its "Architects of Time" slogan and the 1980s Sport Classic and Beluga collections made it one of the defining luxury watch brands of that decade.
