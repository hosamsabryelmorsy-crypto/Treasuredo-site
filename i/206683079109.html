Brand heritage

Alfred Dunhill founded his London house in the early 1900s, starting with motoring accessories and moving into pipes, lighters and writing instruments. Dunhill pens such as the Gemline, AD2000, Sidecar and Sentryman are prized luxury writing instruments, and an original box completes a full set.
