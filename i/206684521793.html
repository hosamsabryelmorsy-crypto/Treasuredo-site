About this watch

A jewellery-style dress watch from Orient's Le Bien line. The black dial is signed Orient and Le Bien in gold script, with gold sword hands and no markers, framed by a ring of small clear stones. Around it, the 12-sided case has a silver-tone star-cut bezel set against gold-plated stepped lugs in an Art Deco style, and the crown is set with a dark cabochon.

The 23mm gold-plated case has a snap caseback that still shows its factory protective sticker. It runs on a quartz movement and is fitted on a new black leather strap with the original Orient-signed gold-tone buckle. It comes with its original Orient box and Le Bien hang tag.

Brand heritage

Orient's roots go back to 1901, when Shogoro Yoshida opened a watch shop in Ueno, Tokyo; his business began making watch cases in 1913 and grew into Toyo Tokei Manufacturing. The Orient Watch Company itself was founded in 1950 in Hino, Tokyo, and became one of Japan's major watchmakers, known for both mechanical and fashion watches.
