Fendi High Speed Ref. 4600M – Swiss Quartz – Red Dial – Stainless Steel with FF-Logo Links – Sapphire Crystal – With Box

A Fendi High Speed, model 4600M, with a rich red dial. The dial has decorative bead-style Arabic numerals at 12, 3, 6 and 9, polished dot markers, a finely printed outer minute track and a dotted inner circle, with silver-tone hands with pale luminous inserts and a circular date aperture between 4 and 5 o'clock. The stainless steel case, approximately 35mm, combines brushed and polished surfaces with an engraved bezel, a signed crown, FENDI engraving on the case side and a decorative Fendi caseback medallion, reference 003-4600M-843. Signature FF-logo links connect the case to a burgundy leather strap with silver-tone pin buckle. It comes with the Fendi presentation case shown.

Brand heritage

Fendi was founded in Rome in 1925 by Adele and Edoardo Fendi as a fur and leather workshop, and was built into an international fashion house by their five daughters. Karl Lagerfeld joined in 1965 and created the double-F "FF" logo, which became one of the most recognisable monograms in fashion. Fendi Orologi, the watch line, was launched in 1988 and is Swiss made.

Model history

The High Speed is one of the sportier Fendi watch collections of the 2000s, combining a round steel case with an engraved bezel and the FF logo worked into the links that join the case to the strap or bracelet. It was offered with a sapphire crystal and a factory water-resistance rating of 50 metres, in a range of bold dial colours.
