About this watch

A genuine early-1950s Tissot self-winding wristwatch from the era when the company still signed its work "Chs Tissot & Fils". The stainless steel case (reference 6582-2, stamped inside the caseback) houses Tissot's in-house 17-jewel bumper automatic movement, signed "Chs Tissot & Fils - Seventeen 17 Jewels - Swiss" and numbered 2665615. The champagne dial carries the period script Tissot logo with "automatic" below, an applied gold double baton at 12, gold batons at 3, 6 and 9, faceted gold pyramid markers, a railroad minute track and gold dauphine hands with a centre seconds hand. The dial has developed an even, warm patina toward the edge, a look many collectors seek out.

Brand heritage

Tissot was founded in 1853 in Le Locle, in the Swiss Jura, by Charles-Félicien Tissot and his son Charles-Émile, which is why watches of this period are signed "Chs Tissot & Fils" (Charles Tissot & Son). In 1930 Tissot produced the first mass-produced antimagnetic wristwatch, and in the 1940s and 1950s it built its own bumper-type self-winding movements like the one in this watch, before the Seastar and Visodate lines of the later 1950s.
