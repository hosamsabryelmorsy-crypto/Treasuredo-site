About this watch

A classic 1960s Zodiac gentleman's watch. The cream dial, signed with the Zodiac emblem and "Hermetic" script, has applied faceted gold baton markers with a triangle at twelve, gold dauphine hands and a small seconds dial at six. Decades of ageing have given it an even, spotted patina.

The 36mm round gold-plated case has long curved lugs and a stainless-steel screw-down back marked "Zodiac, Swiss Made, Shock-Resistant, Antimagnetic, Water-Resistant" with the Zodiac emblem. Inside is the signed Zodiac Ltd calibre 330, a 17-jewel Swiss manual-wind movement. It is fitted with a black woven fabric strap.

Brand heritage

Zodiac was founded in 1882 by Ariste Calame in Le Locle, Switzerland, and adopted the Zodiac name in the early 20th century. The brand is best known for the Sea Wolf of 1953, one of the first purpose-built dive watches sold to the public, and in the 1950s and 1960s it produced elegant, robust dress watches with its own in-house movements, such as the calibre 330 found here.
