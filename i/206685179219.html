About this watch

A Citizen Promaster Aqualand, the brand's famous analog-digital dive computer watch, here in the classic black-dial C520 generation (the JP1010 family). The dial combines a conventional analog time display with an LCD window at 12 o'clock that shows dive information, marked "DIVE" beneath it, and is signed "Citizen Quartz", the Promaster triangle emblem and "Diver's 200M" in orange. The dial is printed "Japan C520-Q00432 KA" at 6 o'clock. The bead-blasted stainless steel case carries the Aqualand's unmistakable round depth-sensor housing at 9 o'clock, pushers at 2, 8 and 10 o'clock, a large knurled crown at 4 o'clock and a black rotating diver's bezel with a knurled edge. The screw-held caseback is signed "Citizen Promaster", "Water Resist 200M", "Stainless", "Citizen Watch Co.", case number C520-Q00371 and serial 520487.

Brand heritage

Citizen Watch Co. was founded in Tokyo in 1930 (its first pocket watch, the "Citizen", dates from 1924). Citizen launched the Aqualand in 1985 as the world's first analog diver's watch with a built-in electronic depth gauge, and it became one of the defining dive watches of the Promaster professional line. The Aqualand remains in Citizen's catalogue today and the earlier quartz generations have a strong collector following.
