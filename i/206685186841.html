About this watch

A robust Swiss dive watch from Revue Thommen's Diver Professional line: a 45mm stainless steel case water resistant to 300 metres / 1000 feet, a black dial with large applied luminous baton markers, a date window at 3 o'clock, a unidirectional 60-minute diver's bezel with a lume pip, and a big knurled screw-down crown signed with the RR emblem. It comes on the original signed Revue Thommen steel bracelet and is supplied with its original inner and outer boxes, booklets, international guarantee card and hang tag.

Brand heritage

Revue Thommen traces its roots to 1853 in Waldenburg, Switzerland ("Swiss Watches Since 1853"), and is known for its long tradition of pilot and aviation instruments as well as sports and diving watches. The brand is today part of the Swiss Grovana group, whose name appears on the guarantee card.
