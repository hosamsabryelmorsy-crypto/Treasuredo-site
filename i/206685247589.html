An elegant vintage pair of Lancel Paris sunglasses for women. Brand heritage

Lancel is a French luxury house founded in Paris in 1876 by Angèle and Alphonse Lancel, best known for its leather goods and accessories. Its eyewear carries the house's distinctive "L" emblem.
