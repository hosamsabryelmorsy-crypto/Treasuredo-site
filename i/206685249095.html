About this item

A genuine mid-century pair of French-made sunglasses with a thick, glossy black acetate front. The squared lenses sit in a frame with sharply pointed, winged outer corners and a sculpted keyhole bridge, a bold mod shape typical of 1960s Parisian eyewear. Each end-piece carries two small brass rivets, and the black acetate temples show their metal wire cores. The inside of the temple is stamped "MADE IN FRANCE".

Brand heritage

France, and especially the Jura region around Oyonnax and Morez, was the heart of European acetate eyewear making in the 1950s and 1960s. Frames from this era were cut from thick acetate sheet, riveted by hand and fitted with wire-cored temples, a construction that gives them their weight and durability.
