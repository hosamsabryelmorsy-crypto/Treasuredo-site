About this watch

A vintage Seiko 5 Automatic, 21 Jewels, in the classic cushion-shaped stainless steel case reference 7019-5080, with a golden sunburst dial carrying a colour portrait of Iraqi president Saddam Hussein in black beret and green uniform. Watches with this portrait were made for and given out in Iraq during the 1980s and are now sought after as historical collectibles. The day wheel is in Arabic (shown on Thursday, 15); the crown is recessed into the case at 3 o'clock. It comes with its red presentation box from the retailer Al-Hussaini & Co., with a SEIKO-printed lining.

Brand heritage

Seiko launched the Seiko 5 line in 1963 as an affordable, robust automatic. The "5" stands for its five key features, including automatic winding, a day-date display and water resistance. Millions were sold worldwide, including models with Arabic day wheels for Middle Eastern markets.
