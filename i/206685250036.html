About this item

A large, glamorous pair of vintage oval sunglasses in glossy amber-brown tortoiseshell acetate with solid brown lenses. The bridge is cut as two slim parallel bars with an open window between them, a detail typical of French mod eyewear of the late 1960s and early 1970s. Two small rivets decorate each end-piece and the temples are in matching tortoise acetate on riveted metal hinges. The lens still carries the original French "VERRES SURFACÉS OPTIQUE" paper sticker, showing the pair was never worn.

Brand heritage

From the 1950s to the 1970s, France (especially the Jura region around Oyonnax and Morez) was Europe's centre of acetate eyewear making. Frames were cut from thick acetate sheet and fitted with riveted hinges, and many were sold through opticians without a maker's name on the frame. "Verres surfacés optique" stickers marked lenses with an optically surfaced finish.
