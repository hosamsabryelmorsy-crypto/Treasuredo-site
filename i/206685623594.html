About this watch

A vintage Atlantic Worldmaster Original with an eye-catching dial: a magenta-red line drawing of a fully rigged sailing ship spreads across the silver sunburst dial. Gold Arabic numerals at the even hours alternate with gold baton markers, and the dial is signed with the Atlantic "a" logo, "atlantic", "Worldmaster" in script and "ORIGINAL" in red, with "T SWISS MADE T" at 6. Gold hands and a seconds hand with a red arrow tip complete the look. The round case has sharp tapered lugs, a gold-tone crown and a domed crystal, and the watch is fitted on a burgundy leather strap with a gold-tone buckle.

Brand heritage

Atlantic was founded in 1888 by Samuel Kocher in Bettlach, Switzerland. Its Worldmaster line, launched in the 1930s and sold worldwide from the 1950s to the 1970s, became one of the best-known affordable Swiss mechanical watches of its era, with the "Original" models instantly recognisable by their red script.
