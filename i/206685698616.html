About this watch

A vintage Omega Seamaster Quartz from the late 1970s, powered by Omega's in-house calibre 1342, one of the brand's first mass-produced quartz movements. The brushed champagne dial carries the Omega symbol with OMEGA QUARTZ beneath it, a cursive Seamaster signature above 6 and SWISS MADE at the bottom, with applied gold baton markers with black inlay, gold baton hands, a thin blue central seconds hand and a date window at 3. The sculpted gold plated C-shape case measures about 35.5 mm across (approx. 38 mm lug to lug). It comes on an OMEGA Swiss Made signed brown crocodile-grain leather strap with a gold-tone buckle, in a red Omega presentation box.

Brand heritage

Founded in 1848 in La Chaux-de-Fonds and based in Biel/Bienne, Omega is one of Switzerland's most celebrated watchmakers. The Seamaster line was introduced in 1948, and in the 1970s Omega brought its precision expertise to quartz, with the calibre 1342 family powering Seamaster and De Ville quartz models of the era.
