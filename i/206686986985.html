About this watch

A seldom-seen Pierre Cardin reference 57251 automatic in a stainless-steel case with matching signed bracelet, a refined silver concentric-textured dial and an unusually complete presentation with branded box and warranty booklet. The silver-dial configuration is considerably harder to find than the black-dial 57251.

The dial combines oversized luminous-style 12 and 6 numerals, elongated hour markers and matching hands, with a date window at three, and is signed pierre cardin, AUTOMATIC, 21 JEWELS. The caseback is marked pierre cardin, 57251, Water Resistant 5ATM, All Stainless Steel, and a protective sticker/film remains on it. The substantial steel bracelet has a Pierre Cardin-signed folding clasp.

Brand heritage

Pierre Cardin founded his fashion house in Paris in 1950. A pioneer of futuristic, space-age design, with collections such as the 1964 "Cosmocorps", he was also one of the first couturiers to build a global brand through licensing, putting his name on everything from ready-to-wear to watches and accessories.
