About this watch

Individually numbered 1425 of 1880 pieces, this CIMIER Manchester City QNET automatic brings Swiss mechanical watchmaking and football collecting together in a bold 49mm design. The charcoal sectored dial carries Manchester City's circular crest and QNET branding, with sky-blue accents on the openworked arrow-tipped hands and minute track, and a framed date window between 4 and 5 o'clock. A broad black bezel with white Arabic numerals gives it a strong sporting character.

The stainless-steel case has substantial angular lugs and an exhibition caseback, engraved with the limited-edition number, Manchester City FC official product markings and WR 10 ATM, revealing the signed rotor of the 25-jewel Swiss automatic movement. It is fitted on a navy crocodile-grain leather strap with a signed buckle, and comes as a complete set.

Brand heritage

CIMIER traces its origins to 1924, when watchmaker Joseph Lapanouse founded Lapanouse SA in Hölstein, Switzerland, selling watches first under the Rego name and later as Cimier. The company specialised in pin-pallet (Roskopf) watches and in the 1950s industrialised a pin-pallet chronograph that sold in the millions; by the late 1960s it employed more than 500 people in Bubendorf. Today Montres Cimier SA is an independent Swiss watchmaker based in Biel. QNET, a Manchester City partner since 2014, launched limited-edition CIMIER collections with the club, with City's crest on the dial and official-product engraving on the caseback.
