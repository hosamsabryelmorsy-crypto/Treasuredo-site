About this watch

The Gucci 6800L is a slim ladies' bangle watch with a rectangular stainless steel case suspended between two stirrup-shaped end links, a nod to the horsebit motif that runs through Gucci design. The rigid open bangle slips over the wrist and sits like a bracelet. This one has a white dial with fine horizontal stripes, a signed GUCCI logo at 12, baton markers at 12 and 6, slim hands and "Swiss Made" at the bottom. Supplied in a Gucci box.

Brand heritage

Gucci was founded in Florence in 1921 by Guccio Gucci, starting as a leather goods house. The horsebit, taken from the equestrian world, became one of its best-known design signatures and is echoed in the stirrup links of this watch. Gucci timepieces are Swiss made.
