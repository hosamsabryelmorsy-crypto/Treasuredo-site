About this watch

A collectible commissioned watch: the white dial carries the colourful emblem of the 79th Squadron with Arabic script at the centre, surrounded by black Roman numerals, a dotted minute track and a date window at 3 o'clock.

The two-tone case has a gold-tone fluted bezel and is paired with a matching two-tone jubilee-style bracelet. The caseback is signed Rotary "since 1895", reference GB0228/C, Swiss made, powered by a Swiss quartz movement. It comes with a Rotary presentation box.

Brand heritage

Rotary was founded in 1895 by Moise Dreyfuss in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland. The company grew into an international brand and became particularly well known in Britain, where it supplied watches to the armed forces during the Second World War. Rotary has also produced many commissioned emblem watches for organisations and military units around the world, and these are collected today as pieces of history.
