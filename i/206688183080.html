About this watch

A charming early-1990s ladies Seiko from the "Age of Discovery" family of globe and map dials. The dial pairs a gold-tone centre printed with a globe meridian grid with a crisp white chapter ring of black Roman numerals, a date window at 6, dark blue hands (the hour hand set with a round gold disc) and a slim gold seconds hand with a crescent tail. The gold-plated case has a wide polished bezel with raised bar markers and dots and elegant curved lugs. It comes on a black crocodile-grain leather strap and in its red Seiko Quartz presentation box.

Brand heritage

Seiko, founded in Tokyo in 1881, introduced the world's first quartz wristwatch, the Astron, in 1969 and has been one of the leading names in quartz watchmaking ever since. In the early 1990s Seiko produced a series of "Age of Discovery" watches with globe, compass and map motifs, today a sought-after theme among collectors of vintage Seiko.
