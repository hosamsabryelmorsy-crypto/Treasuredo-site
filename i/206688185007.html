About this watch

A Swiss made Kolber Geneve wristwatch, reference K8282, with a special dial carrying the red Khanjar national emblem of the Sultanate of Oman (curved dagger over crossed swords). The white dial has applied silver Roman numerals with a plain baton at 1, a thin inner ring, the black script Kolber GENEVE signature below 12, a date window at 6 and SWISS MADE at the bottom; a small grey label reading GENEVE sits below the centre. Slim silver baton hands and a silver seconds hand. The stainless steel case measures 33.7 mm across by caliper (excluding the crown) and 37.6 mm lug to lug, with a polished bezel with raised block segments, angular crown shoulders and a faceted crown at 3. The screw-down steel caseback is engraved Kolber GENEVE, SWISS MADE, K8282, WATER RESISTANT 3ATM, STAINLESS STEEL, SAPPHIRE CRYSTAL GLASS and www.kolber.ch. It comes on a stainless steel bracelet with polished pillow-shaped centre links and a fold-over clasp stamped STAINLESS STEEL, and is shown with a red Kolber Geneve box.

Brand heritage

Kolber Geneve is a Swiss watch brand known for elegant quartz dress watches with sapphire crystals, very popular across the Gulf, where it made many special dials for institutions and commemorations. Watches with the red Khanjar emblem were made for the Sultanate of Oman and are sought after by collectors of Gulf and Omani dial watches.
