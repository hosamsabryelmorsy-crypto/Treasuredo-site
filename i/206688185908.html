About this item

A striking pair of 1960s mod sunglasses in thick acetate. The front is a laminated patchwork mosaic of small rectangular tiles in black, dark olive and smoky brown, almost like a checkerboard, while the inside of the front and both temples are plain glossy black. The wide soft-oval lenses have a sculpted top edge where the frame dips down toward the short, narrow bridge, giving the pair its sleepy-eye mod look. Solid lemon-yellow lenses, two small silver rivets on each end piece and riveted metal hinges with five-barrel joints.
