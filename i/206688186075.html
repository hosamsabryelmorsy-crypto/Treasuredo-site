About this item

An ornate pair of 1990s Italian designer sunglasses from Chagall, the eyewear line made by Visibilia. The slim gold metal oval front has a double-bar bridge, amber nose pads and solid dark grey lenses, the left lens carrying a small gold script Chagall signature. Chunky ribbed gold end pieces lead into the signature temples: a sculpted matte gold openwork section with a round ring medallion holding a silver bar with the Chagall script logo, followed by black acetate temple arms. Inside, one temple reads "Chagall Made in Italy by VISIBILIA CE" and the other "LL 2566F 057" (model and colour code).

Brand heritage

Chagall was an Italian eyewear label of the 1980s and 1990s, produced first for Tura and later by Visibilia in Italy. Its frames are known for bold, decorative metalwork in the baroque spirit of 1990s Italian fashion eyewear.
