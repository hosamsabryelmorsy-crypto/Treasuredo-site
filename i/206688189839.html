A clean, sporty vintage aviator from the Paris house of Courrèges. The large teardrop lenses sit in a matte dark brown frame with a double bridge: a slim top bar with an open keyhole cut-out above the nose. Each temple carries an inset gold plaque printed "Courrèges", and the inside of the temples reads "Courrèges PARIS" and "FRANCE 8117 AC 13". Solid brown lenses complete a classic 1980s look.

Brand heritage

André Courrèges (1923-2016) trained under Cristóbal Balenciaga and founded his own Paris house in 1961. He became one of the defining designers of the 1960s "space age" era, famous for the mini skirt, go-go boots and his iconic slit "Eskimo" sunglasses. Courrèges eyewear made in France remains highly collectable.
