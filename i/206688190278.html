About this item

A vintage pair of France Look ladies' sunglasses in a soft, rounded-square shape. The front is cut from a mottled acetate in blue, green, amber and brown, fitted with solid dark grey lenses. Gold-tone ribbed metal end pieces sweep over the outer corner of each lens in a crossover design and lead into chevron-ribbed hinge plates engraved with the FL monogram. Brand heritage

France Look is a French eyewear house whose hand-made acetate and metal frames were especially popular in the 1980s and 1990s, known for decorative metal details and rich acetate colours.
