Brand heritage

West End Watch Co. was founded in 1886 by Swiss watchmakers who began selling their watches in Bombay and Calcutta, and the brand became a household name across India and the Middle East. Its rugged "Sowar" (Hindi for "horseman") and "Sowar Prima" watches were issued to and worn by soldiers, and the brand is today owned by the Swiss Aubry family. Vintage Sowar Prima automatics are popular with collectors for their Swiss build and military heritage.
