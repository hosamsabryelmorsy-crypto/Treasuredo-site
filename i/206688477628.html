Brand heritage

Rado grew out of the Schlup & Co. movement workshop founded in Lengnau, Switzerland, in 1917, and began selling watches under the Rado name in the 1950s. The brand became known as the "Master of Materials" after launching the scratch-resistant DiaStar in 1962, and it has long been one of the most popular Swiss brands in the Middle East.

Model history

Rado supplied many presentation watches to institutions in the Gulf during the 1970s and 1980s, with crests and dedications printed on the dial. King Faisal Air Academy, founded in 1968, is the officer academy and flight school of the Royal Saudi Air Force in Riyadh. This watch uses the Rado calibre 636, a 25-jewel automatic with day and date based on the ETA 2836. No documentation of its presentation history is included.
