About this watch

An original vintage automatic wristwatch, made in Iraq during Saddam Hussein's presidency, with a colour portrait of Saddam Hussein, President of Iraq, printed on the dial. He is shown smiling, wearing a red-and-white keffiyeh, with his signature in Arabic script ("Saddam Hussein") printed in gold to the right of the centre. The brown-gold sunburst dial has applied gold baton markers, gold baton hands, a date window at 3 o'clock and a white minute-track ring. The dial is marked "Automatic Incabloc" and "Swiss Made" (partly covered by the portrait). It sits in a large 1970s-style gold plated cushion (TV) case with a stainless steel back, worn on a brown leather strap with a gold-tone buckle.

History

This is an original period Saddam Hussein watch, made in Iraq. During the years of the embargo on Iraq, Swiss and other foreign makers would not produce watches for Saddam, so these portrait watches were made in Iraq itself. The dial and the markings on this watch are original to that period, not a later reproduction. Portrait watches of Saddam Hussein, President of Iraq (1979-2003), were given as presentation pieces and are now sought after by collectors of Middle Eastern history and political memorabilia.
