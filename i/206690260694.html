About this item

A genuine mid-century pair of ladies' cat eye sunglasses with sharply upswept, winged outer corners. The front is cut from translucent amber / honey acetate that deepens to a darker brown along the brow line and at the wing tips, and each outer corner is decorated with two small silver-tone star-shaped rivets set one above the other. The slim temples are in matching translucent amber acetate with the metal wire core visible inside, fixed with five-barrel hinges and two pin rivets on the outer side. The frame holds solid pale olive-green tinted lenses.

About the era

The cat eye frame was the defining eyewear shape of the 1950s, when opticians and fashion houses alike decorated acetate fronts with metal rivets, stars and rhinestones. Frames with star rivets and wire-core temples like this pair are typical of that period and are now collected for their authentic vintage look, which modern reproductions rarely match.
