Brand heritage

Alfred Dunhill was founded in London in the late 19th century and became famous for motoring accessories, pipes, lighters and fine men's goods. In the 1970s and 1980s Dunhill eyewear was produced in Austria in Optyl, a lightweight, heat-mouldable material known for comfort and durability, and these frames are now sought-after vintage pieces.
