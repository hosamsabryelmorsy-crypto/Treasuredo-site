About this item

A pair of 1990s Versus by Gianni Versace oval sunglasses, Mod. R48 Col. 029. Squared silver end pieces lead into black temples, each set near the hinge with a silver-framed black panel bearing raised "VERSUS" lettering. The inside of one temple is printed "VERSUS Gianni Versace MADE IN ITALY CE" and the other "MOD. R48 COL. 029".

Brand heritage

Versus was launched in 1989 by Gianni Versace as the younger, more experimental line of the Versace house, later led by Donatella Versace. Its 1990s eyewear was made in Italy and carried the full "Versus Gianni Versace" signature. Today it is collected as part of the 90s and Y2K designer look.
