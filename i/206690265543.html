About this watch

A classic mid-century Swiss dress watch signed LIGA ANTIMAGNETIC with "SWISS MADE" at the bottom of the dial. The warm salmon / gold-tone dial carries full black Arabic numerals 1 to 12, an outer railroad minute track numbered every five minutes (5 to 60), silver leaf-style hour and minute hands and a slim centre seconds hand. The polished round stainless steel case measures 35 mm across (42 mm lug to lug) and is closed by a stainless steel caseback stamped "ACIER INOXYDABLE" with the case number 68333. It is fitted with a brushed steel three-row link bracelet with fold-over clasp.

Brand heritage

Liga was a Swiss watch brand of the mid 20th century, typical of the many independent Swiss makers that cased reliable Swiss movements in simple, well-proportioned steel cases. "Antimagnetic" on the dial was a popular feature of the 1940s and 1950s, when protection from magnetism was a selling point for everyday watches.
