About this watch

A classic Rado Florence reference 129.3645.2 from the Florence line of the late 1980s and 1990s. The brushed gold dial is signed "RADO" and "FLORENCE", with twelve raised gold dot markers, slim gold hour and minute hands, a framed date window above 6 and "Swiss 026" at the base. The round gold-plated case flows into curved integrated lugs and a gold-plated bracelet with sculpted wave links and a fold-over clasp signed RADO. Sapphire crystal and a "Watersealed" Rado caseback. Supplied with a Rado presentation box.

Brand heritage

Rado grew out of the Schlup & Co. watch factory founded in Lengnau, Switzerland, in 1917, and launched its own brand in 1957. Rado became famous for scratch-resistant materials, starting with the hardmetal DiaStar of 1962, and later for high-tech ceramics. The Florence collection was one of Rado's most popular dress lines, made with a sapphire crystal and a fine Swiss quartz movement.
