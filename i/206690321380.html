Brand heritage

Omega launched the Constellation in 1952, and the observatory dome under eight stars on its caseback has marked the line ever since. In 1982 Omega introduced the Constellation Manhattan with its distinctive "claws" on the bezel; the 1431 quartz calibre was used in Constellation models of that period.
