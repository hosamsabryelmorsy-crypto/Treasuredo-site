About this item

An original Omega cushion-shaped case back (case bottom) from a vintage Omega De Ville men's manual-wind watch, Omega case reference 111.0151 / 311.0173. The outer side is flat, brushed stainless steel; the inside is stamped with the Omega Watch Co triangle and the full case markings.

Brand heritage

Omega introduced the De Ville line in the 1960s as its dressier collection, and in the 1970s the De Ville range was known for slim cushion and tonneau cases. Omega references starting 111. belong to this family of 1970s manual-wind De Ville dress watches.
