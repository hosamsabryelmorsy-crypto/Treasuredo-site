About this item

An original, genuine Omega snap-on caseback for the vintage Omega De Ville Quartz, case reference 191.0130 (calibre 1365 era, early 1980s), the slim 32mm round gold-plated dress model. Ideal as a replacement part for a 191.0130 De Ville with a damaged or missing back, or for a watchmaker's parts stock.

Brand heritage

Omega was founded in 1848 in La Chaux-de-Fonds by Louis Brandt and is based in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland. The De Ville line, introduced in 1967, was Omega's slim dress-watch family, and during the late 1970s and 1980s it was one of the main homes of Omega's own Swiss quartz calibres.
