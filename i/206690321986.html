Brand heritage

Omega was founded in 1848 in La Chaux-de-Fonds by Louis Brandt and is based in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland. The Seamaster line was introduced in 1948, and its seahorse caseback emblem has marked Seamaster watches for decades. In the late 1970s and 1980s Omega fitted many Seamaster models with its own Swiss quartz calibres such as the 1430.
