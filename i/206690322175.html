About this item

An original Omega stainless steel back for the round De Ville quartz watch of the late 1970s, Omega case reference 192.0057, made for the Omega calibre 1332 quartz movement. The inside is signed with the Omega Watch Co triangle and stamped with the case reference; the outside is brushed steel with the calibre number 1332 engraved near the edge.

Brand heritage

Omega introduced the De Ville name in the 1960s for its slim dress watches, and in the 1970s it became the home of many of Omega's early quartz models. The calibre 1332 quartz movement powered a family of 192-series De Ville references of that period.
