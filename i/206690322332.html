About this item

An original, genuine Omega round snap-on caseback in stainless steel, with a smoothly domed outer face typical of Omega wristwatches of the 1940s and 1950s. The inside is signed with the Omega triangle hallmark and stamped "FOND ACIER INOXYDABLE" and "FAB SUISSE / SWISS MADE", together with an 8-digit case serial number. A useful part for restoring a vintage Omega round steel case with a missing or damaged back, or for a watchmaker's parts stock.

Brand heritage

Omega was founded in 1848 in La Chaux-de-Fonds by Louis Brandt and is based in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland. In the 1940s and 1950s Omega cases carried their own serial numbers and the Omega triangle hallmark stamped inside the caseback, as on this part.
