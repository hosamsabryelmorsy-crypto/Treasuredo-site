About this item

An original, genuine Omega snap-on caseback for the vintage Omega Seamaster Quartz, case reference 196.0267 (case number 396.0970, calibre 1430 era, 1980s), the slim rounded-rectangle TV-shaped gold-plated Seamaster dress model. The outer face carries the Seamaster hippocampus (seahorse) medallion with the Omega symbol. Ideal as a replacement for a 196.0267 with a damaged or missing back, or for a watchmaker's parts stock.

Brand heritage

Omega was founded in 1848 in La Chaux-de-Fonds by Louis Brandt and is based in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland. The Seamaster, introduced in 1948, is Omega's longest-running line, and the hippocampus emblem has marked Seamaster casebacks since 1958.
