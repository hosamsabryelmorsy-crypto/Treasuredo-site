Brand heritage

Longines was founded in Saint-Imier, Switzerland, in 1832. Its winged hourglass emblem, registered in 1889, is one of the oldest trademarks still in use, and the company traded for many years as Edmond Francillon & Co (E.F.Co), the name stamped inside this back.
