About this watch

A bold, oversized Swiss Made chronograph signed DomosKenos. The cushion-shaped black dial carries the "dk DomosKenos" logo, three sub-dials (including a 1-31 pointer scale at 6 o'clock) and a framed date window at 4 o'clock. The polished steel top case is framed by a double row of set stones and sits on a glossy black outer case with a crown and two chronograph pushers on the right. Supplied with its original DomosKenos wooden presentation box.

Brand heritage

DomosKenos is a scarce Swiss Made watch label, recognisable by its "dk" monogram. Its pieces are rarely seen on the open market, which makes this complete boxed example an interesting find for collectors of unusual designer chronographs.
