About this watch

A vintage Gucci dress watch with an eight-sided (octagonal) silver-tone case and a wide burgundy enamel bezel ring framed by thin silver-tone borders. The plain burgundy dial is signed GUCCI below 12, with the interlocking GG logo and QUARTZ above 6, and has slim gold-tone leaf hands and no markers for a clean, jewellery-like look. The caseback is signed GUCCI, it is fitted on a burgundy lizard-grain leather strap stamped GUCCI on the inside, and it comes in a Gucci presentation box.

Brand heritage

Gucci was founded in Florence in 1921 by Guccio Gucci. In the 1970s the house entered watchmaking, and its quartz watches of the 1970s and 1980s, with bold geometric cases, coloured enamel and the GG monogram, are now popular vintage fashion pieces.
