About this watch

A Pierre Lannier rectangular tank-style men's watch, reference 034E1, with a special-issue dial carrying a gold Kuwait crest: a laurel wreath topped by a crown, with a small shield in the colours of the Kuwait flag. The white dial has a central guilloche (hobnail-pattern) panel, applied polished Arabic numerals at 12, 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10, faceted baton markers, a date window at 3 and luminous sword hands. The polished steel case has stepped curved sides, and the caseback is engraved REGISTERED MODEL, Pierre Lannier and 034E1. Watches with official crests on the dial were usually made as presentation or gift pieces and are hard to find.

Brand heritage

Pierre Lannier is a French watch brand founded in 1977. Its watches are assembled in its own workshops in Ernolsheim-lès-Saverne in Alsace, and the brand is known for elegant, affordable dress watches.
