About this watch

A Christian Balmain dress watch, reference CB5010MB, with a sculpted round silver-tone case whose stepped, three-line ribbed lug arms frame the dial at the four corners, with flat bar lugs at 12 and 6. The black dial has a fine geometric engine-turned pattern, the square "CB" monogram and "Christian Balmain" signature below 12, "SAPPHIRE" above 6 and "SWISS" at the lower edge, with two slim silver hands and a thin gold-tone inner ring. Crown at 3 o'clock. Fitted with a black leather strap and pin buckle.
