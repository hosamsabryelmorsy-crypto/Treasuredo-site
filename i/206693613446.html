About this watch

A vintage Tudor Prince Oysterdate automatic from the rose-logo era. The matte black dial carries the Tudor rose emblem above the TUDOR signature and PRINCE OYSTERDATE, with ROTOR / SELF WINDING above 6 and T SWISS T at the bottom edge, applied baton hour markers, baton hands with luminous inserts, a thin central seconds hand and a date window at 3 under the crystal's magnifier. The screw-down caseback is engraved ORIGINAL OYSTER CASE BY ROLEX GENEVA with the Rolex crown. Inside, the automatic movement carries the gold-lettered rotor TUDOR AUTO-PRINCE, 25 RUBIES SWISS, and the inside of the caseback is stamped MONTRES TUDOR S.A. GENEVA SWITZERLAND PATENTED STAINLESS STEEL C.R.S. It comes on a black crocodile-grain leather strap with a steel crown-logo pin buckle, with a red Tudor presentation box.

Brand heritage

Tudor was registered in 1926 and taken over by Hans Wilsdorf, the founder of Rolex, who created it to offer Rolex reliability at a more accessible price. For decades Tudor watches were delivered in genuine Rolex Oyster cases, signed on the caseback "Original Oyster Case by Rolex Geneva", with Rolex-signed crowns. The rose emblem was used on Tudor dials until the late 1960s, when it was gradually replaced by the shield logo.
