About this watch

A vintage Technos square dress watch from around the 1970s. The silver dial with a soft blue sheen and fine vertical linen texture carries the Technos T logo and TECHNOS signature under 12, 17 JEWELS above 6 and SWISS at the bottom, with applied gold baton markers with black inlay, gold baton hands, a thin gold seconds hand and a framed date window at 4:30. The square gold plated case with a stainless steel back measures about 28.8 mm wide (approx. 37.6 mm lug to lug) and has a knurled gold-tone crown at 3. It comes on a burgundy grained leather strap with cream stitching and a gold-tone buckle, in a Technos Swiss presentation box.

Brand heritage

Technos is a Swiss watch brand that built its reputation in the mid-20th century with robust, well-made watches, from elegant dress models to divers such as The King and Sky Diver. Technos watches were especially popular in Asia and South America.
