About this watch

A classic mid-century Movado gentleman's dress watch: a slim round gold-tone case with a stepped, multi-ring bezel and straight lugs, a cream dial signed MOVADO with applied gold baton markers, gold leaf hands and a small seconds subdial at 6 o'clock. Inside is a manual-wind Movado Factories 17-jewel Swiss movement. The dial has developed a striking, one-of-a-kind patina over the decades (see condition), which makes this piece truly unique; the case, movement and signature are original Movado.

Brand heritage

Movado was founded in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, in 1881 by Achille Ditesheim; the name means "always in motion" in Esperanto. Through the 20th century Movado built its own in-house calibres and became known for elegant, understated dress watches.
