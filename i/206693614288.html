About this watch

A vintage Pierre Cardin dress watch with a caseback signed "pierre cardin CHROMACHRON QUARTZ SWISS 1002". The deep blue dial has a textured mosaic (pixel) pattern, a gold "P" logo, a gold "Pierre Cardin" script signature, small gold dot hour markers and a single gold hand. The rounded octagonal cushion case is gold plated with a steel caseback and a gold-tone crown. It comes on a brown braided leather strap signed Hirsch "Oxford" with a gold-tone buckle, and in a Pierre Cardin presentation box.

Brand heritage

Pierre Cardin (1922-2020) was the Paris couturier behind the space-age designs of the 1960s and one of the first designers to license his name widely. His 1970s and 1980s watches, many with Swiss movements, are collected today for their unusual case shapes and dials.
