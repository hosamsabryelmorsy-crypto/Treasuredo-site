About this watch

A Rado Florence reference 152.3730.4 in stainless steel. The deep blue sunray dial has the RADO logo, FLORENCE signature, fine applied dot markers, dark baton hands and a date window at 6, all under a sapphire crystal. The rounded case flows into an integrated Rado link bracelet with a signed butterfly-style deployant clasp. A clean, understated Swiss dress watch that wears well on men's and women's wrists.

Brand heritage

Rado traces its roots to the Schlup & Co. watch factory founded in 1917 in Lengnau, Switzerland, and has used the Rado name since 1957. The brand is known for scratch-resistant materials such as sapphire crystal and hard metal, and the Florence has been one of its long-running dress collections since the 1980s.
