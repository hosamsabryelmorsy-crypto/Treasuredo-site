About this watch

A slim vintage Rado Elegance dress watch, reference 396.3023.2. The square gold-plated case has flat polished top and bottom bars and a gold mirror-finish square frame around a large round glossy black dial. The dial is pure and minimalist: no markers or numerals, just the gold "RADO" signature below 12, a gold script "Elegance" above 6 and "SWISS MADE" at the lower edge, with two slim gold-tone baton hands. Small knurled crown at 3 o'clock. The steel caseback is secured by four screws and stamped with the Rado seahorse/anchor emblem, "RADO Water-Sealed" and the reference 396.3023.2. Worn on a dark brown croco-embossed leather strap with a pin buckle.

Brand heritage

Rado was founded in 1917 in Lengnau, Switzerland, by the Schlup brothers, and later became famous for scratch-resistant materials such as hardmetal and high-tech ceramic. The Elegance line was Rado's slim dress-watch range of the 1970s and 1980s.
