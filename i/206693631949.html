About this watch

A vintage Rado Golden Gazelle from the 1970s, reference 625.7994.2. The brushed gold dial carries the red rotating Rado anchor emblem above the "RADO" wordmark, "Golden Gazelle" in script and the leaping gazelle logo, with applied gold batons, a black minute track and a day-date window at 4:30 (the day disc is in Spanish, e.g. "JUE"). The gold-plated cushion-shaped case has a fluted coin-edge bezel and a stainless steel caseback with the famous embossed Rado seahorse "Water-Sealed" medallion. Worn on a signed two-tone Rado link bracelet and supplied in a vintage Rado box.

Brand heritage

Rado was founded in 1917 in Lengnau, Switzerland, by the Schlup brothers. In the 1950s-1970s the brand built its name with automatic "Horse" and "Gazelle" lines such as the Green Horse, Purple Horse and Golden Gazelle, recognised by the red rotating anchor on the dial and the seahorse caseback, before becoming known for scratch-resistant hardmetal and ceramic.
