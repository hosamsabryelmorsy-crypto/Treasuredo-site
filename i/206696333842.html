A vintage 1970s Sheaffer Imperial 797 pen set made in the USA: a fountain pen and a matching ballpoint pen, both with gold electroplated caps and barrels decorated with Sheaffer's fluted line pattern, presented in the original Sheaffer case. Both caps still carry the small black Sheaffer factory stickers ("SHEAFFER 797 FINE" on the fountain pen and "SHEAFFER 797 MEDIUM" on the ballpoint).

The fountain pen has Sheaffer's signature inlaid gold nib, stamped "SHEAFFER 585 14K USA", set into a black section. The cap band is stamped "SHEAFFER U.S.A." and "ELECTROPLATED". The ballpoint is fitted with a Sheaffer blue stainless steel tip refill.

Brand heritage

The W.A. Sheaffer Pen Company was founded by Walter A. Sheaffer in Fort Madison, Iowa, in 1913, after he patented a lever-filling fountain pen. Sheaffer became one of the great American pen makers, known for the White Dot of its Lifetime pens and for the inlaid nib, which became the hallmark of the Imperial line from the 1960s onward.
