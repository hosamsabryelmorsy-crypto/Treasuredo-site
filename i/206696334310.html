Brand heritage

Founded by Walter A. Sheaffer in Fort Madison, Iowa, in 1913, Sheaffer became one of America's great fountain pen makers. The White Dot, introduced in the 1920s as a mark of the Lifetime guarantee, remains the brand's signature. The Lady Sheaffer line was made in the USA in the 1960s and 1970s as a slim, decorative pen, and the Tulle pattern is one of its best-known finishes.
