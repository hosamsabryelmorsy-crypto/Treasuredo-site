About Stelux (STL)

Stelux was founded in Hong Kong in 1963 as a maker of stainless steel watch components, exporting to Japan and Europe. Its STL bracelets were fitted to Seiko watches of the 1960s and 1970s, and as Kreisler Stelux it supplied bracelets for Swiss brands such as Bulova, Zodiac and Tissot. Today genuine STL Stelux bracelets are sought after by Seiko collectors restoring vintage pieces, and an unused example still sealed in its original package is very hard to find.
