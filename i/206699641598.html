About this watch

A true piece of Space Age design: the Pierre Cardin "Espace" oval bubble watch, made by Jaeger in France around 1971. The asymmetric egg-shaped brushed steel case carries a wide polished oval bezel and a tall domed crystal that rises over the dial like a helmet visor. The design matches the model collectors list as PC107.

Brand heritage: Pierre Cardin, Jaeger and the Espace collection

Pierre Cardin (1922-2020) was the couturier most closely tied to the Space Age look; in the 1960s his "Cosmocorps" fashion turned the race to the Moon into style.

Around 1971 Cardin launched the Espace watch collection, sold through his Espace boutiques in the early 1970s. These are serious mechanical watches: cases signed "Jaeger France" and hand-wound Jaeger movements. Jaeger, the Paris house of Edmond Jaeger, is the partner behind the Jaeger-LeCoultre name, which is why collectors often call them "Pierre Cardin by Jaeger-LeCoultre", although the partnership was never credited on the watches.

About 26 designs were made, from lucite cubes to sculpted steel forms inspired by space capsules and 2001: A Space Odyssey. Production was short, and they are now sought by both watch and design collectors.
