About this watch

A vintage Zodiac Astrographic SST from the 1970s in a gold-plated rounded-square cushion case. The mystery display uses transparent rotating discs to carry floating hour and minute indicators and an orbiting red seconds dot around a stationary central Zodiac emblem.

This example has a warm silver-toned dial with a contrasting black minute track and a date display at 12 o'clock. The case measures approximately 36mm wide with a screw-down back, and is fitted with a matching gold-tone bracelet with a Zodiac-signed folding clasp.

Brand heritage

Zodiac was founded in Le Locle, Switzerland, in 1882 by Ariste Calame. The firm became known for robust sports watches, launching the Sea Wolf in 1953 as one of the first purpose-built dive watches, and later for its SST models. The Astrographic was introduced in 1969, the year of the Moon landing, and captured the space-age mood of the period. Instead of conventional hands it uses clear discs that make the indicators appear to float above the dial, and it remains one of the most recognisable Zodiac designs; the brand has since reissued it.
