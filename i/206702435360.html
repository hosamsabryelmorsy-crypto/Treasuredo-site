About this watch

A genuine Space Age piece from the 1970s: a Technos direct-read "jump hour" automatic. There are no hands. The hour jumps in a small square window, the minutes turn on a shield-shaped rotating disc beside it, and the date sits in its own window at the bottom of the D-shaped dial opening. The deep red dial fades to black at the edges, and the wide polished gold-plated cushion case gives it that unmistakable 1970s TV-screen look. Inside is a Swiss 21-jewel automatic jump-hour movement with a rotor signed TD (Tenor-Dorly) "Twenty One 21 Jewels Unadjusted Swiss".

Brand heritage

Technos is a Swiss watch brand founded in 1910 by the Gunzinger family in Welschenrohr, in the Swiss Jura. In the 1960s and 1970s Technos was best known for bold sports and design watches sold around the world, and the name is still one of the best-known watch brands in Latin America and Asia. Jump-hour watches like this one were Switzerland's mechanical answer to the new digital age.
