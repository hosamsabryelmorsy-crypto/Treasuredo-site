About this watch

A collectible Iraqi portrait-dial watch from the late 1980s, made by Titan of India. The champagne-gold dial has a cream centre printed in colour with the well-known portrait of Iraqi president Saddam Hussein in a red-and-white keffiyeh and green military uniform. Watches like this were made in many brands for Iraq in the 1980s and are now sought-after historical collectibles. Gold Roman numerals at 12, 9 and 6, slim gold batons, a date window at 3 and a signed Titan dial, on the original Titan gold-tone bracelet with signed clasp, supplied with a Titan box.

Brand heritage

Titan was founded in 1984 as a joint venture between the Tata Group and the Tamil Nadu Industrial Development Corporation, and grew to become India's largest watch maker. In its early years Titan exported watches to the Middle East, which is how a Titan dial carrying this portrait came to be made.
