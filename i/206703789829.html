About this watch

A bold 1970s Candino automatic in a sculpted cushion-shaped "TV" case of brushed stainless steel. The deep navy blue dial carries the Candino emblem and "CANDINO" signature under 12 and "AUTOMATIC" above an unusual vertical day-date window at 6 o'clock, with the day in red over the date in black. Ribbed applied baton markers with cream lume plots, silver baton hands with lume inlay, a fine radial minute track and "T SWISS MADE T" at the base complete the period look. The domed crystal sits inside a wide brushed surround with faceted corners, and the fluted crown is at 3. The screw-down caseback is engraved "Stainless Steel - Antimagnetic - Swiss Made - Waterresistant - Shockresistant" with the case numbers "5ATU" and "10549". Supplied on a black crocodile-grain leather strap with a Candino presentation box.

Brand heritage

Candino is a Swiss watch brand founded in 1953 in Lengnau, near Biel/Bienne, in the heart of the Swiss watchmaking region. In the 1960s and 1970s it became known for dependable Swiss automatic and day-date watches in adventurous case designs, sold around the world.
