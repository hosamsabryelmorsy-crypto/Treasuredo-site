About this watch

A striking 1970s Titoni from the brand's Swiss heyday. The square blue linen-textured dial carries the Titoni plum-blossom logo and "TITONI" below 12, "21 JEWELS" below the centre and "SWISS MADE" at 6, with applied faceted silver baton markers, slim silver baton hands and a date window with red numerals near 4:30. The wide brushed stainless steel case has bold pyramid-bevelled flanks on all four sides, a true 70s design, and flows into an integrated brushed steel brick-pattern bracelet with a fold-over clasp signed with the Titoni plum-blossom emblem. Inside is a Titoni-signed Swiss 21 jewel hand-wound movement ("TITONI" on the bridge, "SWISS 21 JEWELS"). The steel caseback still wears its original green Titoni Swiss foil sticker, which is rarely found today. Supplied in a blue presentation box.

Brand heritage

Titoni was founded in 1919 by Fritz Schluep in Grenchen, Switzerland, as Felca, and adopted the Titoni name in the 1950s. Still independent and family-owned, the company is best known for its Airmaster, Cosmo King and Space Star lines and for the plum-blossom emblem that has marked its watches for decades.
