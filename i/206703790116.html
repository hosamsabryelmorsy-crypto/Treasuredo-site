About this watch

A true piece of 1970s Space Age design: a Swiss Made Sindaco "digital" jump hour that tells the time with rotating discs instead of hands. The blue dial is laid out in three horizontal bands, dark navy, bright blue and dark navy. On the left, a black aperture shows a large white jumping hour numeral, the minutes disc (45 / 50) and a small running seconds disc, read against a thin red index line that runs across the bright blue centre band. "SINDACO" is printed in white on the upper band, "SWISS MADE" on the lower band, and the brand's circular-arrows emblem sits in the middle of the blue band. The brushed TV-screen (tonneau) case has polished bevelled sides and a knurled crown at 3. The stainless steel caseback is marked "STAINLESS STEELBACK - SWISS MADE - BASE METAL BEZEL" and numbered 8222.

Brand heritage

Sindaco was a Swiss watch brand active from the mid 20th century, best known for affordable Swiss Made mechanical watches, divers and, in the 1970s, a range of mechanical jump hour "digital" watches made in the Space Age style that was fashionable before quartz and LED displays took over.
