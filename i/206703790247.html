About this watch

A mid-size Seiko 5 automatic, case reference 4227-00E0, on its integrated Seiko stainless steel bracelet. The silver dial has a fine textured, checkered centre framed by a smooth silver outer ring, applied faceted baton markers with applied Arabic 12 and 6, the SEIKO wordmark with the Seiko 5 shield, and "AUTOMATIC 21 JEWELS" above the 6. A framed day-date window at 3 o'clock shows the day in Arabic (red) and English. The round polished case has broad integrated lug shoulders and a recessed crown at 4 o'clock. The caseback is signed SEIKO, Water Resistant, Made in Japan, St. Steel, 4227-00E0, and the bracelet closes with a signed SEIKO JAPAN fold-over clasp. Supplied in a Seiko box.

Brand heritage

Seiko traces its origins to 1881, when Kintaro Hattori opened a watch and clock shop in Tokyo. The Seiko 5 line was introduced in 1963 as an affordable, robust automatic watch; the "5" stands for its five key attributes: automatic winding, day-date display, water resistance, a recessed crown at 4 o'clock and a durable case and bracelet. This watch shows all of them.
