About this watch

A vintage Seiko 5 Automatic reference 7009-3141, the model collectors nickname the Seiko 5 "Datejust" for its fluted bezel, silver dial and jubilee-style bracelet, powered by Seiko's automatic calibre 7009 with 17 jewels and day-date. The silver sunburst dial carries the applied SEIKO logo with the Seiko 5 shield badge, black-inlaid polished baton markers with lume dots and a double marker at 12, a framed day-date window at 3 o'clock, and "AUTOMATIC 17 JEWELS" above 6, with "MADE IN JAPAN" at the bottom edge. The round stainless steel case is topped by a fine fluted (coin-edge) bezel, the classic dress-sports look. The caseback is engraved "SEIKO", "7009-3141", "ST. STEEL BASE METAL BEZEL", "WATER RESISTANT", "JAPAN" and serial 174394. It is worn on a five-link jubilee-style stainless steel bracelet with a fold-over clasp signed "SEIKO JAPAN", and comes in a Seiko presentation box with its outer sleeve.

Brand heritage

Seiko traces its roots to Kintaro Hattori's shop in Tokyo in 1881 and produced Japan's first wristwatch in 1913. The Seiko 5 line, launched in 1963, was built around five promises (automatic winding, day-date display, water resistance, a recessed crown and a durable case and bracelet), and its robust calibre 7009 automatic powered Seiko 5 watches for decades, earning the range its reputation for reliability.
