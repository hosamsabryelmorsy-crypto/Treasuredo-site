About this watch

A bold 1970s Seiko ladies automatic, reference 2205-3150, powered by the Seiko calibre 2205 Hi-Beat automatic movement with 17 jewels and date. The dial is pure 70s design: a bright coral-orange inner square framed by a silver-grey outer square, thin black baton markers with a double marker at 12, slim black hands and a small date window at 3 o'clock. It is signed with the applied SEIKO logo and "AUTOMATIC" below 12, "17 JEWELS HI-BEAT" with the Seiko lightning symbol above 6, and "JAPAN 2205-1370S" at 6. The brushed stainless steel cushion-square case flows into an integrated brushed steel H-link bracelet. The caseback is engraved "SEIKO", "Water Resistant", "Stainless Steel", serial 721013, "2205-3150" and "Japan-G". Supplied in a Seiko box.

Brand heritage

Seiko traces its roots to Kintaro Hattori's shop in Tokyo in 1881 and produced Japan's first wristwatch in 1913. In the late 1960s and 1970s Seiko's compact Hi-Beat automatic calibres such as the 2205 powered its ladies automatic watches, combining reliable in-house mechanics with daring dial and case designs.
