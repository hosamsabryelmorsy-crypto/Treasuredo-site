About this watch

A classic 1960s Zodiac gentleman's watch. The cream dial, signed with the zodiac emblem and "hermetic" writing, applied faceted gold stick markers with a twelve triangle, golden dolphin hands, and a small second to six dial. Decades of aging have given it a uniform, spotted patina.

The 36mm gold plated round case has long curved legs and a stainless steel screwed back marked "Zodiac, Swiss Made, Shockproof, Anti-magnetic, Water Resistant" with the zodiac emblem. Inside is the signed Zodiac Ltd caliber 330, a hand-wound Swiss movement of 17 jewels. It features a black woven fabric strap.

Brand heritage

Zodiac was founded in 1882 by Ariste Calame au Locle, Switzerland, and adopted the name Zodiac in the early 20th century. The brand is best known for the 1953 Sea Wolf, one of the first specially designed dive watches sold to the public, and in the 1950s and 1960s it produced stylish and sturdy dress watches with its own internal movements, such as the caliber 330 found here.
