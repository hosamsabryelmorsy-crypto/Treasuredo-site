About this watch

A classic 1960s Zodiac men's watch. The cream dial, signed with the zodiac emblem and "hermetic" writing, has faceted gold baton markers with a triangle at twelve, gold dauphine hands and a small seconds dial at six. Decades of aging have given it an even, spotted patina.

The 36mm round gold-plated case has long curved lugs and a screw-down stainless steel back marked "Zodiac, Swiss Made, Shock-Resistant, Antimagnetic, Water-Resistant" with the Zodiac emblem. Inside is the signed Zodiac Ltd calibre 330, a Swiss hand wound movement with 17 jewels. It is equipped with a black woven fabric tape.

Brand Heritage

Zodiac was founded in 1882 by Ariste Calame in Le Locle, Switzerland, and adopted the name Zodiac in the early 20th century. Best known for the 1953 Sea Wolf, one of the first purpose-built divers watches sold to the public, the brand produced sleek, rugged dress watches in the 1950s and 1960s with their own in-house movements, such as the calibre 330 found here.
