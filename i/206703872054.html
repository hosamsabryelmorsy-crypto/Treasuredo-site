About this watch

A classic 1960s Zodiac men's watch. The cream dial, signed with the Zodiac coat of arms and the inscription "Ermetic", applied faceted gold stick indexes with a twelve triangle, gold dolphin hands and a small seconds dial to six. Decades of aging have given it an even, spotted patina.

The 36mm gold plated round case has long curved lugs and a screw-on stainless steel back marked "Zodiac, Swiss Made, Shock-Resistant, Antimagnetic, Water-Resistant" with the Zodiac emblem. Inside is the signed Zodiac Ltd caliber 330, a 17 jewel hand-wound Swiss movement. It features a black fabric strap.

Brand heritage

Zodiac was founded in 1882 by Ariste Calame in Le Locle, Switzerland, and adopted the name Zodiac in the early 20th century. The brand is best known for the 1953 Sea Wolf, one of the first purpose-built dive watches sold to the public, and in the 1950s and 1960s it produced stylish and rugged watches with its own internal movements, such as the caliber 330 found here.
