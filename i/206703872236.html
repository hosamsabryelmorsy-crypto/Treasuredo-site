About this watch

A striking sapphire facade and automatic mechanical movement give this vintage Rado dome an attractive combination of sculptural design and traditional watchmaking. A wide glossy black frame frames a smaller silver dial, creating a floating dial effect, with reflections moving through the crystal as the wrist moves.

Gold hands and delicate gold dot markers bring warmth to the dial, while larger dark markers set quarters of an hour and a discreet date aperture is six. The compact 31mm case, reference 129.3577.4N at the bottom of the case, connects to a black leather strap using unusual polished center accessories, finished with a gold Rado buckle.

Brand heritage

Rado was born out of Schlup & Co., a movement workshop founded by the Schlup brothers in Lengnau, Switzerland, in 1917; the watches were first sold as Rado in the 1950s. In 1962, the brand launched the DiaStar, the first scratch-resistant watch, earning Rado a reputation as a "master of materials" and a pioneer of hard metal, sapphire crystal, and high-tech ceramics.

The Coupole collection reflects Rado's longstanding interest in unconventional shapes and expressive use of sapphire crystal.
