Brand Heritage

Persol was founded in 1917 in Turin, Italy, by optician Giuseppe Ratti, who initially made goggles for pilots and sports riders; the name comes from "per il sole", "for the sun". In the 1930s, Persol introduced the patented Meflecto flexible strap, and the Supreme Silver Arrow became his signature. Frames made before Luxottica took over the brand in 1995 and usually referred to as Persols "Ratti era", are highly prized by collectors for their thick, handcrafted acetate and mineral glass glasses.
