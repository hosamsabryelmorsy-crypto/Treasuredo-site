Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by optician Giuseppe Ratti, who first made goggles for pilots and sports pilots; the name comes from "per il sole", "for the sun". In the 1930s, Persol introduced the patented Meflecto flexible temple, and the Supreme silver arrow became his signature. Frames made prior to Luxottica's acquisition of the brand in 1995, commonly referred to as Persols "Ratti era", are highly prized by collectors for their thick hand-finished acetate and mineral glass lenses.
