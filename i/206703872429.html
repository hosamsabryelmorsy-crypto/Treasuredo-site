About this watch

Particularly evocative of the Rado design of the 1970s, this Senator combines a large 37mm TV-shaped case, an integrated steel bracelet, and an unusual silver-green dial. In direct light the dial may appear cold silver, but from another angle emerges a thin pale green beneath the metal surface; age has added delicate spots and tonal variations.

The applied faceted hour indices frame the dial, the Rado red anchor emblem is at twelve, and the three-day date display shows the day of the week in red (Spanish language wheel of the day) next to the date. The bottom dial bears the signature SENATOR. Rounded square case continues into the articulated steel bracelet with Rado signature clasp. The caseback opens to show a Swiss automatic movement with a gold-decorated rotor.

Brand heritage

Rado was born from the Schlup & Co. movement lab. founded in Lengnau, Switzerland, in 1917, and began selling watches under the Rado name in the 1950s. The brand became known as the "Master of Materials" for pioneering hard metals, sapphire glass, and later high-tech ceramics in watchmaking. Alongside its famous DiaStar, Rado produced a wide range of bold day-date automatic models in the 1970s, such as the Senator, which were particularly popular in the Middle East, Asia, and Latin America.
