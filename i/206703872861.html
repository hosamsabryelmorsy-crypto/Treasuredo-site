About this watch

A distinctive sapphire front and automatic mechanical movement give this vintage Rado Coupole an appealing combination of sculptural design and traditional watchmaking. A wide, lustrous black bezel frames a smaller silver-tone dial, creating a floating dial effect where reflections shift across the crystal as the wrist moves.

Gold-tone hands and delicate gold-tone dot markers bring warmth to the dial, while larger dark markers define the quarter-hour and a subtle date aperture is six. The compact 31mm case, reference 129.3577.4N on the caseback, is connected by unusually polished central tips on a black leather strap fitted with a Rado signed gold-tone buckle.

Brand Heritage

Rado originated from Schlup & Co., a movement workshop founded in 1917 by the Schlup brothers in Lengnau; watches were first sold under the Rado name in the 1950s. In 1962, the brand launched the first scratch-resistant watch, the DiaStar, earning Rado its reputation as a "Master of Materials" and pioneer of carbide, sapphire crystal and high-tech ceramics.

The Coupole collection reflects Rado's longstanding interest in bohemian shapes and the expressive use of sapphire crystal.
