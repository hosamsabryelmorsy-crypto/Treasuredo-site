Vintage Orient Three Star - Automatic 21 Jewels - Diver Style with Internal Swivel Bezel - Blue Gradient Dial - 1970s

A vintage 1970s Orient "Three Star" automatic in a large cushion style stainless steel case. The gradient blue dial fades from a deep navy on the edge to a lighter center and is framed by a rotating inner bezel with a 60-minute scale, its first quarter highlighted in red, operated by the crown at 2 o'clock. The dial carries applied bright stick indexes, the oriental coat of arms, three stars below the center and a date window of day at 3 o'clock with an Arabic day wheel. The case measures 42mm in diameter and 15mm thick, with knurled outer bezel and engraved "Water Resistant: All Stainless Steel" screw caseback with Orient crest and case number 469655A-6B. Comes with a 22mm stainless steel mesh bracelet and an East Japan presentation box.

Brand heritage

Orient has its roots in the watch store opened by Shogoro Yoshida in Tokyo in 1901, and was founded as a watch manufacturer in 1950, taking the name Orient Watch Company the following year. He became one of the three great Japanese watchmakers along with Seiko and Citizen, building his automatic movements in-house and enjoying particular popularity in the Middle East and Asia.

Model History

The Three Star was the best-selling line of daily vending machines in the East from the 1970s onwards, named after the three stars printed on the dial, and was exported in large numbers with Arabic and English day wheels. This large cushioned version with double crowns and internal swivel bezel follows the style of the Orient King Diver, one of the brand's most recognizable sporty designs of the period.
