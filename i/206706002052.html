About this item

A vintage pair of Cottet pilot sunglasses, model 1152, made in France. The gold-tone metal frame has a fine burgundy enamel line running around the front of each lens rim, a straight top brow bar and a lower bridge with a burgundy enamel stripe between two gold bars. Square gold hinge blocks, slim gold temples and burgundy temple tips complete the look, fitted with brown gradient lenses (dark at the top, fading to light at the bottom).

Brand heritage

Cottet is a French optical house known for its metal pilot and aviator frames made in France and supplied through opticians. Vintage Cottet frames are sought after by collectors of classic French eyewear for their build quality and refined details.
