About this item

A pair of vintage Lastes sunglasses, model M. 6008, made in Italy. The squared front is cut from two-tone gradient acetate that runs from smoky dark brown at the outer rims to clear amber at the bridge, fitted with solid olive-green lenses. The temples have a dark front section with a small silver-tone plate at the hinge and two thin silver rings, then a clear marbled amber section with a visible metal core wire and a key-shaped inlay. They come with the original black Lastes International slip pouch and a Lastes International display card from Optician Al-Mokhtar, Alexandria, Egypt.

Brand heritage

Lastes was an Italian eyewear maker active from the 1970s to the 1990s, also known for its "Swing by Lastes" and "Finissimo by Lastes" lines. Its acetate frames from this period were made in Italy, and the temples are stamped "BREV." (brevetto, patented) next to the Lastes oval logo.
