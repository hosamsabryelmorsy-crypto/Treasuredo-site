About this watch

A striking 1980s design watch from Continental, the Swiss maker. The matte cream dial is framed by a dark grey 24-hour chapter ring (24 at the top, 13 to 23 around the hours) and crossed by thin black crosshair lines. Black baton hour markers, a fine minute track, the Continental crosshair-circle emblem and "CONTINENTAL" signature under 12, "SWISS QUARTZ" above 6 and a date window at 6 o'clock complete the clean, technical look. Black baton hands with luminous inlays and a red seconds hand with red centre cap add a sporty accent. The round case and bezel are finished in matte black with a sculpted crown guard on the right side protecting the crown, flowing directly into a matte black integrated bracelet of flat segments with a fold-over clasp. The stainless steel caseback is engraved "Stainless Steel Back - Swiss Made - Waterresistant" with case number "1530". Supplied in its original white Continental Swiss Made presentation box.

Brand heritage

Continental is a Swiss watch brand that has produced Swiss made mechanical and quartz watches for decades, and it became especially well known across the Middle East, where many special and commemorative dials were made for it. Its 1980s quartz pieces are typical of the bold, modern design language of that era.
