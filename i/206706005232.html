About this watch

A genuine 1970s Seiko 5 DX reference 6106-5480, the bold square "TV" case model with faceted corners and an integrated H-link steel bracelet. The sunburst metallic blue dial shades darker toward the edges and carries applied silver baton markers, the Seiko 5 shield with "Automatic 23 Jewels", the italic "DX" signature and a day-date window at 3 o'clock with an Arabic day wheel. Powered by Seiko's in-house automatic calibre 6106 (23 jewels). The caseback serial 661970 dates it to June 1976. It comes in its original Seiko presentation box with outer sleeve.

Brand heritage

Seiko launched the Seiko 5 line in 1963 as a robust, affordable automatic with day-date; the DX ("Deluxe") versions used the higher-grade 6106 calibre. The 6106 family powered many of Seiko's most loved 1960s and 1970s designs, and the angular TV-case models of the mid-1970s are now sought after for their period style.
