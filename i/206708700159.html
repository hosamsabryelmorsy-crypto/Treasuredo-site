About this watch

An elegant, slim S.T. Dupont Paris dress watch, reference 199.11.1 (engraved on the caseback). The centre of the dial is a gold disc decorated with S.T. Dupont's signature pyramid "pointe de diamant" (Clous de Paris) guilloché, carrying a small gold plaque signed "S.T. Dupont PARIS". It is framed by a wide brushed steel chapter ring with applied gold stylised Roman numerals at 12, 3, 6 and 9, gold ball hour markers and "SWISS MADE" at 6. Two slim blued steel hands, a gold crown signed with the Dupont "D", and steel lugs with gold diamond-pattern side panels on all four lugs complete a very refined two-tone look. Supplied in the original black lacquered S.T. Dupont box.

Brand heritage

S.T. Dupont was founded in Paris in 1872 by Simon Tissot-Dupont as a maker of fine leather goods and travel cases for European royalty and high society. The house later became famous for its luxury lighters and writing instruments, made with Chinese lacquer and the characteristic "pointe de diamant" guilloché pattern that is echoed on the dial and lugs of this watch.
