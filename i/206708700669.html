About this watch

A genuine piece of Middle Eastern military history: a Swiss Giroxa manual-wind wristwatch made as a souvenir of the 1948 Palestine war. The stainless steel caseback is engraved in Arabic with a four-line commemorative inscription that reads approximately "Souvenir - Royal ... - Palestine Battlefield - 1948" (تذكار ... الملكية / ميدان القتال بفلسطين / سنة ١٩٤٨), dating the watch to the era of the Kingdom of Egypt. The dial carries the GIROXA name with an Arabic calligraphy signature beneath it, an aged cream dial with black Arabic numerals, blued steel leaf hands and a small seconds subdial at 6.

Brand heritage

Giroxa Watch Co. was a Swiss watch brand of the mid-20th century, supplying mechanical wristwatches with Swiss-made movements to export markets, including the Middle East. Watches made for presentation or commemoration, with Arabic dial and caseback inscriptions, are a small and sought-after niche for collectors of regional and military history.
