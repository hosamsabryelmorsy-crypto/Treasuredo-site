About this watch

A very rare dial: this deep blue lapis-look stone dial is seldom seen on Barlux watches. An elegant vintage Swiss dress watch signed Barlux. The deep royal blue dial has a speckled, lapis-lazuli-like stone finish with fine light and dark flecks, set off by a gold-tone Roman numeral XII, slim gold-tone baton markers at 3, 6 and 9, and a pair of gold-tone pointed lance hands. The dial is signed "BARLUX" under the XII, "INCABLOC" above 6 and "SWISS" at the bottom edge. The clean two-hand layout (no seconds hand) keeps the look pure and refined.

The rectangular case has a strong Art Deco character: polished side bars with three stepped, ribbed lines run the full length of the case and extend into the lugs at top and bottom. The crown sits at 3 o'clock, and the brushed steel caseback with bevelled edges is stamped "ACIER" (French for steel). It is fitted on a black lizard-grain leather strap with a steel-tone buckle.

Brand heritage

Barlux is a Swiss watch name of the mid-20th century, known for mechanical Incabloc-equipped watches that were especially popular in the Middle East. Vintage Barlux pieces turn up with classic dress designs as well as special dials, and today they are a niche, collectible Swiss name.
