About this watch

A clean, modern men's dress watch from the Calvin Klein City collection. The black dial is split into two finishes: a fine vertical-line texture across the left side and a concentric circular finish across the rest, giving a subtle play of light. "CALVIN KLEIN" is printed in slim capitals below 12, with a date window at 3 o'clock and "SWISS MADE" at 6. Thin silver baton markers sit on the textured side, with slim silver baton hands with luminous inserts and a fine centre seconds hand. The slim round case is polished stainless steel with short straight lugs and a crown signed with the CK logo. The caseback is engraved "CALVIN KLEIN - swiss made - ALL STAINLESS STEEL - WATER RESISTANT 3bar" with the case reference "K2G 2G1". Supplied in a Calvin Klein "swiss made" box.

Brand heritage

Calvin Klein, the New York fashion house founded in 1968, has offered Swiss made watches since the late 1990s, produced under licence by the Swatch Group in Switzerland. The collection is known for its minimalist, architectural design, and the City line is one of its signature everyday dress models.
