About this watch

A classic Jovial dress watch from the late 20th century, reference No. 8501-AS. The round 22 electro gold plated case carries a brushed steel-tone bezel ring set with six ribbed gold clips, giving a refined two-tone look. The crisp white dial has bold black Roman numerals, an inner ring of gold dots, the winged J Jovial emblem under 12 and slim gold leaf hands with a gold seconds hand. Powered by a Swiss quartz movement (dial marked "EB 763 Swiss Quartz"). A midsize 32mm that suits a lady's wrist and also a slim men's wrist. Supplied with a Jovial "Swiss Made" presentation box.

Brand heritage

Jovial is a Swiss watch brand founded in 1929, known for well-made, classically styled watches and especially popular across the Middle East for decades.
