About this watch

A rare Eterna presentation watch made for the State of Qatar. The glossy white dial carries a full-colour Qatari military crest at its centre: a spread-winged falcon beneath a gold star, a red and yellow roundel on its chest and an Arabic-script ribbon, all framed by a green laurel wreath. Above it sit the Eterna name and the brand's five-dot pyramid logo. Black serif Roman numerals are printed radially around the dial with a date window at 3 o'clock, small gold dot markers, thin gold hands and "SWISS" at the bottom. The slim yellow gold-plated case measures 33 mm (excluding crown) and has elegant curved hooded lugs. The stainless steel caseback is engraved with the State of Qatar emblem and the words "State of Qatar" in Arabic and English. Swiss quartz movement. It comes with an Eterna box.

Brand heritage

Eterna was founded in Grenchen, Switzerland, in 1856. The company invented the first wristwatch alarm in 1908 and in 1948 introduced the Eterna-Matic automatic with ball-bearing-mounted rotor, the five balls of which became the five-dot pyramid logo still printed on its dials. Eterna's movement division later became ETA, today one of Switzerland's largest movement makers. Eterna also produced special-order dials for Gulf governments and institutions, which are now sought after by collectors.
