About this watch

A slim vintage Rotary dress watch from the 1970s, built around a hand-wound Swiss mechanical movement. The rectangular case with gently curved long sides carries a frosted, hand-textured "bark" finish that catches the light from every angle, a signature look of 1970s Swiss jewellery-style watches. The off-white silver dial has an embossed bark/linen texture, bold black Roman numerals at XII, III, VI and IX, slim applied silver baton markers and slender silver hands, signed ROTARY with the winged Rotary emblem and SWISS MADE at the bottom. The crown is signed with the Rotary R. Supplied in a Rotary Switzerland presentation box.

Brand heritage

Rotary was founded in 1895 by Moise Dreyfuss in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, and grew into one of the best-known Swiss watch names in Britain, where it supplied watches to the British Army during the Second World War. The winged Rotary emblem printed under the logo on this dial is the brand's classic mark.
