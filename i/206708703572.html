About this watch

The dial is signed with the early Seiko "S" emblem above "SEIKO" and "SUPER" at 6, with dark Arabic 12, 3, 6 and 9, applied gold-tone dagger markers, an inner ring of fine concentric circles and an outer railway minute track with 15/30/45/60 numerals and round hour dots. Inside is the hand-wound movement signed "SEIKOSHA". It comes on a black padded leather strap with orange stitching, presented in a Seiko dealer box ("With Compliments... Al-Hussaini & Co. - SEIKO").

Brand heritage

Seiko traces its roots to Kintaro Hattori's shop founded in Tokyo in 1881 and to the Seikosha factory established in 1892. According to the Seiko Museum, the Super of 1950 was Seiko's first wristwatch with a centre seconds hand: earlier models used a small seconds hand at 6 o'clock, and the Super required a completely new wheel train design. It opened the era of centre-seconds watches in Japan and is one of the key models of Seiko's post-war rise.
