About this item

A pair of vintage Lastes sunglasses made in Italy, in a bold flat-top navigator shape with a separate brow bar over the nose bridge (double bridge). The front is cut from light honey amber tortoiseshell acetate that turns translucent golden along the lower rims and bridge, fitted with brown lenses. Each temple carries a small round black logo plate with the gold Lastes "L" just behind the hinge, followed by two thin metal bands, a classic Lastes detail.

Brand heritage

Lastes was an Italian eyewear maker active from the 1970s to the 1990s, also known for its "Swing by Lastes" and "Finissimo by Lastes" lines. Its acetate frames from this period were made in Italy and are marked with the Lastes oval logo on the inside of the temple.
