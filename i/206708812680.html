About this item

A pair of vintage Carrera 5401 aviator sunglasses in colour 91, size Small, made in Germany. Brand heritage

Carrera was founded in Austria in 1956 by Wilhelm Anger and became known for sports and aviator eyewear in the 1970s and 1980s. The 5401 is one of its classic 1980s aviators, made in Small and Large sizes.
