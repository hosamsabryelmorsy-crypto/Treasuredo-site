Brand heritage

Dolce & Gabbana is the Italian fashion house founded in Milan in 1985 by Domenico Dolce and Stefano Gabbana. Its eyewear is made in Italy under licence by Luxottica, and the DG4451 is one of the house's thick-acetate men's rectangular frames.
