About this item

A pair of Persol 9649-S sunglasses in colour 24/58: classic Havana tortoiseshell acetate with solid green polarized lenses. They come with a black Persol hard case.

Brand heritage

Persol was founded in Turin, Italy, in 1917 by Giuseppe Ratti; the name comes from "per il sole" (for the sun). The silver Supreme Arrow on the hinges, inspired by the swords of ancient warriors, has been the brand's signature for decades, and Persol frames are still made in Italy.
