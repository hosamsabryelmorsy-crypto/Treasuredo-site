The dial is signed "Bostonian 17 Jewels" with the brand emblem, "Drifter" in script and "Swiss Made". It is fitted with a navy blue perforated leather rally strap.

Brand heritage

Bostonian is one of the many lesser-known Swiss-made watch names of the 1960s and 1970s, the era when Swiss workshops supplied jewelled mechanical watches under a wide range of brand names. Sporty "skin diver" designs like the Drifter, with a timing bezel and big legible numerals, were the everyday sports watches of that period and are now popular with collectors for their character and affordable charm.
