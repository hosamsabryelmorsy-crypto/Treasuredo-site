About this watch

A classic Timex Expedition field watch: a clean white dial with large black Arabic numerals, an outer minute track with red 24-hour numerals 13 to 24, a date window at 3 o'clock, black sword hands and a red seconds hand. Press the crown and the Timex Indiglo night-light illuminates the whole dial. The brushed stainless steel case measures 40.5 mm across (caliper measured, excluding crown) and is worn on a black leather strap with a steel buckle.

Brand heritage

Timex traces its roots to the Waterbury Clock Company, founded in Connecticut in 1854, and has made affordable, reliable watches for generations of Americans. The Expedition line is Timex's outdoor collection, and the Indiglo electroluminescent night-light, introduced by Timex in the early 1990s, became one of the brand's best-known features.
