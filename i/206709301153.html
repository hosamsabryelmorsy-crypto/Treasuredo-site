About this watch

The Swatch Irony Automatic "Body & Soul" (YAS100G) is one of Swatch's best-loved mechanical watches: a fully open-worked skeleton dial shows the Swiss self-winding movement with its gold-tone wheels and balance, and the exhibition caseback shows the rotor signed "swatch IRONY automatic". The polished stainless steel case carries a bezel engraved with "swatch" all the way around, and it sits on a matching stainless steel link bracelet with a Swatch-signed fold-over clasp. It comes in a Swatch pillow box with its clear plastic cube.

Brand heritage

Swatch was launched in 1983 in Switzerland and grew into the Swatch Group, today the world's largest watchmaker and home of brands such as Omega, Longines and Tissot. The Irony line brought Swatch design to metal cases, and the Irony Automatic models use Swiss mechanical movements from the group's movement maker ETA.
