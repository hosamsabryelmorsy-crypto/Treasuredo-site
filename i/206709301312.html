About this watch

A striking 1970s Swiss dress watch signed Leuba Louis Geneve. The square gold-plated case is built in three stepped tiers, a bold architectural design that frames a deep glossy navy-blue dial. The dial is kept minimal: four fine silver line markers at 12, 3, 6 and 9, the script "Leuba Louis" signature with "GENEVE" beneath it, and a pair of slim gold leaf-shaped hands under a domed crystal. Inside is a Swiss hand-wound movement marked "SWISS - SEVENTEEN 17 JEWELS" with a gold-finished, striped bridge.

Brand heritage

Leuba Louis is a Geneva-signed Swiss watch name found on mechanical and later quartz dress watches of the 1960s and 1970s. These watches were made with Swiss movements and are known for clean, elegant dials and stylish gold-plated cases typical of the era.
