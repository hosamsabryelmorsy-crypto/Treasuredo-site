About this watch

A 1968 Seiko Sportsmatic, reference 7625-8233, powered by the Seiko calibre 7625 automatic movement with 17 jewels and date. The dial is a beautiful charcoal-grey sunburst that fades darker towards the edge, crossed by fine crosshair lines, with applied faceted baton markers, lume plots on the outer minute ring and a framed date window at 3. It is signed "SEIKO AUTOMATIC" under 12 and "WATER PROOF 17 JEWELS" with the Seiko lightning logo above 6. The polished steel case with its wide bezel sits on a stainless steel beads-of-rice bracelet, and it comes in a Seiko box.

Brand heritage

Seiko, founded in Tokyo in 1881, built its reputation in the 1960s with robust, accurate automatic watches. The Sportsmatic line was one of Seiko's first popular automatic families of that decade, and the 7625 calibre is known as a simple, durable workhorse movement.
