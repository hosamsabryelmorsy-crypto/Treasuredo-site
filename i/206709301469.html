About this watch

A men's Orient Crystal automatic powered by the in-house Orient calibre 46941 with 21 jewels. The real star is the dial: an iridescent blue mother-of-pearl / abalone-effect surface that shifts between deep ocean blue, turquoise and violet as it moves in the light, so no two photos look the same. It carries the gold Orient crown-and-lions emblem with its red O shield, a gold ORIENT logo, applied polished baton markers (double at 12) with luminous dots, gold-tone luminous hands and a gold-framed day-date window at 3 o'clock showing the date and the day in Arabic. "Crystal 21 JEWELS" is printed below centre and "JAPAN Y46946866-712" along the bottom edge. The polished stainless steel case measures 37.3 mm without the crown, with the crown at 3 and a quick-set pusher at 2, and the display caseback shows the automatic movement and rotor signed ORIENT 21J 46941. Supplied on a stainless steel Milanese mesh bracelet, with an Orient Automatic presentation box and an Orient International Guarantee booklet.

Brand heritage

Orient traces its roots to 1901 Tokyo and became Orient Watch Co. in 1950. It is known for building its own automatic movements in-house, and the 21-jewel 469-series calibres used in the Orient Crystal and Three Star lines are famous for their durability and day-date convenience.
