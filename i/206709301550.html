About this watch

A classic 1970s Mido Ocean Star Commander, one of the brand's signature designs of the era, in the Datoday (day-date) version, case reference 8479. The silver-grey brushed dial carries the script Mido logo, OCEAN STAR / datoday at 9, a cursive Commander script above 6 and T-SWISS MADE-T at the bottom. Twelve applied faceted square steel markers with black inner frames and luminous centres, polished baton hands with lume inserts and a day-date window at 3 complete the clean, architectural look. The polished round steel monocoque case measures 36.9 mm without the crown and has a signed Mido crown. The solid caseback is engraved Mido, STAINLESS STEEL, SWISS MADE, serial 6996984 and reference 8479. It is worn on the integrated stainless steel H-link bracelet with a Mido-signed fold-over clasp.

Brand heritage

Mido was founded in 1918 in Solothurn, Switzerland, by Georges Schaeren. Its Ocean Star line, launched in 1944, was built around the brand's water-resistant "Aquadura" sealing ideas, and the Commander, introduced in 1959, became one of Mido's most recognisable models with its one-piece monocoque case, which opens from the front rather than the back.
