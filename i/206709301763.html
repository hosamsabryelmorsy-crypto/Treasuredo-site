Brand heritage

Rado was founded in Lengnau, Switzerland, in 1917 as the Schlup & Co. movement workshop and took the Rado name in the 1950s. In 1962 it launched the DiaStar, the world's first scratch-resistant watch, with a case made of hardmetal and a sapphire crystal. The DiaStar became Rado's signature line, and its scratchproof hardmetal cases are why vintage DiaStars still look sharp decades later.
