Brand heritage

Favre-Leuba is one of the oldest Swiss watch houses, with roots going back to 1737 when Abraham Favre registered as a watchmaker in Le Locle. The company became famous in the 20th century for innovative watches such as the Bivouac and Bathy, and its Sea King line was its popular everyday collection of the 1960s and 1970s, sold widely in the Middle East and Asia.
