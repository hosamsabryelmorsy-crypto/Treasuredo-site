About this watch

A bold 1970s Swiss men's watch by Tell in a large cushion ("TV") shaped case with a wide polished bezel. The dial is the highlight: a smoky grey-taupe to gilt gradient with a fine diagonal tapisserie texture that changes colour as it catches the light. It carries the gold Tell crossbow logo above "TELL", "17 JEWELS SHOCKPROTECTED" above 6 and "SWISS MADE" at the bottom edge. Applied gold baton markers (doubled at 12 and 6) each have a lume dot, matched by gold baton hands with lume inserts, a gold centre seconds hand and a date window at 3. The signed crown carries the Tell logo. Fitted with a brown leather strap.

Brand heritage

Tell was a Swiss watch brand named after William Tell, the legendary Swiss crossbowman, whose crossbow appears as the brand's logo. Tell produced affordable Swiss mechanical watches through the mid-20th century, and its 1970s pieces with bold cases and textured dials are popular with collectors of vintage Swiss style.
