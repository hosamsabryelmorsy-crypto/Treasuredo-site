About this watch

A striking 1970s Felca ladies dress watch from the Titoflex line. The sculpted gold-plated cushion case with its wide, softly domed polished bezel frames a vertical oval dial in a warm brown "tiger stripe" finish, with alternating light-gold and dark-brown bands that shimmer as the light moves. Applied silver baton indices at 12, 3, 6 and 9, fine printed markers at the other hours, gold baton hands and a slim centre seconds hand. Signed "FELCA" and "TITOFLEX" on the dial and "FELCA SWISS 375" on the stainless steel caseback. Worn on its integrated gold-tone woven mesh bracelet.

Brand heritage

Felca was founded in Grenchen, Switzerland, in 1918 and became known for well-made, affordable Swiss watches. The brand was an early maker of water-resistant and automatic watches and was widely sold across Europe, the Middle East and Africa during the 1960s and 1970s.
