About this watch

A minimalist Skagen Denmark ladies watch, model 563XSGSC. The slim brushed stainless steel tonneau case holds a matte silver dial framed by a fine gold-tone inner border, with a gold centre ring, gold Arabic numerals at the even hours and slim gold baton markers in between. Signed "SKAGEN DENMARK" with the double-arrow logo and "STEEL" on the dial, and fully signed on the screwed stainless steel caseback. Worn on the classic Skagen Milanese steel mesh bracelet with a signed SKAGEN fold-over clasp.

Brand heritage

Skagen was founded in 1989 by Danish couple Henrik and Charlotte Jorst and named after the seaside town of Skagen in northern Denmark. The brand became known worldwide for its clean Scandinavian design, ultra-thin cases and signature steel mesh bracelets.
