About this watch

A slim, elegant dress watch signed "RD - IRMLA DIMIH - pransis" on the dial. The two-tone dial pairs a brushed silver outer ring, with applied Roman numerals at XII, III, VI and IX and applied baton markers, with a champagne sunburst centre and a guilloche small seconds sub-dial at 6. The round stainless steel case has angular integrated lug shoulders, each corner set with three small white stones, and flows into a fully integrated brushed steel multi-link bracelet. The screwed caseback is engraved "RD IRMLADIMIH pransis Geneve - All Stainless Steel - Sapphire Crystal - Water Resistant - Swiss Movement".
