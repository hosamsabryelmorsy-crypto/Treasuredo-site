A pair of vintage Rodenstock Exclusiv 575 sunglasses from the 1970s. The large squared frame is in translucent honey/olive acetate with a gold-tone wire core visible through the temples, and polished steel Rodaflex hinge covers at each side. The lenses are a warm brown gradient, darker at the top. The temples are stamped "Exclusiv 575 lama", "RODENSTOCK" and "RODAFLEX".

Brand heritage

Rodenstock was founded in 1877 by Josef Rodenstock and is one of Germany's oldest and most respected optical companies, based in Munich. Its Exclusiv line of the 1970s combined quality German acetate frames with the Rodaflex hinge system, and these frames are now sought after by vintage eyewear collectors.
