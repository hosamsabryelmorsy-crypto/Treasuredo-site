About this item

A vintage pair of Lanvin Paris women's sunglasses, model 713, colour 61, made in France. The oval front is clear crystal with a black inner band along the top and outer edge of each lens. At the outer corners, gold-tone metal wires cross over a clear panel set with small rhinestones (the box label reads "STRASS / PASTE"). The slim gold-tone temples are signed LANVIN on the outside and end in black tips. The lenses are a grey gradient. The pair comes with its original labelled Lanvin box and a Lanvin pouch.

Brand heritage

Lanvin is the oldest French couture house still in operation. Jeanne Lanvin founded it in Paris in 1889, and its logo of a mother and daughter, drawn by Paul Iribe, appears on the box and the pouch of this pair.
