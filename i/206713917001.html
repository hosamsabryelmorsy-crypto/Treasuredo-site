About this watch

A clean vintage Citizen Automatic from the late 1970s to 1980s, built around the reliable Citizen calibre 8200 self-winding movement with 21 jewels. The silver-white dial carries the applied CITIZEN logo over AUTOMATIC, the red Citizen eagle emblem with 21 JEWELS, polished applied baton markers with luminous dots (double marker at 12), a framed day-date window at 3 and the dial code P8200-281885-KY at the edge. The angular stainless steel case measures 35 mm without the crown and flows into a stepped-link stainless steel bracelet with a CITIZEN signed fold-over clasp. The screw-down caseback is engraved CITIZEN WATCH CO., 4-280318 KT, GN-4W-S, ALL STAINLESS STEEL, WATER RESISTANT. Supplied in a Citizen presentation box.

Brand heritage

Citizen Watch Co. was founded in Tokyo in 1930 (its first pocket watch appeared in 1924 under the Citizen name) and grew into one of the largest watchmakers in the world. Its calibre 8200 automatic, introduced in the late 1970s, became one of the most widely used Japanese automatic movements thanks to its robustness and easy servicing.
