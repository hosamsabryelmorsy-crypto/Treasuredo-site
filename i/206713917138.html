About this watch

A petite 1970s Rado Jade ladies' dress watch. The silver brushed dial carries black Roman numerals at every hour, the bold RADO name below 12, the "jade" script signature above 6 and "SWISS MADE" at the bottom, with slim black hands. The stainless steel tonneau case has a brushed top and polished bevelled edges, a crown at 3, and a caseback signed with the Rado anchor, "RADO", "JADE 6036 SWISS". Inside is a Rado-signed 17-jewel Swiss manual-wind movement. It comes on a narrow steel bracelet with diamond-cut brick links.

Brand heritage

Rado was founded in 1917 in Lengnau, Switzerland, as the Schlup & Co. watch movement factory and launched the Rado brand in 1957. In the 1960s and 70s Rado produced elegant mechanical ladies' watches such as the Jade line, before becoming famous for its scratch-resistant hardmetal and ceramic designs.
