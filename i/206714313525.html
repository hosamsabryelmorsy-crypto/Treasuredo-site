About this watch

A bold 1970s Swiss skin-diver style watch signed FESTIVA. The yellow dial has developed an even crackle (crazing) pattern that gives it real vintage character, with an orange outer ring and black minute track. It is signed "FESTIVA 21 JEWELS" under 12 and "20 ATM. WATERPROOF SHOCKPROOF" above 6, with "T SWISS MADE T" at the bottom. Applied square steel markers sit at 12, 9 and 6, a framed date window at 3, steel baton hands with lume inserts and a slim seconds hand. The orange-tan rotating bezel carries a 60-minute scale with a triangle at zero and a coin-edge grip. The brushed chrome-plated cushion case measures 34.9 mm wide without the crown and 41.7 mm lug to lug, and closes with a stainless steel screw-down back engraved "STAINLESS STEEL BACK, SWISS MADE, WATERPROOF, ANTIMAGNETIC, GUARANTEED 20 ATM. TESTED" around a scuba diver motif. It is fitted on a 20 mm orange snakeskin-pattern leather strap by Hirsch.

Brand heritage

Festiva was one of the many small Swiss brands of the 1960s and 1970s that offered mechanical sports watches with Swiss-made movements at accessible prices. Their colourful diver styles from this period are now collected for their period design and fun, wearable size.
