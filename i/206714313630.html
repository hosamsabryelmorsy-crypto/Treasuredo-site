The story: Cortebert, Rolex and Panerai

Cortebert, from the village of Cortebert in the Swiss Jura, made robust, precise pocket watch movements. Its 16-ligne Cal. 616 was chosen by Rolex, which modified it into the Rolex Cal. 618 with its own finishing and regulation.

In the late 1930s and 1940s Officine Panerai of Florence built the Ref. 3646 diving watches for the frogmen of the Italian Navy. Inside these large cushion-cased watches was the Rolex Cal. 618, the Rolex version of this Cortebert 616. The Cortebert 616 is therefore the base calibre behind one of the most important military diving watches ever made.

Because it shares its architecture with the Rolex 618, the Cortebert 616 is sought by watchmakers and collectors as a source of parts and as a base movement for restoring Rolex 618 / Panerai 3646 watches.
