About this watch

A petite 1970s AVIA ladies' cocktail watch. The navy blue sunburst dial has applied silver baton markers at every hour, gold-tone stick hands, the AVIA name below 12, "17 RUBIS / INCABLOC" below the centre and "SWISS MADE" at the bottom. The round dial sits in a slim gold-tone bezel on a square case head that flows straight into an integrated gold-tone bracelet with a bark-textured brick pattern, closed by a textured fold-over clasp. The case measures 16 mm across (measured) and has a stainless steel snap caseback. Inside is a Swiss 17-jewel hand-wound mechanical movement with Incabloc shock protection.

Brand heritage

AVIA was a Swiss watch brand of the 20th century, produced in Switzerland and widely sold in Britain and Europe. It was known for well-made, affordable mechanical dress and ladies' watches, especially in the 1960s and 1970s.
