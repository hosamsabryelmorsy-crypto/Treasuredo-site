About this watch

A classic Longines ladies' dress watch from the late 1970s / 1980s. The silver grained dial carries black Roman numerals, the LONGINES name with the winged hourglass logo below 12, "QUARTZ" above 6 and "717 SWISS" along the bottom edge. Slim black baton hands and a silver seconds hand. The cushion-shaped stainless steel case measures 23 mm across (measured) and has a stepped polished bezel and a crown set with a blue cabochon. The snap caseback has a gold-tone centre marked STAINLESS STEEL and an individual case serial number. It comes on a black leather strap with red contrast stitching and a gold-tone buckle.

Brand heritage

Longines was founded in 1832 in Saint-Imier, Switzerland. Its winged hourglass logo is one of the oldest registered trademarks in the world, and the brand is known for elegant dress watches and a long history in aviation and sports timing.
