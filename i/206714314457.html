About this watch

A handsome 1970s Rado Companion in stainless steel. The frosted silver-white dial carries the red Rado anchor emblem above raised RADO letters, "COMPANION" below the centre and "T SWISS MADE T" at the bottom edge, with applied steel baton markers with black inlay, matching baton hands and a framed day-date window at 3 (Spanish day disc, currently showing DOM). The brushed cushion-shaped case with a polished round bezel measures about 36.5 mm across, with the crown at 3. The screw-down caseback is embossed with the Rado seahorse emblem and "RADO Water-Sealed" and is engraved with reference 603.3203.4 and serial 9671298. It comes on its integrated brushed steel three-row bracelet with a fold-over clasp signed RADO.

Brand heritage

Rado was founded in 1917 in Lengnau, Switzerland, as the Schlup & Co. movement factory, and launched the Rado brand in 1957. Through the 1960s and 70s Rado built robust Swiss automatic day-date watches such as the Companion, Green Horse and Voyager lines, before becoming famous for its scratch-resistant hardmetal and ceramic designs.
