About this watch

A striking 1970s Sicura automatic with a bold octagonal case. The octagonal dial glows in a copper-to-burnt-orange gradient with a lighter golden band through the centre, silver Arabic numerals at 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10 and 11, "SICURA" and "17 JEWELS" under 12, "AUTOMATIC" above a framed date window at 6 and "SWISS MADE" below it. Steel sword hands with black centre lines and a slim seconds hand complete the look. The polished octagonal case has stepped, ribbed corners and the crown at 3. The caseback reads "STAINLESS STEEL BACK - SWISS MADE - BASE METAL BEZEL". It is fitted on an orange-tan textured leather strap.

Brand heritage

Sicura was a Swiss watch brand of the 1960s and 70s, known for affordable, colourful and well-built automatic and diver's watches. In 1979 Sicura's owner Ernest Schneider bought the rights to the Breitling name, which is why Sicura is often linked with Breitling's revival.
