About this watch

A handsome vintage Jovial dress watch with a beautiful two-level dial. The champagne-silver sunburst centre carries the black winged-J logo and "JOVIAL" under 12, "17 JEWELS" above 6 and "T SWISS MADE T" at the bottom. It is framed by a raised, stepped rose-gold chapter ring with applied gold double-bar hour markers and an applied gold winged-J emblem at 12. A white date window with a red numeral sits at 3. Gold leaf hands with lume strips, a red seconds hand and a red centre cap add a lovely touch of colour. The gold plated case has long tapered lugs, a domed crystal and the crown at 3. The stainless steel caseback is stamped "STAINLESS STEEL BACK - ANTIMAGNETIC - SHOCKPROTECTED - WATERPROOF - SWISS MADE - 59 1623" with the Jovial winged-J medallion in the centre.

Brand heritage

Jovial is a Swiss watch house founded in 1929 in Bettlach, Switzerland, famous for its winged-J emblem. Jovial watches were especially popular across the Middle East and Asia in the 1960s and 70s and are known for their solid Swiss movements and attractive dial designs.
