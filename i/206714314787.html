About this watch

A military-style Seiko 5 Sports field watch, made in Japan and powered by the Seiko calibre 4R36 automatic movement with 24 jewels, hacking seconds and hand winding. The olive green dial carries the SEIKO name with the Seiko 5 shield and a red SPORTS signature under 12, large white Arabic numerals with lume dots, an outer green 24-hour chapter ring (13 to 24), "AUTOMATIC 24 JEWELS 100M" above the 6 and "MADE IN JAPAN" at the bottom. White sword hands with lume, a red-orange seconds hand and a day-date window at 3 complete the classic field look. The polished and brushed stainless steel case measures 39.3 mm without the crown and has a display caseback showing the movement signed SEIKO TIME CORP, 4R36, TWENTY-FOUR JEWELS, JAPAN. It comes on a stainless steel three-link bracelet with a SEIKO signed fold-over clasp and is supplied with a Seiko box.

Brand heritage

Seiko was founded in Tokyo in 1881 by Kintaro Hattori and is one of the few fully integrated watch manufacturers in the world. The Seiko 5 line, launched in 1963, was built around five promises: automatic winding, day-date display, water resistance, a recessed crown and a durable case and bracelet. The 4R36, introduced in 2011, added hacking and hand winding to the Seiko 5 Sports family.
