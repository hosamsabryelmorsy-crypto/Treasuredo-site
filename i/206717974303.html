About this watch

A classic Swiss automatic dress watch from Royce. The silver-white sunburst dial carries the Royce winged-R emblem with "ROYCE AUTOMATIC" under 12, "25 JEWELS INCABLOC" above 6 and "SWISS MADE" either side of 6. Faceted rose-gold applied baton markers, rose-gold baton hands with black inlay, a slim rose-gold seconds hand and a rose-gold minute track ring give it a warm, elegant look. A white date window sits at 3. The rose-gold plated case has sharp tapered lugs, a high domed crystal and the crown at 3. The stainless steel caseback is stamped "AUTOMATIC - STAINLESS STEEL BACK - SWISS MADE - WATERPROOF - ANTIMAGNETIC - INCABLOC - 11-103" around the Royce crest medallion. The signed movement rotor reads "ROYCE 25 JEWELS SWISS MADE".

Brand heritage

Royce was a Swiss watch brand of the mid 20th century, best known today for its 1970s automatics such as the Leadership and Time King lines. Royce watches used Swiss movements and were sold widely in the Middle East and Asia.
