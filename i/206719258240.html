About this watch

A handsome 1990s Westar complication watch with a full triple calendar and moonphase. The pale cream dial carries three gold-ringed subdials: the month at 12 (JAN to NOV), the date at 3 (1 to 31) and the day at 9 (SUN to SAT), with a moonphase window at 6 showing a deep blue starry sky with gold stars and moon, over the Westar emblem and WESTAR signature. Black baton hands, a fine black centre seconds hand, gold dot markers and a numbered minute track complete the dial. The steel case has a polished bezel engraved with Roman numerals, a thin gold-tone inner ring, a gold-tone crown at 3 and four recessed calendar correctors at 2, 4, 8 and 10. The screw-down style caseback is engraved "WESTAR - 3ATM WATERRESISTANT - SWISS EB - 8838GCB". It is worn on the matching Westar three-row stainless steel bracelet with a signed fold-over clasp and comes in its Westar "activ" presentation box with the Westar tag.

Brand heritage

Westar is a watch brand long associated with Swiss-made movements and classic designs, popular in the Middle East and Asia since the late 20th century. Its 1990s moonphase triple calendar models are among its most sought-after pieces.
