About this watch

A bright and eye-catching Jovial from the 1990s. The gold sunburst dial carries the winged-J Jovial emblem and JOVIAL signature under 12, eleven red stone hour markers (the date window takes the 6 o'clock position) in gold prong settings, and a ring of small clear stones running around the dial edge. QUARTZ is printed above the white date window at 6, with SWISS below it. Slim gold baton hands and a gold seconds hand. The steel case is framed by a sculpted two-tone bezel with alternating gold-tone and steel ribs, with the crown at 3. The caseback is engraved with the Jovial oval logo and "NO. 2536M - ALL STAINLESS STEEL - WATER RESISTANT - SWISS". It is fitted with the matching stainless steel bracelet with domed links and gold-tone spacers, and comes in its Jovial "Since 1929" box with the Jovial card.

Brand heritage

Jovial is a Swiss watch brand founded in 1929, as stated on its boxes ("Jovial Since 1929"). It has long been especially popular in the Middle East, known for classic Swiss-made dress and sport watches.
