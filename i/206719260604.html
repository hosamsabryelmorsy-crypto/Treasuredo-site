About this watch

A classic 1970s Swiss automatic from Breil's "Okay" line. The crisp white dial has bold black Roman numerals around the edge and a fine minute track, the BREIL OKAY signature under 12 and AUTOMATIC INCABLOC above 6. The date window at 3 sits under a cyclops magnifier on the crystal. Slim black baton hands and a black centre seconds hand. The gold plated case has a finely fluted gold bezel, tapered lugs and a gold crown at 3. The stainless steel caseback is engraved "STAINLESS STEEL BACK - WATERRESISTANT - ANTIMAGNETIC - INCABLOC". Inside is a Swiss automatic movement with a rotor signed "21 JEWELS SWISS". Worn on a black leather strap with gold-tone buckle and supplied in its original Breil box.

Brand heritage

Breil is a well-known Italian watch brand, part of the Binda group of Milan. In the 1970s Breil offered Swiss-made mechanical watches under the "Okay" name, today collected for their classic designs.
