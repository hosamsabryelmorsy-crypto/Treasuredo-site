About this watch

A refined Swiss automatic from Baume & Mercier's Capeland collection. The silver-white dial has a fine concentric guilloche finish, bold black serif Arabic numerals, a small seconds subdial at 6, a date window at 3, and an outer minute ring of small open diamond markers numbered 5 to 60 with luminous dots. It is signed with the Baume & Mercier phi logo, "BAUME & MERCIER GENEVE", "AUTOMATIC" and "SWISS MADE". Dark sword hands with luminous inserts. The polished stainless steel case (38.5 mm) has a wide brushed bezel and a screw-down crown, with a solid screw-down caseback stamped with the Baume & Mercier phi emblem. Inside beats the Baume & Mercier calibre BM 11895, a Swiss automatic movement with 30 jewels, its gold-tone rotor signed "THIRTY 30 JEWELS UNADJUSTED SWISS - BAUME & MERCIER - BM 11895". It comes on the integrated Baume & Mercier steel multi-link bracelet with a fold-over clasp, in a Baume & Mercier presentation box with its outer sleeve.

Timegrapher video included: the listing video shows this watch running on a timegrapher after its service.

Brand heritage

Baume & Mercier traces its roots to 1830, when the Baume brothers founded their watchmaking house in the Swiss Jura; the Geneva-based Baume & Mercier name dates from 1918. The Capeland line is known for its classic, highly legible dials inspired by vintage instruments.
