About this watch

A striking rectangular dress watch from Orient of Japan, reference CUTAF002B0 (case UTAF-C0). The dark navy-black dial carries three complications laid out in a beautifully architectural way: a retrograde day-of-week hand at left (Sunday in red), a retrograde date arc at right, and a large silver-ringed 24-hour subdial in the lower half. Polished applied baton markers, silver hands and the Orient crown-and-lions emblem complete a dial that looks far more expensive than it is. The curved, polished stainless steel case sits on a multi-link steel bracelet with fold-over clasp. It comes with its Orient box, the hang tag with the model number, and the bracelet still shows parts of its protective film.

Brand heritage

Orient Watch Co. was founded in Tokyo in 1950 (its roots go back to 1901) and is famous for building its own movements in-house. Today part of Seiko Epson, Orient is known for offering interesting complications such as retrograde displays, power reserves and multi-year calendars at honest prices.
