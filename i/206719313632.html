About this watch

A genuine vintage Eberhard & Co. Goldring Automatic from around the 1960s. The cream/champagne brushed dial carries the signed "Eberhard & Co. automatic" logo, "Goldring" in script above 6, applied gold baton markers (single at 12, double at 6 and 9) and a gold-framed date window at 3 with red numerals. Gold-tone hands and a slim gold seconds hand. The round stainless steel case has sharp, faceted lugs. Please note: replacement caseback in the original style, laser engraved ("Automatic - Eberhard & Co. - Waterproof"); it is not the watch's own factory caseback. Inside is a signed Eberhard & Co. automatic movement, rotor marked "261-123, 25 Jewels, Swiss". Fitted with a dark brown croc-grain leather strap.

Brand heritage

Eberhard & Co. was founded in 1887 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland by Georges-Emile Eberhard and is known for its chronographs and fine dress watches. In the 1950s and 1960s the brand produced elegant automatic date models for everyday wear, of which the Goldring is a classic example.
