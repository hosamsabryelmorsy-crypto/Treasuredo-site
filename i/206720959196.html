About this watch

A genuine Seiko 7016-5001 automatic flyback chronograph from 1973 (the serial number 320313 dates it to March 1973). The deep metallic blue dial carries the applied "SEIKO" logo with "CHRONOGRAPH" printed below, polished baton markers, a day-date window at 3, a grey 30-minute counter at 6 with a bright yellow hand inside an orange rounded-square frame, and the "A" and Seiko lightning symbol at left. Straight steel hour and minute hands with a long central chronograph seconds hand. The large square cushion "TV-screen" stainless steel case has the crown at 3 and chronograph pushers at 2 and 4. The stainless steel caseback is signed SEIKO and reads "Water Resistant, Stainless Steel, 320313, 7016-5001, Japan-A". Inside is the Seiko calibre 7016A automatic column-wheel flyback chronograph movement, 17 jewels. It comes on the signed Seiko stainless steel bracelet ("All Stainless Steel Japan B").

Brand heritage

Seiko launched the 6139 in 1969, one of the first automatic chronographs in the world. The 7016 calibre followed in the early 1970s as a refined automatic chronograph with a flyback function, a single 30-minute register and day-date, and Seiko fitted it in bold 1970s designs such as this square-case 7016-5001, now one of the most sought-after vintage Seiko chronographs.
