About this watch

A genuine Seiko 6602-8050 hand-wound dress watch from 1968 (the serial number 806978 dates it to October 1968). The charcoal-grey sunburst dial carries the applied "SEIKO" logo, faceted double-bar applied hour markers with a wider triple bar at 12, a framed date window at 3, and "WATER PROOF 17 JEWELS" with the Seiko waterproof symbol above 6. Faceted dauphine hour and minute hands and a slim central seconds hand. The round stainless steel case has straight angular lugs and a signed screw-down caseback reading "Stainless Steel 6602-8050 Water Proof", serial 806978, Japan. Inside is the Seiko calibre 6602B manual-wind movement, 17 jewels, with date. It comes on a black padded leather strap with a steel buckle.

Brand heritage

Seiko, founded in Tokyo in 1881 by Kintaro Hattori, became Japan's leading watchmaker and one of the most respected manufactures in the world. The 66-series calibres of the 1960s were Seiko's reliable everyday hand-wound and automatic movements, used in simple, well-built dress watches like this 6602-8050.
