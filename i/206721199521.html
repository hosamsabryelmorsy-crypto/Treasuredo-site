About this watch

A petite vintage ladies dress watch signed Wistar. The plain matte black dial has no markers at all, just the gold "WISTAR" name at upper left, a gold script "Quartz" at lower right and "JAPAN MOVT" along the edge, with slim gold-tone hour, minute and seconds hands. The dial is framed by a full ring of channel-set clear stones inside a polished yellow gold-tone round case with elegant teardrop lugs and a gold-tone crown at 3. The stainless steel snap caseback reads "Stainless Steel Back, Base Metal Bezel, Water Resistant". Worn on a black croc-grain leather strap with a gold-tone buckle.
