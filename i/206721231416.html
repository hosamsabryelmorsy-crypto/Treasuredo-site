About this clock

A genuine vintage Gucci travel and desk alarm clock, model 0900, from the 1990s. The matte black dial carries the gold GUCCI logo below 12, slim gold baton hour markers and "SWISS MADE" at 6, with gold hour and minute hands, a slim gold alarm hand and a gold seconds hand with a red tip. The round yellow gold-plated case has a polished domed bezel set with four raised blocks bearing the black-enamelled interlocking GG logo at 12, 3, 6 and 9, and two GG-signed crowns on the right-hand side, one to set the time and one to set the alarm. A thin gold-plated wire loop is hinged at the top: swing it out behind the clock and it stands on a desk or bedside table. The back is engraved with the Gucci crest and "GUCCI", the model number 0900 (the first digit is worn) and serial number 0001994.

Brand heritage

Founded in Florence in 1921 by Guccio Gucci, the house became one of the great names of Italian luxury. Gucci's Swiss-made timepieces and desk accessories of the 1980s and 1990s, with the interlocking GG emblem worked into the design, are now popular vintage collectibles.
