About this item

A pair of vintage Menrad rimless sunglasses, model 388. The large frameless lenses have a bold octagonal outline with a flat top and cut corners, and a brown gradient tint that is darker at the top and fades to very light at the bottom. The slim metal parts are finished in a metallic copper-red: a curved keyhole bridge, small end pieces screwed to the lenses at the outer corners, and very thin straight temples that end in dark brown temple tips. Clear nose pads sit on adjustable pad arms. A light, airy statement shape from the golden era of rimless eyewear.

Brand heritage

Menrad is a German eyewear maker founded in 1894 in Schwäbisch Gmünd, and one of the long-established names of German optical frame making. Its vintage sunglasses are collected for their quality metalwork and distinctive shapes.
