A vintage Gianfranco Ferré Lunettes optical frame, model GFF 50/N in colour 40F, from the late 1980s / early 1990s. Thin matte black oval rims are joined by a high arched gold-tone keyhole bridge with adjustable clear nose pads. Gold-tone end pieces lead into slim temples that are gold-tone at the front and matte black towards the tips. The frame still carries its original clear demo lenses, with the printed GIANFRANCO FERRE LUNETTES logo on one lens.

Brand heritage

Gianfranco Ferré (1944-2007) trained as an architect in Milan and became known as "the architect of fashion". He founded his own label in 1978 and was artistic director of Christian Dior from 1989 to 1996. His eyewear of the 1980s and 1990s was made in Italy under licence by Safilo, and is collected for its sculpted shapes and fine metal details.
