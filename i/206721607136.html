About this item

A pair of vintage John Sterling aviator sunglasses, model J.S. 2004, colour 003, made in Italy in the 1980s. The thin matte black metal teardrop rims are topped by a wide flat brow bar that steps down into a squared block over the nose, with a clear crystal bridge piece and clear nose pads underneath. Flat black hinge blocks with small gold-tone screws lead into slim matte black temples, and the outer temples carry the John Sterling script logo with its round emblem in gold. The frame is fitted with brown gradient lenses.

Brand heritage

John Sterling was an Italian eyewear label of the 1980s whose frames, marked "J.S." with a model number and "Made in Italy", are sought after today by collectors of 1980s Italian eyewear for their bold shapes and lacquered and gold-plated finishes.
