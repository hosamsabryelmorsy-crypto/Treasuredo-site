About this item

A pair of Emmanuelle Khanh Paris sunglasses, stamped 1601-98-16 inside the temple. The bold black acetate frame has a rectangular wrap shape with wide temples. Both outer corners of the front are set with brown reptile-pattern panels cut by rows of clear rhinestones, and a scattered line of clear stones with small gold-tone studs runs along the top and bottom of each lens rim. Each temple carries the metal "EK" monogram. The lenses are a neutral gray-brown tint. A glamorous, unmistakably French piece.

Brand heritage

Emmanuelle Khanh was a Paris fashion designer who rose to fame in the 1960s as one of the leading names of French ready-to-wear. In the 1970s she launched her own eyewear line, hand-made in France, and her oversized, sculpted frames with snakeskin, lizard and jewelled details became icons worn by stars and collectors alike.
