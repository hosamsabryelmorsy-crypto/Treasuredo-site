About this item

A pair of Ray-Ban Original Wayfarer sunglasses from the Rare Prints collection, stamped SPECIAL SERIES #2 and "Officially Licensed by MTA". The classic trapezoid acetate front is translucent cream, printed all over with small black hand-drawn faces and figures; the outer temples carry the same print and the silver Ray-Ban script plaque, while the inner temples are plain cream. Fitted with light grey gradient lenses with the Ray-Ban script logo at the top corner of the right lens. RB2140-A is the Asian-fit version of the Wayfarer, with a slightly higher nose bridge for a lower-sitting fit.

Brand heritage

Ray-Ban introduced the Wayfarer in 1952, and it became one of the best-known sunglass shapes ever made. The Rare Prints Special Series of the early 2010s dressed the Original Wayfarer RB2140 in limited graphic prints, each series numbered on the temple, and these editions are now sought after by collectors.
