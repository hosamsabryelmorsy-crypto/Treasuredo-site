About this watch

An elegant ladies' Swatch Skin, the ultra-thin line that made Swatch famous for the slimmest plastic watches in the world. The clear transparent case frames a wide brushed gold-tone ring around a plain glossy black centre dial with slim gold baton hands and a small gold "swatch SWISS" signature. Gold-tone end pieces at 12 and 6 are each set with a round black cabochon, and they open into a sculptural bangle made of two curved gold-tone bands that form open loops around the wrist, closed by a small slide clasp. Minimal, dressy and very light on the wrist.

Brand heritage

Swatch was launched in Switzerland in 1983 and grew into the Swatch Group, today the world's largest watchmaker and home of brands such as Omega, Longines and Tissot. The Swatch Skin collection, introduced in 1997, set a record for the thinnest plastic watch case, and it has stayed one of Swatch's most popular lines for dress watches.
