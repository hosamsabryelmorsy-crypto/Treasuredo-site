Brand heritage

S.T. Dupont was founded in Paris in 1872 by Simon Tissot-Dupont as a maker of fine leather goods and travel cases for an elite clientele, before becoming world famous for its luxury lighters and pens. Leather goods remain at the heart of the house today.
