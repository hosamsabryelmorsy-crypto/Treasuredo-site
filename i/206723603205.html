About this watch

A classic late-1960s / 1970s Sicura Submarine diver with a rotating world-time bezel. The bezel is printed with 24 world cities (WELLINGTON / LONDON, PARIS / ROME, HONOLULU / JOHANNESBURG, NEW-YORK / BANGKOK, TOKIO, SYDNEY and more) and has a knurled, dotted outer edge. The glossy black dial carries a fine white crosshair grid, the SICURA logo with its wave emblem, SUBMARINE and 23 JEWELS under 12, and SUPERWATERPROOF / 400 / VACUUM TESTED / ALL SWISS MADE below the centre, with SIC and TRI printed beside the 6. Cream-aged luminous numerals 12, 9 and 6, luminous baton markers, sword hands with aged lume, an arrow-tipped seconds hand and a date window at 3 complete the tool-watch look. The screw-down stainless steel caseback is engraved with the Sicura diver emblem and reads "WATERPROOF 200 VACUUM TESTED STAINLESS STEEL BACK SICURA SWISS". Inside is a Swiss 23-jewel hand-wound movement signed SWISS 23 JEWELS, calibre SIC 48.

Brand heritage

Sicura was a Swiss watch company based in Grenchen, known in the 1960s and 70s for affordable, robust diver's and sports watches. In 1979 Sicura's owner Ernest Schneider bought the rights to the Breitling name, which is why vintage Sicura divers are often linked with Breitling's revival.
