About this watch

A charming mid-century Swiss triple calendar signed TELL. The aged cream dial carries two windows below 12 for the day and month in French (here "VEN" for vendredi and "DEC" for decembre), a printed outer date ring from 1 to 31 read by a slim blued steel pointer hand with an arrow tip, and a small seconds subdial with concentric guilloche above the 6. Black Arabic numerals, a railroad minute track and gold leaf hands complete a classic 1940s-1950s layout. The round gold-plated case has elegant long curved, stepped "horn" lugs and a domed crystal, closed by a stainless steel snap back.

Brand heritage

Tell watches were made in Switzerland in the mid-20th century and are found today mainly as hand-wound dress watches and calendars of the 1940s to 1970s. The triple calendar, showing day, month and date together, was one of the most sought-after complications of the 1940s and 1950s, and French-language versions like this one were made for the French-speaking European markets.
