About this watch

A vintage Swiss Made Rodania dress watch with the colour portrait dial of Saddam Hussein, the former President of Iraq. The champagne gold dial shows his portrait in olive military uniform on the left side, "RODANIA" with the brand's round three-spoke logo at the right, black-filled gold baton hour markers, slim gold hour and minute hands and "SWISS MADE" at the bottom. The round gold plated case measures 32mm (about 36mm lug to lug) with a gold crown at 3 and a stainless steel snap caseback. Worn on a brown leather strap.

Brand heritage

Rodania is a Swiss watch brand founded in 1930 in Grenchen, Switzerland, and best known in Belgium and across export markets. Portrait dials of Saddam Hussein were made on watches from many Swiss and Japanese brands during the 1980s, and they are now sought-after pieces of Middle Eastern history.
