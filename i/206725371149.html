Brand heritage

Zodiac was founded in 1882 in Le Locle, Switzerland, by Ariste Calame and is best known for the Sea Wolf, one of the first purpose-built dive watches of the 1950s. Original signed vintage Zodiac bracelets are sought after by collectors restoring their Zodiac watches.
