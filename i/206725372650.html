About this watch

A rare, documented piece of Middle Eastern history: an Art Deco rectangular Swiss mechanical wristwatch with a Tavannes Watch Co. movement, presented as a souvenir of the royal visit to the Egyptian front line in the 1948 Palestine war, in the era of the Kingdom of Egypt. The caseback is engraved in Arabic calligraphy with a four-line inscription: "تذكار الزيارة الملكية الكريمة لميدان القتال بفلسطين - ٦ يوليو سنة ١٩٤٨", which translates as "Souvenir of the gracious royal visit to the battlefield in Palestine - 6 July 1948". The aged tan/bronze dial has bold black Arabic numerals, a signed gothic-script logo below the 12 and a rectangular small seconds subdial above the 6.

Brand heritage

Tavannes Watch Co. was founded in 1891 in Tavannes, in the Swiss Jura, and became one of the major Swiss movement and watch manufacturers of the early 20th century, well known for its shaped (rectangular) movements for Art Deco wristwatches. Tavannes movements were supplied to many export brands and retailers.
