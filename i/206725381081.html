About this item

A spare link for the Breitling Rouleaux ("bullet") bracelet in brushed stainless steel. It has the typical Rouleaux construction: staggered rows of rounded, bullet-shaped steel tubes held together by a pin, with one short open tube and one capped end piece on its pin. Useful for lengthening a Rouleaux bracelet or replacing a damaged link.

Brand heritage

Breitling introduced the Rouleaux bracelet with its relaunched Chronomat in the 1980s. Its rows of rounded steel "bullets" became one of the most recognisable Breitling bracelet designs and was fitted to many Chronomat and other Breitling models.
