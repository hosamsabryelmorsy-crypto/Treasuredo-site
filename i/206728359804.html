About this watch

A collectible club watch: The textured white-silver dial with bold black Roman numerals carries a blue emblem "Al Nasr SC", the coat of arms of the Dubai sports club, along with the Continental logo and "Swiss Made". The curved tonneau tank case measures 27 x 33 mm and gives the watch an elegant Art Deco profile.

The steel back is stamped with the Continental crown emblem, "Steel Back", "Water Resistant" and reference 5049. It mounts to a dark blue leather strap with the Continental crest on the keeper and comes with its white Continental box.

Brand heritage

The Continental name was registered in 1924 by Schild & Cie, manufacturers of the Hebdomas watch. Since 1953 it has been operated by Continental Watches AG of Sissach, which registered the compass arrow logo still visible on this dial, and in 1967 the brand switched to Economic Swiss Time, becoming its main brand in 1977. Continental enjoyed a renaissance in the late 80s and 90s, when watches like this were sold widely in the Gulf.
