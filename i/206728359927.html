Brand heritage

The Continental name was registered in 1924 by Schild & Cie, manufacturers of the Hebdomas watch. From 1953 it was run by Continental Uhren AG of Sissach, which registered the compass arrow logo still visible on this dial, and in 1967 the brand switched to Economic Swiss Time, becoming its main brand in 1977. Continental experienced a revival in the late 1980s and 1990s, when watches like this were widely sold in the Gulf.
